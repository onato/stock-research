Our points of difference
How is NZAI differentiated in the automotive market?


 1. Our target market is different - High quality, more affordable cars
      • 2 Cheap Cars has been a disruptor in the automotive retail market with its high volume, low
         margin business model
      • Price point $5k-$15k
      • We focus on middle NZ, “helping Kiwis afford great cars”

 2. Our vehicle sourcing is different - Japan-based procurement team
      • We are one of the biggest importers of secondhand vehicles from Japan into New Zealand with
         approximately 7.5% market share - we don’t rely on domestic supply
      • We have our own people on the ground in Japan

 3. Our objectives are different - Accelerating the shift towards climate friendly vehicles
      • We support lower emission vehicles, EV and HEV - opportunity
      • Digital channels - opportunity
      • Powerhouse in the automotive sector focused on used car market

YEAR TO DATE performance

NZAI has had a successful first four months of the year in the lead up to the latest
Covid lockdown.

•   Revenues are up 37% to $24.7m for 4 months to July 21, compared to the same period in 2020

•   Vehicle sales up 28% YTD on the same period last year

•   Stronger finance penetration across the group YTD

•   NZMF loan book up 18% to $4.5m since March 2021

•   Revenues and income across the Group are up 38% to $25.2m to July 2021

•   Cost management focus, responding to inflationary headwinds across labour, shipping and compliance
