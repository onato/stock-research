New Talisman Gold Mines Limited (NTL.NZ)

Pre-commercial narrow-vein gold/silver explorer · Karangahake goldfield, NZ · FY end 31-Mar · NZD

⚠

Going Concern & Audit Disclaimer Warning

Second consecutive full DISCLAIMER OF OPINION (FY2025 and FY2026, Baker Tilly Staples Rodway) — the auditor could not obtain sufficient evidence on the recoverable amount of the NZ$15.6m Talisman Mine cash-generating unit, roughly 92% of total shareholders’ equity.

FY2026 cash of only NZ$1.18m against operating cash outflow of ~NZ$2.0m/year implies well under a year of self-funded runway absent a further capital raise. Management states explicitly that further capital will be required with no assurance it will be available at all or on acceptable terms.

ASX secondary listing has been suspended since June 2024 for inadequate financial condition. Shares on issue have nearly doubled since FY2024 (~625m → ~793m) purely to fund cash burn, with a further 4-for-5 rights issue completed March 2026.

Dilution & Capital Structure

Shares Outstanding

FY2019–FY2023 are NULL by design: a 10:1 share consolidation in FY2023 breaks comparability with pre-consolidation counts. Post-FY2023 growth (~587k → ~1.18bn implied units differ by consolidation basis) reflects an unbroken 8-year pattern of dilutive cash raises.

Shareholders’ Equity

Book equity is propped up by repeated capital injections, not earnings. ~92% of FY2026 equity (NZ$16.98m) is the Talisman Mine CGU, whose recoverable amount the auditor could not verify.

Balance Sheet: Assets, Liabilities & Debt

Total debt has stayed minimal throughout — NTL.NZ funds itself almost entirely through equity issuance, not borrowing.
