Tosoh Corporation is a Japanese chemical company established in 1935
  and listed on the First Section of the Tokyo Stock Exchange. It is the
parent of the Tosoh Group, which comprises 100 companies worldwide
 and a multiethnic workforce of over 11,500 people and generated net
      sales of ¥809.7 billion in fiscal 2015, ended March 31, 2015.
