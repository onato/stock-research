§3 Business Review for the Reporting Period
For the 9 months ended 30 September 2016, the Company realized gross written premiums
(GWPs) of RMB189.101 billion, representing an increase of 17.9% as compared to the same
period of last year. GWPs from CPIC Life amounted to RMB117.335 billion, representing an
increase of 31.0% as compared to the same period of last year. GWPs from CPIC P/C
amounted to RMB71.594 billion, representing an increase of 1.1% as compared to the same
period of last year.


CPIC Life
For the 9 months ended 30 September 2016, GWPs from CPIC Life amounted to RMB117.335
billion, representing an increase of 31.0% as compared to the same period of last year. In
particular, GWPs from new policies amounted to RMB43.483 billion, while GWPs from
renewed policies amounted to RMB73.852 billion. The individual channel maintained its rapid
growth and GWPs from new policies of this very channel reached RMB35.822 billion,
representing an increase of 44.6% as compared to the same period of last year.
