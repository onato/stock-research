§3 Business Review for the Reporting Period
During the reporting period, the Company realized gross written premiums (GWPs) of
RMB62.342 billion, representing an increase of 0.7% as compared to the same period of last
year. GWPs from CPIC Life amounted to RMB37.918 billion, representing a decrease of 1.1% as
compared to the same period of last year. GWPs from CPIC P/C amounted to RMB24.404
billion, representing an increase of 3.8% as compared to the same period of last year.
