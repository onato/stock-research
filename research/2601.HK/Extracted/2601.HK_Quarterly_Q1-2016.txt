§3 Business Review for the Reporting Period
During the reporting period, the Company realized gross written premiums (GWPs) of
RMB78.217 billion, representing an increase of 25.5% as compared to the same period of last
year. GWPs from CPIC Life amounted to RMB52.350 billion, representing an increase of 38.1%
as compared to the same period of last year. GWPs from CPIC P/C amounted to RMB25.830
billion, representing an increase of 5.8% as compared to the same period of last year.
