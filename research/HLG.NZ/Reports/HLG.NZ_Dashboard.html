Hallenstein Glasson Holdings (HLG.NZ)

Apparel Retail — Glassons & Hallensteins | NZX | FY ends ~1 August | NZD millions

Investment case: HLG is an income-oriented compounder, not a high-growth multi-bagger — roughly 80-90% earnings payout (FY2025 DPS $0.51 / EPS $0.66), no debt, no buybacks, negligible SBC, flat share count. The realistic return profile is a dividend yield of about 4.8% at the current price plus modest per-share value growth, rather than capital-light reinvestment compounding. Owner FCF in the DCF below deducts IFRS-16 lease principal repayments (~$30m FY2025) from reported FCF to reflect the true, rent-inclusive cash cost of running the store network.
