       Dividend reinvestment plans
       A dividend reinvestment plan (DRP) will operate in respect of the interim dividend. The key terms of the Tabcorp DRP are:

       No discount is applicable to shares allocated to participants under the DRP.
       No brokerage, commission or other transaction costs will be payable by participants on shares acquired under the DRP.
       Shares will be allocated to DRP participants on 16 March 2016 and will rank equally in all respects with existing shares.
       Shares allocated to participants under the DRP will be acquired on market.
       The price at which shares are allocated under the DRP is the daily volume weighted average market price of Tabcorp shares sold in the
       ordinary course of trading on the Australian Securities Exchange over a period of 10 business days beginning on the second business day
       after the dividend record date.
