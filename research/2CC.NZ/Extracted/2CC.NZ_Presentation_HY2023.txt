     HY23 Business overview
     • Vehicle retail business stabilising

     • Improved sales and marketing capability re-established

     • Total vehicle sales up 11% compared with HY22

     • Online sales accounted for 23% of total sales

     • Sales of EVs and HEVs doubled to 40% of total sales

     • Finance & Insurance penetration down from 32% to 26% (impact of CCCFA lending regulations and rising
       interest rates)

     • NZ Motor Finance lending paused under review by the Board
