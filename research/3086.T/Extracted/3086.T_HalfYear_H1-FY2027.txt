    In the six months ended August 31, 2026 (from March 1, 2026 to August 31, 2026), consolidated sales
    revenue decreased by 2.8% year on year to ¥213,821 million, primarily due to a decline following sales
    in the Department Store Business related to Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai from the previous year, the impact
    of store closures due to large-scale renovations at the Daimaru Umeda store, and a decline following
    large orders related to interior and facility projects in the Developer Business from the previous year.
    While gross profit slightly decreased year on year, selling, general and administrative expenses
    increased, resulting in business profit of ¥26,591 million, a 5.6% decrease year on year, operating profit
    of ¥26,813 million, a 10.5% decrease year on year, and profit attributable to owners of parent of ¥17,379
    million, a 5.3% decrease year on year.
    During the six months ended August 31, 2026, the Japanese economy showed signs of a gradual
    recovery due to firm personal consumption on the back of an improvement in employment and income
    conditions, among other factors. In addition, consumption by foreign tourists visiting Japan remained
    steady on the back of the yen’s depreciation, etc., despite being affected by Japan-China relations. On the
    other hand, we recognize the need to continue monitoring the downside risks to the economy, such as the
    persistently high energy prices due to the situation in the Middle East, the impact of rising prices on
    consumption, and fluctuations in the financial and foreign exchange markets.
    Under these circumstances, we are focusing on further deepening the retail businesses, evolving Group
    synergies, and strengthening the Group’s management foundation as the culmination of the Medium-
    term Business Plan (FY2024-FY2026), which we position as a “period of change” towards achieving the
    Group’s vision of transforming into a “Value Co-creation Retailer.”
    As part of our efforts for deepening the retail businesses, in the Department Store Business and SC
    Business, we strengthened initiatives to expand the customer base in Japan and overseas, improve the
    appeal of customer contact points, and enhance content. In the Department Store Business, we promoted
    a large-scale renovation of the Daimaru Umeda store and carried out a complete renovation of the
    Daimaru Ashiya store for the first time in five years. Additionally, we worked on creating new customer
    touchpoints, such as opening the first physical store of the cosmetics online media “DEPACO.” In the SC
    Business, we continued to proceed with large-scale renovations centered on unique fashion and IP
    content mainly at Shinsaibashi PARCO and Ikebukuro PARCO.
    In evolving Group synergies, we opened “HAERA” on June 11, a new commercial facility that combines
    a department store and PARCO to enhance the appeal of the Nagoya Sakae area. Furthermore, to create
    vibrancy in the area, we worked with other companies in the area to hold an event that promotes foot
    traffic centered on entertainment content called “POP IS YOU SAKAE26,” in collaboration with
    Matsuzakaya, PARCO, and HAERA.
    In the ownership and development of in-house content, we decided to acquire shares of Pieroth Japan
    K.K. to provide experiential value for high-quality, high-excitement consumer segments, thereby
    expanding our business foundation in the food sector. The game label PARCO GAMES, which began its

With regard to the performance of the Department Store Business in the six months ended August 31,
2026, sales revenue was ¥128,351 million, down 1.0% year on year, business profit was ¥15,586 million,
down 2.6% year on year, and operating profit was ¥16,360 million, down 1.7% year on year. Sales
revenue decreased mainly due to floor closures following the renovation of the Daimaru Umeda store
and a decline from the previous year’s sales related to Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai, despite the expansion
of sales to affluent customers, particularly in out-of-store sales, and duty-free sales. Additionally, both
business profit and operating profit decreased due to the increase in advertising expenses associated with
sales promotion, as well as the increase in personnel expenses primarily from base pay raises. Against
the April announced figures, sales revenue decreased, but each profit line increased.
As part of the key strategies based on the Medium-term Business Plan, we carried out a complete
renovation of the Daimaru Ashiya store for the first time in five years to enhance the store’s appeal.
Within the store, we worked on creating new customer touchpoints in ways such as enhancing the
selection of high-quality foods and opening the first physical store of the cosmetics information media
and online store “DEPACO.” Additionally, with the opening of “HAERA” on June 11, we promoted
collaborative measures with companies both inside and outside the Group to further enhance the appeal
of the Nagoya Sakae area.
To further strengthen our affluent business, we worked on enhancing the experiential value at out-of-
store sales events. Additionally, the App Gold Membership service, which started in the current fiscal
year, has seen a steady increase in registered users.
In addition, to ensure steady growth in duty-free sales, we are working on implementing campaigns in
collaboration with overseas payment processors and enhancing CRM for inbound tourism, both of which
are progressing smoothly.
Focusing our efforts on enhancing area value, we will continue to strengthen our affluent business and
strengthen and promote the capture of inbound demand, working towards expanding our customer base
and creating new demand.

With regard to the performance of the SC Business in the six months ended August 31, 2026, sales
revenue was ¥35,515 million, up 7.3% year on year, business profit was ¥8,434 million, up 1.3% year on
year, and operating profit was ¥7,648 million, down 19.8% year on year. Sales revenue increased mainly
due to strong performance at Shibuya PARCO, which underwent large-scale renovations last year, and
HAERA, which opened in June. Although selling, general and administrative expenses exceeded those of
the previous year, business profit increased. Operating profit decreased due to the absence of gains
recorded last year from lease cancellations associated with the sale of the former Matsumoto PARCO’s
real estate and the recording of a loss on business liquidation. Against the April announced figures, sales
revenue increased, but each profit line decreased.
As a key strategy of the Medium-term Business Plan, we are promoting renovations of mainly flagship
stores as part of the building frame restructuring initiative that will structurally evolve store operations.
We advanced large-scale renovations at Shinsaibashi PARCO to create new experiential value, marking
the first major renovation since its opening in 2020. Additionally, at Ikebukuro PARCO, we promoted the
largest renovation since its opening, centered on fashion and popular culture. Additionally, Nagoya
PARCO and Sendai PARCO are promoting renovations from this autumn through winter.
As part of efforts to enhance content, we are also developing initiatives overseas, such as selling
collaboration T-shirts with popular anime in Taipei, Hong Kong, and Seoul through a partnership with
GEEKS RULE. Additionally, the game label PARCO GAMES, which began its publishing business in
2025, is expanding its operations by launching “Finding Polka” as its fourth title and announcing the
publishing of its fifth title “DEPERSON.”
In addition, taking the opportunity of opening of the new “HAERA” commercial facility, we are focusing
on deepening collaboration with companies both inside and outside the Group and further enhancing the
appeal of the Nagoya Sakae area.

With regard to the performance of the Payment and Finance Business in the six months ended August 31,
2026, sales revenue was ¥7,230 million, up 9.2% year on year, business profit was ¥789 million, up
75.7% year on year, and operating profit was ¥756 million, up 76.1% year on year. Sales revenue
increased because of higher affiliated store fee revenue and higher finance fees due to an expansion in
transaction volume, despite an increase in point costs and a decrease in revenue from annual membership
fees. As a result, both business profit and operating profit increased significantly.
Against the April announced figures, sales revenue and each profit line decreased.
Taking the completion of the consolidation of the Group’s cards in the previous fiscal year as an
opportunity, we promoted measures for the acquisition of each card’s members, and the number of cards
issued in the six months ended August 31, 2026 increased year on year.
Card transaction volume increased compared to the same period last year, primarily driven by strong
usage by department stores’ out-of-store sales customers and the effects of expanding and optimizing
credit limits in the finance sector, which has been strengthened in recent years.
In the Affiliated Store Business, the acquisition of affiliates is progressing smoothly, particularly in the
key areas, and the transaction volume from affiliates is also on an increasing trend.
Regarding our initiative to combat the unauthorized use of credit cards, a challenge for the industry, the
effect of various measures has led to a significant reduction in unauthorized use.

(Subsequent events)
    Business combination by acquisition
    On September 1, 2026, we acquired all shares of Claret Holdings Co., Ltd. which owns Pieroth Japan
    K.K., a company engaged in the sale of imported wines, and made it a subsidiary.
    As a result of this share acquisition, PJ Holdings Co., Ltd., a subsidiary of Claret Holdings Co., Ltd.,
    became our sub-subsidiary, and Pieroth Japan K.K., a sub-subsidiary of Claret Holdings Co., Ltd.,
    became our sub-sub-subsidiary.

    1.   Outline of business combination
         (1) Names of acquired companies and descriptions of their businesses
             Name of acquired company     Claret Holdings Co., Ltd.
             Business description         Management of subsidiaries, etc.
             Name of acquired company     PJ Holdings Co., Ltd.
             Business description         Management of subsidiaries, etc.
             Name of acquired company     Pieroth Japan K.K.
             Business description         Sale of imported wine, etc.

         (2) Main reason for business combination
             Pieroth Japan is an imported wine sales company that handles high-quality wines from around
             the world and conducts direct sales to customers through nationwide sales offices, tasting events,
             wine bar operations, and more.
             We determined that Pieroth Japan’s long-standing ability to build direct relationships with
             customers and provide experiential value has a high affinity with the Group’s emphasis on
             building long-term relationships with customers, including in the out-of-store sales business,
             leading to this share acquisition.
             By combining Pieroth Japan’s product strength, sales capabilities, and planning skills with the
             Group’s customer base, stores, events, out-of-store sales, gifts, and digital touchpoints, we will
             work on creating new experiential value centered around wine.

         (3) Date of business combination
             September 1, 2026

         (4) Legal form of business combination
             Acquisition of shares for cash as consideration

         (5) Ratio of voting rights after acquisition
             100%
