    In the three months ended May 31, 2025 (from March 1, 2025 to May 31, 2025), consolidated sales
    revenue was primarily driven by strong performance in the SC (Shopping Center) Business and the
    Developer Business, although duty free sales in the Department Store Business significantly decreased
    compared to the previous year. As a result, consolidated sales revenue was ¥110,802 million, up 9.2%
    year on year, business profit was ¥13,874 million, down 15.4% year on year, operating profit was
    ¥15,990 million, down 0.9% year on year, and profit attributable to owners of parent was ¥10,483
    million, down 7.4% year on year.
    During the three months ended May 31, 2025, the Japanese economy showed signs of a gradual recovery
    on the back of an improvement in employment and income conditions, among other factors. Meanwhile,
    we recognize that we need to keep a close eye on the downward pressure on consumer sentiment due
    mainly to the outlook for domestic and overseas economies and rising prices amid mounting
    uncertainties due to U.S. trade policies and increasing geopolitical risks, etc.
    Under these circumstances, in the Medium-term Business Plan (FY2024-FY2026) which began in the
    previous fiscal year, the Company is focusing on further strengthening the retail businesses, particularly
    the Department Store Business and the SC Business, evolving Group synergies to realize dramatic
    growth, and strengthening the Group’s management foundation to enhance the effectiveness of these
    strategies.
    As part of efforts to further strengthen the retail businesses, in the mainstay Department Store Business
    and SC Business, we strengthened initiatives to expand the customer base in Japan and overseas,
    improve the appeal of customer contact points, and enhance content. As an expansion of content for the
    premium and aspirational consumer group, in the Department Store Business, we implemented a large-
    scale renovation of the Matsuzakaya Nagoya store in the previous year to prepare for deeper cultivation
    of existing customers and the winning of next-generation customers, and conducted a gradual reopening,
    floor by floor. Additionally, the official store opened at the Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai venue has been
    well received by customers due to factors such as the original spatial design where visitors can
    experience a Japanese sense of aesthetics, as well as, wide range of products including varieties of
    original products. In the SC Business, we promoted a large-scale renovation of flagship stores such as
    Shibuya PARCO, Hiroshima PARCO, Sendai PARCO. At Shinsaibashi PARCO, the Daimaru Umeda
    store, and the Daimaru Shinsaibashi store, we ran a South Korean fashion brand event in collaboration
    with South Korean Hyundai Department Store, a strategic partner of PARCO.
    To evolve Group synergies, following the launch of the GINZA SIX Card and PARCO Card last fiscal
    year, we launched the Hakata Daimaru Card in March. Additionally, as part of our efforts to realize our
    goal of becoming a “Value Co-creation Retailer,” we established a new joint venture company with
    Komehyo Co., Ltd. and entered the reuse business.
    As part of efforts to strengthen the Group’s management foundation, under our financial strategy, with
    the aim of improving medium- to long-term return on equity and optimizing equity capital, we decided to
    purchase up to 11.5 million of treasury shares for a maximum amount of 15 billion yen. This purchase
    commenced on April 15. In order to promote sustainability management aimed at tackling social issues

With regard to the performance of the Department Store Business in the three months ended May 31,
2025, sales revenue was ¥63,598 million, up 1.6% year on year, business profit was ¥8,277 million,
down 21.8% year on year, and operating profit was ¥9,052 million, down 12.5% year on year.
While duty-free sales declined primarily for luxury items including luxury brands in conjunction with
exchange rate fluctuations, etc., sales to domestic customers were firm. By store, the Daimaru Umeda
store recorded a sharp increase in sales owning to significantly increased customer traffic following the
start of Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai. On the other hand, the Daimaru Shinsaibashi store and the Daimaru
Kyoto store experienced a decline in sales mainly due to the decrease in duty-free sales. Business profit
decreased due to an increase in fees, as well as higher utilities and business consignment expenses.
As duty-free sales, which grew beyond initial expectations in the last fiscal year, declined, we focused on
out-of-store operating activities to strengthen our response to the robust domestic affluent market. We
recorded record-high sales and attendance at the event held for out-of-store sales customers in May.
Additionally, as part of our initiatives to enhance the attractiveness of stores, for the Matsuzakaya
Nagoya store renovation which started a few years ago, we expanded content for young, affluent
customers who will become next-generation customers, and strengthened new lifestyle proposals that
capture next-generation market needs, including luxury brands, fashion, art, and alcoholic beverages.
Additionally, the official store opened at the Expo 2025 Osaka, Kansai venue handles Company original
merchandise and other items that embody a Japanese sense of aesthetics, culture, and traditions, and
attracts many customers from both Japan and abroad.

With regard to the performance of the SC Business in the three months ended May 31, 2025, sales
revenue was ¥16,636 million, up 4.8% year on year, business profit was ¥4,180 million, up 4.4% year on

Sales revenue increased due to increased store leasing revenue owing to the benefits of store renovations
carried out and continued brisk performance of sales to inbound tourists, as well as the growth of
common service fees, payment fee income, etc.

With regard to the performance of the Payment and Finance Business in the three months ended May 31,
2025, sales revenue was ¥3,140 million, down 1.5% year on year, business profit was ¥91 million, down
76.3% year on year, and operating profit was ¥89 million, down 74.1% year on year.
Sales revenue decreased partly due to an increase in point cost, despite higher affiliated store fee revenue
due to an expansion in card transaction volume, affiliate business transaction volume, etc. Selling,
general, and administrative expenses increased due to actual costs for the issuance of new cards and
advertising expenses, and as a result, business profit decreased.
Under the Medium-term Business Plan, we began issuing the PARCO Card in February 2025 and the
Hakata Daimaru Card in March 2025, completing the consolidation of cards within the Group. Along
with the expansion of the membership base triggered by this card consolidation, we are working on
expanding transaction volume through use promotion initiatives and credit limit optimization, as well as
acquiring affiliates, particularly in key areas of the Affiliated Store Business. Also, regarding our
initiative to combat the unauthorized use of credit cards, a challenge for the industry, the introduction of

(Segment information)
   (1) Overview of reportable segments
            The reportable segments of the Group are constituent units of the Group for which separate financial
            information is obtainable. These segments are periodically examined by the Board of Directors for
            the purpose of deciding the allocation of management resources and evaluating business results.
            The Group is comprised, under a holding company structure, of the reportable segments
            “Department Store Business,” “SC Business,” “Developer Business” and “Payment and Finance
            Business,” with the Department Store Business at its core.
            The Department Store Business carries out the sale of clothing, general goods, household goods,
            food products and others. The SC Business undertakes development, management, supervision and
            operation, etc. of shopping centers. The Developer Business carries out development, sales,
            supervision, operation, interior decorating work, etc. of real estate. The Payment and Finance
            Business undertakes issuance and administration, etc. of credit cards.
            Due to reorganization within the Group as of September 1, 2024, a part of the management business
            of J. Front One Partner Co., Ltd., which had been included in “Other,” was transferred to PARCO
            SPACE SYSTEMS Co., Ltd. that is included in the “Developer Business” and another company. As a
            result, results have been retroactively adjusted as if the transfer took place at the beginning of the
            fiscal year ended February 28, 2025 (as of March 1, 2024).
