III. Other reminders
Other important information about the Company’s operating conditions during the reporting period that investors need
to be reminded of and pay attention to
Applicable Not applicable

During the reporting period, the Company continued to implement the overarching principle of “improving quality,
increasing production, controlling costs, and boosting profitability”, core financial indicators hit a new record high: the
Group realised an operating income of RMB98.5 billion, representing an increase of 25% compared with the same
period last year; net profit attributable to owners of the parent amounted to RMB20.1 billion, representing an increase
of 98% compared with the same period last year. Net cash flows from operating activities amounted to RMB27.8
billion, representing an increase of 122% compared with the same period last year.

For the gold segment, 23.5 tonnes of mine-produced gold were produced, representing an increase of 23% compared
with the same period last year. The Akyem Gold Mine in Ghana and the Raygorodok Gold Mine in Kazakhstan, both
newly acquired by Zijin Gold International in 2025, have begun to contribute incremental output. For the copper
segment, 260 thousand tonnes of mine-produced copper were produced, excluding the impact of production decline at
the Kamoa-Kakula Copper Mine, other mines advanced their full-year production plans in an orderly manner. Since
phase 2 of the Julong Copper Mine commenced production in late January 2026, its production capacity has been
ramping up continuously. In the first quarter, mine-produced copper output reached 60 thousand tonnes. For the
lithium segment, 16 thousand tonnes of lithium carbonate equivalent were produced. After the production
commencement, the production capacity of the Tres Quebradas Salar, Lakkor Tso Salar and Xiangyuan Hard Rock
Lithium Mine are steadily ramping up. The construction of the Manono Northeast Lithium Project is progressing
steadily. At present, the main process lines for mining and dense medium processing have been fully connected. The
project is expected to complete construction and commence production in June 2026. The Company expects to achieve
120 thousand tonnes of lithium carbonate equivalent in 2026 and has also planned to increase output to 270 thousand
to 320 thousand tonnes by 2028. By then, the Company will become one of the world’s largest lithium producers.
Benefiting from the wave of the energy transition and the upward trend in lithium prices, the lithium segment is
expected to become the Company’s new core growth engine for profits.
