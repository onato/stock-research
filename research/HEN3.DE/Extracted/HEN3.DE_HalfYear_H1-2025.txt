                               One-time expenses in the first half of 2025 of 51 million euros mainly relate to an adjustment of pension
                               provisions, incidental costs in connection with acquisitions and divestments, as well as with the merger of
                               the former Beauty Care and Laundry & Home Care business units into the Consumer Brands business unit.

                               expenses from the termination of business relationships with business partners. The reconciliation statement

                               amortized cost, equity, and the amounts stated on the consolidated statement of income must be indexed
                               on the basis of a general price index and represented at current purchasing power from the time of initial
                               recognition in the financial statements. Monetary items are not restated. Corresponding gains and losses
                               from current inflation are recognized in financial result.
