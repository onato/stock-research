                               markets and customers, we spent 110 million euros on restructuring in the first half of 2024 (previous year:
                               155 million euros). This figure primarily comprises expenses arising from the termination of employment
                               relationships, impairment losses on non-current assets and inventories, expenses from the termination of
                               business relationships with business partners, and the reclassification of currency translation reserves in
                               connection with the discontinuation of our business activities in Venezuela. The reconciliation statement

AT A GLANCE                    Financial statements of subsidiaries whose functional currency is the currency of a hyperinflationary economy
                               as defined in IAS 29 Financial Reporting in Hyperinflationary Economies must be restated for the change in
INTERIM GROUP                  purchasing power resulting from inflation prior to conversion into the Group currency and before consoli-
MANAGEMENT REPORT
                               dation. Non-monetary items on the statement of financial position that are measured at cost or amortized
                               cost, equity, and the amounts stated on the consolidated statement of income must be indexed on the basis
INTERIM CONSOLIDATED
FINANCIAL STATEMENTS           of a general price index and represented at current purchasing power from the time of initial recognition
                               in the financial statements. Monetary items are not restated. Corresponding gains and losses from current
REVIEW REPORT                  inflation are recognized in financial result.

RESPONSIBILITY STATEMENT       After restatement to current purchasing power, all items on the statement of financial position and all income
                               and expenses on the consolidated statement of income are translated to the functional currency of the
REPORT OF THE AUDIT            Group (euros) at the closing rate on the reporting date. When performing consolidation, Henkel recognizes
COMMITTEE OF THE
SUPERVISORY BOARD              changes resulting from the current inflation of the equity of its subsidiaries in the currency translation reserve.

                               Determining whether an economy is classifiable as hyperinflationary is based on qualitative and quantitative
MULTI-YEAR SUMMARY
                               criteria, including in particular whether cumulative inflation has exceeded 100 percent over the past three years.
CREDITS                        On this basis, the Henkel Group has classified Türkiye as a hyperinflationary economy for the current and
                               the previous reporting period and has applied IAS 29 accordingly. For the purpose of preparing the interim
CONTACTS                       consolidated financial statements, a change of 25.2 percent in general purchasing power was assumed,
                               based also on input from experts, as the actual inflation rate for the month of June 2024 was not yet available
FINANCIAL CALENDAR             when the financial statements were being prepared. The price index assumed for June 30, 2024 was 2,319.
                               The price index stood at 1,859 as of December 31, 2023, and at 1,352 as of June 30, 2023.
