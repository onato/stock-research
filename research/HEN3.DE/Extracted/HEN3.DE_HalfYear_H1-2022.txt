                                         IFRS 13 Fair Value Measurement defines fair value as the price that would be payable in a principal market – or
                                         in the most favorable market, in the absence of the former – if an asset were to be sold or a liability transferred.
                                         Valuation parameters as close to market reality as possible must be used as input factors to determine fair value.
                                         The fair value hierarchy prioritizes the input factors used in the valuation methods in three descending levels,
                                         depending on market proximity:

                                          Level 1: Fair values which are determined on the basis of quoted, unadjusted prices in active markets.
                                          Level 2: Fair values which are determined on the basis of parameters for which either directly or indirectly
                                           derived market prices are available.
                                          Level 3: Fair values which are determined with the aid of parameters for which the input factors are not
                                           derived from observable market data.
