In order to improve dividend predictability and enhance shareholder value, Woori Financial Group is executing Shareholder Return Policy and Capital Management Plan in consideration of the Total Shareholder Return Rate (“TSR”) (dividend + share buyback) as follows.

After stably exceeding CET1 12%, we will re-evaluate our mid to long-term Shareholder Return Policy, such as offering a TSR level of equal to or above 30%.

 

  3.

TSR is subject to change, due to rapid changes in the business environment such as those caused by increased financial market uncertainty or legal and regulatory restrictions imposed by supervisory authorities.
