  Related party transactions
  The Fund fully invests into a separate wholesale portfolio also managed by BIML – the Private Land and
  Property Portfolio (Wholesale Portfolio). The Wholesale Portfolio is a managed fund established under the
  Booster Investment Scheme, a separate scheme also managed by BIML.

  The Fund holds 100% of the units of the Wholesale Portfolio, and the Wholesale Portfolio holds the underlying
  investments directly that the investors in the Fund are indirectly exposed to.

  The Wholesale Portfolio enters into related party transactions in relation to some of its investments on
  occasion. During the Year this included the acquisition of certain orchard properties from a related party for
  $17.540m and the lease back of the properties to a related party.

  There were no other material changes to the nature or scale of any related party transactions during the Year
  and all related party transactions that provided for a related party benefit were on arms’ length terms.

  Further information on the Fund can be found in the Scheme’s Product Disclosure Statement and Other
  Material Information document available at www.booster.co.nz.
