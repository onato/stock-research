Company Overview

Fiserv (NYSE: FI) is a global financial technology company providing payment processing, digital banking, and financial services technology to banks, credit unions, and merchants. Following its 2019 acquisition of First Data, the company operates through two segments: Merchant Solutions (Clover SMB payments, Carat enterprise) and Financial Solutions (core banking, card issuing, digital banking platforms).

After a catastrophic October 2025 guidance collapse that wiped ~70% of market value, the company is executing its "One Fiserv" turnaround plan in 2026 — a transition year with declining EPS and low single-digit revenue growth before targeted recovery starting in 2027.

Business Model

Processing and services revenue (~81% of total) driven by account- and transaction-based fees under multi-year contracts. Clover — the SMB point-of-sale and operating platform — is the primary growth engine, reaching $3.3B revenue in FY2025.
