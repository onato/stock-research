Valuation Commentary

Dell's FCF-based DCF intrinsic value of $232 (base), $424 (bull), and $59 (bear) reflects a hardware company benefiting from the AI infrastructure cycle but constrained by thin margins and significant legacy debt (~$20.0B net debt). After a ~242% run, the stock at ~$399 trades roughly 41% above the probability-weighted IV of $237 - the market is pricing bull-case execution, leaving a negative margin of safety. Note: Dell carries a shareholders' equity deficit (-$2.5B at FY2026 year-end), so equity/book-value valuation is inapplicable and an FCF-DCF is used.

On forward multiples Dell is not egregiously expensive: ~23x FY2027 guided GAAP EPS (~$17.31), ~22x non-GAAP EPS (~$17.90), ~21x EV/EBIT and ~26x P/FCF. The thesis hinges on the record $51.3B AI backlog converting at FY2026-like ~7.5% FCF margins (the base case conservatively assumes 5.5%) while gross margins compress toward ~18% on AI server mix. The 8c sanity check flagged the DCF gap (caution banner above); multiple-based checks pass.
