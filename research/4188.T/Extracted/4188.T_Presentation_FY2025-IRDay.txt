    Today’s message


          ✓ While Specialty Materials is growing beyond our expectations, challenges remain in
                 the materials business which is subject to market volatility.


          ✓ With the "Three disciplined approaches in business operations“ - the core policy of
                 our Medium-Term Management Plan – we are delivering steady results and will be
                 executing as planned.


          ✓ We will further concentrate our management resources on “Growth driver” and
                 “Next-generation” quadrants to steadily trace the growth trajectory beyond FY26.


          ✓ We will rigorously execute strategy on our "Management commitment".
