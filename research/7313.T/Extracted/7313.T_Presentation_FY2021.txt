A Note About FY2022 Forecasts

 We are currently unable to make reasonable
 calculations regarding the consolidated forecasts for
 FY2022 due to disruptions in the automobile industry
 supply chain, including a semiconductor shortage.
 As a result, earnings and dividend forecasts are
 currently pending.
 Accordingly, forecast information is not included in this
 document, so we ask for your understanding.
 Forecasts will be announced as soon as it is possible to
 make reasonable calculations.
