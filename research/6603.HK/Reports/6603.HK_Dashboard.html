Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: The multiples sanity check tripped on a thin and misleading historical comparison: only 17 monthly closes exist since the June 2025 IPO, the stock has fallen monotonically from HK$36.60 to HK$4.23, and the single FY2025 historical multiple (P/B 192x, EV/EBITDA 1226x) is an IPO-peak data point on a tiny pre-IPO equity base — not a market-set ceiling. The underlying diagnosis was nonetheless applied in full: WACC was raised 11.0% → 14.2% across all three scenarios to price in real structural risks — small-cap illiquidity (+2.0%), single-distributor concentration (+1.5%, largest customer 50.9% of FY2025 sales), and China geographic/regulatory concentration (+1.5%). The key framing: at HK$4.23 the market cap (≈US$143m) sits below net cash (≈US$161m), so the market is pricing the operating business (the “if” and Innococo coconut-water brands) below zero even after the higher discount rate. This is a net-cash / special-situation thesis, not a growth-multiple bet — though the Innococo channel collapse and ~90% China revenue concentration shown below are real, ongoing risks to monitor, not resolved by the WACC adjustment.
