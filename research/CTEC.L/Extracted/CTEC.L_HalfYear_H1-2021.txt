Exchange rate sensitivity



           ▪ ConvaTec’s geographic profile can lead to transactional currency impacts.
           ▪ We monitor key rates against the US dollar.
           ▪ 27th July 2021 spot rates would indicate a ~$35m gain on revenue and ~$11m reduction in EBIT compared with average
              FY20 rates. This would equate to an 90bps reduction in FY21 EBIT margin. The H2 impact is relatively benign.
