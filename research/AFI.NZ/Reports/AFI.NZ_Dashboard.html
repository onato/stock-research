NTA-Based Valuation (Not a Standard FCF DCF)

Model approach: AFIC is a closed-end listed investment company (LIC), not an operating business — it simply holds a portfolio of listed equities and passes through dividends and capital gains. A standard discounted-cash-flow model built on operating free cash flow, SBC add-backs and a Gordon-growth terminal value is not meaningful here (reported "free cash flow" is just portfolio dividend flow-through). Instead this section uses an NTA (net tangible assets) total-return compounding model: pre-tax NTA per share is projected forward using a total-return assumption (split between per-share NTA growth and dividend payout), and a scenario exit premium/discount to NTA is applied to derive intrinsic value — since P/NTA, not a P/E or EV/EBITDA multiple, is the metric that actually clears the market for LICs.
