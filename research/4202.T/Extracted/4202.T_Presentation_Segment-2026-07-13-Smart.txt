Overview of Polymers for Photoresists
⚫As semiconductor devices continue to advance and scale down, quality requirements for photoresists are becoming
 increasingly demanding, including molecular design, high purity, and low-metal control.
⚫Leveraging strengths in monomer design and polymer synthesis technologies for EUV and ArF applications.

In front-end semiconductor manufacturing, photolithography is the core process for
forming fine circuit patterns. For advanced semiconductors, molecular design, high purity,
and low-metal control of resist polymers are critical factors that determine resolution,
defect reduction, and yield.
⚫ Our strengths
  • Monomer design and precision synthesis technologies for EUV and ArF polymers
  • Production and quality control capabilities supporting high purity and low-metal
    requirements

                                                                                             Production equipment utilizing microfluidic devices


⚫ Growth initiatives
  • Development of EUV and ArF resist polymers for advanced semiconductor scaling
  • Meeting customer quality requirements through integrated technologies spanning monomer design to polymer
    synthesis
  • Expanding adoption in advanced semiconductor applications through high-purity and low-metal control
  • Quality enhancement and production efficiency improvement through the use of microfluidic devices

5. Functional Material BU Overview
⚫ Expand business domains through the integration of materials and processing technologies by understanding
  the needs of key specification decision-makers across the supply chain.
⚫ Develop new applications by combining material development based on our proprietary oxidation technology
  (peracetic acid) with processing technologies utilizing phase separation.

            Strength of Functional Material BU : Integrated Materials and Processing Solutions
       Materials: Lactones and epoxies
       • Product design based on our proprietary oxidation technology
       • Unique functional properties (low chlorine content, high heat resistance, low dielectric properties,
         and dimensional stability)
       • Sustainability initiatives

       Processing: High-performance films
       • Phase separation coating technology for anti-glare and low-reflection functionality
       • Precision coating and film-forming technologies

⚫ Growth initiatives
  • High-performance films: Development of new applications through the integration of materials (epoxy) and processing
    (High-performance films)
  • Display surface treatment films: Entry into the polarizing film market through phase separation coating technology
  • Lactones and epoxies: Introduction of new grades tailored to application-specific performance requirements
