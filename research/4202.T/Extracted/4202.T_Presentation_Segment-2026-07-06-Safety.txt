 China. We aim to achieve a 30% global market share in FY2030.
◇ Further sales expansion to Chinese OEMs in the Chinese market, expansion in the Indian market, and promotion of collaboration with other
 companies in the European and North American market.

       2-6. Improving Labor Productivity
◇ The direct labor departments will pursue initiatives toward lights-out manufacturing, while the indirect labor departments will drive
  productivity improvements through digitization, standardization, and outsourcing. As a result of these initiatives, labor productivity
  in FY2030 will reach 1.5 times in the direct operations division and 1.7 times in the indirect operations division compared to FY2025.
