Highlights

FY2024/3 1st Quarter (Apr. to Jun.) Financial Results
Net Sales:130.8 billion yen, Operating Income:9.1 billion yen, EBITDA:16.6 billion yen
•   In our plan, we expected the Group‘s main markets, such as electronic devices and
    automobile-related businesses, to recover in the second half. In the first quarter, the
    recovery of these markets was slower than expected. Consequently, the sales volume of
    many products fell short of the plan.
•   On the other hand, acetate tow performed well on the back of strong demand.
•   As a result, net sales in the first quarter increased year-on-year due to the above and the
    impact of exchange rates, and operating income decreased due mainly to decreases sales
    of main products.
•   We will aim to achieve targets by steadily capturing sales opportunities that will increase
    due to future market recovery and accelerating efforts to improve annual earnings.

Actions for Achievement of Targets and Future Growth
Accelerate the following actions in the segments where the market environment in the 1st quarter was
more severe than expected and performance recovery was delayed, aiming at achieving the full-year
forecast and to lead to future growth.
                  •   Strengthen competitiveness of TAC by a quality improvement on introducing production process
     Smart            improvement (filtration process) and taking full advantage of low-grade pulp
                  •   Recovery in sales volumes of TAC by expanding its market share against the slow recovery in LCD
                      panel market

                  •   Increase in sales volumes by recovery in demand in Japan and Europe market, coming to an end of
                      inventory adjustments in China market and acquiring new programs
     Safety       •   Reduce costs per investment and lead time for construction of facility further for responding market
                      in China with great demand in automotive including electrical vehicle (EV)
                  •   Complete the consolidation of production regions within this fiscal year and promote the autonomy
                      of manufacturing lines to get more competitiveness in North America
                  •   Getting back customers who we could not provide our products due to the shortage of inventory in
                      Covid-19 (POM, LCP)
                  •   Maintain sales volume through flexible production and pricing system in response to the trend in
                      recovery in demand for automotive (POM)
  Engineering     •   Sales expansion of newly developed domains, such as POM for medical use and for biotechnology
    Plastics          for multinational enterprises in Europe, United States and China
                  •   Develop new applications for materials for thermal management of electrical vehicle (POM)
                  •   Focus on to the growing market (5G / 6G) and developing new applications (LCP)
                  •   Cost reduction by an improvement of occupancy rate and a review of maintenance program in
