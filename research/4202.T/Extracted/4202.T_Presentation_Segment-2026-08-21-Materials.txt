       Development and implementation of the Autonomous Production System
Deployment of the Autonomous Production System across the Acetyl Chain has generated an additional ¥2 billion in cost
reductions beyond those achieved through conventional methods (FY2021–FY2025). The scope of application is now being
expanded beyond production optimization to include production planning and equipment maintenance.
<What is the Autonomous Production System?>
◼ Daicel developed a methodology that incorporates AI into its production systems by converting approximately 8.4 million
  patterns of standardized operational know-how generated through DAICEL Production Innovation.
◼ By continuously monitoring combinations of production data in real time, the system detects early signs of potential issues and
  enables a shift from reactive responses to preventive actions.
◼ The system can accommodate raw materials with significant quality variations, including natural materials, enabling optimized
  and stable production while simultaneously predicting product quality and manufacturing costs.
