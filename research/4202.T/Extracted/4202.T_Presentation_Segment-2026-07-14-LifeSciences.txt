 Medical Devices（Next-Generation）：
             Regulatory Submissions in Overseas Markets
◼ Seeking Overseas Regulatory Approvals Based on the MHLW-Approved Daicel Injector



                 Significance of Obtaining Overseas Regulatory Approvals

                 To advance the global expansion of Daicel's medical device business, we are targeting overseas
                 regulatory approvals, including U.S. FDA approval, during the current medium-term
                 management plan period.

                 Strategy for Overseas Regulatory Submissions

                 Building on the MHLW-approved Daicel Injector, we plan to seek overseas regulatory approvals for
                 vaccine delivery applications. Clinical evidence generated through specified clinical research in Japan
                 will support differentiation in global markets.

                 Strategy Following Overseas Regulatory Approval

                 Following overseas regulatory approval, we will capitalize on Daicel’s global network to
                 accelerate the global expansion of our medical device business. We will also expand
                 application opportunities in line with the administration routes and requirements of target
                 pharmaceuticals.
