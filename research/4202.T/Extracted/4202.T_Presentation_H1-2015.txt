Return to Shareholders
◆ The Company will increase the dividend by 2 yen;
  the dividend forecast for the 2nd half is 10 yen per share.
  Thus, we will pay an annual dividend of 18 yen per share,
  a 3 yen per share increase over the previous year including
  the interim dividend.

     The shareholder return ratio

     We have set a target of 30% for the shareholder return ratio*
     ＊ Dividends plus Acquired treasury stocks divided by Consolidated net income for the year

 As of January 2015, the Company will change the number
 of common shares per share unit from 1,000 to 100.
