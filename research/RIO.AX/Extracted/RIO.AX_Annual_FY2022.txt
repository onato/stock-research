                    Annual Report 2022
                    The Rio Tinto Annual Report 2022 and Strategic Report 2022 are being released to the Australian Securities

                    Exchange Commission on or around 24 February 2023. American Depositary Receipt holders will be able to
                    view Rio Tinto’s Annual Report 2022 and the Annual Report on Form 20-F 2022 on the Rio Tinto website.
