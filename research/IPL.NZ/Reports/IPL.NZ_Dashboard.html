                REIT Valuation Model (AFFO-Capitalization / NTA-Based)

                Valuation Philosophy

                ⓘ This is an income/NTA REIT valuation, not an FCF/WACC discounted cash flow. Investore's Distributable Profit is capitalized at its cost of equity (never a blended WACC) because it is already a post-interest, equity-level cash flow — net debt is never subtracted a second time. The result is blended 50/50 with a projected book-NTA-plus-dividends leg. Because the sanity check passed with no trips, the base case landing just below the current price ($1.028 vs $1.05) is the honest answer for a mature, low-growth income REIT trading at a large discount to its own NTA — this is a buy-for-yield thesis, not a multi-bagger.
