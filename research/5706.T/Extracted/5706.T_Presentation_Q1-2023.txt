Results of FY2022 Q1


・FY2022 Q1 YoY：Sales increased due to increased sales volume of MicroThin™ and high zinc
 prices.
 Operating income decreased due to energy cost increases and the PGM price difference on
 Catalysts, etc. However, ordinary income increased due to large foreign exchange gains, etc.
・ As for the FY2022 forecast, operating income is expected to be lower than the May 11
 forecast due to falling metal prices and energy cost increases, etc. However, ordinary income
 and net income are expected to be the same as the May 11 forecast due to foreign exchange
 gains, etc.
