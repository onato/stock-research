 [Segment Information]
   1. Outline of Reportable Segments
      The Company’s reportable segments are components of the Company for which separate financial statements
      are available. Operating results of the reportable segments are subject to periodical review by the Board of
      Directors for the purpose of determining allocation of management resources and evaluating business
      performance.
      The main businesses of the Company and its subsidiaries are management and operation of theme parks and
      hotels. Therefore, operating results are reported for “Theme Park” and “Hotel Business” segments, based on
      factors that include the types and nature of products as well as the similarities of the sales market.
      The “Theme Park” segment undertakes management and operation of theme parks. The “Hotel Business”
      segment conducts management and operation of hotels.
