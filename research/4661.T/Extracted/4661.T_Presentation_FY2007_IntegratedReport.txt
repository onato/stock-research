       Overview of Tokyo Disney Resort’s Facilities



    A Large-Scale Theme Resort
    Offering Versatile
    Entertainment Facilities
    The Tokyo Disney Resort is a large-scale theme resort located
    near the center of metropolitan Tokyo (approximately 15 min-
    utes by train from Tokyo Station) and comprises two theme
    parks, two Disney hotels, a commercial complex, the
    Disney shop, six official hotels and a monorail that links
    these facilities. The concentration of these facilities, each
    with its own appeal, offers a complete entertainment
    selection for playing, staying, eating and shopping. The
    Tokyo Disney Resort continues to be treasured as a resort
    that can be enjoyed across generations by many guests,
    including families, friends and couples.
