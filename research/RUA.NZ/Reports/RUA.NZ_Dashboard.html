Revenue Ramp &amp; Cash Burn

The two things that actually decide this stock: is revenue compounding fast enough, and is cash running out first?

Revenue Ramp

FY2022 $0.024m → FY2023 $0.358m → FY2024 $0.086m (real dip, not an error) → FY2025 $1.511m. H1 FY2026 $1.334m vs H1 FY2025 $0.694m is +92% YoY at the half.

Operating Cash Burn vs Cash on Hand &mdash; the Survival Clock

The single most important chart on the page. Burn has shrunk every period (FY2023 -$5.92m → FY2024 -$3.74m → FY2025 -$2.79m → H1 FY2026 -$1.52m) but cash was only $0.241m at 30 June 2025, rebuilt to $2.166m solely by the Dec-2025 capital raise. Going concern is explicitly conditional on hitting revenue forecasts and completing the Gisborne facility sale.

Net Income

FY2024's -$13.72m is an impairment (~$8.5m+ of goodwill/PP&E written down after the owned-cultivation strategy failed), not an operational collapse — the cash-burn chart above is the better read on operational trajectory. The company has never reported a profit in any period on record.

Dilution &amp; Balance Sheet

Serial, deeply discounted capital raises are central to the investment case — not a footnote.

Shares Outstanding
