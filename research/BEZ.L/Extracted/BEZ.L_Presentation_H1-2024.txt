Beazley approach to capital




Capital strategy
• Our ambition is to remain above SCR ratio of 170%
• Our priority is to invest in sustainable, profitable growth in a balanced,
  well diversified business
• We factor in growth for the year ahead and opportunities which may
  emerge in subsequent 1-2 years
• We are focused on managing peak tail risk and absorbing volatility
• Where surplus capital cannot be profitably deployed, we will return to
  shareholders
