    avoid double counting, this methodology accounts for the largest volume in the oil, biofuels and gas value chains, i.e., the higher of the two production volumes or sales to end
    customers. The highest point for each value chain for 2023 will be evaluated considering realizations over the full year, TotalEnergies providing half-year estimate.
