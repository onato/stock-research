               Review of results and operations
               Revenues of the consolidated entity for the half year to 31 December 2016 were $339,168,000, an increase of
               2.4% from the revenues for the previous corresponding half year (2015: $331,242,000).

               The increase in revenue as compared to the previous corresponding half year was primarily a result of strong
               growth in the Global Product segment, with revenue for the half year increasing by 8.0% to $264,826,000 (2015:
               $245,126,000). The Distribution segment revenues of $74,342,000 (2015: $86,116,000) declined by 13.7%
               primarily due to products sold through the mass channel, with discount retailers continuing to favour their private
               label brands in the entry to mid-price points. In channels outside of the mass channel, however, the Distribution
               segment revenue was only slightly down over the prior comparative period (pcp).

               Group EBIT increased by 6.7% to $49,142,000 (2015: $46,053,000) for the half year ended 31 December 2016.
               The Global Product segment EBIT for the half year increased by 12.0% to $45,886,000 (2015: $40,965,000) as the
               sale of higher margin products resulted in a more favourable product mix which, together with cost efficiencies,
               more than offset the adverse impact of the stronger transactional USD and the incremental increase in marketing
               and R&D expenditure. The Distribution segment EBIT of $3,256,000 (2015: $5,088,000) reflects the lower sales
               and reduction in EBIT margin to 4.4% from 5.9% in the pcp, driven mainly by the impact of a stronger transactional
               USD, offset marginally by lower marketing expenses and cost efficiency savings.

               The basic earnings per share for the consolidated entity was 25.9 cents per share (2015: 23.7 cents per share).
