                    Correlation is a measure of the relationship between the movements of two variables (i.e. how the change in one variable influences
                    a change in the other variable). Correlation is a key input of derivatives with more than one underlying and is generally used to value
                    hybrid and exotic instruments.
                    Volatility
                    Volatility is a measure of the variability or uncertainty in returns for a given derivative underlying. It represents an estimate of the
                    amount a particular underlying instrument, parameter or index will change in value over time. Volatility is an input in the valuation of
                    derivatives containing optionality. Volatility and skew are impacted by the underlying risk, term and strike price of a derivative.
                    Inputs for unlisted equity securities (discount rate, earnings multiple)
                    Unlisted equity instruments are generally valued based on earnings multiples of comparable companies. Significant unobservable
                    inputs may include earnings multiple, discount rate and forecast earnings of the investee companies.
