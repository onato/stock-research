 Group Financial Results
 Key Takeaways

      FY22/23

      Record operating profit of $2,692.1 million, a reversal of $3,301.8 million from last year
      ▪ Passenger revenue rose $10,559.7 million on the back of record PLF and RASK
      ▪ Cargo revenue fell $735.6 million with lower loads and yields as demand for air freight declined
      ▪ Fuel cost increased on higher fuel prices and volumes uplifted

      Net profit of $2,156.8 million, a $3,118.8 million swing from the net loss of $962.0 million last year
      ▪ Better operating performance, lower net finance charges (+$338.4 million), and partially offset by tax expense against credit
         last year (-$615.4 million)

      Robust near term forward passenger sales across all cabin classes

      Airline industry continues to navigate geopolitical and economic uncertainties, high cost inflation, and increasing
      global passenger capacity

      Proposed final dividend of 28 cents per share
