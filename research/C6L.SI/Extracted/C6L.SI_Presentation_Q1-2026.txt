 Group Financial Results
 Key Highlights
  Operating profit of $404.5 million for Q1 FY25/26, 14.0% lower than a year ago
   Singapore Airlines and Scoot achieved record passenger numbers amid robust demand for air travel
   Passenger yields declined due to industry-wide capacity growth, while rising non-fuel costs from inflationary pressures offset lower
     fuel prices
