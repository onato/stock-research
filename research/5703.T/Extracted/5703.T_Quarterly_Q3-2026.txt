1. Overview of operating results
(1) Overview of operating results for the current quarter
    During the first nine months of the current consolidated fiscal year, the Japanese economy continued its moderate
    recovery. However, the outlook remained uncertain amid international instability, China’s economic slowdown, and
    developments in U.S. tariff policies, among other factors.
    Within the Group, on the sales side, in the sheet & extrusion business group, both the aluminum sheet division and
    the extrusions division continued to experience stagnation in sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing
    equipment, stemming from the delay in demand recovery. In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot
    division maintained solid performance in the U.S., regardless of weak sales of products for automobiles in Japan.
    The transport equipment business group’s truck body operations and the automotive parts business group showed a
    significant recovery compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year. In addition, favorable demand for heat
    dissipation products in the chemicals business group’s chemicals division and the powder and paste division,
    together with strong sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion batteries in the aluminum sheet division, contributed to
    higher net sales compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year. Profitability improved significantly in the
    fabricated products and others segment, and profits exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level in the aluminum foil,
    powder and paste segment. Cost increases reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions affected both the aluminum
    ingot and chemicals segment and the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment. Despite this, operating profit,
    ordinary profit, and profit attributable to owners of parent all exceeded the levels in the same period of the previous
    fiscal year, partly due to the effect of sales price revisions.

An overview of each business segment is provided below.

<Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division’s sales exceeded the level of the same period of the previous
fiscal year in its mainstay aluminum hydroxide and alumina, supported by continued strong demand for use in heat-
dissipating and fire-resistant fillers, among other factors. Despite persistent stagnation in sales of inorganic chlorine
compounds among chemical products, net sales came in above the same period in the previous fiscal year, thanks in
part to revenue gains from sales price revisions implemented in response to rising raw material costs. On the
profitability side, operating profit was higher than in the same period of the previous fiscal year, reflecting factors
including increased sales and the effect of sales price revisions.
In the metal business group, domestic sales in the secondary alloy ingot division’s mainstay products for
automobiles remained at the same level as the previous fiscal year due to continued weakness in automobile
production. Overseas, sales in China remained sluggish, while the U.S. continued to experience a favorable sales
environment. In Thailand, sales volume increased despite challenging market conditions. In addition, full-scale
production at the plant in India, which began operations in 2024, contributed to net sales surpassing the same period
in the previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, although the secondary alloy ingot division recorded higher
profits, the impact of fluctuations in aluminum ingot market prices and other factors led to the operating profit
falling below the previous year’s level.
As a result of the above, net sales of the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment for the first nine months of the current
consolidated fiscal year increased by 10,912 million yen (up 8.9%) year on year, from 122,994 million yen to 133,906
million yen. Operating profit decreased by 1,218 million yen (down 12.2%) year on year, from 9,983 million yen to
8,765 million yen.


<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, despite a delayed recovery in demand for products for semiconductor
manufacturing equipment in the aluminum sheet division, net sales surpassed the same period of the previous fiscal
year. This was driven by strong sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages and higher sales prices
reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions. On the profit side, contributing factors included higher sales of
aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages and the effect of revisions to processing fees. However, operating
profit fell below the same period of the previous fiscal year due to a decline in sales of products for semiconductor
manufacturing equipment and higher costs reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions.
In the extrusions division, despite experiencing a delayed recovery in the sales of products for semiconductor
manufacturing equipment, strong sales of products for truck bodies, alongside higher sales prices reflecting
aluminum ingot market conditions, led to higher net sales compared with the same period of the previous fiscal
year. In terms of profitability, despite the effect of revisions to processing fees, operating profit fell below the same
period of the previous fiscal year, due to a decline in sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment
and higher costs reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment for the first nine months of the current
consolidated fiscal year increased by 4,797 million yen (up 6.2%) year on year, from 77,252 million yen to 82,049
million yen. Operating profit decreased by 823 million yen (down 19.7%) year on year, from 4,178 million yen to
3,355 million yen.


<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, although unit sales of truck bodies declined slightly compared to the
same period in the previous fiscal year, net sales exceeded the previous year’s level due to the impact of the sales
price revision materialized. On the profit side as well, operating profit rose well above the previous year’s level,
driven by the effect of the sales price revision and increased sales of after-sales services.
In the automotive parts business group, on the sales side, the pickup in domestic automobile production, along with
the launch of new projects and an increase in new product lineups, pushed net sales above the previous year’s level.
On the profitability side, higher sales, an improved product mix, better productivity, and optimized sales prices led
to a significant improvement compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year.

     In the engineering business group, the panel system division saw continued demand in the freezing and refrigeration
     field, driven by the expansion of logistics sites for food factories and low‑temperature distribution warehouses, the
     replacement of aging facilities, and the renovation of existing stores for supermarkets and convenience stores.
     Despite ongoing delays in construction periods caused by soaring construction costs and labor shortages, net sales in
     this field came in above the same period in the previous fiscal year. In the clean room field, semiconductor-related
     demand continued; however, a reactionary decline following large‑scale orders in the previous fiscal year caused net
     sales to fall below the previous year’s level. Overall, the division’s net sales surpassed the same period in the
     previous fiscal year. However, in terms of profitability, the decline in large-scale orders compared to the previous
     fiscal year, together with higher costs, including labor expenses, kept operating profit in line with the previous fiscal
     year.
     As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment for the first nine months of the current
     consolidated fiscal year increased by 4,427 million yen (up 3.6%) year on year, from 121,474 million yen to 125,901
     million yen. An operating profit of 2,580 million yen was recorded, representing an improvement of 4,048 million
     yen from the operating loss of 1,468 million yen recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal year.


     <Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
     In the aluminum foil business group, the foil division saw foils for automotive lithium‑ion battery exteriors enter an
     adjustment phase in the third quarter, and sales of processed foil for medical product packaging fell below the
     previous year’s level. Nevertheless, reflecting higher aluminum ingot market prices and revised processing fees,
     sales prices rose, resulting in net sales for the division overall exceeding the previous year’s level.
     In the powder and paste division, sales of powder products, such as aluminum powder for electronic materials in
     heat dissipation applications and aluminum nitride, remained strong. Sales of paste products for the mainstay
     automobile paints decreased due to weak automobile production in Japan. In overseas markets, on the other hand,
     sales grew in China, as well as in North America and Southeast Asia, resulting in overall net sales for the division
     surpassing the same period of the previous fiscal year.
     In the daily necessities division, house-care products posted growth among consumer products, but sales of products
     for foodstuff, including aluminum foil, fell below the previous fiscal year’s level due to lower sales after price
     revisions. Meanwhile, sales of aluminum containers for frozen foods and paper containers remained strong, and,
     together with the effect of price revisions implemented in response to rising costs, overall net sales for the division
     exceeded the same period in the previous fiscal year.
     As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment for the first nine months of the
     current consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,973 million yen (up 3.6%) year on year, from 82,236 million yen to
     85,209 million yen. Operating profit increased by 821 million yen (up 14.8%), from 5,556 million yen to 6,377 million
     yen.


(2) Overview of financial position for the current quarter
    Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
    As of the end of the first nine months of the current consolidated fiscal year, total assets increased by 25,160 million
    yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 569,467 million yen, due to an increase in cash
    and deposits and other factors. Liabilities increased by 14,895 million yen compared with the end of the previous
    consolidated fiscal year to 308,721 million yen, due to an increase in long‑term borrowings and other factors. Net
    assets increased by 10,265 million yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 260,746
    million yen, due to an increase in retained earnings from the recording of profit attributable to owners of parent and
    other factors. The equity ratio fell from 42.8% at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 42.5%.


(3) Explanation of forward-looking information, including consolidated earnings forecasts
    The consolidated earnings forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, remains unchanged from the
    announcement made on May 15, 2025. Although our performance during the first nine months of the current
    consolidated fiscal year progressed steadily, uncertainties persist for the fourth quarter, including unstable
    international conditions and geopolitical risks. In light of these circumstances, including the continued need to closely
    monitor trends in the automotive and semiconductor‑related markets, the Company has left its full‑year consolidated
    earnings forecast unchanged.
