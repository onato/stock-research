1. Overview of operating results
(1) Explanation of consolidated operating results
     During the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, economic activity in Japan showed a
     gradual recovery. However, the outlook still remains uncertain, given unstable exchange rates,
     persistently high raw material prices, as well as international instability, such as the worsening
     conditions in the Middle East, the prolonged invasion in Ukraine, and China’s economic slowdown,
     among other factors.

     The Group experienced continued sluggish sales in the Chinese automobile sector and the lingering
     impact of automobile production cuts in Japan. The panel system division was impacted by factors
     causing delays and revisions to construction periods due to soaring construction costs and labor
     shortages. Nevertheless, net sales surpassed the levels of the previous fiscal year, as the truck body
     business continued to be solid overall; semiconductor-related products, which are on a path to gradual
     recovery, also saw higher sales than in the same period of the previous fiscal year; and the aluminum
     ingot market was on the upswing. On the profit side, the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment
     improved significantly over the same period of the previous fiscal year, a continuation from the first
     quarter, along with higher profits in both the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment and the aluminum
     foil, powder and paste segment than the levels in the previous fiscal year. As a result, operating profit,
     ordinary profit, and profit attributable to owners of parent increased compared with the same period
     of the previous fiscal year.

An overview of each business segment is provided below.

As described in “2. Semi-annual Consolidated Financial Statements and Important Notes (4) Notes
regarding the semi-annual consolidated financial statements (Notes on segment information, etc.)”
effective June 25, 2024, the Company has adopted a business operating structure under business groups
and functional organizations, with the objective of maximizing its corporate value as the Group.
Accordingly, from the first quarter of the fiscal year ending March 2025, the divisions and products
belonging to the reportable segments are presented under business groups as follows, and some
divisions and products have been reclassified.

Segment information on net sales and operating profit in the previous semi-annual consolidated
accounting period as well as comparisons to the same period of the previous fiscal year, are presented
after the above reclassification.




<Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division saw strong sales of its mainstay aluminum
hydroxide and alumina-related products for ceramics compared to the same period of the previous
fiscal year, and the chemicals also saw solid sales of inorganic chlorine compounds, bringing net sales
to a higher level than in the same period of the previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, however,
soaring raw material prices resulted in a slightly lower operating profit than in the same period of the
previous fiscal year.

In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s domestic sales of its mainstay
products for automobiles continued to be affected by the decline in automobile production. Overseas,
sales volumes declined partly due to lower sales in China and Thailand, although sales in the U.S. were
solid. Nevertheless, net sales were above the same period of the previous fiscal year due to higher sales
prices reflecting the aluminum ingot market conditions. On the profitability side, despite the impact of
lower sales in the secondary alloy ingot division, operating profit was higher than in the same period
of the previous fiscal year, as it was significantly impacted by improvement in the aluminum ingot
market.

As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment in the first half of the
current consolidated fiscal year increased by 3,805 million yen (4.9%) year on year, from 77,051
million yen to 80,856 million yen. Operating profit in this segment increased by 624 million yen
(10.2%) year on year, from 6,119 million yen to 6,743 million yen.

<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, in terms of sales, the aluminum sheet division, albeit on a
path to gradual recovery, posted higher sales volumes of products for semiconductor manufacturing
equipment than the previous fiscal year’s level. In addition, sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion
battery packages were also solid. As a result, net sales were above the same period of the previous
fiscal year. On the profitability side, operating profit recovered significantly from the same period of
the previous fiscal year thanks to increased sales volumes, coupled with higher sales prices reflecting
market conditions for aluminum ingot and the effect of sales price revisions for processing fees.

The extrusions division saw solid sales of products for truck bodies, with a gradual recovery in sales
of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, coupled with the effect of higher sales prices
reflecting the aluminum market. As a result, both net sales and operating profit were above the levels
of the previous fiscal year.

As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusion products segment in the first
half of the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 4,094 million yen (9.1%) year on year, from
45,191 million yen to 49,285 million yen. An operating profit of 2,020 million yen was recorded,
representing an improvement of 2,329 million yen from the operating loss of 309 million yen recorded
in the same period of the previous fiscal year.


<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, net sales were higher than the previous fiscal year’s level
on the back of the strong demand for sales of products for truck bodies overall following the recovery
in the previous fiscal year, coupled with sales price revisions. On the profitability side, despite the
impact of persistently high raw material prices, profit improved compared with the same period of the
previous fiscal year, reflecting the effect of sales price revisions.

In the automotive parts business group, net sales were below the previous fiscal year’s level due to the
lingering impact of automobile production cuts in Japan and the continued slump in the Chinese market
for Japanese automobile manufacturers, among other factors. On the profit side, the impact on sales
was significant and the performance worsened compared to the previous fiscal year’s level, regardless
of the effect of sales price revisions and reductions in fixed costs.

In the engineering business group, the panel system division saw a trend toward expansion and
continued demand for the reconstruction of food factories and low-temperature distribution
warehouses in the freezing and refrigeration field. However, in the clean-room field, while soaring
construction costs and delays in construction periods due to labor shortages led to a continued demand
for new clean rooms for semiconductor-related and manufacturing equipment manufacturers, there
was a reactionary decline against the previous fiscal year’s increase in sales of large projects. As a
result, both net sales and operating profit fell below the levels of the previous year.

As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 929
million yen (1.2%) to 77,956 million yen in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year,
compared with 77,027 million yen in the same period of the previous fiscal year. An operating loss of
1,779 million yen was recorded, representing a downward change of 544 million yen from the 1,235
million yen loss recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal year.


<Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
In the aluminum foil business group, alongside the ongoing adjustment of foils for automotive lithium-

    ion battery exteriors, sales of processed foil for medical product packaging fell below the previous
    fiscal year’s level. As a result, net sales for the division overall were lower than in the same period of
    the previous fiscal year.

    The powder and paste division experienced a recovery in demand for heat dissipation, such as
    aluminum powder for electronic materials and aluminum nitride, resulting in sales above the previous
    fiscal year’s level. In addition, although inventory adjustments affected sales of paste products for the
    mainstay automobile paints to domestic customers, sales to overseas markets were solid. As a result,
    net sales for the division overall exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level.

    In the daily necessities division, the effect of price increases resulting from revisions to the sales prices
    of consumer products including aluminum foil more than offset lower sales. In addition, sales of
    packaging products for frozen food and sales of paper containers were both strong. As a result, net
    sales for the division overall exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level.

    As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segments in the first half of
    the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 1,860 million yen (3.6%) year on year, from 51,183
    million yen to 53,043 million yen. Operating profit increased by 338 million yen (11.6%) year on year,
    from 2,903 million yen to 3,241 million yen.


(2) Explanation of consolidated financial position
    Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
    At the end of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, total assets increased by 2,802
    million yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 546,897 million yen due
    to an increase in cash and deposits as well as an increase in inventories caused by higher aluminum
    ingot prices, among other factors. Liabilities increased by 589 million yen compared with the end of
    the previous consolidated fiscal year to 305,687 million yen, due to an increase in long-term
    borrowings and other factors. Net assets increased by 2,213 million yen compared with the end of the
    previous consolidated fiscal year to 241,210 million yen due to an increase in retained earnings from
    the recording of profit attributable to owners of parent and other factors. The equity ratio rose from
    40.8% at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 41.1%.

     Cash flow
     Cash flows from operating activities
     Net cash provided by operating activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year
     amounted to 1,214 million yen. This was because non-cash profit/loss items such as profit before
     income taxes and depreciation more than offset cash outflows, including an increase in working capital
     and income taxes paid. Operating activities provided 21,856 million yen in net cash in the same period
     in the previous fiscal year, but in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, operating
     activities provided 1,214 million yen in net cash, primarily due to factors such as an increase in
     working capital.

     Cash flows from investing activities
     Net cash used in investing activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year amounted
     to 9,619 million yen. This was primarily due to purchase of property, plant and equipment. Net cash
     used in investing activities was up 164 million yen compared with the same period in the previous
     fiscal year, which was primarily due to such as an increase in purchase of property, plant and
     equipment.

     Cash flows from financing activities
     Net cash provided by financing activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year
     amounted to 19,852 million yen. This was primarily due to proceeds from long-term borrowings.

(3) Explanation of forward-looking information, including consolidated earnings forecasts
    With regard to the forecasts from the third quarter and beyond, we expect to see continued recovery
    in semiconductor-related demand and a turnaround in the performance of truck body and other
    businesses. However, in light of the uncertainties about market trends related to the automotive sector
    in Japan and overseas markets, including China, the impact of fluctuations in raw material prices,
    particularly for aluminum ingot, and others, we have not changed our consolidated forecast for the
    fiscal year ending March 31, 2025, released on May 15, 2024.

    Basic Agreement on the Management Integration, and that the Company resolved at its Board of
    Directors meeting held today to enter into an agreement on the termination of the Basic Agreement
    on the Management Integration and terminated the Basic Agreement on the Management Integration
    as of today.
    The impact of the cancellation of the Management Integration on the Company's consolidated
    financial results for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2025 is expected to be insignificant.

    (Notes on changes in accounting policies)
    (Application of “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes,” etc.)

    The Company has applied the “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes” (ASBJ Statement No.
    27, October 28, 2022; the “Revised Accounting Standard 2022”), etc. from the beginning of the first
    half of the current consolidated fiscal year.

    Previously, calculated amounts of current income taxes on income, etc. (“Income Taxes”) were
    recorded in profit or loss in accordance with laws and regulations, but now Income Taxes on income
    are recorded separately in profit or loss, shareholders’ equity, or other comprehensive income
    according to the transaction, etc., from which it occurred. For Income Taxes recorded in accumulated
    other comprehensive income, when a transaction, etc. is recorded in profit or loss that gives rise to
    Income Taxes, the corresponding tax amount will be recorded in profit or loss. Additionally, if the
    transaction, etc., subject to taxation is related to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income
    in addition to profit or loss, and it is difficult to calculate the amount of Income Taxes, to be applied
    to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income, the amount of such taxes is recorded in profit
    or loss.

    For the treatment of the tax effect of the deferral for tax purposes of gains or losses on sale of shares
    in subsidiaries, etc. between consolidated companies when deferred tax assets or liabilities are
    recorded for temporary differences related to such gains or losses in the financial statements of the
    entity that sold the shares in subsidiaries, etc., the amount of deferred tax assets or liabilities related to
    such temporary differences was previously not adjusted in the consolidation process, but now deferred
    tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences are reversed.

    The amendment to categories in which Income Taxes should be recorded (taxes on other
    comprehensive income) follows the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso of paragraph 20-3
    of the Revised Accounting Standard 2022 and the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso (2)
    of paragraph 65-2 of Implementation Guidance on Tax Effect Accounting (ASBJ Guidance No. 28,
    October 28, 2022) (“Revised Implementation Guidance 2022”).

    Accordingly, the cumulative effects of retrospectively applying the new accounting policies prior to
    the beginning of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year were added to or subtracted from
    the balance of retained earnings at the beginning of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year,
    and the corresponding amount was added or subtracted to the appropriate category of capital surplus
    or accumulated other comprehensive income, with the new accounting policies applied from the
    beginning balance. As a result, retained earnings at the beginning of the first half of the current
    consolidated fiscal year increased by 24 million yen and valuation difference on available-for-sale
    securities decreased by the same amount.

    In response to the amendment related to the revised accounting treatment for consolidated financial
    statements when gains or losses on sale of shares in subsidiaries, etc. between consolidated companies
    were deferred for tax purposes, the Company has applied the Revised Implementation Guidance 2022
    from the beginning of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year. This change in accounting
    policies was applied retrospectively. Hence, the semi-annual consolidated financial statements for the
    first half of the previous consolidated fiscal year and the consolidated financial statements for the
    previous fiscal year have been modified retrospectively. As a result, retained earnings of the previous
