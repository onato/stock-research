1. Qualitative Information on Quarterly Results
(1) Explanation of consolidated operating results position
    During the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, the Japanese economy showed a gradual recovery.
    However, the outlook continues to be uncertain, given the need to closely monitor trends in U.S. trade policy, in
    addition to international instability, such as the conditions in the Middle East, the prolonged invasion in Ukraine, and
    China’s economic slowdown.
    The Group, on the sales side of automobile-related products, experienced continued weak automobile production in
    Japan and the ongoing impact of sluggish sales in China, as well as a delayed recovery in demand for semiconductor-
    related products. Nevertheless, demand for truck body-related products remained strong, coupled with sales growth in
    the chemicals division and powder products for heat dissipation, among other factors. Additionally, sales prices, which
    reflected aluminum ingot market conditions, exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level. As a result, net sales increased
    compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year. Profitability improved significantly in the fabricated products
    and others segment, and profits exceeded the previous year’s level in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment.
    Cost increases reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions affected both the aluminum ingot and chemicals and the
    aluminum sheet and extrusions segments. Despite this, operating profit was higher than in the same period of the
    previous fiscal year, partly due to the effect of sales price revisions. Ordinary profit and profit attributable to owners
    of parent decreased as compared to those levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year, due to foreign exchange
    losses.

    An overview of each business segment is provided below.

    <Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
    In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division saw higher sales of its mainstay aluminum hydroxide and
    alumina for heat-dissipating, fire-resistant fillers and other applications than in the same period of the previous fiscal

                                                             2
year. Despite a slowdown in sales of inorganic chlorine compounds among chemical products, net sales exceeded the
same period of the previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, operating profit was higher than in the same period
of the previous fiscal year, partly due to the effect of sales price revisions, coupled with the impact on sales.
In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s domestic sales of its mainstay products for
automobiles remained sluggish. Overseas, sales declined in China but remained solid in the U.S. and surpassed the
same period of the previous fiscal year in Thailand. In India, where a plant that began operating last year has now
reached full capacity, sales increased. Additionally, sales prices were above the previous fiscal year’s level, reflecting
aluminum ingot market conditions. As a result, net sales increased from the same period of the previous fiscal year.
On the profitability side, while sales were impacted in the secondary alloy ingot division, operating profit was lower
than in the same period of the previous fiscal year, as it was significantly impacted by higher costs reflecting aluminum
ingot market conditions.
As a result of the above, net sales of the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment in the first three months of the current
consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,873 million yen (up 7.1%) year on year, from 40,563 million yen to 43,436
million yen. Operating profit decreased by 476 million yen (down 12.4%) year on year, from 3,849 million yen to
3,373 million yen.


<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, the aluminum sheet division saw a slowdown in sales of products for
semiconductor manufacturing equipment, which had been on a path to recovery, but sales of aluminum sheets for
lithium-ion battery packages remained strong, and sales prices, which reflected aluminum ingot market conditions,
were above the previous fiscal year’s level. As a result, net sales increased compared to the same period of the previous
fiscal year. On the profit side, the effect of revisions to processing fees, alongside the impact on sales, contributed to
the results. However, operating profit fell below the same period of the previous fiscal year, as it was significantly
impacted by higher costs reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions.
The extrusions division saw lower sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment than in the previous
fiscal year. Nevertheless, sales of products for truck bodies continued to be strong, and sales prices, which reflected
aluminum ingot market conditions, were above the previous year’s level. As a result, net sales increased compared to
the same period of the previous fiscal year. In terms of profitability, despite the effect of revisions to processing fees,
operating profit fell below the same period of the previous fiscal year, as it was significantly impacted by higher costs
reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment for the first three months of the
current consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,482 million yen (up 10.3%) year on year, from 24,153 million yen to
26,635 million yen. Operating profit decreased by 16 million yen (2.5%) year on year, from 632 million yen to 616
million yen.


<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, net sales exceeded levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year due
to price revisions taking effect, coupled with continued strong demand for products for truck bodies in terms of sales.
On the profit side, the softening of material prices and other factors led to an improved performance from the same
period of the previous fiscal year.
In the automotive parts business group, sales in Japan recovered from last year's automobile production cuts, alongside
the launch of new projects. Despite the ongoing slump in the Chinese market, net sales as a whole exceeded levels in
the same period of the previous fiscal year. On the profit side, increased sales, improvement in the product mix, the
effect of fixed cost reductions, and other factors resulted in improved performance from the same period of the
previous fiscal year.
In the engineering business group, the panel system division continued to see demand for expansion of food factories
and the logistics sites of low-temperature distribution warehouses in the freezing and refrigeration field, as well as the
reconstruction of these facilities due to aging. Additionally, demand continued for small- and medium-sized projects
for stores and kitchen spaces in supermarkets and convenience stores. Regardless of the impact of delays in
construction due to soaring costs and labor shortages, net sales exceeded levels in the same period of the previous
fiscal year. In the clean room field, while demand for sales of semiconductor-related products has shown signs of
stabilization in some areas, net sales exceeded levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year. As a result,

     operating profit also exceeded the previous fiscal year's level.
     As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 1,842 million yen (up
     4.9%) to 39,200 million yen in the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, compared with 37,358
     million yen in the same period of the previous fiscal year. An operating loss of 130 million yen was recorded,
     representing an improvement of 995 million yen from the operating loss of 1,125 million yen recorded in the same
     period of the previous fiscal year.


     <Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
     In the aluminum foil business group, the foil division saw increased demand in foils for automotive lithium-ion battery
     exteriors, which continued to be in an adjustment phase. Despite decreased sales of processed foil for medical product
     packaging in Japan from the previous fiscal year’s level, net sales for the division overall exceeded levels in the same
     period of the previous fiscal year, partly due to the impact of the overseas sales product mix.
     The powder and paste division experienced favorable demand for heat dissipation, such as aluminum powder for
     electronic materials and aluminum nitride, resulting in higher sales than in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
     Although sales of paste products for the mainstay automobile paints fell below the previous fiscal year’s level due to
     weak automobile production in Japan, sales in overseas markets continues to be strong. As a result, net sales for the
     division overall exceeded levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
     In the daily necessities division, lower sales offset the positive effect of price increases resulting from revisions to the
     sales prices of consumer products, including aluminum foil for food products and house-care products. Sales of
     packaging products increased from the same period of the previous fiscal year, driven by strong sales of products for
     frozen food and sales of paper containers, as well as sales price revisions due to soaring raw material prices. Overall,
     however, the division’s net sales fell below the previous fiscal year’s level.
     As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment in the first three months of the
     current consolidated fiscal year increased by 1,274 million yen (up 4.8%) year on year, from 26,548 million yen to
     27,822 million yen. Operating profit increased by 380 million yen (up 21.1%) year on year, from 1,802 million yen
     to 2,182 million yen.


(2) Explanation of consolidated financial position
    Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
    As of the end of the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, total assets decreased by 14,873 million
    yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 529,434 million yen, due to a decrease in notes
    and accounts receivable - trade, and contract assets, among other factors. Liabilities decreased by 12,842 million yen
    compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 280,984 million yen due to a decrease in income taxes
    payable as a result of the filing and payment of income taxes, repayments of borrowings, and other factors. Net assets
    decreased by 2,031 million yen compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 248,450 million yen
    due to a decrease in retained earnings as a result of the payment of dividends and other factors. The equity ratio rose
    from 42.8% at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 43.7%.


(3) Explanation of forward-looking information, including consolidated earnings forecasts
    As announced on May 15, 2025, there are increasingly looming concerns in the global economy going forward due
    to uncertainties in U.S. trade policy and trade fragmentation in various countries. In Japan as well, the situation
    remains the same, requiring vigilance on changes in the business environment caused by the effects of factors such
    as rising prices and fluctuations in financial and capital markets. Accordingly, the consolidated earnings forecasts for
    the first six months and full year of the fiscal year, which were estimated based on these factors, remain unchanged.
