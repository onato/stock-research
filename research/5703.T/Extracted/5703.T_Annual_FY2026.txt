1. Overview of operating results
(1) Overview of operating results in the fiscal year under review
    (i) Operating results in the fiscal year under review
    During the period under review, the global economy continued to show signs of recovery, underpinned by fiscal
    measures implemented in various countries. However, in addition to global policy uncertainty stemming from trade
    frictions, particularly those associated with U.S. trade policy, geopolitical risks intensified in the latter half of the
    period amid escalating tensions in the Middle East, while heightened market volatility further amplified uncertainty
    over the economic outlook. In Japan, the economy remained on a gradual recovery path, supported by improvements
    in employment and income conditions, with signs of a pickup in consumer spending. However, U.S. trade policy
    weighed particularly on the automotive industry, and the recovery in corporate earnings appeared to be leveling off.
    In the aluminum industry, total domestic demand for aluminum fell below the level of the previous fiscal year, due in
    part to a decline in demand from the automotive sector for the second consecutive year, as well as a decrease in demand
    for metal-related products for the first time in two years. In addition, prices of aluminum ingots and other raw materials
    continued to rise.
    In the final fiscal year of the Medium-term Management Plan which commenced in FY2023 (the “23MTP”), the
    Group has implemented initiatives based on its basic policies, namely promoting the “Initiatives for new Team Nippon
    Light Metal” and “providing products and businesses that contribute to the creation of social value,” while steadily
    pursuing management reforms.
    Despite significant changes in the external environment, including sluggish demand for EV- and semiconductor-
    related products, which are key products and services under the 23MTP, and rising raw material prices such as
    aluminum ingots, the Group achieved steady results through ongoing restructuring of its production portfolio and
    supply chain, resulting in increases in both revenue and profit for the third consecutive fiscal year.
    Additionally, based on the business group structure introduced in 2024, with the aim of strengthening intra-group
    collaboration, creating new products and businesses, and improving capital efficiency, we worked to lay the
    foundation for the Group’s growth strategy. For example, in the first half of 2025, we decided to participate in a capital
    investment in a recycled aluminum business in the Republic of India, thereby allocating management resources to
    capture opportunities in the rapidly growing Indian market and to establish a global closed-loop supply chain. At the
    same time, we also promoted organizational restructuring within the Group and advanced initiatives to establish a
    framework that enables optimal allocation of resources across the Group.
    Furthermore, regarding the initiatives developed in 2023 to prevent the recurrence of inappropriate quality-related
    behaviors, we completed a series of measures in March 2026, as initially planned. Through these initiatives, we
    advanced management reforms and strengthened internal control functions, thereby establishing a foundation not only
    to prevent recurrence but also to drive the Group’s transformation toward its envisioned future state of management.
    The final report on the Group's progress in preventing the recurrence of quality problems within the Group is available
    on the Company’s website below.
    Company website: https://www.nikkeikinholdings.co.jp/company/quality-control.html (available only in Japanese)


    Financial results in the fiscal year under review were as follows.
    Net sales increased compared to the previous fiscal year, despite continued delays in the recovery of demand for
    products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment in the aluminum sheet and extrusions divisions of the sheet &
    extrusion business group. This was driven by strong performance in the secondary alloy division for automotive
    applications in the metal business group—sales in the U.S. remained robust despite sluggish domestic demand—
    together with a recovery in truck body operations in the transport equipment business group compared to the previous
    fiscal year, as well as continued favorable results in the chemicals division of the chemicals business group, and in the
    powder and paste divisions of the aluminum foil business group. With respect to profitability, despite higher costs
    reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions and other factors, the fabricated products and others segment recorded a
    significant improvement, supported also by sales price revisions. Consequently, operating profit, ordinary profit, and
    profit attributable to owners of parent all surpassed the previous fiscal year’s results.

    <Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
   In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division’s sales exceeded the level of the previous fiscal year in its
   mainstay aluminum hydroxide and alumina, supported by continued strong demand for use in heat-dissipating and
   fire-resistant fillers, among other factors. Despite a period of stagnation in sales of inorganic chlorine compounds

                                                                  6
among chemical products, net sales came in above the previous fiscal year, thanks in part to revenue gains from sales
price revisions implemented in response to rising raw material costs. Profitability also improved significantly, with
operating profit rising significantly above the previous fiscal year, reflecting factors including increased sales and the
effect of sales price revisions.
In the carbon products division, sales of our mainstay carbon blocks for the steel industry increased, and an improved
sales mix with a higher proportion of high‑margin projects helped drive strong performance, with both net sales and
operating profit rising significantly above the previous fiscal year.
In the metal business group, domestic sales in the mainstay secondary alloy ingot division for automotive applications
were in line with the previous fiscal year due to continued weakness in automobile production. In China, sales also
remained sluggish, while the U.S. continued to experience a favorable sales environment, and sales volume in Thailand
increased despite challenging market conditions. In addition, full‑scale operations at the subsidiary in India, which
began production in 2024, contributed to results, and net sales for the business group significantly surpassed the
previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, although the secondary alloy ingot division recorded higher profits, the
impact of fluctuations in aluminum ingot market prices and other factors led to the operating profit falling below the
previous year’s level.


As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment increased 10.9% over the previous
fiscal year to 183,583 million yen, but operating profit decreased 13.9% compared to the previous fiscal year to 9,939
million yen.


<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, although demand for products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment
remained sluggish in the aluminum sheet division, net sales came in higher than in the previous fiscal year. This was
driven by strong sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages and higher sales prices reflecting aluminum
ingot market conditions. In terms of profitability, despite the effect of revisions to processing fees, the decline in sales
of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment caused operating profit to fall below the previous fiscal year’s
level.
In the extrusions division, despite stagnant demand for products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, strong
sales of products for truck bodies, alongside higher sales prices reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions, led to
higher net sales compared with the previous fiscal year. In terms of profitability, despite the effect of revisions to
processing fees, operating profit fell short of the previous fiscal year due to a decline in sales of products for
semiconductor manufacturing equipment, in addition to higher costs reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions.
In the electronic materials division, despite stagnant demand in the overall electronic components industry, net sales
significantly exceeded those of the previous fiscal year, driven by increased sales of anodized foil for electric
capacitors for automotive applications and by growing demand for high-voltage products, which in turn led to an
increase in the average sales price per unit. On the profitability side, higher costs for materials and labor had a
significant impact, and operating loss deteriorated from the previous fiscal year.


As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment increased 7.4% over the previous
fiscal year to 111,219 million yen, and operating profit increased 1.9% over the previous fiscal year to 5,659 million
yen, reflecting improvements outside the main divisions.


<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, although unit sales of truck bodies were in line with the previous fiscal
year, net sales exceeded the previous year’s level as a result of the sales price revision taking effect. On the profit side
as well, operating profit rose well above the previous year’s level, driven by the effect of the sales price revision and
increased sales of after-sales services.
In the automotive parts business group, on the sales side, the partial recovery in domestic automobile production,
along with the launch of new projects and an increase in new product lineups, pushed net sales above the previous
year’s level, despite continued sluggish performance by Japanese automobile manufacturers overseas. On the
profitability side, higher domestic sales, an improved product mix, better productivity, and optimized sales prices led

                                                          7
to a significant improvement compared with the previous fiscal year.
In the engineering business group, the panel system division saw steady demand in the freezing and refrigeration field,
driven by the expansion of logistics sites for food factories and low‑temperature distribution warehouses, the
replacement of aging facilities, and renovations of supermarkets and convenience stores. Despite delays in
construction periods caused by soaring construction costs and labor shortages, net sales in this field came in above the
previous fiscal year. In the clean room field, semiconductor-related demand continued; however, a reactionary decline
following large‑scale orders in the previous fiscal year caused net sales to fall below the previous fiscal year. Overall,
the division’s net sales exceeded those of the previous fiscal year. However, in terms of profitability, the decline in
large-scale orders compared to the previous fiscal year, coupled with higher costs, including labor expenses, led to
operating profit falling below the previous fiscal year’s level.
In the landscape engineering division, although demand continued mainly in the road and bridge sectors, rising
construction material costs and labor shortages led to adjustments to order plans and extensions of construction
periods, causing net sales to fall below the level of the previous fiscal year. In terms of profitability, the decline in
sales, together with higher costs including labor expenses, drove operating profit to fall well below the previous fiscal
year’s level.


As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased 3.1% over the previous
fiscal year to 177,660 million yen, and operating profit increased 87.9% over the previous fiscal year to 5,962 million
yen.

<Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
In the aluminum foil business group, the foil division saw foils for automotive lithium‑ion battery exteriors enter an
adjustment phase in the third quarter, and sales of processed foil for medical product packaging fell below the previous
year’s level. Nevertheless, reflecting higher aluminum ingot market prices and revised processing fees, sales prices
rose, resulting in net sales for the division overall exceeding the previous year’s level.
In the powder and paste division, sales of powder products, such as aluminum powder for electronic materials in heat
dissipation applications and aluminum nitride, remained strong. Sales of paste products for the mainstay automobile
paints decreased due to weak automobile production in Japan. In overseas markets, on the other hand, sales increased
in China, as well as in North America and Southeast Asia. This resulted in overall net sales for the division surpassing
the previous fiscal year.
In the daily necessities division, sales of filter products posted growth among consumer products, but sales of products
for foodstuff, including aluminum foil, fell below the previous fiscal year’s level due to lower sales after price
revisions. Meanwhile, in the packaging products, sales of aluminum containers and paper cups remained firm, and the
division saw the effect of price revisions implemented in response to rising costs, while sales of paper containers
decreased due to the impact of price increases for frozen foods. As a result, overall net sales for the division were
lower than in the previous year.


As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment increased 3.8% over the previous
fiscal year to 113,011 million yen, and operating profit increased 40.1% over the previous fiscal year to 7,650 million
yen.

(2) Overview of the financial position in current fiscal year
    The Group focuses on reducing interest-bearing debt and augmenting its shareholders’ equity to improve its
    financial position, while also implementing various measures in its medium-term management plan aimed at
    improving the health and solidity of its management structure.
    As of the end of the consolidated fiscal year under review, total assets increased by 12,008 million yen compared with
    the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 556,315 million yen, due to an increase in inventories resulting from
    the rise in aluminum ingot prices, among other factors. Liabilities decreased by 5,445 million yen compared with the
    end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 288,381 million yen, due to the repayment of borrowings and other
    factors. Net assets increased by 17,453 million yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to
    267,934 million yen, due to an increase in retained earnings from the recording of profit attributable to owners of
    parent for the fiscal year and other factors. The equity ratio rose from 42.8% at the end of the previous consolidated
    fiscal year to 44.7%.
    Cash flows from operating activities
    Net cash provided by operating activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 25,623 million yen.
    This was because non-cash profit/loss items such as profit before income taxes and depreciation more than offset cash
    outflows, including increases in working capital. Net cash provided by operating activities increased by 13,564 million
    yen compared with the previous consolidated fiscal year, which was largely due to an increase in profit before income
    taxes.
    Cash flows from investing activities
    Net cash used in investing activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 18,082 million yen. This
    was primarily due to the purchase of property, plant and equipment. Net cash used in investing activities decreased by
    1,025 million yen compared with the previous consolidated fiscal year, primarily due to an increase in proceeds from
    the sale of investment securities.
    Cash flows from financing activities
    Net cash used in financing activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 9,768 million yen. This
    was primarily due to the repayment of borrowings. Net cash used in financing activities in the current consolidated
    fiscal year amounted to 9,768 million yen, compared with net cash provided by financing activities totaling 6,243
    million yen in the previous consolidated fiscal year. This was mainly due to an increase in repayments of borrowings.
    As a result, cash and cash equivalents on a consolidated basis were down 1,571 million yen (4.5%) compared with the
    end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, to 33,119 million yen.

(3) Basic policy regarding profit distribution and dividends for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2026, and the fiscal
    year ending March 31, 2027
    The Company’s basic policy on profit distribution for the fiscal year ended March 2026 is “to return profits to its
    shareholders by comprehensively taking into consideration its consolidated financial results and other factors from
    medium- and long-term perspectives while reinforcing its financial strength and management foundation.”

■ Basic Policy 1: Creating new value
Leveraging the Group’s core strengths, we will pursue strategic focus and selection in our business areas, while

streamline our business processes. We will promote digitization centered on the use of AI and data, to increase

restructuring the Group, to build an efficient, resilient management structure.
