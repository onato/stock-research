1. Overview of operating results
(1) Explanation of consolidated operation results
    During the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, the Japanese economy remained on a
    moderate recovery track. However, the outlook continued to be uncertain amid international instability,
    such as the prolonged invasion in Ukraine, tensions in the Middle East, the economic slowdown in
    China, and developments of U.S. tariff policies.
     In the Group, sales of heat dissipation products in the chemicals and powder and paste divisions
     remained strong, despite stagnation stemming from the delayed recovery in demand for products for
     semiconductor manufacturing equipment in the aluminum sheet and extrusions divisions, as well as
     the weak sales of products for domestic automobiles in the secondary alloy ingot division. The
     automotive parts business recovered from the same period of the previous fiscal year, and the truck
     body-related products business also maintained solid performance. As a result, net sales exceeded the
     levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year. Profitability improved significantly in the
     fabricated products and others segment, and profits exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level in the
     aluminum foil, powder and paste segment. Cost increases reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions
     affected both the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment and the aluminum sheet and extrusions
     segment. Despite this, operating profit, ordinary profit, and profit attributable to owners of parent were
     all higher than those of the same period in the previous fiscal year, partly due to the effect of sales
     price revisions.

An overview of each business segment is provided below.
<Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division recorded higher sales of its mainstay aluminum
hydroxide and alumina than in the same period of the previous fiscal year, supported by strong demand
for use in heat-dissipating and fire-resistant fillers, among other factors. Despite a slowdown in sales
of inorganic chlorine compounds among chemical products, net sales came in above the same period
in the previous fiscal year, thanks in part to the effect of sales price revisions implemented in response
to rising raw material costs. On the profitability side, operating profit was higher than in the same
period of the previous fiscal year, reflecting factors including increased sales and the effect of sales
price revisions.
In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s domestic sales of its mainstay
products for automobiles remained stagnant due to continued weakness in automobile production.
Overseas, sales in China continued to be sluggish, while the U.S. experienced a favorable sales
environment. In Thailand, regardless of its weak market conditions, sales volume surpassed the same
period of the previous fiscal year. Full-scale production at a plant in India that began operating last
year also contributed to increased sales. As a result, net sales increased from the same period of the
previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, the secondary alloy ingot division recorded higher
profits; however, the impact of fluctuations in aluminum ingot market prices and other factors led to a
lower profit than in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
As a result of the above, net sales of the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment in the first half of the
current consolidated fiscal year increased by 6,980 million yen (up 8.6%) year on year, from 80,856
million yen to 87,836 million yen. Operating profit increased by 21 million yen (up 0.3%) year on
year, from 6,743 million yen to 6,764 million yen.

<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, although the aluminum sheet division continued to experience
stagnation in sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, net sales increased
compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year, thanks to the strong sales of aluminum
sheets for lithium-ion battery packages, alongside sales prices that reflected aluminum ingot market
conditions, which were above the level in the previous fiscal year. On the profit side, contributing
factors included higher sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages and the effect of
revisions to processing fees. However, operating profit fell below the same period of the previous
fiscal year, as it was significantly impacted by higher costs reflecting aluminum ingot market
conditions.
The extrusions division maintained strong sales of products for truck bodies but recorded lower sales
of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment than in the previous fiscal year. Nevertheless,
sales prices, which reflected aluminum ingot market conditions, were above the previous year’s level.
As a result, net sales increased compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year. In terms of
profitability, despite the effect of revisions to processing fees, operating profit fell below the same
period of the previous fiscal year, as it was significantly impacted by higher costs reflecting aluminum
ingot market conditions.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment for the first half of
the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 4,575 million yen (up 9.3%) year on year, from 49,285
million yen to 53,860 million yen. Operating profit decreased by 672 million yen (down 33.3 %) year
on year, from 2,020 million yen to 1,348 million yen.

<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, despite a slight year-on-year decline in the number of truck

bodies sold, net sales came in above the same period in the previous fiscal year due to price revisions
taking effect. Profitability improved significantly from the previous year’s level due to the sales price
revisions and the softening of material prices.
In the automotive parts business group, sales in Japan recovered partially from last year's automobile
production cuts, alongside the launch of new projects. Despite the ongoing slump in the Chinese
market, net sales exceeded levels in the same period of the previous fiscal year. On the profit side,
increased sales and improvement in the product mix, along with a better productivity, resulted in
improved performance compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year.
In the engineering business group, the panel system division continued to see demand for expansion
of food factories and the logistics sites of low-temperature distribution warehouses in the freezing and
refrigeration field, as well as the reconstruction of these facilities due to aging. Additionally, demand
for renovating existing stores and kitchen facilities for supermarkets and convenience stores remained
strong, while the impact of delays in construction periods due to soaring costs and labor shortages
continued. Nevertheless, net sales came in above the same period in the previous fiscal year. In the
clean room field, while demand for sales of semiconductor-related products has shown signs of
stabilization in some areas, net sales surpassed the same period of the previous fiscal year, reflecting
continued demand for new plant construction and the expansion of existing facilities. In terms of
profitability, despite cost increases such as labor expenses, operating profit came in above the previous
year’s level.
As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 3,452
million yen (up 4.4%) to 81,408 million yen in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year,
compared with 77,956 million yen in the same period of the previous fiscal year. An operating profit
of 1,356 million yen was recorded, representing an improvement of 3,135 million yen from the
operating loss of 1,779 million yen recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal year.

<Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
In the aluminum foil business group, the foil division saw an increase in orders of foils for automotive
lithium-ion battery exteriors, compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year when demand
was in an adjustment phase. Despite decreased sales of processed foil for medical product packaging,
net sales for the division overall came in above the previous year’s level.
The powder and paste division experienced stronger demand for heat dissipation, such as aluminum
powder for electronic materials and aluminum nitride, resulting in higher sales than in the same period
of the previous fiscal year. Sales of paste products for the mainstay automobile paints fell below the
previous fiscal year's level due to weak automobile production in Japan. In overseas markets, sales to
Europe underperformed, while sales to China, North America, and Southeast Asia increased. As a
result, net sales for the division overall surpassed the same period of the previous fiscal year.
The daily necessities division recorded a growth in sales of house-care products among consumer
products, but sales of products for foodstuff, including aluminum foil, fell slightly below the previous
fiscal year’s level due to lower sales following sales price revisions. At the same time, thanks in part
to strong sales of packaging products, including aluminum containers for frozen food and paper
containers, net sales for the division overall slightly exceeded the same period of the previous fiscal
year.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment in the first half of
the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,526 million yen (up 4.8%) year on year, from 53,043
million yen to 55,569 million yen. Operating profit increased by 499 million yen (up 15.4%) year on
year, from 3,241 million yen to 3,740 million yen.

(2) Explanation of consolidated financial position
    a. Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
    At the end of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, total assets increased by 13,328
    million yen compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 557,635 million yen due
    to an increase in cash and deposits and other factors. Liabilities increased by 9,129 million yen
    compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 302,955 million yen, due to an
    increase in long-term borrowings and other factors. Net assets increased by 4,199 million yen
    compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 254,680 million yen due to an
    increase in retained earnings from the recording of profit attributable to owners of parent and other
    factors. The equity ratio fell from 42.8% at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 42.5%.
    b. Cash flow
    Cash flows from operating activities
    Net cash provided by operating activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year
    amounted to 23,084 million yen. This was because non-cash profit/loss items such as profit before
    income taxes and depreciation more than offset cash outflows, including income taxes paid. Net cash
    provided by operating activities increased by 21,870 million yen year on year, primarily due to factors
    such as a decrease in working capital.
    Cash flows from investing activities
    Net cash used in investing activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year amounted
    to 11,838 million yen. This was primarily due to purchase of property, plant and equipment. Net cash
    used in investing activities was up 2,219 million yen compared with the same period in the previous
    fiscal year, which was primarily due to factors such as an increase in purchase of shares of subsidiaries
    and associates.
    Cash flows from financing activities
    Net cash provided by financing activities in the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year
    amounted to 9,426 million yen. This was primarily due to proceeds from long-term borrowings. Net
    cash provided by financing activities decreased by 10,426 million yen year on year, which was
    primarily due to factors such as an increase in repayments of long-term borrowings.
    As a result, at the end of the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year, cash and cash equivalents
    were up 20,426 million yen (58.9%) compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year,
    to 55,116 million yen.

(3) Explanation of forward-looking information, including consolidated earnings forecasts
    The consolidated earnings forecast for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, remains unchanged
    from the announcement made on May 15, 2025. Although our performance during the first half of
    the current consolidated fiscal year progressed steadily, uncertainties remain for the third quarter and
    beyond, including the impact of U.S. tariff policies on global economies and markets, as well as
    geopolitical risks. In light of the prevailing conditions, including the need to closely monitor trends
    in the automotive and semiconductor-related markets, the Company has left its full-year consolidated
    earnings forecast unchanged.
