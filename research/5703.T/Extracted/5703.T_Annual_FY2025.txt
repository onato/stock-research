1. Overview of operating results
(1) Overview of operating results in fiscal year under review
      (i) Operating results in fiscal year under review
     In the global economy during the period under review, there were overall signs of recovery, such as economic
     expansion centered on consumer spending in the U.S. However, in addition to the slowdown of the Chinese economy
     and fluctuations in financial and capital markets, the latter half of the period required close monitoring of trends in
     U.S. trade policy and other factors. In Japan, although there was a gradual improvement in the employment and income
     conditions and corporate earnings, the situation remained unpredictable due to such factors as U.S. policy trends and
     the impact of rising prices on corporate activities and consumer spending.
    In the aluminum industry, total domestic demand for aluminum fell short of the previous fiscal year’s level, mainly
    due to weak, albeit recovering, demand from the automotive sector. In addition, prices of aluminum ingots and other
    raw materials continued to rise.
    The Group has been promoting management reforms under its Medium-term Management Plan (FY2023–FY2025),
    which is based on the basic policies of “initiatives for new team Nippon Light Metal” and “Provide products and
    businesses that contribute to the creation of social value.” In this rapidly changing business environment, we are
    decisively carrying out reforms based on a sense of crisis that sustainable growth will be difficult to achieve with
    conventional group management, and that therefore a new Group management is urgently needed.
    Since last June, as part of our management reforms, we have grouped businesses with market areas in close proximity
    (transitioned to a business group system), accelerated the creation of new products and businesses by strengthening
    intra-Group collaboration, and started formulating various KPIs to improve capital efficiency. At the same time, we
    have been working to strengthen the supervisory function of the Board of Directors and improve the speed and
    governance of decision-making in management. The newly established Marketing & Incubation Administration Office
    is leading activities related to new product development for existing businesses and future business creation. As a
    specific example, we have started to develop low-cost, low-CFP (carbon footprint of products) mass-production
    technology for lithium-ion batteries used in large-scale power storage batteries and electric vehicles, in collaboration
    with other companies.
    In addition, we believe that the needs of customers and society for reducing environmental burdens, including
    decarbonization, are an opportunity to grow the corporate value of the Group. Therefore, we have positioned carbon-
    neutral products, such as lightweight structural materials for automobiles, EV battery-related materials and parts
    (device cooling units), and battery-related materials, as important products and services for the Group, and have been
    focusing on the development of new products and technologies with this in mind.
    With regard to the initiatives to prevent recurrence of the inappropriate quality-related behavior that was discovered
    at Group companies in 2021, which were formulated and announced in 2023 and that center on management reforms
    and stronger internal control functions, the Group will continue to make concerted efforts. These efforts include the
    creation of a corporate culture in which all officers and employees can speak out frankly.
    (Progress in preventing recurrence within the Group is posted on the Company’s website below. Company website:
    https://www.nikkeikinholdings.co.jp/company/quality-control.html (Japanese only))
    Regarding the share transfer and management integration of Toyo Aluminium K.K., which was announced in August
    2022, the parties concerned agreed to terminate the management integration in October of last year. This was because
    the conditions for implementing the management integration were not met. Toyo Aluminium K.K. will aim to make
    further progress as the aluminum foil business group, one of our business groups.
    Financial results in the fiscal year under review were as follows.
    The automobile-related products have been impacted by domestic automobile production, which remains weak despite
    a recovery trend, continued sluggish sales in China, and construction delays due to soaring construction costs and
    labor shortages in the panel system division. However, sales of truck body-related products were solid and
    semiconductor-related products, which are on a gradual path, saw higher sales than in the previous fiscal year, resulting
    in net sales exceeding the previous fiscal year’s level. On the profit side, operating profit, ordinary profit, and profit
    attributable to owners of parent increased year on year. This was due to a rising phase for the aluminum ingot market
    and the effect of sales price revisions leading to significant improvements in aluminum sheets and extrusions, despite
    the impact of persistently high raw material prices.

     <Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
    In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division saw higher sales of its mainstay aluminum hydroxide and
    alumina-related products for ceramics than in the previous fiscal year. For chemicals, there were solid sales of


                                                                 - 3 -
inorganic chlorine compounds. As a result of these factors, as well as a positive impact from sales price revisions, net
sales and operating profit were higher than the previous fiscal year’s level.
In the carbon products division, sales of mainstay carbon blocks for the steel industry declined, resulting in lower sales
than in the previous fiscal year and a significant deterioration in operating loss from the previous fiscal year in terms
of profitability.
In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s domestic sales of its mainstay products for
automobiles remained sluggish despite a recovery trend in domestic automobile production. Overseas, sales volumes
declined due to lower sales in China and Thailand, except in the U.S. where sales were solid. As a result, net sales
increased from the previous fiscal year due to higher sales prices reflecting aluminum ingot market conditions, but
operating profit was significantly lower than the previous year in terms of profitability.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment increased 6.2% over the previous
fiscal year to 165,499 million yen, but operating profit decreased 2.4% over the previous fiscal year to 11,542 million
yen.
<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, in terms of sales, the aluminum sheet division, while still on the path to
gradual recovery of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, posted higher sales volumes. In
combination with strong sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages, higher sales prices reflecting
aluminum ingot market conditions, and the effect of sales price revisions for processing fees, net sales and operating
profit were both significantly higher than the previous fiscal year.
The extrusions division saw higher sales of products for truck bodies than the previous fiscal year’s level, with a
gradual recovery in sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, coupled with higher sales prices
reflecting the aluminum ingot market conditions and the effect of sales price revisions for processing fees. As a result,
both net sales and operating profit were above the levels of the previous fiscal year.
In the electronic materials division, despite stagnant demand in the overall electronic components industry, net sales
and operating profit both exceeded those of the previous fiscal year due to higher sales prices and increased sales of
anodized foil for electric capacitors for automotive applications.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment increased 9.3% over the previous
fiscal year to 103,555 million yen, and operating profit amounted to 5,555 million yen, up 141.4%.


<Fabricated products and others>
In the transport equipment business group, net sales were higher than the previous fiscal year’s level due to sales price
revisions, coupled with continued strong demand for products for truck bodies overall in terms of sales. In terms
of profitability, operating profit was posted, a significant improvement from operating loss in the previous fiscal
year , thanks to the effect of sales price revisions, despite the impact of persistently high material prices.
In the automotive parts business group, net sales were below the previous fiscal year’s level due to the sluggish
automobile production in Japan, despite a recovery trend, and the continued slump in the Chinese market for Japanese
automobile manufacturers, among other factors. On the profitability side, the impact of reduced sales was significant
and operating profit worsened from the previous fiscal year’s level, regardless of the effect of sales price revisions and
reductions in fixed and other costs.
In the engineering business group, although the panel system division continued to see demand for expansion of food
factories and the logistics sites of low-temperature distribution warehouses in the freezing and refrigeration field, as
well as the reconstruction of these facilities due to aging, soaring construction costs and labor shortages caused delays
to construction periods. In the clean room field, although demand for clean rooms for semiconductor-related and
manufacturing equipment manufacturers continued, overall division net sales and operating profit fell below the
previous fiscal year’s levels due to the impact of soaring raw material prices.
In the landscape engineering division, net sales increased from the previous fiscal year due to strong sales of
scaffolding products used for inspections for roads and bridges. Profits, however, declined due to higher construction
material prices.
As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 2.7% to 172,249 million
yen over the previous fiscal year, and operating profit increased 20.6% to 3,173 million yen.

<Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
In the aluminum foil business group, adjustment of foils for automotive lithium-ion battery exteriors continues, and
sales of processed foil for medical product packaging were slightly lower than the previous fiscal year’s level. This
contributed to flat net sales for the division overall, at the same level of the previous fiscal year, due to higher sales
prices reflecting the aluminum ingot market conditions. However, operating profit fell below the level of the previous
fiscal year.
The powder and paste division experienced a recovery in demand for heat dissipation, such as aluminum powder for
electronic materials and aluminum nitride, and sales continue to be above the previous fiscal year’s level. In addition,
although sales of paste products for the mainstay automobile paints fell below the same period of the previous fiscal
year, sales in overseas markets were strong. As a result, net sales and operating profit for the division overall exceeded
the previous fiscal year’s level.
In the daily necessities division, the effect of price increases resulting from revisions to the sales prices of consumer
products, including aluminum foil for food products and house-care products, more than offset lower sales. In addition,
sales of packaging products for frozen food and sales of paper containers were both strong. As a result, net sales for
the division overall exceeded the previous fiscal year’s level. Meanwhile, on the profit side, operating profit was in
line with the previous fiscal year due to the inability to fully absorb the impact of soaring raw material prices.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment increased 3.4% over the previous
fiscal year to 108,877 million yen, and operating profit fell 3.1% to 5,460 million yen.

(2) Overview of financial position in current fiscal year
      The Group focuses on reducing interest-bearing debt and augmenting its shareholders’ equity to improve its
      financial position, while also implementing various measures in its medium-term management plan aimed at
      improving the health and solidity of its management structure.
      As of the end of the consolidated fiscal year under review, total assets increased by 212 million yen compared
      with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 544,307 million yen, due to an increase in inventories
      resulting from the rise in aluminum ingot prices among other factors. Liabilities decreased by 11,272 million yen
      compared with the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 293,826 million yen, due to a decrease in
      accounts payable and notes payable resulting from the fact that the end of the previous fiscal year fell on a
      holiday, and other factors. Net assets increased by 11,484 million yen compared with the end of the previous
      consolidated fiscal year to 250,481 million yen, due to an increase in retained earnings resulting from the
      recording of profit attributable to owners of the parent and other factors. The equity ratio rose from 40.8% at the
      end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 42.8%.

      Cash flows from operating activities
      Net cash provided by operating activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 12,059 million
      yen. This was because non-cash profit/loss items such as profit before income taxes and depreciation more than
      offset cash outflows, including increases in working capital. Net cash provided by operating activities decreased
      by 25,982 million yen compared with the previous consolidated fiscal year, which was largely due to increases in
      working capital.



      Cash flows from investing activities
      Net cash used in investing activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 19,107 million yen,
      primarily due to purchase of property, plant and equipment. Net cash used in investing activities decreased by
      4,824 million yen compared with the previous consolidated fiscal year, primarily due to a decrease in purchase of
      property, plant and equipment.

      Cash flows from financing activities
      Net cash provided by financing activities in the consolidated fiscal year under review amounted to 6,243 million
      yen. This was primarily due to proceeds from long-term borrowings. Net cash provided by financing activities in
      the current consolidated fiscal year amounted to 6,243 million yen, compared with net cash used in financing
      activities totaling 11,049 million yen in the previous consolidated fiscal year. This was mainly due to an increase
      in income from long-term borrowings.

      As a result, cash and cash equivalents on a consolidated basis were down 397 million yen (1.1%) compared with
      the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, to 34,690 million yen.

(3) Basic policy regarding profit distribution and dividends for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025, and the fiscal
    year ending March 31, 2026
    The Company’s basic policy on profit distribution is “to return profits to its shareholders by comprehensively taking
    into consideration its consolidated financial results and other factors from medium- and long-term perspectives while
    reinforcing its financial strength and management foundation.”
    The Group decides on dividend amounts using a total dividend payout ratio, including the acquisition of treasury
    stock, of 30% or more as its indicator for profit returns.
    The Group plans to pay year-end dividends of 50 yen per share for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025.
    The Group will decide on dividend amounts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, using a total dividend payout

  (Notes on changes in accounting policies)
  (Application of “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes,” etc.)
  The Company has applied the “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes” (ASBJ Statement No. 27, October 28,
  2022; the “Revised Accounting Standard 2022”), etc. from the beginning of the first quarter of the current consolidated
  fiscal year.

  Previously, calculated amounts of current income taxes on income, etc. (“Income Taxes”) were recorded in profit or loss
  in accordance with laws and regulations, but now Income Taxes on income are recorded separately in profit or loss,
  shareholders’ equity, or other comprehensive income according to the transaction, etc., from which it occurred. For
  Income Taxes recorded in accumulated other comprehensive income, when a transaction, etc. is recorded in profit or
  loss that gives rise to Income Taxes, the corresponding tax amount will be recorded in profit or loss. Additionally, if the
  transaction, etc., subject to taxation is related to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income in addition to profit
  or loss, and it is difficult to calculate the amount of Income Taxes, to be applied to shareholders’ equity or other
  comprehensive income, the amount of such taxes is recorded in profit or loss.

  For the treatment of the tax effect of the deferral for tax purposes of gains or losses on sale of shares in subsidiaries, etc.
  between consolidated companies when deferred tax assets or liabilities are recorded for temporary differences related to
  such gains or losses in the financial statements of the entity that sold the shares in subsidiaries, etc., the amount of
  deferred tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences was previously not adjusted in the consolidation
  process, but now deferred tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences are reversed.

  The amendment to categories in which Income Taxes should be recorded (taxes on other comprehensive income)
  follows the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso of paragraph 20-3 of the Revised Accounting Standard
  2022 and the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso (2) of paragraph 65-2 of Implementation Guidance
  on Tax Effect Accounting (ASBJ Guidance No. 28, October 28, 2022) (“Revised Implementation Guidance 2022”).
  Accordingly, the cumulative effects of retrospectively applying the new accounting policies prior to the
  beginning of the current consolidated fiscal year were added to or subtracted from the balance of retained earnings at
  the beginning of the current consolidated fiscal year, and the corresponding amount was added or subtracted to the
  appropriate category of capital surplus or accumulated other comprehensive income, with the new accounting
  policies applied from the beginning balance. As a result, retained earnings at the beginning of the current
  consolidated fiscal year increased by 24 million yen and valuation difference on available-for sale securities
  decreased by the same amount. In response to the amendment related to the revised accounting treatment for
  consolidated financial statements when gains or losses on sale of shares in subsidiaries, etc. between consolidated
  companies were deferred for tax purposes, the Company has applied the Revised Implementation Guidance 2022
  from the beginning of the current consolidated fiscal year. This change in accounting policies was applied
  retrospectively. Hence, the consolidated financial statements for the consolidated financial statements for the
  previous fiscal year have been modified retrospectively. As a result, compared with before the retroactive application,
  the consolidated balance sheet for the previous fiscal year shows an increase of 902 million yen in retained earnings
  and an increase of the same amount in deferred tax assets. The consolidated income statement for the previous fiscal
  year shows a decrease of 902 million yen in income taxes - deferred, resulting in an increase of the same amount in
  profit attributable to owners of parent. The impact on per share information is disclosed in the relevant section.

 1. Summary of reportable segments
    The Group’s reportable segments are those units for which discrete financial information is available and that are
    periodically evaluated so that the Board of Directors can decide on the allocation of management resources and
    assess performance.
    The Group primarily carries out business related to the four areas of aluminum ingot and chemicals, aluminum
    sheet and extrusions, fabricated products and others, and aluminum foil, powder and paste; these products cover a
    wide range of areas related to aluminum.
    The aluminum ingot and chemicals segment sells aluminum, aluminum hydroxide, various chemicals and
    aluminum ingot and alloys. The aluminum sheet and extrusions segment sells aluminum sheet and aluminum
    extruded products. The fabricated products and others segment sells transport-related products, freezer and
    refrigeration panels, and other aluminum fabricated products. The aluminum foil, powder and paste segment sells
    foil and powder products.
    (Notes on changes in reporting segments, etc.)
    Effective from the current consolidated fiscal year, we have adopted a business operating structure under business
    groups and functional organizations, with the objective of maximizing its corporate value as the Group.
    Accordingly, some divisions belonging to the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment, the aluminum sheet and
    extrusions segment, and the fabricated products and others segment have been reclassified. For segment
    information for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2024, the information prepared based on the reporting segments
    for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025 is provided.

    The accounting methods for reported business segments are generally the same as those for statements in the “Basis
    of Presenting the Consolidated Financial Statements.” Profit by reportable segment is based on operating profit.
    Intersegment sales and transfers are based on actual market prices.
