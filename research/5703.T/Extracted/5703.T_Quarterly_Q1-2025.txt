1. Qualitative Information on Quarterly Results
(1) Explanation of consolidated operating results position
    During the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, economic activity in Japan generally showed a
    gradual recovery. However, the outlook still remains uncertain, given foreign exchange fluctuations including the
    depreciating yen, persistently high and rising prices of raw materials, fuels, and materials, as well as unstable
    international conditions, such as the prolonged invasion in Ukraine, worsening conditions in the Middle East, and
    China's economic slowdown, among other factors.
    The Group saw continued sluggish sales in the Chinese automobile sector, the impact of automobile production cuts
    in Japan, and a decline in the panel system division’s sales of large projects in the freezing and refrigeration field.
    Nevertheless, net sales surpassed the levels of the previous fiscal year, as the secondary alloy ingot division to the
    automobile sector continued to perform well in the U.S., the truck body business continued to enjoy solid sales, and
    the aluminum ingot market was on the upswing. On the profit side, the aluminum sheet and extrusions segment
    improved significantly over the same period of the previous fiscal year, along with higher profits in both the aluminum
    ingot and chemicals segment and the aluminum foil, powder and paste segment than the levels in the previous fiscal
    year. As a result, operating profit, ordinary profit, and profit attributable to owners of parent increased compared to
    the same period of the previous fiscal year.

<Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division posted sales of its mainstays, aluminum hydroxide and
alumina-related products for refractories and abrasives, on a par with the levels of the previous fiscal year. However,
a slowdown in sales of organic chlorine compounds among chemical products, as well as weak sales of products for
flocculants, had a negative impact, resulting in both net sales and operating profit slightly lower than in the same
period of the previous fiscal year.
In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s overseas sales of its mainstay products for
automobiles continued to be strong in the U.S., while sales volume in Japan declined, partly due to continued cuts in
auto production that began in the fourth quarter of the previous fiscal year. On the other hand, net sales were above
the same period of the previous fiscal year due to the effect of higher sales prices reflecting the aluminum ingot market
conditions. On the profitability side, despite the impact of lower sales and higher material prices in the secondary
alloy ingot division, operating profit was higher than in the same period of the previous year, which was significantly
impacted by the upswing in aluminum market prices.
As a result of the above, net sales of the aluminum ingot and chemicals in the first quarter of the current consolidated
fiscal year increased by 2,197 million yen (5.7%) year on year, from 38,366 million yen to 40,563 million yen.
Operating profit in this segment increased by 574 million yen (17.5%) year on year, from 3,275 million yen to 3,849
million yen.
<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, sales volume in the aluminum sheet division increased compared with the
same period of the previous fiscal year, despite a correction still to be made in sales of thick-gauge plate for
semiconductor manufacturing equipment. In addition, higher sales prices reflecting the aluminum market conditions
resulted in both net sales and operating profit exceeding the levels of the previous fiscal year.
In the extrusions division, although sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment need more time to
recover, sales of products for truck bodies have been on a solid path since the recovery of sales from the previous
fiscal year, and sales price increases reflecting the aluminum market conditions, in addition to the effect of sales price
revisions, resulted in both net sales and operating profit surpassing the levels of the previous fiscal year.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusion products segment for the first three months of
the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,055 million yen (9.3%) year on year, from 22,098 million yen to
24,153 million yen. An operating profit of 632 million yen was recorded, representing an improvement of 1,365
million yen from the operating loss of 733 million yen recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal year.

    <Fabricated products and others>
    In the transport equipment business group, sales of products for truck bodies have been on a solid path since the
    recovery of sales from the previous fiscal year. In addition, the effect of sales price revisions resulted in higher net
    sales over the same period of the previous fiscal year. Profitability continued to improve from the same period of the
    previous fiscal year, despite the impact of higher material prices.
    In the automotive parts business group, sales in Japan were affected by continued automobile production cuts that
    began in the fourth quarter of the previous fiscal year, despite an increase in sales due to the launch of new products.
    Overseas, although mass-production operations were launched at the new U.S. plant, the Chinese market remains
    sluggish. This resulted in lower net sales and a deterioration in profitability compared with the previous fiscal year.
    In the engineering business group, the panel system division posted solid sales in the clean room field, with continued
    demand for clean rooms for semiconductor-related manufacturers. However, the division’s sales in the freezing and
    refrigeration field declined in large projects for food factories and low-temperature distribution warehouses as a result
    of trends such as the revision of construction periods and plans due to rising costs of materials, as well as labor
    shortages and regulation of working hours in the construction industry. As a result, both net sales and operating profit
    fell below the levels of the previous fiscal year.
    As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 664 million yen (1.8%)
    to 37,358 million yen in the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, compared to 36,694 million yen
    in the same period of the previous fiscal year. An operating loss of 1,125 million yen was recorded, representing a
    downward change of 51 million yen from the 1,074 million yen loss recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal
    year.
    <Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
    In the aluminum foil business group, although the aluminum foil division posted solid sales of processed foil for
    medical product packaging, net sales for the division overall were flat at the level of the previous fiscal year, partly
    due to the ongoing inventory adjustment of foils for automotive lithium-ion battery exteriors.
    In the powder and paste division, sales of powder products for heat dissipation, such as aluminum powder for
    electronic materials and aluminum nitride, surpassed the levels of the previous fiscal year. In addition, sales of paste
    products for mainstay automobile paints were on par with the previous fiscal year both in domestic and overseas
    markets. As a result, net sales for the division overall were higher than in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
    In the daily necessities division, sales of kitchen utensils to consumers fell below the levels of the previous fiscal year,
    but sales of packaging products for frozen food products remained strong. As a result, net sales for the division overall
    exceeded the levels of the previous fiscal year.
    As a result of the above, net sales of the aluminum foil, powder and paste segments in the first three months of the
    current consolidated fiscal year increased by 1,235 million yen (4.9%) year on year, from 25,313 million yen to 26,548
    million yen. Operating profit increased by 280 million yen (18.4%) year on year, from 1,522 million yen to 1,802
    million yen.


(2) Explanation of consolidated financial position
    Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
    As of the end of the first three months of the current consolidated fiscal year, total assets increased by 1,918 million
    yen compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year, to 546,013 million yen due to an increase in
    inventories caused by rising aluminum ingot prices and the depreciating yen, among other factors. Liabilities
    decreased by 617 million yen compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 304,481 million yen due
    to a decrease in income taxes payable as a result of the filing and payment of income taxes and other factors. Net
    assets increased by 2,535 million yen compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 241,532 million
    yen due to an increase in foreign currency translation adjustment in response to the depreciating yen and other factors.
    The equity ratio rose from 40.8% at the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year to 41.1%.

    (Notes on changes in accounting policies)
    (Application of “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes,” etc.)
    The Company has applied the “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes” (ASBJ Statement No. 27, October
    28, 2022; the “Revised Accounting Standard 2022” ), etc. from the beginning of the first quarter ended June 30, 2024.
    Previously, calculated amounts of current income taxes on income, etc. (“Income Taxes”) were recorded in profit or
    loss in accordance with laws and regulations, but now Income Taxes on income are recorded separately in profit or
    loss, shareholders’ equity, or other comprehensive income according to the transaction, etc., from which it occurred.
    For Income Taxes recorded in accumulated other comprehensive income, when a transaction, etc. is recorded in profit
    or loss that gives rise to Income Taxes, the corresponding tax amount will be recorded in profit or loss. Additionally,
    if the transaction, etc., subject to taxation is related to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income in addition
    to profit or loss, and it is difficult to calculate the amount of Income Taxes, to be applied to shareholders’ equity or
    other comprehensive income, the amount of such taxes is recorded in profit or loss.
    For the treatment of the tax effect of the deferral for tax purposes of gains or losses on sale of shares in subsidiaries,
    etc. between consolidated companies when deferred tax assets or liabilities are recorded for temporary differences
    related to such gains or losses in the financial statements of the entity that sold the shares in subsidiaries, etc., the
    amount of deferred tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences was previously not adjusted in the
    consolidation process, but now deferred tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences are reversed.
    The amendment to categories in which Income Taxes should be recorded (taxes on other comprehensive income)
    follows the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso of paragraph 20-3 of the Revised Accounting Standard
    2022 and the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso (2) of paragraph 65-2 of Implementation Guidance on
    Tax Effect Accounting (ASBJ Guidance No. 28, October 28, 2022) (“Revised Implementation Guidance 2022 ”).
