1. Overview of operating results
(1) Overview of operating results for the current quarter
    During the first nine months of the current consolidated fiscal year, economic activity in Japan showed a gradual
    recovery. However, the outlook still remains uncertain, given the impact of the change in the U.S. administration on
    trade policy, in addition to unstable exchange rates and persistently high levels in raw material prices, as well as
    international instability, such as the conditions in the Middle East, the prolonged invasion in Ukraine, and China’s
    economic slowdown, among other factors.
    In the Group, in terms of sales, automobile-related products have been impacted by domestic automobile production,
    which remains weak despite a recovery trend, and by continued sluggish sales in China. The panel system division,
    albeit enjoying strong demand, has continued to be impacted by factors causing delays and revisions to construction
    periods due to soaring construction costs and labor shortages. Nevertheless, the truck body business was solid overall
    and semiconductor-related products, which are on a gradual path, saw higher sales than in the same period of the
    previous fiscal year. In addition, aluminum ingot prices were on the upswing and the effect of sales price revisions
    took effect. As a result, net sales surpassed the levels of the previous fiscal year. On the profit side, the aluminum
    sheet and extrusions segment continued to improve significantly over the same period of the previous fiscal year,
    along with higher profits in both the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment and the aluminum foil, powder and paste
    segment than the levels in the previous fiscal year. As a result, operating profit, ordinary profit, and profit attributable
    to owners of parent increased compared with the same period of the previous fiscal year.

<Aluminum ingot and chemicals>
In the chemicals business group, the chemicals division saw higher sales of its mainstay aluminum hydroxide and
alumina-related products for ceramics than in the same period of the previous fiscal year. The chemicals saw solid
sales of inorganic chlorine compounds. As a result, net sales were higher than the previous fiscal year’s level. On the
profitability side, despite the impact of soaring raw material prices, operating profit was slightly higher than in the
same period of the previous fiscal year due to the effect of sales price revisions.
In the metal business group, the secondary alloy ingot division’s domestic sales of its mainstay products for
automobiles remained sluggish despite a recovery trend in domestic automobile production from production cuts.
Overseas, sales volumes declined due to lower sales in China and Thailand, except in the U.S. where sales were solid.
Nevertheless, net sales were above the same period of the previous fiscal year with higher sales prices reflecting the
aluminum ingot market conditions. On the profitability side, despite the impact of lower sales in the secondary alloy
ingot division, operating profit was slightly higher than in the same period of the previous fiscal year, as it was
significantly impacted by higher sales prices reflecting the aluminum ingot market.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum ingot and chemicals segment in the first nine months of the current
consolidated fiscal year increased by 4,657 million yen (3.9%) year on year, from 118,337 million yen to 122,994
million yen. Operating profit increased by 498 million yen (5.3%) year on year, from 9,485 million yen to 9,983
million yen.
<Aluminum sheet and extrusions>
In the sheet & extrusion business group, in terms of sales, the aluminum sheet division, while still on the path to
gradual recovery of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, posted higher sales volumes than the
previous fiscal year’s level. In addition, sales of aluminum sheets for lithium-ion battery packages were strong. As a
result, net sales were above the same period of the previous fiscal year. On the profitability side, operating profit was
significantly higher than in the same period of the previous fiscal year thanks to increased sales volumes, coupled
with higher sales prices reflecting market conditions for aluminum ingot and the effect of sales price revisions for
processing fees.
The extrusions division saw higher sales of products for truck bodies than the previous fiscal year’s level, with a
gradual recovery in sales of products for semiconductor manufacturing equipment, coupled with higher sales prices
reflecting the aluminum ingot market conditions and the effect of sales price revisions for processing fees. As a result,
both net sales and operating profit were above the levels of the previous fiscal year.
As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum sheet and extrusion products segment for the first nine months of
the current consolidated fiscal year increased by 7,618 million yen (10.9%) year on year, from 69,634 million yen to

    <Fabricated products and others>
    In the transport equipment business group, net sales were higher than the previous fiscal year’s level due to sales price
    revisions, coupled with continued strong demand for products for truck bodies overall in terms of sales. Profitability
    improved significantly from the same period of the previous fiscal year due to the effect of sales price revisions,
    despite the impact of persistently high raw material prices.
    In the automotive parts business group, net sales were below the previous fiscal year’s level due to the sluggish
    automobile production in Japan, despite a recovery trend, and the continued slump in the Chinese market for Japanese
    automobile manufacturers, among other factors. On the profitability side, the impact on sales was significant and the
    performance worsened from the previous fiscal year’s level, regardless of the effect of sales price revisions and
    reductions in fixed and other costs.
    In the engineering business group, the panel system division continues to see demand for expansion of food factories
    and the logistics sites of low-temperature distribution warehouses as well as reconstruction of these facilities due to
    aging in the freezing and refrigeration field, while delays in construction periods due to soaring construction costs and
    labor shortages. In the clean room field, despite continued demand for semiconductor-related and manufacturing
    equipment manufacturers, there was a decline due to the influence of the sales of large projects in the previous fiscal
    year. As a result, both net sales and operating profit fell below the levels of the previous year.
    As a result of the above, net sales in the fabricated products and others segment increased by 1,306 million yen (1.1%)
    to 121,474 million yen in the first nine months of the current consolidated fiscal year, compared with 120,168 million
    yen in the same period of the previous fiscal year. An operating loss of 1,468 million yen was recorded, a negative
    increase from the loss of 236 million yen recorded in the same period of the previous fiscal year.
    <Aluminum foil, powder and paste>
    In the aluminum foil business group, adjustment of foils for automotive lithium-ion battery exteriors continues, and
    sales of processed foil for medical product packaging were slightly lower than the previous fiscal year’s level.
    Nevertheless, net sales for the division overall were flat, at the same level of the previous fiscal year, due to higher
    sales prices reflecting the aluminum ingot market conditions.
    The powder and paste division experienced a recovery in demand for heat dissipation, such as aluminum powder for
    electronic materials and aluminum nitride, and sales continue to be above the previous fiscal year’s level. In addition,
    although sales of paste products for the mainstay automobile paints fell below the same period of the previous fiscal
    year, sales to overseas markets were strong. As a result, net sales for the division overall exceeded the previous fiscal
    year’s level.
    In the daily necessities division, the effect of price increases resulting from revisions to the sales prices of consumer
    products, including aluminum foil for food products and house-care products, more than offset lower sales. In
    addition, sales of packaging products for frozen food and sales of paper containers were both strong, alongside sales
    price revisions due to soaring raw material prices. As a result, net sales for the division overall exceeded the previous
    fiscal year’s level.
    As a result of the above, net sales in the aluminum foil, powder and paste segments in the first nine months of the
    current consolidated fiscal year increased by 2,662 million yen (3.3%) year on year, from 79,574 million yen to 82,236
    million yen. Operating profit increased by 177 million yen (3.3%) year on year, from 5,379 million yen to 5,556
    million yen.

    (Notes on changes in accounting policies)
    (Application of “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes,” etc.)

    The Company has applied the “Accounting Standard for Current Income Taxes” (ASBJ Statement No.
    27, October 28, 2022; the “Revised Accounting Standard 2022”), etc. from the beginning of the first
    quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year.

    Previously, calculated amounts of current income taxes on income, etc. (“Income Taxes”) were
    recorded in profit or loss in accordance with laws and regulations, but now Income Taxes on income
    are recorded separately in profit or loss, shareholders’ equity, or other comprehensive income
    according to the transaction, etc., from which it occurred. For Income Taxes recorded in accumulated
    other comprehensive income, when a transaction, etc. is recorded in profit or loss that gives rise to
    Income Taxes, the corresponding tax amount will be recorded in profit or loss. Additionally, if the
    transaction, etc., subject to taxation is related to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income
    in addition to profit or loss, and it is difficult to calculate the amount of Income Taxes, to be applied
    to shareholders’ equity or other comprehensive income, the amount of such taxes is recorded in profit
    or loss.

    For the treatment of the tax effect of the deferral for tax purposes of gains or losses on sale of shares
    in subsidiaries, etc. between consolidated companies when deferred tax assets or liabilities are
    recorded for temporary differences related to such gains or losses in the financial statements of the
    entity that sold the shares in subsidiaries, etc., the amount of deferred tax assets or liabilities related to
    such temporary differences was previously not adjusted in the consolidation process, but now deferred
    tax assets or liabilities related to such temporary differences are reversed.

    The amendment to categories in which Income Taxes should be recorded (taxes on other
    comprehensive income) follows the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso of paragraph 20-3
    of the Revised Accounting Standard 2022 and the transitional treatment prescribed in the proviso (2)
    of paragraph 65-2 of Implementation Guidance on Tax Effect Accounting (ASBJ Guidance No. 28,
    October 28, 2022) (“Revised Implementation Guidance 2022”).

    Accordingly, the cumulative effects of retrospectively applying the new accounting policies prior to
    the beginning of the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year were added to or subtracted
    from the balance of retained earnings at the beginning of the first quarter of the current consolidated
    fiscal year, and the corresponding amount was added or subtracted to the appropriate category of
    capital surplus or accumulated other comprehensive income, with the new accounting policies applied
    from the beginning balance. As a result, retained earnings at the beginning of the first quarter of the
    current consolidated fiscal year increased by 24 million yen and valuation difference on available-for-
    sale securities decreased by the same amount.

    In response to the amendment related to the revised accounting treatment for consolidated financial
    statements when gains or losses on sale of shares in subsidiaries, etc. between consolidated companies
    were deferred for tax purposes, the Company has applied the Revised Implementation Guidance 2022
    from the beginning of the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year. This change in accounting
    policies was applied retrospectively. Hence, the quarterly consolidated financial statements for the first
    nine months of the previous consolidated fiscal year and the consolidated financial statements for the
    previous fiscal year have been modified retrospectively. As a result, retained earnings of the previous
