Energy Networks at a glance

   What we do
        Energy Networks provides the infrastructure for the new energy world. We manage our
         power and gas grids in a smart and a digitalized way.
        We enable economic growth by connecting new residential and industrial areas and we help
         societies in their sustainable transformation, by including a growing number of renewable
         generation and charging stations.
        Our grid share is sizeable in the countries of operation, and we operate predominantly in the
         regulated business.
        In Energy Networks, we count on 43,390¹ employees.
