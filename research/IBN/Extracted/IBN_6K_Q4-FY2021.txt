In terms of Regulation
33 of the SEBI (Listing Obligations and Disclosure Requirements) Regulations, 2015, we forward herewith in the prescribed format, a copy

attached.

above Meeting also recommended a dividend of
₹ 2/-
(Rupees Two Only) per
equity share of face value of
₹
2/- each, subject to requisite approvals.

shares, will be paid/despatched on or after the same is approved by the shareholders at the ensuing Annual General Meeting (AGM) of the
Bank.

to inform you that the Board of Directors of the Bank at its Meeting held today also approved:

                                            a period of one year, from the date of passing of resolution by the Board.

                                            Associates provides professional services in the field of corporate laws, SEBI Regulations,

                                            Audits.
