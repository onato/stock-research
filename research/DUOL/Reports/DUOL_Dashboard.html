Company Overview

Duolingo is the world's leading mobile learning platform, offering language learning in 40+ languages alongside Math, Music, and Chess through a gamified freemium app. As of Q1 2026 the company had 56.5M daily active users, 12.5M paid subscribers, and generated $1.037B in FY2025 revenue — its first billion-dollar year.

The company has declared 2026 an "investment year," deliberately compressing margins to ~26% Adjusted EBITDA while targeting 100 million DAUs over the medium term, accepting near-term financial deceleration to build a larger monetizable base.

Business Model

Freemium model with approximately 86% of Q1 2026 revenue from subscriptions, supplemented by advertising (7%), Duolingo English Test (4%), and in-app purchases (3%). FY2025 delivered $1.037B revenue (+39% YoY) and $1.158B bookings (+33% YoY) with Adjusted EBITDA margin of 29.5%.

For FY2026, management is deliberately moderating margins to ~26% to reinvest in user growth, AI content, and product quality. The three-tier subscription structure (Free, Super ~$7/mo, Max ~$14/mo) monetizes across the willingness-to-pay spectrum.

  • Subscriptions (Super + Max)86% growing
  • Advertising7% stable
  • Duolingo English Test4% growing
  • In-App Purchases3% stable

Competitive Position

Competitors: Babbel, Rosetta Stone, Busuu, Memrise, ChatGPT/OpenAI, Google Gemini, Pimsleur, AI-native language apps

Market: Dominant leader. FY2025 revenue $1.037B dwarfs Babbel's ~$270M. #1 grossing Education app on both Google Play and Apple App Store. 6,100+ institutions accept Duolingo English Test.

Moat factors:

  • Habit & streak mechanics: 10M+ users with 1+ year streaks. Streak mechanics and social features (leaderboards, friend streaks) drive engagement competitors cannot replicate.
  • Brand & viral marketing: AdAge Marketer of the Year. Organic word-of-mouth is the primary user acquisition channel — a significant cost advantage.
  • Data flywheel: AI now allows 20,500 course units published per quarter in Q1 2026 (vs. 1,800/quarter in 2024) — a 10x content velocity acceleration.
  • Free tier as acquisition funnel: 133M+ MAUs creating an enormous top-of-funnel that converts to paid (12.5M subscribers = ~9.4% DAU-to-paid penetration).

Growth Drivers

  • AI-accelerated content creation: 20,500 course units per quarter in Q1 2026 (vs. 1,800/quarter in 2024), enabling deeper B2-level courses across 9 core languages
  • Speaking & conversation features: Spoken Tokens, Speaking Adventures, Flashcards added to free and paid tiers; Video Call users speaking 2x more words YoY
  • Duolingo Max AI tier: ~$14/month with Video Call, Roleplay, Explain My Answer; Max penetration growing from ~5% to ~9% of paid base over past year
  • International expansion: China achieving profitable performance marketing unit economics; English learners in China, India, Japan, Korea are the largest global demand pool
  • Duolingo English Test (DET): accepted at 6,100+ institutions including 24 of top 25 US undergraduate programs by international enrollment; $11.3M in Q1 2026
  • Adjacent subjects: Chess fastest-growing subject ever launched; Math and Music broaden daily app utility and open new user acquisition cohorts
  • $400M share buyback program (no expiration) authorized alongside growth investments; $1.036B cash balance, zero debt

Key Risks

  • HIGH: DAU growth decelerating from 49% (Q1 2025) to 21% (Q1 2026); top-of-funnel described as "about flat" in Q1 2026 call
  • HIGH: Bookings growth collapsing: 33% in FY2025, guided 10.5% for FY2026, just ~6% guided for Q2 2026
  • HIGH: AI competition — ChatGPT, Gemini, and AI-native apps improving rapidly at conversational practice that Duolingo's Max tier charges premium for
  • HIGH: Strategic shift execution: if DAU growth does not re-accelerate despite reduced monetization friction, management loses both growth narrative and margins
  • HIGH: Gross margin structural compression: guided from 73.0% (Q1 2026) to ~69% by Q4 2026 as AI feature costs scale — a permanent reset, not temporary
  • MOD: CFO transition — Gillian Munson started February 23, 2026 and is navigating her first full year of guidance-setting during a deliberate strategy reset
  • MOD: Max tier ARPU cannibalization: Video Call reportedly migrating from Max ($14/mo) toward Super ($7/mo), compressing revenue per subscriber
  • MOD: Platform dependency on Apple and Google app stores (15-30% take rates on IAP)
  • MOD: Currency exposure: 50%+ bookings from outside US; Q2 2026 bookings guidance +4.1% constant currency vs. +5.8% reported
  • MOD: SBC dilution: ~15% of revenue in FY2026, net 3.5-4% dilution despite $400M buyback

Bull Case

  • AI content flywheel is a structural moat: 20,500 course units in Q1 2026 alone (10x the 2024 pace) means Duolingo's app depth is pulling away from every human-curated competitor
  • FY2026 investment cycle mirrors the successful 2022-2023 playbook that drove DAUs from 20M to 50M+ and built the durable monetization engine — management has done this before
  • 100M DAU target is compelling: at 56.5M DAUs and 12.5M paid subscribers (~22% penetration), if DAUs reach 100M at current paid penetration and ARPU, revenue would roughly double
  • Fortress balance sheet: $1.036B cash, zero debt, >$350M FCF guided for FY2026, $400M buyback — no existential risk and strong capital return optionality
  • Valuation reset may be excessive: stock at ~$121 trades at ~10x FY2026 revenue on a $5.6B market cap — 78% below May 2025 peak. Analyst consensus Buy with avg. PT ~$267 (120% upside)
  • DCF base case IV $226 (+86%) with bear case still implying $134 (+10.7%): downside appears bounded by the balance sheet floor ($23.24/share in net cash)

Bear Case

  • Top-of-funnel is "about flat" while bookings growth is collapsing to 6% in Q2 2026: the company currently has neither the growth nor the margins — the most dangerous near-term setup
  • Gross margin is structurally eroding: drift from 73% to ~69% by Q4 2026 driven by AI feature costs that scale with the product roadmap, not a one-time item
  • AI substitution risk: free AI tutors (ChatGPT, Gemini) improving rapidly at conversational practice. If Max tier's Video Call moves to Super, ARPU collapses
  • Bookings leading indicator signals sustained deceleration: Q2 2026 bookings guided just +6% (+4% constant currency); revenue pressure extends into early 2027
  • Analysts model 36% YoY EPS decline in Q2 2026 — a significant headline number that could re-pressure the stock
  • At $121 and ~$5.6B market cap, DUOL trades at ~10x FY2026 revenue and ~39x FY2026 Adj. EBITDA: premium multiple for decelerating growth and compressing margins that requires near-perfect long-term execution to justify

DCF Valuation

Model approach — component-built owner FCF (29 Jul 2026): FY2025 owner FCF was $166.6M ($360.4M reported − $148.6M SBC − $45.2M interest income). Crucially, that margin is not carried forward. Each projected year is built from components: adjusted EBITDA, less SBC (a declining share of revenue), less D&A, taxed at a ramping cash rate, plus D&A, less capex, plus working capital captured on the change in revenue. That exposes three things a flat margin hides. (1) Deferred revenue is a growth tailwind, not a margin — the FY2025 increase was $123.3M, 34% of all reported FCF. It scales with bookings growth, so it shrinks as growth falls from 33% toward 10%; strip it and the underlying FY2025 margin was 4.2%, not 16.1%. Year-1 owner-FCF margin is therefore 11.2%, not the 15.4% a flat model would assume. (2) The FY2025 tax rate is not repeatable — a $256.7M valuation-allowance release drove a guided −6% cash tax benefit for 2026, ramped here to 21% by 2030. (3) A 5-year horizon would undervalue the business — margin is still climbing at year 5 (13.2%) and only matures near 16.4% by year 10, so the model runs 10 years and capitalizes a matured margin (5 years would give $76). Terminal value is the lower of Gordon growth and 20× terminal FCF. Duolingo repurchases nothing, so SBC dilution is uncovered and shares grow ~2.15%/yr to 60.6M. Effect: base IV $87.97 (−37.5%), probability-weighted $91.84 (−34.7%) against $140.73. All 25 sensitivity cells sit below the current price. Multiples in the sanity check use the same owner-FCF basis and will not match Yahoo or screener figures.
