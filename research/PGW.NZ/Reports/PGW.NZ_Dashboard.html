                Revenue, Profitability & Segments

                Revenue

                FY2018 revenue ($1,193m) includes the PGG Wrightson Seeds business, sold to DLF in May 2019. FY2019 onward is continuing operations only — the FY2018→FY2019 drop is a divestment, not a business collapse.

                Operating EBITDA & Margin

                IFRS 16 (adopted FY2020) inflated reported EBITDA by capitalising lease payments — FY2019 EBITDA ($24.4m) is not comparable to FY2021 onward. PGW is a thin-margin distributor: margin sits near 5–7%, plotted here on its own right-hand axis.

                Segment Operating EBITDA (Annual)

                The single most important operational view for PGW: two very different economic engines (transactional Retail & Water vs. commission-based Agency) plus unallocated corporate ("Other") costs. Sourced from each annual report's segment note ($000s, converted to $m). FY2020 excluded (pre/post IFRS-16 transition boundary within segment reporting).

                Free Cash Flow vs Operating EBITDA

                FY2019 FCF (-$65.5m) reflects the Seeds divestment year, not underlying operations. Log scale is not used here because FCF is frequently negative.

                Net Debt

                Bank debt less cash (excludes IFRS-16 lease liabilities, consistent with PGW's own net-debt definition). H1 net debt is structurally elevated by seasonal working-capital build (GO-STOCK receivables, inventory).

                H1 Operating Cash Flow Seasonality

                H1 (Jul–Dec) operating cash flow is negative in nearly every year — farmer trade-credit terms and GO-STOCK/inventory build absorb cash in the first half; it reverses in H2 as receivables collect ahead of the June year-end.

                Net Income & EPS

                FY2019 NPAT ($131.8m) includes a ~$134.3m one-off after-tax gain on the Seeds sale — continuing-ops NPAT was only $4.0m. Excluded from the bar to avoid distorting the axis; see modal. EPS pre/post the Aug-2019 1:10 share consolidation is NOT continuous (754.8m shares pre-consolidation vs 75.5m after) — shown as two series, not one line.

                Gross Margin & Net Margin

                Percentage series — no log toggle. FY2019 net margin (16.3%) is inflated by the Seeds sale gain.

                Dividend Per Share

                Post-consolidation (FY2019 onward) cents-per-share basis. FY2026 total dividend 10.0cps (final 5.5cps + interim 4.5cps), fully imputed. Note: DCF cash-flow analysis finds recent dividends have been funded largely by rising net debt, not by owner free cash flow.
