7. Expansion of Advanced Materials Business (3)
Development of products contributing to lower carbon emissions
 [CWO® (near-infrared light absorbing material)]
    ◆ CWO® is an inorganic material that powerfully absorbs light with certain
      wavelength that makes up nearly 40% of the energy of solar rays.
      → It keeps pleasant temperature inside buildings, vehicles, etc., thereby
        reducing the use of air conditioning and helping create a low-carbon
        society.
    ◆ To improve the recognition of CWO® and increase competitiveness, we
      aggressively pursue our differentiation strategy through branding. We also
      launched the materials technology brand SOLAMENT TM.
    ◆ In October 2024, the prototype of DOWN-LESS DOWN JACKET, won the Red
      Dot Design Award 2024, a prestigious international competition, and the Good
      Design Award.
      The prototype of DOWN-LESS DOWN JACKET, among other products,
