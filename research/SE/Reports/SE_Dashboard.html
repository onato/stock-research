Group Operating Margin: The Operating-Leverage Story

Sea does not report a clean consolidated EBITDA line (D&A/lease add-backs vary by segment), so operating margin is used here. FY2025 segment adj EBITDA (a management non-GAAP measure, not this CSV): Shopee $880.6m (5.3% margin, up from just $155.8m FY2024), Garena $1,700m (56.1% margin on bookings), Monee $1,000m (26% margin).

Capital Expenditure

Shopee's logistics network (SPX Express) and Monee's technology/licensing footprint are the main capex drivers; capex intensity has stayed low relative to revenue growth, a key enabler of the FY2023+ FCF inflection.
