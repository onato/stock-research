Net Income vs Free Cash Flow

Reported FCF vs Owner FCF (TTM)

Owner FCF strips finance income earned on Adyen's own cash (EUR143.2m in H1 2026) so the cash pile is not counted twice: once as a flow, again when added back as a balance-sheet asset in the DCF bridge. This is a single TTM snapshot from the DCF JSON, not a CSV time series.

Headline Cash vs Merchant Payables

Only H1 2026 has both CashAndEquivalents and TotalAssets/TotalLiabilities populated; earlier CashAndEquivalents points do not have a matching PayablesToMerchants figure in every period. Gap between the two lines is float plus Adyen's own cash -- not a growing cash pile.
