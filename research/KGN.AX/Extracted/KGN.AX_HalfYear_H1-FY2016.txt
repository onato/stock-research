                        ASIC RELIEF UNDER SECTION            302   AND    SUBSECTION      323D   OF   THE
                        CORPORATIONS ACT 2001

                        Kogan.com Ltd has been granted relief by the Australian Securities and Investments
                        Commission (ASIC) from the requirements under section 302 and subsection 323D
                        of the Corporations Act 2001 to prepare statutory financial and directors reports
                        for the half year commencing on the date of incorporation of Kogan.com Ltd on 19
                        May 2016 to 18 November 2016. A copy of the Order is attached.

                        Kogan.com Ltd intends to rely on the order made by ASIC under subsection 340 of
                        the Corporations Act 2001 granting this relief.

                        Kogan.com Ltd will prepare its half year accounts as if the period from 19 May 2016
                        to 31 December 2016 was the first financial half year.
