1. Summary of Operating Results for First Quarter Fiscal Year 2025
(1)    Operating Results
Our group’s Revenue increased 1.3% year on year to ¥77,668 million due to robust demand for
semiconductors, despite sluggish overseas demand for automotive machinery components. Business profit
increased 13.9% year on year to ¥9,009 million as a result of improvement in the profit structure, such as
improvement in the cost structure due to impairment implemented in the previous fiscal year, a shift to
high-value-added products, and a sales price revision despite an increase in personnel expenses mainly in
overseas subsidiaries. Operating profit increased 5.2% year on year to ¥8,596 million. Profit attributable to
owners of parent increased 6.4% year on year to ¥7,448 million.

The financial results by business segment for Q1 FY2025 are as follows:
1.    Semiconductor materials
[Revenue ¥24,428 million (up 8.4% year on year), Business profit ¥4,818 million (up 5.6% year on year)]


In Epoxy resin molding compounds for encapsulation of semiconductor devices, Revenue increased because
demand for semiconductors in China continued to be robust and demand for AI-related applications
expanded.
In Photosensitive materials for semiconductor devices, Revenue decreased due to adjustments in the
memory market.
In Bonding pastes for semiconductor devices, Revenue increased due to strong demand and new sales
expansion in China while sales for information and communications equipment and automotive
semiconductors in Southeast Asia were sluggish.
In “LαZ®” series of Semiconductor substrate materials, Revenue increased due to growth in sales for mobile
devices as well as expanded use in power devices for AI servers.




                                                     2
2.   High-performance plastics
[Revenue ¥26,029 million (down 5.5% year on year), Business profit ¥1,738 million (up 1.5% year on year)]


In Resins for industrial use, Revenue decreased due to withdrawal from unprofitable products and
downsizing at North American bases, despite growth in sales for semiconductor applications in Japan.
In Molding compounds, Revenue decreased due to continued sluggish demand in the North American
automobile market due to the impact of tariff policies in the United States.
In Laminates, Revenue decreased due to continued sluggish demand for automotive and air-conditioning
applications.
In Aerospace components, Revenue decreased despite a recovery in volume following a decline in demand
caused by a strike at the United States customers in the middle of last year.


3.   Quality of life products
[Revenue ¥27,050 million (up 2.4% year on year), Business profit ¥3,689 million (up 39.3% year on year)]


In   Medical     devices   and   pharmaceuticals,    Revenue     increased      due   to   strong   sales   of
products related to enteral nutrition, stent grafts, micro catheter and blood container bags in Asia and the
United States.
In In vitro diagnostics and Biotechnology-related products, Revenue decreased because sales of In vitro
diagnostics in Japan decreased and in regenerative medicine in North America were delayed to the following
quarter.
In Films and sheets, in Electrical and Electronic Equipment packaging, Revenue remained firm due to
inventory adjustments for dicing films despite an expanding market share of cover tapes. However, in
Pharmaceutical packaging applications, Revenue increased due to the launch of new generic drugs and an
increase in inventory of existing products. In Food packaging applications, Revenue increased due to sales
of cut vegetables and portion remained firm.
In Industrial functional materials, Revenue decreased due to sluggish demand for some products for the
United States, mainly due to the impact of tariffs, while sales of Optical sheet and film products for
automotive applications grew significantly, and sales of Sheet material for architectural and interior
decoration for stores remained firm.
In Waterproof business, Revenue increased due to shifting a rise in material prices and construction costs to
sales prices, as well as sales expansion of low-energy house (ZEH) materials, although demand for mass-
produced housing declined due to a decrease in the number of housings start.

  Segment Information
   1. Reportable Segments
     The reportable segments of the Group refer to business units for which separate financial information is available and
     that are reviewed regularly at the Board of Directors meeting in order to determine the allocation of management
     resources and evaluate the business performance of each business unit.
     The Group divides its operations into business sectors identified by products and services, which manage manufacturing,
     sales, and research in an integrated manner. Each business sector is responsible for developing comprehensive domestic
     and overseas strategies with respect to its products and services, and operates its business activities.
     Accordingly, the Group has three reportable segments based on its products and services in accordance with its business
     sectors, including "Semiconductor materials", "High-performance plastics", and "Quality of life products".
     Major products and services categorized in each reportable segment are as follows:

       Reportable segments             Major products and services
    Semiconductor materials      Epoxy resin molding compounds for encapsulation of semiconductor devices,
                                 Photosensitive materials for semiconductor devices, Bonding pastes for semiconductor devices,
                                 Semiconductor substrate materials
    High-performance plastics    Resins for industrial use, Molding compounds, Molded parts and molding dies, Laminates,
                                 Aerospace components
    Quality of life products     Medical devices and pharmaceuticals, In vitro diagnostics and Biotechnology-related products,
                                 Films and sheets, Industrial functional materials, Waterproof business
