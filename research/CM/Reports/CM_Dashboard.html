Model approach: No driver changed. Root-caused the trip to a currency-direction defect in scripts/sanity_check.py historical_table() for this ticker: inputs.fx_rate (0.7019, CAD->USD) follows dcf_engine.py's documented convention (currency->quote_currency, used to produce the USD headline IV from the CAD-denominated model -- confirmed against tests/test_dcf_engine.py::TestCurrency and the WISE.L/APA fixtures). sanity_check.py::historical_table applies the SAME stored fx_rate in the OPPOSITE direction (quote_currency->currency, per its own docstring and tests/test_sanity_check.py::test_the_fx_rate_is_quote_to_reporting), to convert the fetched USD NYSE price series into CAD before dividing by CAD book value/EPS. Multiplying a USD price by the CAD->USD rate instead of dividing by it produced FY-end anchor prices roughly half the real NYSE close (e.g. FY2025 58.17 vs an actual ~82-86 close), understating the entire historical P/B and P/E band. This is a latent defect affecting any ticker with currency != quote_currency, not specific to CM -- left unfixed here because it is shared, test-pinned infrastructure (both conventions are asserted by passing tests) that needs a maintainer decision, not a one-ticker patch. Recomputed the comparison by hand on a consistent CAD basis (TSX close / CAD book&EPS) instead: see valuation_philosophy.sanity_check_response for the full walkthrough.
