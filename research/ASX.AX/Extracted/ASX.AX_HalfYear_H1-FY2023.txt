             NET INTEREST INCOME
             Net interest income consists of interest earned on ASX’s cash balances
             and interest earned from the investment of collateral balances
             lodged by participants, less interest paid on borrowings and paid to
             participants. Net interest income was $32.6 million, up 50.4% on pcp.

             Net interest on ASX’s cash balances and financing costs from
             borrowings and leases for the period was $12.2 million up
             $14.5 million compared to pcp, mainly driven by higher investment
             returns following the increase in RBA target cash rates during
             the period.

             Net interest earned from the investment of participant balances was
             $20.4 million, down 14.9% on pcp. Investment earnings remained
             in line with pcp at 10 bps. However average participant balances
             excluding excess contributions, decreased by 10% compared to pcp,
             which was further exacerbated by a decrease in the average interest
             charged to participants on initial margins.
