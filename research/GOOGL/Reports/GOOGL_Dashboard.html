Company Overview

Alphabet Inc. is the parent company of Google, operating across three segments: Google Services (Search, YouTube, Android, Maps, Chrome, Workspace, Pixel), Google Cloud (infrastructure, analytics, AI platform), and Other Bets (Waymo, Verily). Alphabet crossed $400B in annual revenue for the first time in FY2025 ($402.8B), driven by Search advertising (~90% global market share) and Google Cloud's acceleration to 36% YoY in FY2025 and 63% in Q1 2026.

GOOGL is the Class A voting share. GOOG is the economically identical Class C non-voting share representing the same underlying business. Both track the same financials within a small trading premium/discount.

Key Risks

  • Search antitrust remedies: ban on exclusive distribution deals (~$20B/yr TAC); mandatory data-sharing; 6-year oversight; DOJ cross-appeal seeks stronger structural remedies.HIGH
  • Ad tech antitrust: Judge Brinkema found illegal monopoly; DOJ seeks AdX divestiture — could strip $30B+ in network revenue; remedy ruling expected 2026.HIGH
  • AI-driven search displacement: ChatGPT Search and Perplexity cannibalizing informational queries; AI Overview CTR collapse for triggered queries; Gartner projects 25% decline in traditional search volume by end 2026.HIGH
  • CapEx spiral: $180-190B guided FY2026; 2027 to "significantly increase"; $84.75B equity raise (Jun 2026) dilutes shareholders; FCF near-zero FY2026.HIGH
  • Cloud distant third: 13% share vs AWS 30%; Azure OpenAI partnership creates enterprise AI credibility advantage.MODERATE
  • Advertising cyclicality: ~75% revenue from ads; TikTok $34.8B 2026 ad revenue competing for Gen Z budgets.MODERATE
  • EU Digital Markets Act: gatekeeper obligations across Search, Play, Chrome, Android; ongoing enforcement investigations.MODERATE

Bull Case

  • Cloud hypergrowth inflection: 63% YoY Q1 2026 with $462B backlog nearly doubling sequentially; enterprise AI Solutions became primary growth driver; Cloud alone could approach $100B annual revenue by 2027.
  • Search AI strengthening the moat: Search +19% YoY Q1 2026 with queries at all-time highs; AI Overviews appear in 48% of searches without cannibalizing monetization; Android/Chrome distribution is structurally impossible for OpenAI to replicate.
  • Waymo optionality: $126B standalone valuation (~$10/share) as an embedded free option not in the DCF; 14M+ paid trips in 2025; commercial expansion to 20+ cities in 2026.
  • Berkshire Hathaway invested $10B in June 2026 equity raise at ~$350/share — strong institutional validation of AI buildout thesis and Alphabet's durable competitive position.
  • Antitrust less severe than feared: Chrome/Android divestiture rejected; behavioral remedies only; DOJ leadership turnover slows enforcement momentum; appeals process extends to 2028+.
  • Attractive valuation relative to growth: mid-20s forward P/E with 20%+ revenue growth and 36%+ operating margins; one of the cheaper Magnificent Seven stocks on a PEG basis.

Bear Case

  • CapEx without visible ROI ceiling: $180-190B in 2026, 2027 even higher; $84.75B equity raise (largest tech equity dilution ever) signals organic FCF insufficient for the AI arms race; stranded assets risk if demand disappoints or cost curves improve faster than expected.
  • Antitrust remedies are structural and lasting: search data-sharing order erodes the data moat; exclusive deal ban impairs distribution; ad tech divestiture still possible (AdX); 6-year oversight with DOJ cross-appeal seeking more aggressive remedies.
  • Search query shift to AI is real and accelerating: despite Q1 2026 strength, AI Overview CTR collapse signals structural erosion of the web-traffic-to-advertiser pipeline; ChatGPT Search sessions average 2x Google engagement depth.
  • Cloud is capital-intensive to close the gap: 13% share vs AWS 30%; must spend enormously to maintain position; the supply-demand tightness driving today's numbers could ease if competitors add capacity faster.
  • Equity dilution is permanent: June 2026 raise impairs per-share value; if AI revenue doesn't materialize at sufficient scale and speed, the dilution compounds the long-term damage to per-share economics.
