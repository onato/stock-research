WSP has in place a US$1.5-billion revolving credit facility with a syndicate of financial institutions comprised of:
  - a senior unsecured revolving credit facility to a maximum amount of US$500.0 million maturing in June 2028; and
  - a senior unsecured revolving credit facility to a maximum amount of US$1.0 billion maturing in June 2030.
The amount available under the US$1.5-billion revolving credit facility was $1,674.6 million (US$1,222.9 million) as at
June 28, 2025.

WSP has in place fully-committed US$900.0-million term loans with various tenors of up to 2.3 years. As at June 28,
2025, these were fully drawn.

WSP has in place two term loans totalling US$350 million, with maturities in October 2026 and 2027, fully drawn under
an incremental facility agreement.

In June 2025, WSP purchased a 19.9% interest in Ricardo plc (“Ricardo”) and reached an agreement to acquire the entire
issued and to be issued share capital of Ricardo for 430 pence per share (the “Ricardo Acquisition”). Also in June 2025,
WSP obtained a fully committed “certain funds” £230-million term loan facility, concurrently with the announcement of
the planned acquisition of Ricardo. The "certain funds" facility was undrawn as at June 28, 2025 and may be drawn in the
form of term loans to finance the Ricardo Acquisition, which is expected to be completed in Q4 2025. If the facility is
drawn upon, the term loans will mature three years from the closing date of the Ricardo Acquisition.
