Earnings & Profitability (EBITDAF-anchored)

Genesis's headline earnings measure is EBITDAF — earnings before net finance costs, tax, depreciation, depletion, amortisation, impairment, unrealised fair-value changes and other gains/losses. Reported NPAT/EPS below are shown for completeness only: they are heavily distorted by unrealised derivative fair-value swings and generation-asset revaluations (FY2018-21 P/E of 59-123x are fair-value artefacts, not real valuation signals) and should not be read as a clean earnings trend.

Revenue vs EBITDAF (FY)

Revenue is dominated by wholesale fuel-cost pass-through and is a poor performance signal at GNE — e.g. H1 FY26 revenue FELL 13% YoY while normalised EBITDAF ROSE 38%. Watch EBITDAF, not revenue.

Reported vs Normalised EBITDAF, H1 (NZ$m)

H1-2026: normalised $306.5m vs reported $303.2m. H1-2025: normalised $222.0m vs reported $216.5m. The gap reflects one-off/weather-related adjustments management strips out to show underlying earnings power.

Balance Sheet & Leverage

Net Debt & Leverage (FY + latest H1)

Net debt = TotalDebt − Cash. Leverage ratio = Net Debt / FY (or annualised) EBITDAF. H1-2026 net debt of ~NZ$1,394m (1,490.5 debt − 96.8 cash) against a ~2.2x leverage ratio, down from 2.6x at FY2025 — helped by the Feb-2026 equity raise.

Shares Outstanding (m)

Cash Flow & Capital Programme

Operating Cash Flow, CapEx & Free Cash Flow (FY)

FCF = OperatingCashFlow − CapEx. Reported FCF has been structurally squeezed as capex ramps for the Gen35 growth pipeline — the historical FCF CAGR is actually NEGATIVE (-0.2% 3yr / -3.6% 5yr) despite growing EBITDAF, which is exactly why the Feb-2026 equity raise happened.

FY26-32 Capex Plan: SIB vs Gen35 Growth (NZ$m, base case)

Stay-in-business (SIB) capex runs ~$130-140m/yr and is non-discretionary. Growth capex funds the ~$2bn Gen35 pipeline (solar, wind, BESS, Huntly firming) and is heavily front/mid-loaded, peaking around FY28-29 before tapering as projects complete.

Retail & Guidance Trajectory

FY26 Normalised EBITDAF Guidance — Twice Upgraded

FY26 guidance moved from $455-485m (original) to $490-520m (Jan-2026) to $515-545m (23-Apr-2026), citing cost discipline, improved hydrology and favourable wholesale conditions. Longer-term outlook: mid-to-upper $500m by FY28, and $650-750m by FY32.

Retail Customer Numbers vs Netback ($/MWh)

Total customers fell 5.8% YoY to 490,227 (Q4 FY26) from 520,519, while retail electricity netback improved to $175.5/MWh (FY25: $155.3) — margin quality was deliberately prioritised over customer volume.

Sanity Check: DCF vs Trading Multiples

FY-end share price implied multiples, FY2018-FY2025, plus current (FY2026F) and DCF base-case implied:

At $2.63, current implied multiples (~8.9x EV/EBITDAF, ~1.02x P/B) sit squarely inside GNE's own historical trading bands — the market is pricing the stock consistently with its own history. The DCF base-case IV of $1.53 implies only ~6.2x EV/EBITDAF and ~0.59x P/B, BELOW the historical floor on both measures. The gap is genuine — the 10-year explicit-period free cash flow is heavily suppressed by the capex programme, which is the entire point of the Feb-2026 equity raise and the FY32 EBITDAF step-up thesis.
