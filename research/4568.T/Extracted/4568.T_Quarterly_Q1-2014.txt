             Depreciated Indian Rupee likely to cause derivatives related losses at Ranbaxy in 2Q.

             Consequently, net income forecast for the Q2’ 13 has been revised to 25.0 JPY Bn,
             decline of 5.0 JPY Bn. As DS group also expects gain on sales of unutilized assets,
             forecast for its full fiscal year has been revised to 65.0 JPY Bn, decline of 3.0 JPY Bn.
