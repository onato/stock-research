Statutory Operating Margin

This is the STATUTORY margin (operating income / revenue), not Capgemini's guided “operating margin” APM. The APM excludes restructuring (Fit-for-Growth, ~€700m cumulative FY2026-27), M&A amortization, SBC and pension items. H1 FY2026: statutory 7.3% vs APM 12.5% — a 5.2pp gap. FY2026 APM guidance is 13.6-13.8%.

Cash Generation & Balance Sheet

Three different free-cash-flow numbers get reported for Capgemini every year — they are not interchangeable, see the FCF chart help.

Free Cash Flow (Statutory OCF &minus; Capex)

H1 FY2018 FreeCashFlow is −€2m — note the series crosses zero, so no log toggle is offered. H1 rows run structurally near zero every year; Capgemini's cash generation is heavily H2-weighted (working-capital unwind + year-end collections).

Three FCF Definitions (FY2025)

Single FY2025 snapshot from the DCF JSON. Statutory OCF−capex: €2,195m. Capgemini's own ‘organic free cash flow’ APM: €1,949m — this additionally deducts lease principal and net interest and is heavily H2-weighted (H1 FY2026 was only €37m; never annualize the H1 figure). DCF owner FCF: €1,633m = OCF − capex − lease principal (€296m) − SBC (€266m), the fully-costed distributable-cash basis the valuation runs on.

Total Debt vs Cash

Net debt (company-disclosed, ex-lease-liabilities basis, not simply TotalDebt − Cash) tripled in 18 months: €2,107m (Dec-2024) → €5,306m (Dec-2025) → €6,454m (Jun-2026) funding the ~$3.3bn WNS Holdings acquisition that closed 2025-10-17. Goodwill (~€14.7bn) now exceeds shareholders' equity (~€11.6bn).

Stock-Based Compensation

Grew from €32m (FY2015) to €266m (FY2025), roughly 1.2% of revenue — small by US tech standards but fully expensed in the DCF's owner-FCF base rather than added back.
