                DCF Valuation

                ⚠️
                Bank valuation approach: Nu is a deposit-funded digital bank, so conventional free-cash-flow DCF is meaningless — operating cash flow is dominated by deposit inflows and loan-book growth, not owner earnings. This model instead discounts distributable earnings (net income less the capital that must be retained to fund balance-sheet growth at a stable equity/assets ratio) at the cost of equity (labeled "wacc" in the underlying data for dashboard compatibility, but it is NOT a blended debt/equity WACC). net_debt = 0 because deposits and borrowings are operating liabilities of a bank, not financing debt to net against enterprise value. The terminal value uses a separate, mature-bank retention ratio (terminal growth ÷ terminal ROE) rather than carrying forward the hypergrowth-era retention rate, avoiding double-counted reinvestment.

                Sanity check: PASSEDPASS — the base-case intrinsic value of $9.52 implies a 16.3x trailing P/E and 4.1x P/B, both well below Nu's own historical averages (~31x P/E, up to 7.3x P/B). All three scenarios assume material multiple contraction (implied exit P/E of 7-10x vs. ~21.7x today), making this a deliberately conservative model — and the market currently prices NU well above the model's probability-weighted intrinsic value.
