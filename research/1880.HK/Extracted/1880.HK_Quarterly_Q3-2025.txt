III. OTHER REMINDERS

(I)   Overview of Quarterly Business Performance

      During the Reporting Period, by adhering to strategic guidance, while further developing
      its duty-free business, the Company continued to deepen the innovative model of “product
      + scenario + service ” by closely following changes in consumer demand, persistently
      innovating consumption scenarios, and stimulating consumer vitality.

      During the Reporting Period, stores in Hainan region deepened the integration of the
      “duty-free + cultural tourism” scenarios, building a powerful matrix of “first stores”. The
      Company introduced the nationwide debut exhibition of POP MART’ s DIMOO Shapes
      in Nature and Disney Store’s first pop-up shop in Sanya. Through a composite model of
      “duty-free + experience + social interaction”, the duty-free shopping space was upgraded
      into a cultural tourism destination that is visitable, playful and shareable. According to data
      released by Haikou Customs, the monthly sales of Hainan offshore duty-free market increased
      by 3.4% year-on-year in September 2025, marking a return to positive growth after nearly 18
      months.

      During the Reporting Period, the Company newly opened downtown duty-free stores
      in Shenzhen, Guangzhou and Chengdu, all of which adopted a “ duty-free + duty-paid ”
      dual-track operational model. They deeply integrated local culture and characteristics
      to create new consumption scenarios that deeply blend “ international quality + local
      characteristics”, covering both “domestic + international” customer groups and effectively
      unlocking consumption potential. The Company meticulously created and launched a new
      concept store, cdf Macau Avenida de Almeida Ribeiro Store, and actively promoted the
      construction of the departure store at Macau International Airport, further improving the
      “resort-scenic spot-airport” full-chain strategic layout in the Macau market. At the same time,
      the high-quality operation of the duty-paid project at Lanzhou Zhongchuan International
      Airport provided an excellent example for subsequent promotion to more airports and
      commercial projects. In addition, projects of the Company such as Phase III of Sanya
      International Duty-Free Shopping Complex continued to progress steadily.

(II) Profit Distribution Plan for the First Three Quarters of 2025

     To enhance investor returns, taking into consideration factors such as the Company’s actual
     business development situation, financial position, cash flow position and sustainable
     development, the Company intends to implement its first interim dividend distribution. As
     considered and approved at the twenty-sixth meeting of the fifth session of the Board held
     on October 30, 2025, the Board proposes to distribute a cash dividend for the first three
     quarters of 2025. According to the profit distribution plan, based on the total share capital of
     ordinary shares registered on the record date for the implementation of distribution, a cash
     dividend of RMB0.25 per share (including tax) will be distributed to all shareholders. The
     total cash dividend amounts to RMB517.2148 million (including tax), accounting for 16.95%
     of the net profit attributable to shareholders of the Company for the first three quarters
     of 2025. The above proposed cash dividend distribution is subject to consideration and
     approval at the general meeting of the Company. If approved, the dividend for the first three
     quarters of 2025 is expected to be paid to shareholders within two months after the general
     meeting. A circular containing, among other things, further details of the Company’s profit
     distribution plan for the first three quarters of 2025, and a notice of the general meeting
     will be published on the websites of the Hong Kong Exchanges and Clearing Limited
     (www.hkexnews.hk) and the Company (www.ctgdutyfree.com.cn) in due course.
