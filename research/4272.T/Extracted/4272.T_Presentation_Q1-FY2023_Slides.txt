      FY 2023 1Q Business Environments and Issues
 Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
  •    YOY increase in net sales and income
  •    Sales for Functional Chemicals and Safety Systems grew
  •    Operating income, ordinary income, and net income exceeded the targets
       due to a reduction in the cost of sale ratio and the exchange rate impact             《Epoxy resin》

 Functional Chemicals Business
  •    Continued strong sales for semiconductor-related epoxy resins, etc., for circuit boards and encapsulations
  •    Strong demand for semiconductors in MEMS materials
  •    Consumer inkjet printer colorants held steady
  •    Sales of catalysts were pushed back due to lockdowns in various parts of China and logistical disruptions
  •    The optical film business struggled due to the Ukrainian issue, although the shortage of
       semiconductors is being resolved
  •    Projector materials showed a recovery

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  •    ALAGLIO®, a photodynamic diagnostics agent, achieved market penetration
  •    DASATINIB tablets, generic anti-cancer agent, launched in June
  •    Solasia Pharma K. K., the manufacturer and supplier, received approval to manufacture and sell
       DARVIAS® Injection 135mg (generic name: DARINAPARSIN)
  •    Expand use of PEMETREXED, generic anticancer agent (launched in July 2021)

 Safety Systems Business
  •    Supply chains became unstable due to new geopolitical risks in addition to the worldwide supply
       shortage of automotive components, and the pace of recovery in demand remains moderate
  •    Market penetration and usage in demonstration trials of PARASAFE®, safety devices for industrial drones

 Agrochemicals, Others
  •    In the Agrochemicals business,
       strong performance of flometoquin (insecticides) for overseas markets

                            ESG: FY 2023 1Q Issues
 Establishment of Basic Sustainable Management Policy
  •   In line with our KAYAKU spirit, ensure management transparency and fairness, and practice
      sustainable management that repays the confidence of all stakeholders by contributing to the
      realization of a sustainable environment and society through business activities
 Establishment of Nippon Kayaku Group Human Rights Policy
  •   Established the Nippon Kayaku Group Human Rights Policy based on the UN Guiding Principles on
      Business and Human Rights, with the aim of promoting initiatives to respect human rights across the
      entire Group
  •   Fostering a corporate culture that respects human rights, aiming to be a company that can command
      the trust of all stakeholders and provide happiness and joy
 Endorsement of the recommendations of the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial
 Disclosures “TCFD”
  •   Disclose information on risks and opportunities related to climate change, their financial impact, and
      initiatives to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and build a recycling-oriented society in accordance
      with TCFD recommendations
