FY 2021 1Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
 Functional Materials Business
  ▸ Increased demand for equipment for telecommuting contributed to firm
    sales of resins for circuit boards and semiconductor encapsulation.
  ▸ The LCD and semiconductor cleaner business was launched.

 Color Materials Business
  ▸ Demand was firm for colorants for inkjet printers for consumer use due to
    the increase in telecommuting.
  ▸ Demand declined for colorants for inkjet printers used in industrial applications,
    materials for thermal paper, and dyes for textiles.

 Catalysts Business
  ▸Some plans for the first quarter were postponed, but we expect to achieve
    the plan for the first half.

 Polatechno
  ▸Sales were sluggish in the optical film business and the precision components
     business.

FY 2021 1Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸ Biosimilars: Sales growth for TRASTUZUMAB BS and INFLIXIMAB BS
   was strong.
 ▸Generic anti-cancer drugs: Sales of core products such as
  OXALIPLATIN, TEMOZOLOMIDE grew.
 ▸ We began co-promotion of NUBEQA® Tab.


 Safety Systems Business
 ▸ Vehicle sales were sluggish.
 ▸ Some operations were suspended due to government directions and a
   decline in demand.

 Other: Agrochemicals Business
 ▸ Sales to the domestic market were firm. Difficulty in procuring
   raw materials led to slow sales overseas.

Initiatives Planned in 2Q and Beyond
 Functional Chemicals Business
 Functional Materials Business
  ▸ Sales of resins for 5G will increase.
  ▸ Integration of the LCD and semiconductor cleaner business will be completed.

 Color Materials Business
  ▸ Growth in demand for digital printing along with the trend to small lot, diversified
     commercial printing is anticipated, and we will focus on expanding sales of
     colorants for inkjet printers used in industrial applications.

 Catalysts Business
  ▸ The use of catalysts by customers will be promoted, by pursuing remote support
     for technical services overseas.

 Polatechno
  ▸ Integrated management will be advanced through integration of the Polatechno
     business.

Initiatives Planned in 2Q and Beyond
 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸ New products: Penetrate into the market with Portrazza® and expand
   market share for APREPITANT.
 ▸ Work to co-promote NUBEQA® Tab. and pursue alliance activities.


 Safety Systems Business
 ▸ Chinese market has almost recovered and we anticipate a gradual
   rebound in other regions as well from 2Q onward.
  However, there will be significant differences in the degree of rebound,
  depending on the country.



 Other: Agrochemicals Business
 ▸ We will consider expanding raw material sources.
