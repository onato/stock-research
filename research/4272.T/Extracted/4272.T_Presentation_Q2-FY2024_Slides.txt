      FY 2024 1H Business Environments and Issues
Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
  •   YOY decreased in net sales and income. Despite higher sales in Mobility & Imaging and Life Science,
      net sales decreased overall due to lower sales in Fine Chemicals
  •   Profits decreased due to the impact of soaring raw material prices and a decrease in sales of Fine Chemicals

Mobility & Imaging
 Safety Systems
  •   Domestic business recovered only moderately in line with the automobile production pick-up due to the
      impact of semiconductor shortages and other factors
  •   Overseas demand in Europe, North America, China, and ASEAN was generally solid

 Polatechno
  •   The optical film business struggled mainly with automotive applications due to sluggish sales and the
      discontinuation of existing models
  •   Solid sales of X-ray analyzer materials
                                                                                                                     4
      FY 2023 1H Business Environments and Issues

Fine Chemicals
 Functional Materials
  •    Epoxy resins remained sluggish due to inventory adjustments in the semiconductor market for both
       circuit boards and encapsulation materials, a decline in demand for consumer electronics, and
       restrained investment in infrastructure, including base stations and servers

 Color Materials
  •    Consumer inkjet printer colorants performed sluggishly partly due to market inventory adjustments
       and a decline in demand itself
  •    Impact of inventory adjustments in some industrial inkjet inks

 Catalysts
  •    Decreased from the previous year during replacement period

Life Science
 Pharmaceuticals
  •    Launched generic anti-cancer drug, ERLOTINIB Tablet 25mg「NK」, 100mg「NK」, 150mg「NK」

  •    Construction of Integrated Quality Assurance Building
  •    Obtained the manufacture and sales approval for
       ADALIMUMAB BS Subcutaneous Injection 20mg, 40mg, 80mg 「CTNK」in September

 Agrochemicals
  •    Lingering price increase impact on mainstay DIAZION® in domestic market
  •    Overseas sales firm centered on EVISECT and flomethoquin products

             Initiatives Planned in 3Q and Beyond

Mobility & Imaging
 Safety Systems
  •   Further expansion of inflator business:
        Expand sales by strengthening ties with local Chinese customers, including EV
        Expansion to ASEAN region and India, with Malaysia as the starting point
        Expansion of lightweight disk-type inflators, and lightweight and small-diameter cylinder-type inflators
  •   Expand sales and lineup of safety devices for industrial drones, PARASAFE®
        Release of new compact, lightweight products and development of parachutes for large drones

 Polatechno
  •   Promotion for the new automotive applications such as head-up displays
  •   Expansion of X-ray analyzer components and promotion of cost reduction actions

Fine Chemicals
 Functional Materials
  •   Full-fledged recovery in the semiconductor market is expected from 4Q onward
  •   Expansion of epoxy resins and maleimide resins for encapsulations and circuit boards
  •   Expansion of dry film resist for RF filters
 Color Materials
  •   Launch of new products and horizontal development of technologies and applications to expand
      industrial inkjet inks
  •   Expansion of developers, mainly non-phenol product for thermal paper

  •   Expansion of pilot evaluation facilities and experimental facilities

      Challenges to Achieve KV25 and Future Priority Initiatives
 The challenge is to improve profitability (earning power) in order to achieve
 the Mid-term Business Plan (KV25 ) targets and grow thereafter
 Aggressively invest in growing markets and focus on them
  •   Mobility & Imaging
       Expand business mainly in inflators by increasing production capacity in China and Malaysia
       Expand the X-ray business and polarized film components for automotive applications,
       which are our focus areas
  •   Fine Chemicals
        Expand business centered on epoxy resins and other semiconductor-related materials and
        industrial ink jet inks
  •   Life Science
        Development of production facilities to ensure a stable supply of pharmaceuticals
        Introduction of new pharmaceutical products: Aim to achieve 2030 Vision through
        aggressive investment
