FY 2017 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

 Functional Chemicals Business
  ＜Functional Materials＞
  ▸Sales were firm for epoxy resins for both semiconductor encapsulation and printed
   boards, achieving the plan for the first quarter.
  ▸MicroChem Corporation of the U.S. acquired Paratronix, Inc. (targeting expansion of
   the MEMS manufacturing materials segment).

  ＜Color Materials＞
  ▸Unprofitable items in textile dyes were removed and costs were reduced to improve
   profitability.
  ▸Sales of textile dyes to the Chinese market were firm.

  ＜Catalysts＞
  ▸There was no change in the operating rate for plants of Chinese customers.

  ＜Polatechno Group＞
  ▸First quarter results were sluggish, impacted by deceleration of the Chinese
   economy and appreciation of the yen.
  ▸The business results forecast for the full year has been revised downward because the
   impact of these factors will also remain a concern for the second quarter onward.

FY 2017 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸NK012, a new polymeric micelle anti-cancer drug under development, has been
   designated as an orphan drug by the FDA.
  ▸Cultivecs Inc. was established as a joint venture with Mitsubishi Gas Chemical Co., Inc.
   to manufacture monoclonal antibody pharmaceuticals (MABs) in Japan.
  ▸BICALUTAMIDE OD Tab. was launched as a generic anti-cancer drug.
  ▸Results from a Phase III Study of NK105, a drug for metastatic or recurrent breast
   cancer, did not meet the prespecified statistical criteria of the primary endpoint.
    (The policy on development is being reviewed and it will be difficult to meet the
   intended deadline for approval application during the first half of the fiscal year.)



 Safety Systems Business
  ▸Work on putting the production structure in place to meet the increased demand for
   automotive safety systems over the medium-term will proceed.
  ▸Work on strengthening the global quality assurance system will proceed.
