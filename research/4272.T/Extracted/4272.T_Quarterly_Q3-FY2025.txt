1. Qualitative Information Concerning Results for the First Three Quarters
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
During the first three quarters of this consolidated fiscal year (April 1 to December 31, 2024), the global economy
saw continuing uncertainty as the price inflation eased but remained at a high level and the high level of geopolitical
risk persisted.
      The Nippon Kayaku Group entered the third year of KAYAKU Vision 2025, the mid-term business plan which
began in fiscal year ended March 31, 2023, amid such conditions. We continue to implement the roadmap to the vision
specified for each business while advancing initiatives to address key company-wide issues aimed at achieving the
vision.
      As a result, net sales for the first three quarters of this consolidated fiscal year totaled 167,955 million yen, an
increase of 18,201 million yen (12.2%) year-on-year. Sales in the Life Science Business Unit underperformed slightly
while sales in the Mobility & Imaging Business Unit and Fine Chemicals Business Unit outperformed the first three
quarters of the previous fiscal year.
      Operating income outperformed the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year in all segments, reaching
18,114 million yen, an increase of 14,003 million yen (340.6%) year-on-year.
      Ordinary income totaled 21,003 million yen, an increase of 13,530 million yen (181.1%) year-on-year.
      Profit attributable to owners of parent was 13,312 million yen, an increase of 10,482 million yen (370.5%) year-
on-year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.
[Mobility & Imaging Business Unit]
Sales rose to 69,292 million yen, an increase of 8,583 million yen (14.1%) year-on-year.
      In the safety systems business, problems with irregularities in certificates of conformance at some major auto
manufacturers were resolved, but this did not result in a full rebound in demand and sales of airbag inflators and micro
gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners to underperformed year-on-year. Overseas, automobile sales slumped due to
an stricter screening of auto loans in Thailand and Indonesia, the main markets in the ASEAN region. In contrast,
demand in the Chinese market was supported by subsidies and incentives and sales to local manufacturers in China
were strong. Overseas sales of airbag inflators, micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners, and squibs all
outperformed year-on-year as sales were boosted by increasing yen weakness. The safety systems business overall
outperformed year-on-year as a result.
     The Polatechno business outperformed year-on-year as demand for components for X-ray analysis systems
remained firm and was also boosted by gains from yen weakness. Polarizing films outperformed year-on-year, owing
to the price changes on some products implemented in the previous period. The Polatechno business overall
outperformed year-on-year as a result.
     Segment profit totaled 10,814 million yen, an increase of 4,953 million yen (84.5%) year-on-year. This increase
was due to growth in sales in both businesses.

[Fine Chemicals Business Unit]
Sales rose to 50,527 million yen, an increase of 9,785 million yen (24.0%) year-on-year.
      The functional materials business as a whole outperformed the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
This outperformance resulted from firm demand for epoxy resins and other products groups as the semiconductor
market rebounded.
      The color materials business as a whole outperformed the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year. This
outperformance resulted from firm sales of industrial inkjet ink, home inkjet printer colorants, and developer for
thermal paper.
      The catalyst business outperformed year-on-year owing to strong sales.
      Segment profit totaled 7,983 million yen, an increase of 4,708 million yen (143.8%) year-on-year. Sales growth
in all businesses contributed to the growth in segment profit.

[Life Science Business Unit]
Sales were 48,134 million yen, a decrease of 166 million yen (0.3%) year-on-year.
     In the pharmaceuticals business, pharmaceuticals for the Japanese domestic market outperformed the first three
quarters of the previous fiscal year due to increased penetration of ALAGLIO® divided granules, a photodynamic
diagnostic agent, in the market and the contribution from growth of the antibody biosimilar BEVACIZUMAB BS,
mainly resulting from requests from other companies to supply it as a substitution.
     Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients for the Japanese domestic market and exports, sales from contract
production, and diagnostic drugs underperformed the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year, resulting in year-
on-year underperformance for the pharmaceuticals business as a whole.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
Total assets were 378,006 million yen, an increase of 14,832 million yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main increases were in notes and accounts receivable-trade, an increase of 8,359 million yen; raw
materials and stores, an increase of 6,972 million yen; and buildings and structures, net, an increase of 3,711 million
yen. The main decreases were in securities, a decrease of 5,877 million yen; investment securities, a decrease of 4,686
million yen; and cash and deposits, a decrease of 3,032 million yen.
      Liabilities were 106,122 million yen, an increase of 13,498 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in short-term loans payable, an increase of 9,862 million yen; notes
and accounts payable-trade, an increase of 5,924 million yen; and long-term loans payable, an increase of 5,487
million yen. The main decreases were in current portion of bonds payable, a decrease of 8,000 million yen; and
accounts payable-other, a decrease of 2,455 million yen.
      Net assets were 271,883 million yen, an increase of 1,334 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increase was in treasury stock, an increase of 1,869 million yen.

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, including Consolidated Business Results Forecasts
We expect the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku Group to pose the risk of an economic
downswing due to the Russian invasion of Ukraine and other geopolitical risks, increasing global inflation from high
fuel and raw material prices, prolonged adjustment of real estate prices in China, and monetary tightening in various
countries, despite expectations of a firm U.S. economy.
     Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to respond flexibly to changes in the business
environment and pursue optimal use of operating capital to increase shareholder value, as well as expand existing
businesses in global growth markets, accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance
profits.
     The business results forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2025 announced on November 11, 2024 have
been revised. See “Notice of Revisions to the Business Results Forecasts and Dividend Forecast (Dividend Increase)”
disclosed on February 3, 2025 for more information.

(Notes in Case of Significant Change in Shareholders’ Equity)
[1] Acquisition of Treasury Stock
Based on resolutions of the Board of Directors on May 13, 2024 and November 11, 2024, the Company repurchased
3,859,600 shares of treasury stock for 4,890 million yen during the first three quarters of the fiscal year ending March
31, 2025.

[2] Distribution of Treasury Stock
The Company distributed 89,543 shares of Treasury stock as restricted share compensation on July 19, 2024, based
on a resolution of the Board of Directors on June 26, 2024.
      The Company also distributed 37,400 shares of Treasury stock as restricted shares for the Company’s employee
stock ownership plan on October 18, based on a resolution of the Board of Directors on August 27, 2024. This resulted
in an increase of 3 million yen in other additional paid-in capital and a decrease of 44 million yen in Treasury stock.

[3] Cancellation of Treasury Stock
The Company cancelled 5,500,000 shares of Treasury stock on November 25, 2024, based on a resolution of the Board
of Directors on November 11, 2024. This resulted in decreases of 15 million yen in other additional paid-in capital,
6,595 million yen in other retained earnings, and 6,611 million yen in Treasury stock.

As a result, additional paid-in capital stood at 15,857 million yen, retained earnings at 198,518 million yen, and
Treasury stock at 3,543 million yen as of December 31, 2024.
