FY 2018 Business Environments and Topics (1)
Functional Chemicals Business:
                           Increase in revenues and increase in profit
 • Functional Materials：Semiconductor market conditions remained favorable and sales of
 epoxy resins grew

 • Color Materials：Colorants for inkjet printers saw growth in demand for industrial
 applications, and in consumer demand in emerging countries

 • Catalysts：Acrylic acid manufacturing facilities in China showed signs of a rebound in
 operations

 • Polatechno Group ： The group saw strong growth for components for X-ray analysis
 systems produced by Moxtek (U.S.), and established a business base in components for X-
 ray analysis systems with the acquisition of RaySpec (UK).
 The group also began mass production of new products (High Contrast High Durability Dye-
 type Polarizers, Iodine-type Polarizers, Achromatic Polarizers).




                                                                                            4
FY 2018 Business Environments and Topics (2)
Pharmaceuticals Business:
                    Slight decrease in revenues and increase in profit
 • In biosimilars, INFLIXIMAB BS recorded 150% year-on-year growth in sales, and we obtained approval
  for the sale and manufacture of Trastuzumab BS (CT-P6)
 • TEMOZOLOMIDE Tab., a generic anti-cancer drug indicated for brain tumors, was launched on the
  market
 • A Phase II clinical trial of the polymeric micelle anti-cancer drug NK105 began for breast cancer
 • The Niigata plant of Cultivecs Inc., a manufacturing subcontractor for biomedicines, was completed

Safety Systems Business: Increase in revenues and decrease in profit
 • The safety systems business saw growth on the back of strong demand in automotive safety
  components
 • We continued to strengthen quality assurance systems and build a safe production structure for
  increasing capacity on a global basis
 • Profits were impacted by exchange rates and increased costs results from expansion of production
  capacity to meet increased demand

Other: Slight increase in revenues and decrease in profit
 •   We acquired registration of the new insecticide FINESAVE® as an agrochemical

Functional Chemicals Business

 • Functional Materials:
  Accelerate the pace of expansion in new uses of epoxy resins

  Achieve results for new products (High Contrast High Durability Dye-type Polarizers,
