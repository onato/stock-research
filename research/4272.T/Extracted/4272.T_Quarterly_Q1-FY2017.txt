1. Qualitative Information Concerning Results for the First Quarter
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
The global economy staged a continued gradual rebound in the U.S. and Europe in the first quarter of the current
consolidated fiscal year (April 1 through June 30, 2016), while a gradual decline in the pace of growth was seen in
China. Japan’s economy showed signs of a modest rebound, but concerns over deceleration of the global economy
and appreciation of the yen continue to cast doubt over the future.
     The Nippon Kayaku Group worked to reduce costs even further amid these conditions, addressing key themes
and grappling with mid- and long-term key issues in the new mid-term business plan, “Take a New Step 2016,”
which began this fiscal year, and working to improve profitability in addition to strengthening R&D, allocating
management resources to key businesses in an optimal manner, and expanding overseas business.

Net sales for the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year consequently declined by 859 million yen
(2.2%) year-on-year to 38.986 billion yen despite growth in the safety systems business as net sales for the
functional chemicals and pharmaceuticals businesses declined over the first quarter of the previous fiscal year.
      The decline in net sales led to a decline in operating income of 748 million yen (13.2%) year-on-year to 4.938
billion yen.
      Ordinary income declined by 2.588 billion yen (42.6%) year-on-year to 3.494 billion yen as a result of an
increase in foreign exchange losses.
      Net income attributable to owners of parent declined by 2.309 billion yen (49.0%) year-on-year to 2.4 billion
yen.

Performance by business segment is as described below.
[Functional Chemicals Business]
Sales declined by 1.749 billion yen (9.8%) year-on-year to 16.176 billion yen.
      In the functional materials business, epoxy resins for semiconductor encapsulation saw strong sales and the
functional materials business overall recorded growth over the first quarter of the previous fiscal year.
      In the color materials business, developer for thermal paper recorded year-on-year growth while year-on-year
declines were seen in sales of colorants for inkjet printers, and textile and paper dyes. This resulted in an overall
decline in sales for the color materials business on a year-on-year basis.
      The catalyst business saw year-on-year growth in sales due to strong shipments of catalysts used in the
production of Acrylic acid and catalysts used in the manufacture of Methacrylic acid.
      The Polatechno Group experienced a decline in sales from the first quarter of the previous fiscal year due to
stagnant sales of dye-type polarizing films and ProFluxTM, an inorganic polarizer.
      Segment profit declined by 629 million yen (30.1%) year-on-year to 1.459 billion yen.
[Pharmaceuticals Business]
Sales declined by 255 million yen (2.1%) year-on-year to 11.917 billion yen.
      Pharmaceuticals in Japan recorded growth in sales for the biosimilar, INFLIXIMAB BS for I.V. Infusion (an
antibody drug), and FILGRASTIM BS Inj. (which reduces the chance of infection in chemotherapy patients).
Meanwhile, drug prices revisions caused sales of PACLITAXEL Inj. NK, CARBOPLATIN Intravenous Inj. NK, and
other injectable anti-cancer drugs, and BICALUTAMIDE Tab. NK, ANASTROZOLE Tab. NK, and other oral
anti-cancer drugs to decline from the first quarter of the previous fiscal year.
      Sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan declined overall, despite the contribution made by new products such as
OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an anti-cancer drug) and DOCETAXEL for I.V. Infusion (an anti-cancer
drug).
      Exports recorded year-on-year growth overall, despite a year-on-year decline in sales of ETOPOSIDEs and
BLEOs (both anti-cancer drugs).
      Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients for the Japanese domestic market saw year-on-year growth overall
on the back of growth in raw materials for pharmaceuticals and fermented products. Diagnostic agents as a whole
declined as diabetes diagnostic agents underperformed compared to the first quarter of the previous fiscal year.
      Segment profit declined by 652 million yen (32.7%) year-on-year to 1.342 billion yen.
[Safety Systems Business]
Sales rose by 535 million yen (6.3%) year-on-year to 9.037 billion yen.
      Domestic sales declined from the first quarter of the previous fiscal year despite strong domestic business in
Japan and growth in airbag inflators. This was caused by the transfer of the production of micro gas generators for
seatbelt pretensioners for the ASEAN market to an overseas group company that began production from fiscal year
2015.
      In the overseas business, sales were strong in the North American, European, and Chinese markets and
expansion in demand for automotive safety products in emerging countries and other factors led to year-on-year
growth in airbag inflators, micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners, and squibs.
      Segment profit increased by 127 million yen (7.9%) year-on-year to 1.732 billion yen.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 265.402 billion yen, a decrease of 7.276 billion yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main sources of decrease were decreases of 3.944 billion yen in notes and accounts receivable-trade,
2.816 billion yen in property, plant and equipment, and 2.140 billion yen in securities. The main sources of increase
were increases of 829 million yen in Other (under “Investments and other assets”) and 779 million yen in investment
securities.
      Liabilities were 72.087 billion yen, a decrease of 98 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main sources of decrease were declines of 2.320 billion yen in income taxes payable,
1.220 billion yen in accounts payable-other, and 1.119 billion yen in long-term loans payable. The main sources of
increase were increases of 3.549 billion yen in short-term loans payable and 1.434 billion yen in notes and accounts
payable-trade.
      Net assets were 193.314 billion yen, a decrease of 7.178 billion yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main sources of decrease were decreases of 4.116 billion yen in foreign currency
translation adjustments, 3.073 billion yen in dividend payments, and 2.679 billion yen in purchase of treasury shares.
The main sources of increase were increases of 2.4 billion yen in net income attributable to owners of parent and
530 million yen in unrealized holding gains on other securities.

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, Including Consolidated Business Forecasts
We see continued strong growth in the U.S. and are concerned about an economic downswing in Europe due to
increased geopolitical risk as factors affecting the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku
Group. While a gradual rebound in Japan’s economy is anticipated as employment and income continue to improve,
we need to pay attention to the impact that a downswing in the global economy would have.
      Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
      There has been no change in the forecasts announced on May 11, 2016 for the first half and full-year business
results for fiscal year ending March 2017.

2. Items Related to Summary Information (Notes)
(Changes to accounting policies, changes to accounting estimates, and restatements)
(Changes to accounting policies)
The Practical Solution on a Change in Depreciation Method due to Tax Reform 2016 (Practical Solution Report No.
32, June 17, 2016) was adopted for the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year in conjunction with the
Corporation Tax Act reform, and the method of depreciation for buildings and accompanying facilities and
structures acquired from April 1, 2016 onward was changed from the declining balance method to the straight-line
method.
      This change had no material effect on operating income, ordinary income, and income before income taxes and
non-controlling interests in the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year.
