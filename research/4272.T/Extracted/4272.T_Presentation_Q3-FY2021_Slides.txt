FY 2021 3Q: Business Environments and Topics

 Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
 ・ Net sales and income for 3 months progressed over to the plan
 ・ Gross operating income was increased 0.7 billion yen due to net sales growth,
   and General and administrative expenses, etc. could save 0.9 billion yen

 Functional Chemicals Business
 ・ Sales of epoxy resins for semiconductors and circuit boards grew, primarily for
   use in 5G-capable device and IT tools. In addition, its sales for automobile
   application was recovering
 ・ The Cleaner Business fairly launched
 ・ Sales of colorants for consumer inkjet printers expanded due to tele-working
 ・ Sales of colorants for industrial inkjet printers, textile dyes, developer of thermal
   paper was affected by COVID-19, but it showed a trend of recovery
 ・ The Catalyst Business was healthy sales over the sales of last year
 ・ The Polatechno Business was affected by COVID-19, but it showed a trend of
   recovery, especially for automobile application
