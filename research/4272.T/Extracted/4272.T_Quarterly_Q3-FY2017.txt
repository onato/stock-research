1. Qualitative Information Concerning Cumulative Results for the First Three Quarters
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
In the first three quarters of the current consolidated fiscal year (April 1, 2016 to December 31, 2016), the global
economy staged a continued gradual recovery underpinned by strong consumer spending in the U.S. Europe also
saw a continued gradual rebound despite heightened geopolitical risk. There was a modest deceleration in the pace
of growth in China. Japan’s economy saw signs of a mild rebound in firm consumer spending that accompanied
improvement in the employment and income environment, and other indicators. However, the uncertainty of the
future has persisted amid concerns over deceleration of the global economy and effect of massive fluctuations in
exchange rates, among other factors.
     Amid these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group worked to implement the key themes and resolve the mid-
and long-term key issues outlined in “Take a New Step 2016,” the mid-term business plan launched from the
current fiscal year. We carried out additional cost cuts aimed at reinforcing our profit structure, in addition to
focusing on strengthening R&D, optimal allocation of business resources into core businesses, and expanding our
global operations.

Reflecting these undertakings, consolidated net sales in the first three quarters of the fiscal year ending March 31,
2017 totaled 117.885 billion yen, a decrease of 3.329 billion yen (2.7%) year-on-year. Although sales for the safety
systems business exceeded the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year, the functional chemicals and
pharmaceuticals businesses saw a year-on-year decline in sales.
     Operating income declined by 1.172 billion yen (7.1%) year-on-year to 15.371 billion yen, impacted by the
decline in net sales.
     Ordinary income was 15.707 billion yen, a decline of 589 million yen (3.6%) year-on-year.
     Profit attributable to owners of parent declined by 5.909 billion yen (35.3%) year-on-year to 10.852 billion
yen.

Performance by business segment is as described below.

[Functional Chemicals Business]
Sales were 47.510 billion yen, a decline of 3.616 billion yen (7.1%) year-on-year.
      In the functional materials business, epoxy resins for semiconductor encapsulation and UV-curable resins saw
steady sales, achieving growth over the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
      The color materials business as a whole underperformed the first three quarters of last fiscal year. This resulted
from low demand for colorants for inkjet printers and dyes for textiles and paper, despite the firm demand for
developer for thermal paper.
      The catalysts business as a whole saw growth over the first three quarters of last fiscal year, despite low
demand for catalysts used in the production of acrylic acid. This growth was due to strong demand for catalysts used
in the production of methacrylic acid.
      In the Polatechno Group, dye-type polarizing films and ProFluxTM, an inorganic polarizer were sluggish,
recording a year-on-year decline.
      Segment profit was 4.619 billion yen, a decline of 30 million yen (0.7%) year-on-year.

[Pharmaceuticals Business]
Sales were 36.296 billion yen, a decline of 1.900 billion yen (5.0%) year-on-year.
      Pharmaceuticals in Japan recorded growth in sales for the biosimilar, INFLIXIMAB BS for I.V. Infusion (an
antibody drug), and the specially controlled medical device, EMBOSPHERE (embolization material). In contrast,
drug prices revisions caused sales of PACLITAXEL Inj. NK, CARBOPLATIN Intravenous Inj. NK, and other
injectable anti-cancer drugs, and BICALUTAMIDE Tab. NK, ANASTROZOLE Tab. NK, and other oral anti-cancer
drugs to decline from the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year. Sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan declined
overall, despite the contribution made by new products such as OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an anti-cancer
drug) and DOCETAXEL for I.V. Infusion (an anti-cancer drug).
      Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients for the Japanese domestic market saw year-on-year growth overall
on the back of strong growth in raw materials for pharmaceuticals and fermented products.
      Exports recorded year-on-year growth overall.
      Diagnostic agents as a whole declined as diabetes diagnostic agents underperformed compared to the first three
quarters of the previous fiscal year.
      Segment profit was 4.590 billion yen, a decline of 1.958 billion yen (29.9%) year-on-year.

[Safety Systems Business]
Sales were 28.265 billion yen, an increase of 1.987 billion yen (7.6%) year-on-year.

     Domestic business remained firm and sales of airbag inflators recorded year-on-year growth; however,
domestic sales of micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners declined from the first three quarters of the

outperformed compared to the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
     In the overseas business, sales were strong in the North American, European, and Chinese markets and
expansion in demand for automotive safety components in emerging countries and other factors led to year-on-year
growth in airbag inflators, micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners, and squibs.
     Segment profit was 5.263 billion yen, an increase of 244 million yen (4.9%) year-on-year.

[Other]
Sales increased by 200 million yen (3.6%) year-on-year to 5.813 billion yen.
     The agrochemicals business recorded year-on-year growth in domestic sales, while exports declined
year-on-year, resulting in overall growth in sales over the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
     Sales for real estate and other businesses exceeded the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
     Segment profit was 1.005 billion yen, an increase of 408 million yen (68.4%) year-on-year.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 271.384 billion yen, a decline of 1.294 billion yen year-on-year. The main decrease was in
securities, a decline of 6.966 billion yen; and the main increases were in cash and deposits, an increase of 2.834
billion yen; investment securities, an increase of 1.229 billion yen; inventories, an increase of 917 million yen; and
other investments (included in investments and other assets), an increase of 853 million yen.
      Total liabilities were 67.552 billion yen, a decline of 4.633 billion yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main decreases were in long-term loans payable, a decrease of 3.850 billion yen;
income taxes payable, a decrease of 2.040 billion yen; and short-term loans payable, a decrease of 639 million yen.
The main increase was in notes and accounts payable-trade, an increase of 3.028 billion yen.
      Net assets were 203.831 billion yen, an increase of 3.338 billion yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main increases were in profit attributable to owners of parent, an increase of 10.852 billion yen;
unrealized holding gains on other securities, an increase of 703 million yen; non-controlling interests, an increase of
435 million yen; and remeasurements of defined benefit plans, an increase of 315 million yen. The main decreases
were in translation adjustments, a decrease of 1.061 billion yen; dividends paid, a decrease of 5.238 billion yen; and
purchase of treasury stock, a decrease of 2.689 billion yen.

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, including Consolidated Business Results Forecasts
We see continued firm growth in the U.S. and are concerned about an economic downswing in Europe due to
increased geopolitical risk as factors affecting the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku
Group. A gradual rebound in Japan’s economy is anticipated as the employment and income environment improves
and due in part to the beneficial effects of various government policies. However, the impact of a downswing in the
global economy bears watching.
     Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
     Nippon Kayaku Group did not revise its full-year forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2017, which
were announced on November 7, 2016.

2. Items Related to Summary Information (Notes)
(Changes to accounting policies, changes to accounting estimates, and restatements)
(Changes to accounting policies)
       The Practical Solution on a Change in Depreciation Method due to Tax Reform 2016 (Practical Solution
Report No. 32, June 17, 2016) was adopted for the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year in conjunction
with the Corporation Tax Act reform, and the method of depreciation for buildings and accompanying facilities and
structures acquired from April 1, 2016 onward was changed from the declining balance method to the straight-line
method.
       This change had no material effect on operating income, ordinary income, and profit before income taxes in the
first three quarters of the current consolidated fiscal year.
