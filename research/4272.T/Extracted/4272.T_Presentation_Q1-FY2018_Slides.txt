FY2018 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

 Functional Chemicals Business
  ＜Functional Materials＞
  ▸Sales of epoxy resins were firm on the back of a strong semiconductor market.
  ▸Materials for MEMS also recorded strong growth in sales volume.

  ＜Color Materials＞
  ▸Sales of colorants for inkjet printers for consumer use were firm due to growth
   in emerging markets.
  ▸Growth in sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications and
   materials for thermal paper were in line with the plan.

  ＜Catalysts＞
  ▸Sales of catalysts were in line with the plan.

  ＜Polatechno Group＞
  ▸Sales for the Polatechno Group remained strong as sales of existing dye-type
   polarizing films rebounded, despite the uphill battle in TEP* sales.
   (* TEP: Temperature-compensated elliptical polarizing film)

FY2018 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸In biosimilars, growth of INFLIXIMAB BS expanded market share to 5%
   by volume.
  ▸In generic anti-cancer drugs, there was growth in OXALIPLATIN sales.
   All indications were approved for NKS-1 tablets.
   Folinate tablets, a new generic drug in the cancer field, was launched.
  ▸Planning is underway on an additional clinical trial for NK105 in the breast
   cancer field.
  ▸A Phase III clinical trial on NK012 is being considered with licensing use in
   overseas markets also in mind.

 Safety Systems Business
  ▸Demand for automotive safety components remained firm overall.
  ▸The trend of depreciation in the yen was a positive factor.
  ▸Capital investments proceeded according to plan.
