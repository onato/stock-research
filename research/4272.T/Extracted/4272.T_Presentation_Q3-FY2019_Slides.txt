FY 2019 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
  ▸The absorption-type merger of NIPPON KAYAKU FUKUYAMA Co., Ltd.
   and NIPPON KAYAKU TOKYO Co., Ltd. built a production structure that
   enables integrated management from production through sales.


  ▸The difficulty in procuring raw materials as a result of Chinese
   environmental regulations persisted and prices remained at a high
   level.


  ▸The Polatechno Group saw slow sales of dye-type polarizing films, but
   sales of components for X-ray analysis systems were steady.




                                                                           5
FY 2019 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸INFLIXIMAB BS and the generic anti-cancer drug TEMOZOLOMIDE
   recorded sales growth.
  ▸Trastuzumab BS was indicated for breast cancer treatment regimen A
   and approval was obtained for the dosage and administration.


 Safety Systems Business
  ▸Growth in global vehicle sales has halted and growth in automotive
   safety components has slowed.



 Other: Agrochemicals Business
  ▸Prices were increased on core products in the agrochemicals business.

Future Initiatives for FY 2019
 Functional Chemicals Business
 ▸We will watch semiconductor prices and trends in other indicators of demand,
  and respond accordingly.


 ▸Prices will be raised to compensate for high material prices, and we will
  continue to search for and expand raw material sources.


 ▸Preparations are underway for beginning mass production of maleimide
  resins next fiscal year.




                                                                                 9
Future Initiatives for FY 2019
 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸We will focus on increasing penetration of INFLIXIMAB BS and expanding the
  number of facilities that use Trastuzumab BS.
 ▸The focus will be on volume growth of generic anti-cancer drugs, mainly in
  key products.


 Safety Systems Business
 ▸We will keep an eye on market trends in China and Europe as we continue to
  invest mainly in production facilities for new products.



 Other: Agrochemicals Business
 ▸Prices will be raised on more products in the agrochemicals business.
