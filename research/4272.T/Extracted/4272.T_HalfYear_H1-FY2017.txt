1. Qualitative Information Concerning Results for the Second Quarter
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
In the first half of the current consolidated fiscal year (April 1, 2016 to September 30, 2016), the global economy
saw a continued gradual rebound in the economies of the U.S. and Europe and a modest deceleration in the pace of
growth in China. Japan’s economy saw signs of a mild rebound in firm consumer spending that accompanied
improvement in the employment and income environment, and other indicators. However, the uncertainty of the
future has persisted amid concerns over deceleration of the global economy and further appreciation of the yen,
among other factors.
     Amid these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group worked to implement the key themes and resolve the mid-
and long-term key issues outlined in “Take a New Step 2016,” the mid-term business plan launched from the
current fiscal year. We carried out additional cost cuts aimed at reinforcing our profit structure, in addition to
focusing on strengthening R&D, optimal allocation of business resources into core businesses, and expanding our
global operations.

Reflecting these undertakings, consolidated net sales in the first half of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2017 totaled
76.877 billion yen, a decrease of 3.352 billion yen (4.2%) year-on-year. Although sales for the safety systems
business exceeded the first half of the previous fiscal year, the functional chemicals and pharmaceuticals businesses
saw a year-on-year decline in sales.
     Operating income declined by 1.929 billion yen (17.3%) year-on-year to 9.237 billion yen, impacted by the
decline in net sales.
     Ordinary income was 7.626 billion yen, a decline of 2.515 billion yen (24.8%) year-on-year due in part to
foreign exchange losses.
     Profit attributable to owners of parent declined by 1.447 billion yen (21.9%) year-on-year to 5.175 billion yen.

Performance by business segment is as described below.

[Functional Chemicals Business]
Sales were 31.269 billion yen, a decline of 3.804 billion yen (10.8%) year-on-year.
     In the functional materials business, epoxy resins for semiconductor encapsulation and UV-curable resins saw
strong sales, achieving growth over the first half of the previous fiscal year.
     In the color materials business, colorants for inkjet printers, dyes for textiles, and dyes for paper were sluggish,
underperforming sales in the first half of the previous fiscal year.
     In the catalysts business, sales of catalysts for the production of Acrylic acid were sluggish, recording a
year-on-year declines.
     In the Polatechno Group, dye-type polarizing films and ProFluxTM, an inorganic polarizer were sluggish,
recording a year-on-year decline.
     Segment profit was 2.517 billion yen, a decline of 1.106 billion yen (30.5%) year-on-year.

[Pharmaceuticals Business]
Sales were 23.887 billion yen, a decline of 913 million yen (3.7%) year-on-year
      Pharmaceuticals in Japan recorded growth in sales for the biosimilar, INFLIXIMAB BS for I.V. Infusion (an
antibody drug), and the specially controlled medical devices, HEPASPHERE and EMBOSPHERE (embolization
material). In contrast, drug prices revisions caused sales of PACLITAXEL Inj. NK, CARBOPLATIN Intravenous Inj.
NK, and other injectable anti-cancer drugs, and BICALUTAMIDE Tab. NK, ANASTROZOLE Tab. NK, and other
oral anti-cancer drugs to decline from the first half of the previous fiscal year. Sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan
declined overall, despite the contribution made by new products such as OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an
anti-cancer drug) and DOCETAXEL for I.V. Infusion (an anti-cancer drug).
      Exports recorded year-on-year growth overall, despite a year-on-year decline in sales of ETOPOSIDEs and
BLEOs (both anti-cancer drugs).
      Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients for the Japanese domestic market saw year-on-year growth overall
on the back of growth in raw materials for pharmaceuticals and fermented products.
      Diagnostic agents as a whole declined as diabetes diagnostic agents underperformed compared to the first half
of the previous fiscal year.
      Segment profit was 2.796 billion yen, a decline of 1.259 billion yen (31.1%) year-on-year.

[Safety Systems Business]

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
[1] Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 259.329 billion yen, a decrease of 13.349 billion yen year-on-year. The main decreases were in
securities, a decline of 5.154 billion yen; notes and accounts receivable-trade, a decline of 5.012 billion yen, and
property, plant, and equipment, a decline of 3.501 billion yen. The main increases were in other investments
(included in investments and other assets), a rise of 828 million yen.
      Total liabilities were 64.779 billion yen, a decrease of 7.407 billion yen from the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main decreases were in long-term loans payable, a decline of 2.582 billion yen;
accounts payable-other, a decrease of 1.723 billion yen; income taxes payable, a decrease of 1.258 billion yen; and
short-term loans payable, a decrease of 942 million yen. The main increase was in notes and accounts payable-trade,
an increase of 696 million yen.
      Net assets were 194.55 billion yen, a decrease of 5.942 billion yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main decreases were in translation adjustments, a decrease of 4.921 billion yen; dividends paid, a
decrease of 3.073 billion yen; and the purchase of treasury stock, a decrease of 2.684 billion yen. The main increase
was in profit attributable to owners of parent, an increase of 5.175 billion yen.

[2] Cash Flows Status
Net cash provided by operating activities came to 15.255 billion yen (versus a cash inflow in the first half of the
previous fiscal year of 9.450 billion yen). Although cash flow was negatively impacted by an increase in inventories
of 1.663 billion yen, and a rise in income tax paid of 3.473 billion yen, positive cash flow was primarily generated
by profit before income taxes of 7.294 billion yen, depreciation and amortization of 5.435 billion yen, a decrease in
trade receivables of 3.242 billion yen, and exchange loss of 2.003 billion yen.
      Net cash used in investing activities came to 8.804 billion yen (versus a cash outflow in the first half of the
previous fiscal year of 6.072 billion yen). This mainly reflects a purchase of property, plant and equipment of 6.730
billion yen.
      Net cash used in financing activities was 9.646 billion yen (versus a cash outflow in the first half of the
previous fiscal year of 5.259 billion yen). This mainly reflects 3.544 billion yen used for the repayment of long-term
loans, 3.064 billion yen for dividends paid, and 2.684 billion yen for the purchase of treasury stock.
      Consequently, at the end of the first half, the balance of cash and cash equivalents stood at 42.180 billion yen, a
decrease of 5.284 billion yen from the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year (the balance of cash and cash
equivalents at the end of the first half of the previous fiscal year was 51.819 billion yen).

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, including Consolidated Business Results Forecasts
We see continued firm growth in the U.S. and are concerned about an economic downswing in Europe due to
increased geopolitical risk as factors affecting the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku
Group. A gradual rebound in Japan’s economy is anticipated as the employment and income environment improves
and due in part to the beneficial effects of various government policies. However, the impact of a downswing in the
global economy bears watching.
     Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
     The business forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 2017 announced on May 11, 2016 has been revised.

2. Items Related to Summary Information (Notes)
(Changes to accounting policies, changes to accounting estimates, and restatements)
(Changes to accounting policies)
      The Practical Solution on a Change in Depreciation Method due to Tax Reform 2016 (Practical Solution
Report No. 32, June 17, 2016) was adopted for the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year in conjunction
with the Corporation Tax Act reform, and the method of depreciation for buildings and accompanying facilities and
structures acquired from April 1, 2016 onward was changed from the declining balance method to the straight-line
method.
      This change had no material effect on operating income, ordinary income, and profit before income taxes in the
first half of the current consolidated fiscal year.
