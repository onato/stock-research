1. Qualitative Information Concerning Cumulative Results for the First Three Quarters
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
In the first three quarters of this consolidated fiscal year (April 1, 2017 to December 31, 2017), the global economy
as a whole continued to rebound gradually. This was underpinned by steady consumer spending and growth in capital
investment in the U.S. Europe also maintained signs of a recovery due to growth in exports and other factors, despite
uncertainty surrounding the political environment. China continued to see solid growth on the back of strong capital
investment. The Japanese economy staged a gradual rebound as corporate profits improved on the back of continued
improvement in the employment and income environment.
     The Nippon Kayaku Group worked to reduce costs even further amid these conditions, addressing key themes
and grappling with mid- and long-term key issues in the mid-term business plan, “Take a New Step 2016,” which
began from the previous fiscal year, and working to improve profitability in addition to strengthening R&D, allocating
management resources to key businesses in an optimal manner, and expanding overseas business.

This resulted year-on-year growth in sales in the functional chemicals business, the pharmaceuticals business, and the
safety systems business, increasing total net sales for the first three quarters of this consolidated fiscal year to 124,799
million yen, an increase of 6,913 million yen (5.9%) over the same period of the previous fiscal year.
      Growth in net sales boosted operating income to 18,132 million yen, an increase of 2,760 million yen (18.0%)
over the same period of the previous fiscal year.
      Ordinary income increased to 18,520 million yen owing to a decrease in foreign exchange losses and other factors,
an increase of 2,813 million yen (17.9%) over the same period of the previous fiscal year.
      Profit attributable to owners of parent was 12,969 million yen, an increase of 2,116 million yen (19.5%) from
the same period of the previous year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.

[Functional Chemicals Business]
Sales were 49,277 million yen, an increase of 1,766 million yen (3.7%) year-on-year.
     In the functional materials business, overseas sales of epoxy resins were steady, recording year-on-year growth.
     In the color materials business, dyes for textiles and paper underperformed sales in the first three quarters of the
previous fiscal year. However, sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications and developer for thermal
paper increased over the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year, resulting in year-on-year growth for color
materials overall.
     The catalysts business underperformed compared to the same period of the previous fiscal year.
     In the Polatechno Group, sales of polarizing film used in LCD panels for automobiles and industrial applications
were steady, as were sales of components for X-ray analysis systems. This resulted in year-on-year growth in sales.
     Segment profit was 6,282 million yen, an increase of 1,663 million yen (36.0%) year-on-year.

[Pharmaceuticals Business]
Sales were 37,218 million yen, an increase of 921 million yen (2.5%) year-on-year.
     Pharmaceuticals in Japan saw an increase in sales of the biosimilars, INFLIXIMAB BS for I.V. Infusion (an
antibody drug) and FILGRASTIM BS Inj. (which reduces the chance of infection in chemotherapy patients). Overall
sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan increased over the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year due to the
contribution of new products such as OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an anti-cancer drug), despite the year-on-
year decline in long-listed products caused by a switch to generic drugs.
     Exports saw a year-on-year decline in sales overall, despite year-on-year growth in ETOPOSIDEs (anti-cancer
drugs). This was due to year-on-year declines in sales of BLEOs (anti-cancer drugs) and high potency active
pharmaceutical ingredients.
     Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients and diagnostic agents for the Japanese domestic market decreased
from the first three quarters of the previous fiscal year.
     Segment profit was 5,962 million yen, an increase of 1,371 million yen (29.9%) year-on-year.

[Safety Systems Business]
Sales were 32,402 million yen, an increase of 4,137 million yen (14.6%) year-on-year.
      Business in Japan saw year-on-year growth in sales for both airbag inflators and micro gas generators for seatbelt
pretensioners.
      Business overseas was strong in the North American, European, and Chinese markets. Demand for automotive
safety components in emerging countries also expanded. This resulted in year-on-year growth in sales of airbag
inflators, micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners and squibs.
      Segment profit was 5,301 million yen, an increase of 37 million yen (0.7%) year-on-year.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 286,830 million yen, an increase of 14,038 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in notes and accounts receivable-trade, an increase of 6,579 million
yen; investment securities, an increase of 4,615 million yen; property, plant and equipment, an increase of 3,331
million yen; inventory assets, an increase of 2,969 million yen; and goodwill, an increase of 1,232 million yen. The
main decrease was in cash and deposits, a decrease of 4,612 million yen.
     Liabilities were 65,885 million yen, a decline of 1,038 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main decreases were in long-term loans payable, a decrease of 3,604 million yen;
accounts payable-other, a decrease of 2,107 million yen; and income taxes payable, a decrease of 992 million yen.
The main increases were in notes and accounts payable-trade, an increase of 2,967 million yen; and short-term loans
payable, an increase of 1,951 million yen.
     Net assets were 220,944 million yen, an increase of 15,077 million year compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in profit attributable to owners of parent, an increase of 12,969
million yen; translation adjustments, an increase of 4,243 million yen; and unrealized holding gains on other securities,
an increase of 2,548 million yen. The main decrease was in dividend payments, a decrease of 5,627 million yen.

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, including Consolidated Business Results Forecasts
In the future, the Nippon Kayaku Group anticipates continued firm growth in the global economy as economic growth
in the U.S. and Europe continues. However, there is concern over a downswing in the global economic environment
due to increasing uncertainty over the future political situation. While a gradual rebound in Japan’s economy is
anticipated as employment and income continue to improve, we need to pay attention to the impact that a downswing
in the global economy would have.
      Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
      Nippon Kayaku Group did not revise its full-year forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2018, which
were announced on November 8, 2017.
