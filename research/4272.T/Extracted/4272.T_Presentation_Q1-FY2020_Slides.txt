FY 2020 1Q: Business Environments and Topics
  Functional Chemicals Business
 Functional Materials Business
   ▸ The semiconductor market seems to have bottomed out and we expect it to
     rebound in the second half.
   ▸ We expect to bring the production equipment for maleimide resins for use in 5G
     circuit boards online from the second half
 Color Materials Business
   ▸ Sales of colorants for inkjet printers for consumer use were firm.
   ▸ Growth was also steady for colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications.
   ▸ We have decided to invest in an increase in production capacity of developer for
     thermal paper at the Fukuyama Plant.
 Catalysts Business
   ▸ The shipment schedule for this fiscal year is concentrated in the second half.
     Shipments during the first quarter were in line with the plan.
 Polatechno Group
   ▸ The optical film business was sluggish due to the slump in the Chinese market.
      Sales of components for X-ray analysis systems were firm.

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸ Biosimilars: Sales of INFLIXIMAB BS grew and the number of clinics using
    TRASTUZUMAB BS expanded
  ▸ Generic anti-cancer drugs: Sales of OXALIPLATIN, TEMOZOLOMIDE, and other
    core products grew, and GEFITINIB (Indication: Non-small cell lung cancer)
    was launched on June 14

  ▸ We will acquire approval for the manufacturing and sale of the biomedicine
    PORTRAZZA (Indication: Squamous cell non-small cell lung cancer) from Eli
    Lilly Japan K.K. (acquisition scheduled for August 1)

 Safety Systems Business
  ▸ Global auto sales were slow, particularly to China

 Other: Agrochemicals Business
  ▸ Sales were firm for FINESAVE and existing core products
