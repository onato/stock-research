                   FY 2023 Business Summary
 Net sales increase YoY and hit record highs; YoY operating income also increased
 Net income decreased YoY due to impairment loss on fixed assets, etc.

 Major changes in the business environment between 1H and 2H
  • Sales and profits increased YoY in 1H
  • Record highs for both net sales and profits in 1H

  • In 2H, the functional chemicals and the safety systems businesses were affected
    by a slowdown in the semiconductor–related market and a decline in domestic
    automobile production. In addition, rising prices for raw materials and energy
    led to higher costs which impacted profits

      FY 2023 Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
  •   For epoxy resins, demand for circuit boards slowed in the latter stage of 1H due to declining demand
      for consumer products
  •   Dry film resists held firm due to expansion of next generation communication devices

  •   Consumer inkjet printer colorants sales slowed in 2H, despite robust 1H performance
  •   Industrial inkjet colorants and inks held firm as the market recovered

  •   Catalysts performance remained solid despite logistical disruptions in 1H

  •   Optical films struggled mainly through declining automotive applications due to a shortage of
      semiconductors and the Ukrainian problem
  •   Solid sales of X-ray analyzer materials

      FY 2023 Business Environments and Topics
 Pharmaceuticals Business
  •   DARVIAS® Infusion 135mg (generic name: DARINAPARSIN) launched in August
  •   BEVACIZUMAB BS Intravenous infusion 100mg「CTNK」・400mg「CTNK」launched in November
  •   Established the Nishi-Nihon Distribution Center
 Safety Systems Business
  •   The domestic business saw demand remain sluggish due to continued production cutbacks by
      automobiles makers, in turn caused by a shortage of semiconductors and other factors
  •   Overseas, demand remained sluggish in Europe, but showed continued signs of recovery due to
      various policies in ASEAN and other regions

 Agrochemicals
  •   Overseas sales continued to expand, mainly for EVISECT and flomethoquin products
  •   Domestic sales reflected higher raw materials costs in sales prices

             FY 2024 Business Forecast Overview
 Sales and Profit Forecasts and Assumptions
  •   In 1H, the functional chemicals business impacts which emerged in last year’s 2H, pertaining
      to inventory adjustments by customers and related mainly to semiconductor-related
      materials, are expected to continue, as are the impacts of reduced domestic automobile
      production on the safety systems business
  •   Both businesses are expected to recover in 2H
  •   High prices for raw materials and energy are expected to continue
 Initiatives for Improvement
  •   We have not been able to reflect the impact of rising costs in sales prices, and we will
      continue to focus on reducing costs along with other efforts to achieve reasonable prices
  •   We will accelerate the development of new businesses and products while expanding the
      four existing businesses
