FY 2020 : 1H Topics (1)
Functional Chemicals Business
 • Sales of epoxy resins for circuit boards grew.
 • Production of maleimide resins for 5G-capable circuit boards
   began.
 • Sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications
   grew as the market expanded.
 • Strong demand resulted in sales growth of materials for thermal
   paper.
 • Catalyst sales proceeded according to plan.
 • Polatechno struggled in the optical film business.
 • MicroChem Corp. in the U.S. changed its name to “KAYAKU
   Advanced Materials, Inc”. (on October 1, 2019)
 •




 • Polatechno was made a wholly owned subsidiary.
     August 28 to October 10, 2019: Tender offer
     November 8, 2019: Delisted (from JASDAQ Standard market)

FY 2020 : 1H Topics (2)
Pharmaceuticals Business
 • INFLIXIMAB BS and generic anti-cancer drugs saw growth in
   sales.
 • TRASTUZUMAB BS eliminated the indication mismatch with the
   originator drug.
 • The approval for manufacture and sale of the biomedicine
   Portrazza® was transferred from Eli Lilly Japan K.K. and
   preparations are underway for the launch by fiscal year-end.

Safety Systems Business
 • The slump in the global auto market suppressed growth.

Other
 • The agrochemicals business improved profit margin by switching
   the sourcing for product materials.

FY 2020 : Initiatives for 2H
Functional Chemicals Business
 • Full-scale production of maleimide resins for 5G-capable circuit
   boards will begin.
 • Nippon Kayaku will strengthen ties with Polatechno.

Pharmaceuticals Business
 • Biosimilars and generic anti-cancer drugs will see greater growth.
 • The new product line-up will be expanded.

Safety Systems Business
 • Use of the new type of inflator will expand.

Other
 • The agrochemicals business will focus efforts on continued
   reduction of product costs.

KAYAKU Next Stage : Financial Plan

We aim to achieve sustainable growth in corporate value by fully
utilizing our stable financial base to actively invest in growth areas.
  1. Acquire POLATECHNO Co., Ltd. as a wholly owned subsidiary.
     Improve the profitability of the polarizer business.
     Achieve benefits from integration of R&D and overseas operations.
     Improve the consolidated financial structure.

     Capital investments of 50 billion yen and R&D investments of 40 billion yen
     over the three years of the mid-term business plan.
     Also take growth during the next mid-term business plan aimed at achieving
     the “Vision” in mind when investing.

  3. Keep capital cost in mind in formulating financial plan, in managing equity
     capital, and in external procurement.

  4. Actively provide returns to shareholders depending on financial position and
     external environment.
