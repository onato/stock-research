FY 2018 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
  ▸ EP resin sales remained firm due to the continued strength of the semiconductor
    market.
  ▸ Negotiations on the selling price of EP resin are underway due to the increase in
    the price of the raw materials.
  ▸ Changes were made to the distribution channels for dyes for textiles to reduce
    costs further.
  ▸ POLATECHNO CO., Ltd. acquired RaySpec Limited, making it a wholly owned
    subsidiary.
    (RaySpec operates a business in components for X-ray analysis systems.)

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸ TEMOZOLOMIDE Tab., a generic anti-cancer drug, was launched on the market.
  ▸ Clinical trial results mainly in Europe for INFLIXIMAB BS were announced,
    strengthening the evidence concerning comparability to the original drugs (same
    quality, safety, and efficacy).

 Safety Systems Business
  ▸ Sales remained steady, supported by global demand for automotive safety
    components, and due in part to the favorable exchange rates.

Future Initiatives for FY 2018
 Functional Chemicals Business
 ▸ Functional materials business: Capturing demand steadily and
   expand uses.
 ▸ Color materials business: Focus on growing sales of colorants
   for inkjet printers in industrial applications.
 ▸ Catalysts business: Drive development of high yield catalysts.
 ▸ Polatechno Group: Generate sales for mass production of new
   products and incorporate RaySpec technology.

Future Initiatives for FY 2018
 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸ Focus on further expansion of biosimilar and generic anti-
   cancer drug sales.
 ▸ The Niigata plant of Cultivecs Inc. is scheduled for completion
   in March.
 ▸ Preparations are underway for the start of a clinical trial for
   NK105.
 Safety Systems Business
 ▸ Prepare for mass production of two new types of inflators next
   fiscal year.

 Other
 ▸ Prepare for the launch of the new agrochemical, ANM138
   (product name: FINESAVE).
