FY 2021 : 1H Topics (1)
Functional Chemicals Business
  • Sales of epoxy resins and maleimide resins were firm for use in servers and
    other equipment due to the growth in remote working devices while use in 5G
    base stations was negatively impacted by U.S.-China trade friction
  • Sales of epoxy resins for use in automobile applications were sluggish
  • The cleaner for LCD and semiconductor industry business we acquired is
    incorporated in results and performed well
  • Sales of colorants for inkjet printers for consumer use were strong, owing to
    the positive impact of working from home and distance learning during the
    COVID-19 pandemic
  • Sales of colorants for inkjet printers used in industrial applications declined
    abruptly due to the decrease in demand for printed advertising and
    promotional materials
  • Sales of dyes for textiles declined substantially due to the slump in apparel
    sales
  • Materials for thermal paper saw sales shrink substantially due to a rapid
    decline in demand for airline boarding passes and movie and theater tickets
  • Catalyst sales outperformed the plan for the 1H due to front-loading of 2H
    plans and shipments that were not part of the plan
  • The Polatechno business faced difficulties in both the optical film business
    and precision components business                                               4
FY 2021 : 1H Topics (2)

Pharmaceuticals Business
•   Biosimilars: Sales growth for INFLIXIMAB BS and TRASTUZUMAB BS was strong
•   New products: The new biomedicine PORTRAZZA® achieved market penetration
     The cancer-related generic APREPITANT recorded strong growth
     We began co-promotion of NUBEQA® Tab., a therapeutic drug for prostate cancer

    TEMOZOLOMIDE were firm

Safety Systems Business
•   Sales declined steeply due to the stagnant global automotive market
    Sluggish demand was anticipated in the 2Q onward in countries other than China,
    but demand has rebounded faster than expected in Japan and North America
Agrochemicals and Other Businesses
•   The agrochemicals business saw firm sales in Japan and sluggish sales to
    overseas markets due to difficulty in procuring active ingredients

FY 2021 COVID-19 Business Impact
Nippon Kayaku is continuing to operate production bases to fulfill our responsibility to
supply products while taking steps to protect the safety of our stakeholders and
employees and their families in order to mitigate the risk of infection with COVID-19.
•    COVID-19 is having a large impact on performance of the safety systems business and the
     functional chemicals business.
        The global slump in the automotive market is having a negative impact on automotive safety
     components and optical film, and the large impact on the dye market is having a substantial
     negative impact on color materials.
        Meanwhile, the pandemic is having a positive impact on resins for circuit boards and colorants
     for inkjet printers for consumer use, due to demand from telecommuting.

Financial Plans
We will strive to achieve sustainable improvement in corporate value by maximizing
use of our stable financial base to actively invest in growth areas.
1. Investment in the information and communications area
     ・ A manufacturing plant for maleimide resins and other materials for 5G-capable
       circuit boards was built at the Asa Plant in Yamaguchi pref. (commercial production
       began in April 2020).
     ・ The cleaner business used in the LCD and semiconductor manufacturing
       processes was acquired from Henkel AG & Co. KGaA of Germany. (April 2020)
2. Capital investment & investment in R&D
     ・ Capital investment will be realigned to the trend of lower demand for automotive
       safety components due to the slump in the auto market.
     ・ Around 40 billion yen will be invested in R&D over three years, in line with the
       level of investment at the beginning of the Mid-term Business Plan.

3. Financial plans that keep capital cost in mind
     ・ We will continue to pay stable dividends of 30 yen/year, and expect a dividend
       payout ratio of 51.2% for FY 2021.
     ・ Treasury shares were acquired in FY 2020 and the total return ratio was 63.7%.
     ・ 12 billion yen in corporate bonds were issued (in December 2019) for the first time
       in 10 years, diversifying the means of financing.
     ・ We will manage equity appropriately and pay shareholder returns according to the
       external environment and the company’s cash position.
