FY 2019 : 1H Topics (1)
Functional Chemicals Business
 • Sales of epoxy resins were strong for both semiconductor encapsulation and circuit
   boards due to continued strength of the semiconductor market
 • Completed installation of mass production equipment for new maleimide resins for use
   in next-generation circuit boards at the Asa Plant
 • Strong growth of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications continued
 • In the color materials business, we launched operations at the technical service center
   in Wuxi, China and at NKTH, a sales subsidiary in Thailand
 • The business environment for catalysts showed signs of a rebound and inquiries also
   increased
 • The Polatechno Group saw growth in demand in components for X-ray analysis systems
   and an increase in sales
 • The decision was made to merge NIPPON KAYAKU FUKUYAMA Co., Ltd. and NIPPON
   KAYAKU TOKYO Co., Ltd. through an absorption-type merger




                                                                                             5
FY 2019 : 1H Topics (2)
Pharmaceuticals Business
 • Recorded year-on-year growth in sales of INFLIXIMAB BS and launched the humanized
   monoclonal antibody anti-cancer drug, Trastuzumab BS
 • Increased our market share in terms of volume sales for TEMOZOLOMIDE, a generic
   anti-cancer drug in devised dosage forms (indicated for malignant glioma (brain
   tumor))

Safety Systems Business
 • Began mass production of the new types of inflators (disk-type and cylinder-type)
 • Changed the name of our Czech subsidiary, ISS, to Kayaku Safety Systems Europe a.s.
   (KSE), and reinforced global operations

Other
 • Launched the new insecticide FINESAVE® in the agrochemicals business

FY 2019 : Initiatives for 2H
Functional Chemicals Business
 • Completed the production line for the new maleimide resins at the Asa Plant
 • Expand sales of color materials in China and the ASEAN market through the technical
   service center in China and our subsidiary NKTH in Thailand
 • Continue to focus on the trends in raw material prices and diversify raw material sources
   to ensure a continued stable supply
Pharmaceuticals Business
 • Achieve greater growth in the volume of INFLIXIMAB BS, the generic anti-cancer drug
   OXALIPLATIN, TEMOZOLOMIDE, and other key products sold
 • Focus on the adoption and market penetration of Trastuzumab BS
 • Promote Phase II clinical trials of NK105
Safety Systems Business
 • Continue capital investments aimed at strengthening the production structure
Other
 • Focus on achieving market penetration and distribution of the new insecticide
   FINESAVE®
