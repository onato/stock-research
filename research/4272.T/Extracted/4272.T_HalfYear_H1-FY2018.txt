1. Qualitative Information Concerning Results for the Second Quarter
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
In the first half of this consolidated fiscal year (April 1 to September 30, 2017), the global economy as a whole
continued to rebound gradually. The U.S. saw continuing improvement in the employment environment and growth
in consumer spending. Despite uncertainty surrounding the political environment, Europe saw a gradual rebound in
the economy, underpinned by growth in exports and other factors. China continued to see solid growth on the back
of strong capital investment. The Japanese economy staged a gradual rebound as the improvement in the
employment and income environment persisted and corporate profits improved.
     The Nippon Kayaku Group worked to reduce costs even further amid these conditions, addressing key themes
and grappling with mid- and long-term key issues in the new mid-term business plan, “Take a New Step 2016,”
which began from the previous fiscal year, and working to improve profitability in addition to strengthening R&D,
allocating management resources to key businesses in an optimal manner, and expanding overseas business.

This resulted year-on-year growth in sales in the functional chemicals business, the pharmaceuticals business, and
the safety systems business, increasing total net sales for the first half of this consolidated fiscal year to 82,326
million yen, an increase of 5,449 million yen (7.1%) over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Growth in net sales boosted operating income to 11,943 million yen, an increase of 2,706 million yen (29.3%)
over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Ordinary income increased to 12,108 million yen owing to a decrease in foreign exchange losses and other
factors, an increase of 4,482 million yen (58.8%) over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Profit attributable to owners of parent was 8,580 million yen, an increase of 3,404 million yen (65.8%) from
the same period in the previous year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.

[Functional Chemicals Business]
Sales were 33,412 million yen, an increase of 2,143 million yen (6.9%) year-on-year.
      In the functional materials business, overseas sales of epoxy resins were steady, recording year-on-year growth.
      In the color materials business, dyes for textiles and paper underperformed sales in the first half of the previous
fiscal year. However, sales of colorants for inkjet printers and developer for thermal paper increased over the first
half of the previous fiscal year, resulting in year-on-year growth for color materials overall.
      In the catalysts business, sales of catalysts for the production of acrylic acid were strong, outperforming the
first half of the previous fiscal year.
      In the Polatechno Group, sales of polarizing film used in LCD panels for automobiles and industrial
applications were steady, as were sales of components for X-ray analysis systems. This resulted in year-on-year
growth in sales.
      Segment profit was 4,417 million yen, an increase of 1,900 million yen (75.5%) year-on-year.

[Pharmaceuticals Business]
Sales were 24,090 million yen, an increase of 203 million yen (0.9%) year-on-year.
     Pharmaceuticals in Japan saw an increase in sales of the biosimilars, INFLIXIMAB BS for I.V. Infusion (an
antibody drug) and FILGRASTIM BS Inj. (which reduces the chance of infection in chemotherapy patients).
Overall sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan increased over the first half of the previous fiscal year due to the
contribution of new products such as OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an anti-cancer drug), despite the
year-on-year decline in long-listed products caused by a switch to generic drugs.
     Exports saw a year-on-year decline in sales overall, despite year-on-year growth in ETOPOSIDEs (anti-cancer
drugs). This was due to year-on-year declines in sales of BLEOs (anti-cancer drugs) and high potency active
pharmaceutical ingredients.
     Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients and diagnostic agents for the Japanese domestic market decreased
from the first half of the previous fiscal year.
     Segment profit was 3,459 million yen, an increase of 662 million yen (23.7%) year-on-year.

[Safety Systems Business]
Sales were 21,046 million yen, an increase of 2,953 million yen (16.3%) year-on-year.
      Business in Japan saw year-on-year growth in sales of micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners, while
sales of airbag inflators remained flat.
      Business overseas was strong in the North American, European, and Chinese markets. An expansion in demand
for automotive safety components in emerging countries also resulted in year-on-year growth in sales of airbag
inflators, micro gas generators for seatbelt pretensioners and squibs.
      Segment profit was 3,562 million yen, an increase of 219 million yen (6.6%) year-on-year.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
[1] Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 281,834 million yen, an increase of 9,043 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in notes and accounts receivable-trade, an increase of 4,688
million yen; investment securities, an increase of 2,740 million yen; property, plant and equipment, an increase of
2,662 million yen; inventory assets, an increase of 2,032 million yen; and securities, an increase of 1,007 million
yen. The main decreases were in cash and deposits, a decrease of 3,590 million yen; and accounts receivable-other
(included in “other” under current assets), a decrease of 1,622 million yen.
      Liabilities were 64,910 million yen, a decrease of 2,014 million yen compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main decreases were in accounts payable-other, a decrease of 2,627 million yen; and
long-term loans payable, a decrease of 2,406 million yen. The main increases were in notes and accounts
payable-trade, an increase of 2,229 million yen; and income taxes payable, an increase of 433 million yen.
      Net assets were 216,923 million yen, an increase of 11,057 million year compared to the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in profit attributable to owners of parent, an increase of 8,580
million yen; translation adjustments, an increase of 3,518 million yen; and unrealized holding gains on other
securities, an increase of 1,303 million yen. The main decrease was in dividend payments, a decrease of 3,030
million yen.

[2] Cash Flows Status
Net cash provided by operating activities came to 9,127 million yen (versus a cash inflow of 15,255 million yen in
the first half of the previous fiscal year). Although cash flow was negatively impacted by an increase of 4,283
million yen in accounts receivable and 3,136 million yen in income tax paid, positive cash flow was generated from
profit before income taxes of 11,950 million yen and depreciation and amortization of 5,596 million yen.
      Net cash used in investing activities came to 6,839 million yen (versus a cash outflow of 8,804 million yen in
the first half of the previous fiscal year). This was mainly due to expenditures of 6,307 million yen on the purchase
of property, plant and equipment.
      Net cash used in financing activities was 6,172 million yen (versus a cash outflow of 9,646 million yen in the
first half of the previous fiscal year). This mainly reflects 3,022 million yen in dividend payments, and 2,623 million
yen expended for repayment of long-term loans.
      Consequently, the balance of cash and cash equivalents at the end of the first half stood at 49,182 million yen, a
decrease of 2,602 million yen compared to the end of the previous consolidated fiscal year (the balance of cash and
cash equivalents at the end of the first half of the previous fiscal year was 42,180 million yen).

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, including Consolidated Business Results Forecasts
In the future, the Nippon Kayaku Group anticipates continued firm growth in the global economy as economic
growth in the U.S. and Europe continues. However, there is concern over a downswing in the global economic
environment due to increasing uncertainty over the future political situation. While a gradual rebound in Japan’s
economy is anticipated as employment and income continue to improve, we need to pay attention to the impact that
a downswing in the global economy would have.
     Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
     The business forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 2018 announced on May 10, 2017 has been revised.
