FY 2019 1Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
  ▸The semiconductor market conditions remained favorable, epoxy resin
   sales were steady
  ▸Sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications continued
   to grow
  ▸Preparations are underway for commencement of business at
   NIPPONKAYAKU (THAILAND) CO., LTD., our subsidiary in Thailand
  ▸Demand for catalysts continued to show signs of a rebound
  ▸Polatechno Group focused on launching new products (High Contrast
   High Durability Dye-type Polarizers, Iodine-type Polarizers, and
   Achromatic Polarizers)

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸Focused on increasing sales volume of INFLIXIMAB BS
  ▸TEMOZOLOMIDE Tab. sales grew, among the generic anti-cancer drugs
  ▸Trastuzumab BS was listed in the NHI price list (on May 30) and
   preparations for sale are underway

 Safety Systems Business
  ▸Demand for automotive safety components was steady, and sales grew

  ▸The name of our Czech subsidiary, ISS, was changed to Kayaku Safety

Future Initiatives for FY 2019
 Functional Chemicals Business
 ▸Maintain stable product supply for the robust demand
 ▸Work to diversify material sources while keeping an eye on the trend in
  material prices

 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸Work to increase sales volume of biosimilars and generic anti-cancer drugs
 ▸Proceed with the Phase II clinical trial for the polymeric micelle anti-cancer
  drug NK105 without delay

 Safety Systems Business
 ▸Continue to improve the quality assurance systems and expand products at
  each location worldwide
 ▸Focus on expanding the vehicle models that the new types of inflators are
  installed in

 Other: Agrochemicals Business
 ▸Focus on achieving market penetration and distribution of FINESAVE®
