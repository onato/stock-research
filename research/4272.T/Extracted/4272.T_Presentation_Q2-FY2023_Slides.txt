      FY 2023 1H Business Environments and Issues
 Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
  •    YOY increase in net sales and income     Record-high sales and profits in the 1H of the fiscal year
  •    Operating income, ordinary income, and net income exceeded the targets
       due to maintaining the same cost of sales ratio, the exchange rate impact and other factors

 Functional Chemicals Business
  •    Epoxy resins: Demand for circuit boards slowed due to consumer electronics falls in 2Q, but sales for
       encapsulations held steady
  •    Strong demand for MEMS materials due to next-generation communications devices expansion
  •    Consumer inkjet printer colorants held steady
  •    Catalysts remained firm as logistics disruptions stabilized
  •    The optical film business struggled mainly with automotive applications, although the semiconductor
       shortages are tending towards alleviation
  •    Strong sales of projector materials and X-ray analyzer materials


                                                                                                               4
      FY 2023 1H Business Environments and Issues
 Pharmaceuticals Business
  •    DARVIAS® Injection 135mg (generic name: DARINAPARSIN) launched in August
  •    Generic anti-cancer drug, AZACITIDINE Injection 100mg「NK」・150mg「NK」launched in August
  •    BEVACIZUMAB BS Intravenous Infusion 100mg「CTNK」・400mg 「CTNK」received approval for
       manufacture and sale in September
  •    Established the Nishi-Nihon Distribution Center

 Safety Systems Business
  •    Domestically, demand was sluggish due to a reduction in automobile production caused by the impact
       of semiconductor shortages and other factors
  •    Overseas business continued to recover from sluggish demand related to the impact of the COVID-19,
       in part due to various policies enacted in each country

 Agrochemicals
  •    Strong sales of flometoquin (insecticides) and diazinon (animal pharmaceuticals) for overseas market
  •    Solid domestic sales
