1. Overview of Operating Results, Financial Position, and Future Outlook
(1) Overview of Operating Results for Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2017
In the consolidated fiscal year under review (April 1, 2016 to March 31, 2017), the global economy continued a
trend of recovery supported by strong consumer spending in the U.S., and the continuing gradual recovery in Europe,
which is seeing heightened geopolitical risk. The Chinese economy held firm due to the recovery seen in capital
investment and other factors. The Japanese economy continued to rebound as both employment and income levels
improved, however concerns over deceleration of the global economy and the impact of changes in the financial and
capital markets continue to cloud the future, leaving the outlook uncertain.
      In the functional chemicals industry, which encompasses functional materials, the information and
communications market saw even greater expansion in networking of personal electronic devices with home
electronics or motor vehicles. The miniaturization and increase in performance of semiconductor devices installed in
electronic devices and improvement in display panel resolution of such devices also advanced at a rapid pace. The
global trends in energy and resource conservation are also creating demand for new high-strength, lightweight
materials and highly heat-resistant materials.
      In the pharmaceuticals industry, the generic drug market is growing rapidly due to the government policy of
promoting generic drug use to curb medical costs. Competition in the generic drug market has nevertheless,
intensified. This partly reflects scale expansion by manufacturers specializing in generics, and the entry of overseas
and major domestic pharmaceutical companies into the market. Biosimilars, which are expected to make a major
contribution to lowering medical costs, are also drawing attention.
      In the automotive industry, demand faltered in Japan and in emerging economies, including ASEAN.
Meanwhile, demand was strong in the United States and Europe. Sales volume in China also grew substantially.

Amid these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group worked to implement the key themes and resolve the mid- and
long-term key issues outlined in “Take a New Step 2016,” the mid-term business plan launched from the current
fiscal year. We carried out additional cost cuts aimed at reinforcing our profit structure, in addition to focusing on
strengthening R&D, optimal allocation of business resources into core businesses, and expanding our global
operations.

Owing to these actions, consolidated net sales for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2017 totaled 159,117 million yen,
a decrease of 3,804 million yen (2.3%) year-on-year. Sales in the safety systems business outperformed the previous
fiscal year while sales in the functional chemicals and pharmaceuticals businesses declined.
      Consolidated operating income totaled 19,646 million yen, a decrease of 2,067 million yen (9.5%) year-on-year.
The functional chemicals and safety systems businesses saw growth in segment profit, while the pharmaceuticals
business saw segment profit decline.
      Consolidated ordinary income totaled 19,947 million yen, a decrease of 1,069 million yen (5.1%) year-on-year.
      Profit attributable to owners of parent totaled 15,635 million yen, a decrease of 1,656 million yen (9.6%)
year-on-year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.
 [1] Functional Chemicals Business
Sales stood at 64,029 million yen, a decrease of 4,759 million yen (6.9%) year-on-year.
      The functional materials business recorded overall growth in sales over the previous fiscal year. Epoxy resins
for semiconductor encapsulation and MicroChem Corp. in the U.S. both achieved year-on-year growth in sales.
      The color materials business saw an overall decline in sales from the previous fiscal year. Sales of developer
for thermal paper outperformed the previous fiscal year, while sales of colorants for inkjet printers, dyes for textiles,
dyes for paper, and functional colorants underperformed the previous fiscal year.
      The catalyst business underperformed the previous fiscal year.
      The Polatechno Group saw sales decline from the previous fiscal year due to sluggish sales of dye-type
polarizing films and ProFluxTM, an inorganic polarizer.
      Segment profit totaled 6,786 million yen, an increase of 472 million yen (7.5%) year-on-year.

anti-cancer drugs, and ANASTROZOLE Tab., BICALUTAMIDE Tab., and other oral anti-cancer drugs to decline
from the previous fiscal year. Sales of pharmaceuticals in Japan declined overall, despite the contribution made by

(2) Overview of Financial Position for Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2017
Total assets were 272,791 million yen, an increase of 111 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year. The
main increases were in cash and deposits, an increase of 7,468 million yen; inventories, an increase of 994 million
yen; other investments, an increase of 945 million yen; and accounts receivable-other, an increase of 681 million yen
(included in other, current assets). The main decreases were in notes and accounts receivable-trade, a decrease of
3,977 million yen; securities, a decrease of 3,004 million yen; and investment securities, a decrease of 2,891 million
yen.
      Total liabilities stood at 66,924 million yen, a decrease of 5,262 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal
year. The main decreases were in long-term loans payable, a decrease of 4,258 million yen; provision for
environmental measures, a decrease of 2,058 million yen; accrued expenses, a decrease of 720 million yen;
long-term deposits received, a decrease of 710 million yen, and short-term loans payable, a decrease of 675 million
yen. The main increases were in notes and accounts payable-trade, an increase of 1,496 million yen; and notes and
accounts payable-other, an increase of 1,452 million yen.
      Net assets were 205,866 million yen, an increase of 5,373 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year.
The main increases were in profit attributable to owners of parent, an increase of 15,635 million yen;
remeasurements of defined benefit plans, an increase of 598 million yen; and non-controlling interests, an increase
of 230 million yen. The main decreases were in dividends paid, a decrease of 5,238 million yen; acquisition of
treasury stock, a decrease of 2,698 million yen; translation adjustments, a decrease of 2,211 million yen; and
unrealized holding gains on other securities, a decrease of 994 million yen.

(4) Future Outlook
We see continued firm growth in the U.S. and are concerned about an economic downswing in Europe due to
increased geopolitical risk as factors affecting the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku
Group. The trend of recovery in China will continue and firm growth is anticipated. Although the recovery of the
Japanese economy is expected to continue as the economy benefits from various governmental policies in addition
to continued improvement in employment and income levels, we need to keep a cautious eye on the impact that a
downswing in the global economy would have.
      Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits. The strategy for each business
segment is described below.
      • In the functional chemicals business, we will contribute to the creation of an “ultra-smart society” by
providing functional chemical materials that offer special characteristics for the fields of information and
communication, and energy and resource conservation. Our core resin, colorant and catalyst technologies will
continue to deliver value by addressing market opportunities and global needs.
      • In the pharmaceuticals business, we will expand our business by concentrating on achieving market
superiority in the three key domains of research and development of polymeric micelle anti-cancer drugs,
establishing a business base and solid position in the biosimilars market, and expanding our product line-up in the
cancer-related field.
      • In the safety systems business, we will work to expand our business as a global business with operations in
five regions: Japan, North America, Europe, China, and ASEAN.

     In the fiscal year ending March 31, 2018, Nippon Kayaku forecasts net sales of 168,500 million yen, an
increase of 5.9% year-on-year, operating income of 20,000 million yen, an increase of 1.8%, ordinary income of
20,500 million yen, an increase of 2.8%, and profit attributable to owners of parent of 14,000 million yen, a decrease
of 10.5%.
     The exchange rate assumption for the fiscal year is JPY107/USD.

(5) Basic Policy Concerning Profit Dividends and Dividends for Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2017 and
    Fiscal Year Ending March 31, 2018
The Nippon Kayaku Group focuses heavily on returning profits to shareholders. A medium-term payout ratio has
been set at roughly 40% of profit attributable to owners of parent. This takes into account stable and consistent profit
returns and the level of retained earnings. Retained earnings are to be allocated for capital investment and R&D
investment in growing businesses to enhance the value of the Nippon Kayaku Group.
     The year-end dividend for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2017 is expected to be 17.5 yen per share.
Combined with the dividend paid at the end of the second-quarter, total dividends per share for the full year will be
30 yen.
     We expect to maintain the same dividend level for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2018: 15 yen per share for
the second quarter dividend and 15 yen per share for the year-end dividend, for a total annual dividend of 30 yen per
share.

2. Basic Stance on Selection of Accounting Principles
The Nippon Kayaku Group, for the time being, employs generally accepted accounting principles in Japan to
prepare its consolidated financial statements. This decision takes into account comparability of different fiscal
periods in the consolidated financial statements and with industry peers.
     It should be noted that the Company plans to adopt the use the International Financial Reporting Standards
(IFRS) in a timely and adequate fashion should certain circumstances in Japan and abroad give rise to this need.

Changes to Accounting Policies
      The Practical Solution on a Change in Depreciation Method due to Tax Reform 2016 (Practical Solution
Report No. 32, June 17, 2016) was adopted for the first quarter of the current consolidated fiscal year in conjunction
with the Corporation Tax Act reform, and the method of depreciation for buildings and accompanying facilities and
structures acquired from April 1, 2016 onward was changed from the declining balance method to the straight-line
method.
      This change had no material effect on operating income, ordinary income, and profit before income taxes in
current consolidated fiscal year.

Segment Information
[1] Summary of reportable segments
The reportable segments of the Nippon Kayaku Group are structural units of the Group for which separate financial
information can be acquired. These segments are subject to be examined periodically by the Board of Directors in
order to evaluate business results and make decisions on the allocation of business resources.
     Business groups are established by product for each reportable segment within the Nippon Kayaku Group. Each
group develops comprehensive domestic and overseas strategies for the products it handles, and introduces business
activities accordingly.
     In light of this, the Nippon Kayaku Group primarily consists of product- and service-specific segments that are
based on business divisions. “Functional chemicals business,” “Pharmaceuticals business,” and “Safety systems
business” are three reportable segments.
