FY2022 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
Functional Chemicals Business
Functional Materials
   •   Continued strong sales of semiconductor-related epoxy resins, etc., such as for circuit
       boards and encapsulations
   •   In UV-curable resins, strong sales of materials
       for resists and display products
Color Materials
   •   Continued strong demand for                               Epoxy resins
       consumer inkjet printer colorants
   •   Good performance of industrial inkjet inks
       due to recoveries in printing demand and capital investment
       In addition, favorable sales of textile inkjet dyes due to market recovery
   •   Developers for thermal paper and textile dyes improved in the first half, but are still
       mid-recovery
Catalyst
   •   Sales higher than expected due to orders received ahead of schedule
Polatechno
   •   Optical Films business for automobile applications declined from the forecast
       due to the impact of a production decrease caused by a semiconductor shortage
   •   Precision Components business has seen in-vehicle camera applications is affected by
       a semiconductor shortage, but projector components remain solid and X-ray analyzer
       components perform strongly

FY2022 3Q: Business Environments and Topics

Pharmaceuticals Business
  •   PORTRAZZAⓇ, a new biomedicine for lung cancer treatment,
      achieved market penetration
  •   ALAGLIOⓇ, a photodynamic diagnostics agent, started in-house distribution
  •   Good performance for generic anti-cancer drugs and biosimilars
  •   PEMETREXED, antimetabolic malignancies, increase in new adoptions
  •   Start production of TEMOZOLOMIDE at Takasaki Plant, and launched of FOSAPREPITANT

Safety Systems Business
  •   The pace of recovery is slowing from the ongoing impact of
      slowdown in automobile production due to the worldwide semiconductors shortage
  •   Commencement of sales of PARASAFE Ⓡ, a safety device for drones

Others: Agrochemicals
  •   In the agrochemicals business, domestic sales of the soil fumigants, TERONTM and
      ASAHI D-D were firm

Future Initiatives for FY2022
Functional Chemicals Business
  •   In response to rising demand for 5G boards, devices and semiconductors, expansion of
      related materials and semiconductor-related equipment
  •   Focus on expanding industrial inkjet colorants and inks with anticipated growth in
      demand for digital printing
  •   Promoting next period adoption (new and repeat) for new products and further
      improvement of yields and life expectancy catalysts
  •   Expansion of highly durable polarizers for automotive applications, high-power X-ray
      generators, and new sunglasses products

Pharmaceuticals Business
  •   Market penetration of PORTRAZZAⓇ, a new biomedicine for lung cancer treatment
  •   Expansion of biosimilars and generic anti-cancer drugs
  •   Proceed to co-promotion of NUBEQAⓇ Tab.
  •   Commencement of preparations to commercialize a new compound, Darinaparsin,
      introduced from Soladia Pharma K.K.
Safety Systems Business
  •   Respond to customer demand through a stable supply system from five global bases
  •   Expand global market share by leveraging new lightweight inflators

Others: Agrochemicals
  •   Expanding domestic sales of primary products and strengthening the production system
      for thiocyclam products, such as LEAF GUARDⓇ and EVISECTⓇ
