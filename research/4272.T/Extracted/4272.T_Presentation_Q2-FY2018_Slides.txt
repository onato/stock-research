FY 2018 : 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (1)
Functional Chemicals Business
＜Topics＞
 • Demand for epoxy resins was steady due to the continued strength of
   the semiconductor market.
 • Sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications have
   begun to grow.
 • Signs indicate that the business environment for catalysts has
   bottomed out.
 • The Polatechno Group saw steady sales of existing dye-type polarizing
   films.
＜Initiatives for 2H＞
 • Accelerate the global roll-out of dry film for MEMS.
 • Focus on achieving deeper penetration into the market for colorants for
   inkjet printers in industrial applications.
 • Drive development of high yield catalysts.
 • The Polatechno Group will focus on new product growth.


                                                                             9
FY 2018 : 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (2)
Pharmaceuticals Business
＜Topics＞
 • Sales volume of the biosimilar, INFLIXIMAB BS, grew based on stronger
   evidence for switching prescriptions from the original drugs.
 • Generic drugs and long-listed products with differentiation (through
   different dosage forms) saw growth in sales volume.
 • Approval for the sale and manufacture of new generic drugs was
   obtained.
 • The results of a Stage III clinical trial on NK105 for breast cancer were
   announced at the ESMO (European Society for Medical Oncology)
   conference.

＜Initiatives for 2H＞
 • Achieve greater growth in the volume of INFLIXIMAB BS, OXALIPLATIN,
   and other key products sold.
 • Move forward with plan for additional clinical trials of NK105.



                                                                               10
FY 2018 : 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (3)
Safety Systems Business
＜Topics＞
 • Demand for automotive safety components remained steady during the
   first half.
 • The company began preparations for mass production of new types of
   inflators (disk-type, cylinder-type) in FYE March 2019.

＜Initiatives for 2H＞
 • Continue to build a mass production structure to accommodate growth
   in demand.
 • Move ahead with preparations for mass production of new types of
   inflators (disk-type, cylinder-type).
 • Continue to focus on improving quality control and quality control
   systems globally.
Other
＜Initiatives for 2H＞
 • Proceed with preparations for the launch of a new agrochemical
   (insecticide) in the agrochemicals business.
