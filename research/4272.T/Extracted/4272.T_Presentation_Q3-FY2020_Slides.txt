FY 2020 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
 ・ Sales of epoxy resins grew, primarily for use in 5G-capable circuit boards.

 ・ Preparations for maleimide resins are underway to prepare for the
   commencement of 5G commercial services in Japan.

 ・ Sales of colorants for inkjet printers for use in industrial applications expanded,
   while sales for consumer use declined.

 ・ The catalyst business progressed according to plan.

 ・ POLATECHNO CO., LTD. shares were acquired, making it a wholly owned
   subsidiary and we are working to strengthen collaboration.

 ・ The Company concluded an agreement with Henkel AG & Co. KGaA for the
   acquisition of Henkel’s LCD and semiconductor cleaner business.
   (December 24, 2019)

FY 2020 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Pharmaceuticals Business
  <Specialty>
  ・Portrazza®, a new drug for lung cancer treatment, was launched.
  (November 22, 2019)


  <Generic>
  ・Sales of core generic anti-cancer drugs grew.
  ・CAPECITABINE Tab., an anti-cancer drug, and APREPITANT Cap., an anti-emetic
  drug used during chemotherapy were launched. (December 13, 2019)


  <Biosimilars>
  ・INFLIXIMAB BS sales grew as the recognition of biosimilars became more
  widespread.
  ・Sales of TRASTUZUMAB BS grew after the indication mismatch with the
  originator drug was eliminated.

Functional Chemicals Business
