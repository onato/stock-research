FY2017 3Q: Business Environments and Topics
 Functional Chemicals Business
  ＜Functional Materials＞
  ▸Sales of epoxy resins and UV-curable resins were firm. In addition,
   materials for MEMS saw a steady growth, mainly in MicroChem Corp.
  ＜Color Materials＞
  ▸The benefits from cost reduction at the WAC manufacturing plant (in
   China) and the elimination of unprofitable products from the textile
   dye line-up improved profitability despite sluggish sales of colorants
   for inkjet printers in industrial applications.
  ＜Catalysts＞
  ▸There were no major changes in the market environment in China
   and sales were flat year-on-year.
  ＜Polatechno Group＞
  ▸It focused on launching new products in its polarizing film line-up
   amid the severe business environment.

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  ▸Sales of INFLIXIMAB BS have begun to grow, although they did
   not reach the level forecast in the plan.
  ▸We focused on growth of new generic anti-cancer drugs to make
   up for the negative impact of drug price revisions.

 Safety Systems Business
  ▸Steady sales at each of our global locations resulting in sales
   growth.
  ▸Our location in Malaysia (KMY) launched squib production and the
   start-up is proceeding according to plan.

 Other
  ▸The agrochemicals business saw growth on par with last year in
   existing core products.

Future Initiatives for FY 2017
 Corporate
 ▸Endeavor to achieve our goal of maintaining both net sales and
  operating income at the same level as last year.

 Functional Chemicals Business
 ▸Focus on expanding sales of existing core products and products
  in growth areas (adhesives for touch panels, materials for MEMS,
  colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications, and
  materials for thermal paper).

 Pharmaceuticals Business
 ▸Continue to work to expand our share of the INFLIXIMAB BS and
  generic anti-cancer drugs in the markets.

 Safety Systems Business
 ▸Continue to provide a stable supply of good-quality safety
  components that can meet global demand.
