FY 2017: 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (1)
Functional Chemicals Business
Topics
 ・Epoxy resins saw strong sales, mainly overseas.
 ・MicroChem Corp. of the U.S. acquired Paratronix, Inc. and Conductive Compounds Inc. to
  expand the scope of materials for MEMS.
 ・Colorants for inkjet printers saw shrinkage in the market of consumer use, but expansion
  in the market of the industrial applications.
 ・In the catalysts business, there was no change in the operating rate for plants of Chinese
  customers, but global demand for SAP (superabsorbent polymer) and methionine
  expanded.
 ・The Polatechno Group failed to achieve its plan due to the deteriorating business
  environment (yen appreciation and deceleration of the Chinese economy) and stiffening
  competition.

Initiatives for 2H
 ・We will focus on expanding sales of materials for MEMS, adhesives for touch panels and
  colorants for inkjet printers in industrial applications (dye-based and pigment-based), and
  materials for thermal paper.
 ・In the catalysts business, we will work steadily to achieve the plan and acquire new
  customers.
 ・The Polatechno Group will focus on launching new products (dye-type and iodine-type
  polarizing films, and achromatic polarizers).

                                                                                                8
FY 2017: 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (2)

Pharmaceuticals Business
Topics
 ・Launched sales of three new generic anti-cancer drugs (GEMCITABINE I.V. Infusion,
  BICALUTAMIDE OD Tab., and NKS-1 Combination OD Tab.)
 ・Positive trends for INFLIXIMAB BS (biosimilar) which will lead to an increase in volume
  share.
 ・Cultivecs Inc. was established as a joint venture with Mitsubishi Gas Chemical Co., Inc.
 ・Development of polymeric micelle anti-cancer drugs:
  NK012: Received the orphan drug designation in the U.S. for small cell lung cancer
  NK105: Results from a Phase III Study of NK105 administered once a week for
            metastatic or recurrent breast cancer did not meet the prespecified statistical
            criteria of the primary endpoint.

Initiatives for 2H
 ・Minimize the impact of drug price revisions.
   Focus on increasing volume share of INFLIXIMAB BS and generic anti-cancer drugs.
 ・Pursue development of a polymeric micelle anti-cancer drug.

FY 2017: 1H Topics and Initiatives for 2H (3)

Safety Systems Business
Topics
 ・Developed a structure to increase production capacity with ensured quality on a global
  basis.

Initiatives for 2H
 ・Continue to build a global quality assurance system and a structure for a safe and
  increased production.


Strengthen R&D Structure
Topics
 ・We switched to a structure that put each of the research laboratories under the direct
  control of each business group, segmenting research into R&D directly tied to our
  businesses and corporate research focused on the future.

Initiatives for 2H
 ・Improve operations under the new structure.
