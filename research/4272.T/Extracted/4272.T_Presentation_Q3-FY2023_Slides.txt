      FY 2023 3Q Business Environments and Issues
 Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
  •   3Q YTD increased YOY in net sales and income; sales increased in all segments other than Pharmaceuticals
  •   In the 3Q, sales in the Functional Chemicals decreased 7% YOY
  •   Income exceeded due to holding of the same cost of sales ratio, increased in sales and other factors

 Functional Chemicals Business
  •   Epoxy resins slowed due to a decline in demand resulting from the accumulation of inventory in markets
      such as semiconductors
  •   Strong demand for MEMS materials due to next-generation communications devices expansion
  •   Consumer inkjet printer colorants demand fell
  •   Industrial inkjet printer colorants and inks remained firm
  •   Catalysts remained firm as logistics disruptions settled down
  •   The optical film business struggled mainly with automotive applications, although the semiconductor
      shortages are being alleviated
  •   Strong sales of projector materials and X-ray analyzer materials

      FY 2023 3Q Business Environments and Issues

 Pharmaceuticals Business
  •    BEVACIZUMAB BS Intravenous Infusion 100mg「CTNK」・400mg「CTNK」launched in November
  •    Market penetration of DARVIAS® Injection 135mg (generic name: DARINAPARSIN)

 Safety Systems Business
  •    Domestically, demand was sluggish due to reduced automobile production caused by the impact of
       semiconductor shortages and other factors
  •    Overseas business remained steady on the whole, despite the slowdown in the pace of demand
       recovery in some regions

 Agrochemicals
  •    Sales increased due to lively demand prior to the revision of domestic agrochemical prices and strong
       sales to overseas markets

      Business Environments in 4Q and Beyond

 Further normalization after the impact of the COVID-19

 Decline in demand for semiconductors for consumer electronics devices

 Rising tensions in Europe due to Russia’s invasion of Ukraine

 Worldwide inflation due to high fuel and resource prices

 Exchange rates impacts, such as the correction of the yen depreciation

             Initiatives Planned in 4Q and Beyond

 Functional Chemicals Business

  •   Expansion of epoxy resins and maleimide resins for encapsulations and circuit boards
  •   Expansion of dry film resist for RF filters mainly required for 5G communications
  •   Expansion of the cleaner business for semiconductor manufacturing processes
  •   Expansion of resist materials for OLED

  •   Expansion of industrial inkjet inks and production system improvement

  •   Strengthened catalyst evaluation capabilities and technical service systems

  •   Strengthened sales activities and technical services in China to recover automotive optical films
  •   Strengthened component procurement and production system for X-ray analyzer components
