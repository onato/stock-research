Impact from the Spread of the Novel Coronavirus
• Group Initiatives
   – Nippon Kayaku is implementing measures to protect the safety of all of our stakeholders as
     well as our employees and their families. The Central Headquarters for Disaster
     Countermeasures, chaired by the president, is taking the lead on these measures. No
     employee of the Nippon Kayaku Group have been infected, including those at overseas
     locations.
   – Reduce infection risk by switching to online meetings for face-to-face conferences with
     stakeholders.
   – In response to the Japanese government's request to reduce the number of people coming
     into the office by 70%, work from home was promoted and the head office attendance rate
     after the declaration of a state of emergency was suppressed to about 25% in both April
     and May 2020.
   – Secure production site operations to fulfill our responsibility to supply products while
     controlling infection risk.

• Impact on Business Results for the Fiscal Year Ending March 31, 2021
   – The estimated impact has been incorporated into the 1H forecasts disclosed.
   – We intend to disclose full-year forecasts once they become clear.

FY 2020: Business Topics
                                                                              Slide 5/34




 • Functional Chemicals Business
    – Sales of epoxy resins grew, mainly for 5G-capable circuit boards.
    – Maleimide resin having high heat resistance and a low dielectric
      constant was launched and is targeted at the dissemination of 5G
      commercial service in Japan.
    – Sales of colorants for inkjet printers for use in industrial applications
      expanded, while sales for consumer use declined.
    – The catalyst business progressed according to plan.
    – POLATECHNO CO., LTD. was made a wholly owned subsidiary and we
      are working to strengthen collaboration.
    – The Company acquired the LCD and semiconductor cleaner business
      of Henkel AG & Co. KGaA.
FY 2020: Business Topics

 • Pharmaceuticals Business
   – Biosimilars
      • Sales growth for INFLIXIMAB BS and TRASTUZUMAB BS was
        strong.
   – Generic anti-cancer drugs and their relations
      • Sales of core products such as OXALIPLATIN and TEMOZOLOMIDE grew.
      • GEFITINIB was launched. (June 2019)
      • CAPECITABINE and APREPITANT, an anti-emetic drug, were launched.
        (December 2019)
   – Portrazza® biomedicine launched (November 2019)
      • Indication: Unresectable advanced/recurrent squamous cell non-small cell
        lung cancer.
   – Agreement concerning co-promotion of NUBEQA® Tabs
     concluded with Bayer Yakuhin, Ltd.

Future Initiatives for FY 2021
 • Functional Chemicals Business
   – Expand epoxy resins for 5G and maleimide resins further.
   – Expand colorants for inkjet printers for use in industrial applications
     and functional colorants.

     cleaner business.

   – Achieve further growth of biosimilars and generic anti-cancer drugs.

     appropriate use.

     scheduled for completion. (December 2020)
   – Expand the new product line-up.
