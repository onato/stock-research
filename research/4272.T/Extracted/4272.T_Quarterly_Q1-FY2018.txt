1. Qualitative Information Concerning Results for the First Quarter
(1) Analysis of Operating Results
The overall global economy continued its gradual rebound during the first quarter of this consolidated fiscal year
(April 1 to June 30, 2017). The U.S. saw steady growth in personal consumption, and Europe showed signs of a
rebound as exports recovered, despite future uncertainty over the political situation. The economy in China remained
strong, supported by capital investments and other factors. The Japanese economy staged a gradual rebound on the
back of firm personal consumption, capital investments, and other aspects of domestic demand.
     The Nippon Kayaku Group worked to reduce costs even further amid these conditions, addressing key themes
and grappling with mid- and long-term key issues in the new mid-term business plan, “Take a New Step 2016,”
which began from the previous fiscal year, and working to improve profitability in addition to strengthening R&D,
allocating management resources to key businesses in an optimal manner, and expanding overseas business.

This resulted year-on-year growth in sales in the functional chemicals business, the pharmaceuticals business, and
the safety systems business, increasing total net sales for the first quarter of this consolidated fiscal year to 40,880
million yen, an increase of 1,893 million yen (4.9%) over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Growth in net sales boosted operating income to 6,233 million yen, an increase of 1,294 million yen (26.2%)
over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Ordinary income increased to 6,570 million yen owing to a decrease in foreign exchange losses and other
factors, an increase of 3,076 million yen (88.0%) over the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Profit attributable to owners of parent was 4,527 million yen, an increase of 2,126 million yen (88.6%) from
the same period in the previous year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.
[Functional Chemicals Business]
In the functional chemicals business, sales rose to 16,419 million yen, an increase of 243 million yen (1.5%) from
the same period in the previous year.
      Functional materials recorded year-on-year growth in overall sales, boosted by strong sales of epoxy resins
overseas.
      Overall sales in the color materials business increased from the same period in the previous fiscal year. This
growth was due to year-on-year growth in sales of colorants for inkjet printers and developer for thermal paper,
despite a decline in sales of dyes for textiles and paper from the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Sales of catalysts decreased from the same period in the previous fiscal year. This decline was caused by
sluggish sales for catalysts used in the production of acrylic acid and methacrylic acid.
      The Polatechno Group saw an overall increase in sales due to strong demand for dye-type polarizing films,
despite flat demand for ProFlux TM, an inorganic polarizer, and components and materials for projectors.
      Segment profit for the functional chemicals business was 2,152 million yen, an increase of 692 million yen
(47.5%) over same period in the previous fiscal year.

[Pharmaceuticals Business]
In the pharmaceuticals business, sales were 12,200 million yen, an increase of 283 million yen (2.4%) over the same
period in the previous fiscal year.
      Sales of pharmaceutical agents in Japan increased with growth in sales of the biosimilars, INFLIXIMAB BS
for I.V. Infusion (antibody drug) and FILGRASTIM BS Inj. (which reduces the chance of infection in chemotherapy
patients). Sales of pharmaceutical agents in Japan in general were adversely affected by the year-on-year decline in
long-listed products caused by a switch to generic drugs. However, the contribution of new products such as
OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (an anti-cancer drug) resulted in an increase in sales over the same period in
the previous fiscal year.
      Exports saw a year-on-year decline in BLEOs (anti-cancer drugs). However, growth in exports of
ETOPOSIDEs (anti-cancer drugs) and high potency active pharmaceutical ingredients resulted in growth in overall
sales from the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Sales of active pharmaceutical ingredients for the Japanese domestic market and diagnostic agents decreased
from the same period in the previous fiscal year.
      Segment profit for the pharmaceuticals business was 1,971 million yen, an increase of 628 million yen (46.8%)
from the same period in the previous fiscal year.

[Safety Systems Business]
Sales in the safety systems business rose to 10,421 million yen, an increase of 1,383 million yen (15.3%) from the
same period in the previous fiscal year.
     Business in Japan was strong due to year-on-year growth in sales of both airbag inflators and micro gas
generators for seatbelt pretensioners.

      Business overseas was strong in the North American, European, and Chinese markets. An expansion in demand
for automotive safety components in emerging countries also resulted in year-on-year growth in sales of airbag

      Segment profit for the safety systems business was 1,834 million yen, an increase of 101 million yen (5.9%)

[Other]
Sales in the other segment were 1,839 million yen, a decrease of 16 million yen (0.9%) from the same period in the
previous fiscal year.

period in the previous fiscal year.
     Sales for real estate and other businesses were lower than in the first quarter of the previous fiscal year.
     Segment profit in the other segment were 364 million yen, a decrease of 47 million yen (11.4%) from the same
period in the previous fiscal year.

(2) Analysis of Financial Position
Status of Assets, Liabilities, and Net Assets
Total assets were 274,103 million yen, an increase of 1,312 million yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main increases were in inventory assets, which increased by 1,610 million yen; property, plant and
equipment, which increased by 1,542 million yen; investment securities, which increased by 1,539 million yen;
securities, which increased by 1,499 million yen; and notes and accounts receivable-trade, which increased by 805
million yen. The main decreases were in cash and deposits, which decreased by 4,198 million yen; and accounts
receivable-other, which decreased by 1,723 million yen (including other current assets).
      Liabilities were 63,627 million yen, a decrease of 3,296 million yen from the end of the previous consolidated
fiscal year. The main decreases were in income taxes payable, which decreased by 1,564 million yen; long-term
loans payable, which decreased by 1,444 million yen; and accounts payable-other, which decreased by 1,308 million
yen. The main increases were in notes and accounts payable-trade, which increased by 606 million yen; and
short-term loans payable, which increased by 352 million yen.
      Net assets were 210,475 million yen, an increase of 4,609 million yen from the end of the previous
consolidated fiscal year. The main increases were in profit attributable to owners of parent, which increased by
4,527 million yen; translation adjustments, which increased by 2,161 million yen; and unrealized holding gains on
other securities, which increased by 677 million yen. The main decrease was in dividend payments, which decreased
by 3,030 million yen.

(3) Analysis of Forward-looking Statements, Including Consolidated Business Forecasts
In the future, the Nippon Kayaku Group anticipates continued firm growth in the global economy as economic
growth in the U.S. and Europe continues. However, there is concern over a downswing in the global economic
environment due to increasing uncertainty over the future political situation. While a gradual rebound in Japan’s
economy is anticipated as employment and income continue to improve, we need to pay attention to the impact that
a downswing in the global economy would have.
      Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits.
      There has been no change in the forecasts announced on May 10, 2017 for the first half and full-year business
results for the fiscal year ending March 2018.
