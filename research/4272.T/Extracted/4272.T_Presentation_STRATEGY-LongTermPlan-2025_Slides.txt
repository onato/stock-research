                             Measures to improve profit ratios for the next Medium-term Management Plan


                            The short term will see us respond to rising raw material costs and seek greater efficiency.
                            The medium term will see us promote portfolio reform through investment in
                            high-value-adding businesses and products while maintaining existing products.

                                Short-term response measures

                                ⚫ Mobility & Imaging Business Unit, Fine Chemicals Business Unit

                                • Pricing forecasts: Responses to rising raw material costs
                                • Strengthening of activities which see our products’ value recognized
                                • Reaping the benefits of KV25 growth investments: promotion of increased utilization rates and reduced
                                  labor involvement for epoxy resin and industrial inkjet manufacturing equipment

                                ⚫ Life Science Business Unit

                                • Expanded sales of new drugs
                                • Price stability via increased market share through taking measures to supply products discontinued by
