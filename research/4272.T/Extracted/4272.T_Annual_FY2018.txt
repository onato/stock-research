1. Overview of Operating Results, Financial Position, and Future Outlook
(1) Overview of Operating Results for the Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2018
In the consolidated fiscal year under review (April 1, 2017 to March 31, 2018), the global economy as a whole
continued to rebound gradually. The steady growth in consumer spending and capital investment continued in the
U.S., while growth in exports underpinned the sustained rebound in Europe. China continued to see stable growth on
the back of strong domestic demand. The Japanese economy showed signs of a gradual recovery as the employment
and income environment and corporate profits continued to improve, supported by the rebound in the global
economy.
      In the functional chemicals industry, which encompasses functional materials, the market for materials used in
semiconductor memory and electronic devices is expanding with the advent of big data use and the age of IoT.
Digitalization is also progressing in the printing industry with the development of high-speed, precise inkjet printers
for industrial applications. This is increasing the need for various types of color materials for printing. The global
trends in energy and resource conservation are also creating demand for new high-strength, lightweight materials
and highly heat-resistant materials.
      In the pharmaceuticals industry, the generic drug market is growing rapidly due to the government policy of
promoting generic drug use, and other measures, in the face of rapidly rising medical costs. Competition in the
generic drug market has nevertheless, intensified. This partly reflects scale expansion by manufacturers specializing
in generics, and the entry of overseas manufacturers into the market. Biosimilars, the successors to high priced
biopharmaceuticals, are also drawing attention.
      In the automotive industry, demand in Europe remained strong while it faltered in Japan, the U.S., and in
ASEAN and other emerging countries. The number of vehicles sold in China grew substantially.

Amid these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group continued to work to implement the key themes and resolve the
mid- and long-term key issues outlined in “Take a New Step 2016,” the mid-term business plan launched in the
fiscal ended March 31, 2017, during the current fiscal year, which marks the second year of the plan. We worked to
cut costs further to reinforce our profit structure, in addition to focusing on strengthening R&D, optimal allocation
of business resources into core businesses, and expanding our overseas business.

     The above factors led to growth in consolidated net sales to 167,888 million yen for the fiscal year ended
March 31, 2018, an increase of 8,770 million yen (5.5%) year-on-year. This growth resulted from sales growth in the
functional chemicals and safety systems businesses, despite a decline in sales in the pharmaceuticals business.
     Consolidated operating income was 22,606 million yen, an increase of 2,960 million yen (15.1%) year-on-year.
This growth resulted from profit growth in the functional chemicals and pharmaceuticals businesses, which more
than compensated for lower profits in the safety systems business.
     Consolidated ordinary income was 22,471 million yen, an increase of 2,524 million yen (12.7%) over the
previous fiscal year.
     Profit attributable to owners of parent was 15,472 million yen, a decline of 162 million yen (1.0%)
year-on-year.

Performance by business segment is as described below.
 [1] Functional Chemicals Business
Sales reached 67,664 million yen, an increase of 3,634 million yen (5.7%) compared to the previous fiscal year.
     In the functional materials business, sales of epoxy resins for semiconductor encapsulation grew both in Japan
and overseas, and the functional materials business as a whole also saw sales increase over last fiscal year.
     The color materials business saw year-on-year growth in sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial
applications. This resulted in year-on-year growth in sales for the color materials business as a whole.
     Catalyst business sales outpaced last fiscal year due to strong overseas sales.
     The Polatechno Group achieved sales growth over the previous fiscal year due to strong sales of dye-type
polarizing films and components for X-ray analysis systems.
     Segment profit totaled 8,610 million yen, an increase of 1,824 million yen (26.9%) year-on-year.

 [2] Pharmaceuticals Business

such as OXALIPLATIN for I.V. Infusion NK (anti-cancer drug), TEMOZOLOMIDE Tab. (anti-cancer drug),
despite the year-on-year decline in long-listed products caused by a switch to generic drugs.

(2) Overview of Financial Position for the Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2018
Total assets were 287,565 million yen, an increase of 14,774 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year.

decrease of 1,540 million yen; accounts payable-other, a decrease of 1,088 million yen; and income taxes payable, a
decrease of 1,075 million yen.
      Net assets were 220,602 million yen, an increase of 14,736 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year.

yen; and non-controlling interests, an increase of 483 million yen. The main decrease was in dividends paid, a

(4) Future Outlook
We see continued firm growth in the U.S. and are concerned about an economic downswing in Europe due to
increased geopolitical risk as factors affecting the future business environment surrounding the Nippon Kayaku
Group. Stable growth is expected in China, underpinned by strong demand. Although the recovery of the Japanese
economy is expected to continue as the economy benefits from various governmental policies in addition to
continued improvement in employment and income levels, we need to keep a cautious eye on the impact that a
downswing in the global economy would have.
      Under these conditions, the Nippon Kayaku Group aims to construct a solid financial structure which flexibly
adapts to changes in the business environment, as well as expand existing businesses in global growth markets,
accelerate the development of new businesses and new products, and enhance profits. The strategy for each business
segment is described below.
      • In the functional chemicals business, we will contribute to the creation of an “ultra-smart society” by
providing functional chemical materials that offer special characteristics for the fields of information and
communication, and energy and resource conservation. Our core resin, colorant and catalyst technologies will
continue to deliver value by addressing market opportunities and global needs.
      • In the pharmaceuticals business, we will expand our business by concentrating on achieving market
superiority in the three key domains of research and development of polymeric micelle anti-cancer drugs,
establishing a business base and solid position in the biosimilars market, and expanding our product line-up in the
cancer-related field.
      • In the safety systems business, we will work to expand our business as a global business with operations in
five regions: Japan, North America, Europe, China, and ASEAN.

(5) Basic Policy Concerning Profit Dividends and Dividends for the Fiscal Year Ended March 31, 2018
    and Fiscal Year Ending March 31, 2019
The Nippon Kayaku Group focuses heavily on returning profits to shareholders. A medium-term payout ratio has
been set at roughly 40% of profit attributable to owners of parent. This takes into account stable and consistent profit
returns and the level of retained earnings. Retained earnings are to be allocated for capital investment and R&D
investment in growing businesses to enhance the value of the Nippon Kayaku Group.
      The year-end dividend for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2018 is expected to be 15.0 yen per share.
Combined with the dividend paid at the end of the second-quarter, total dividends per share for the full year will be
30 yen.
      We expect to maintain the same dividend level for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2019: 15 yen per share for
the second quarter dividend and 15 yen per share for the year-end dividend, for a total annual dividend of 30 yen per
share.

2. Basic Stance on Selection of Accounting Principles
The Nippon Kayaku Group, for the time being, employs generally accepted accounting principles in Japan to
prepare its consolidated financial statements. This decision takes into account comparability of different fiscal
periods in the consolidated financial statements and with industry peers.
     It should be noted that the Company plans to adopt the use of the International Financial Reporting Standards
(IFRS) in a timely and adequate fashion should certain circumstances in Japan and abroad give rise to this need.

Segment Information
[1] Summary of reportable segments
The reportable segments of the Nippon Kayaku Group are structural units of the Group for which separate financial
information can be acquired. These segments are subject to be examined periodically by the Board of Directors in
order to evaluate business results and make decisions on the allocation of business resources.
     Business groups are established by product for each reportable segment within the Nippon Kayaku Group. Each
group develops comprehensive domestic and overseas strategies for the products it handles, and introduces business
activities accordingly.
     In light of this, the Nippon Kayaku Group primarily consists of product- and service-specific segments that are
based on business divisions. “Functional chemicals business,” “Pharmaceuticals business,” and “Safety systems
business” are three reportable segments.
