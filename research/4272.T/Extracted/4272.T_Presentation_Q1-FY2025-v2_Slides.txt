  Factors behind the Forecast Revision in Operating income
Mobility & Imaging                   +2.9 billion yen (+31.8%）in operating income by segment
 ・Continuing robustness of automotive market
    (Safety Systems; automotive safety parts, Polatechno; automotive components)
 ・Improved profitability by altering the mix of Polatechno products, improving yields, and increasing sales
 ・The 2H saw Safety Systems invested in China and Malaysia, and increased depreciation and labor costs in
  preparation for the start of line operations
Fine Chemicals                       +2.4 billion yen (+40.2%) in operating income by segment
 ・Recovery in semiconductor-related market conditions and improvement in operating rates
     (epoxy resins, semiconductor production equipment, etc. )
 ・Elimination of inventory adjustments in consumer inkjet colorants, and continued solid in industrial inkjet inks
 ・Delay risks in the order period for Catalysts

Life Science                         +0.2 billion yen (+3.5%) in operating income by segment
 ・Generally in line with initial forecasts
                                  * Impact of exchange rate differences (1Q) +0.6 billion yen
