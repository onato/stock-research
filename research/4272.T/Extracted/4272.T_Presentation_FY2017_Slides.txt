FY 2017 Business Environments and Topics (1)
Functional Chemicals Business:
                        Decrease in sales, increase in operating profit

 • Functional Materials: The resin business remained strong on the back of firm
                         semiconductor market prices. The growth field, materials for
                         MEMS, saw growth and MicroChem Corp. acquired two
                         companies, enabling it to expand its sales channels.

 • Color Materials:     The elimination of unprofitable product from dyes for
                        textiles improved the profit margin.

 • Catalysts:           The state of oversupply of acrylic acid in China is gradually
                        being brought under control.

 • Polatechno Group:    Changes in the market have exacerbated the competition
                        faced by existing core products.

                                                                                        4
FY 2017 Business Environments and Topics (2)
Pharmaceuticals Business: Decrease in sales and operating profit
 • The increase in sales volume did not sufficiently compensate for the influence of
   revisions to drug prices. This resulted in a decrease in both sales and profits.
 • Differentiation of new generic anti-cancer drugs by launching drugs in different
   dosage forms boosted growth.
 • Publication of international data coupled with an increasing need in society
   began to increase the market penetration of INFLIXIMAB BS.
 • Cultivecs Inc., a joint venture with Mitsubishi Gas Chemical Company Inc., was
   established to prepare for manufacture of biosimilars and other biomedicines in
   Japan.
Safety Systems Business: Increase in sales and operating profit
 • The Safety Systems business saw steady growth on the back of growth in
   automobile production.
 • We focused on strengthening quality assurance systems and building a structure
   for safe, increased production worldwide.
Other: Increase in sales and operating profit
 • Plans for launching the new agrochemical, ANM138, next fiscal year were
   solidified in the Agrochemicals Business.

                                                                                       5
          Future Initiatives for FY 2018 (1)
Functional Chemicals Business

 • Functional Materials: Take advantage of the good opportunity presented by the
                         high functionality of information and communication device
                         and smart home electronics to focus on expanding sales of
                         epoxy resins.
                         Also, focus on new adoption for materials for MEMS and
                         adhesives for touch panels.

 • Color Materials:    Expand sales of colorants for inkjet printers in industrial
                       applications and materials for thermal paper.

 • Catalysts:          Launch new higher-yield catalysts, which lead to orders.

 • Polatechno Group:   Promote the launch of new products.




                                                                                      6
         Future Initiatives for FY 2018 (2)

Pharmaceuticals Business
 • Achieve sales growth in biosimilars (INFLIXIMAB BS, FILGRASTIM BS), and
   generic anti-cancer drugs.
 • Keep development of new biosimilars (CT-P6, CT-P10) on schedule.
 • Move forward with development of polymeric micelle anti-cancer drugs (NK105,
   NK012).
Safety Systems Business
 • Continue to increase production capacity to meet demand.
 • Further strengthen quality assurance systems and safe production systems
   worldwide.

Other
 • Make certain that the new agrochemical, ANM138, is launched for sale in the
   fourth quarter.
