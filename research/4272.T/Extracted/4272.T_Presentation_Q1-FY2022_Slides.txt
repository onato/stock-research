FY 2022 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
 Net sales and income increased year-on-year,
 and business forecasts revised upward
 ・ The progress ratio were 55% for net sales, 86% for operating income by industry
  segment, 112% for operating income, 118% for profit (owners of parent) compared
  to forecasts announced on May 11, 2021
 ・ Gross profit increased due to robust sales of Functional Chemicals business and
  others and cost reductions resulting from an increase in the capacity utilization rate
 ・ Profits increased due to restraint selling, general and administrative expenses

 Decision to acquire of Treasury Stock (Announced on May 21, 2021)
 ・ Total number of shares to be acquired: 3.1 million shares (up to)
 ・ Total acquisition cost: 3.0 billion yen (up to)

FY 2022 1Q: Business Environments and Topics

Functional Chemicals Business
 Functional Materials Business
 ・ Epoxy resins and other products used in semiconductor-related industries continue
  to perform well
 ・ Sales of UV curable resins for tablets and notebook PCs were strong
 ・ Sales of LCD and semiconductor cleaners were strong for semiconductors, such as
  PCs and mobile devices, and LCDs for TVs
 Color Materials Business
 ・ Strong demand for inkjet colorants for consumer use
 ・ Recovery in inkjet inks used in industrial applications, dyes for textile inkjet,
  developer for thermal paper
 Catalyst Business
 ・ Both domestic and overseas sales were strong, partly due to unplanned orders
 Polatechno Business
 ・ Optical Film business was strong, mainly for automotive applications
 ・ In Precision Components business, demand recovered in the projector market and
  for components for X-ray analyzers

Pharmaceuticals Business
 ・ ALAGLIO®, a photodynamic diagnostic agent, was decided to introduce, and
  promotions started on June 1
 ・ PORTRAZZA®, a new biomedicine for lung cancer treatment, achieved market
  penetration
 ・ Biosimilars: INFLIXIMAB BS and TRASTUZUMAB BS grew
 ・ Generic anti-cancer drug: PEMETREXED, antimetabolite anti-cancer drug listed
  on the NHI price
Safety Systems Business
 ・ Strong performance despite an impact on sales due to instability in automobile
  production affected by the global shortage of semiconductors
 ・ Impact of lockdown in Malaysia covers orders received by local subsidiaries
  within the Group
Others: Agrochemicals Business
 ・Solid domestic sales of LEAFGUARD®, an insecticide,
  and TERONTM and ASAHI DD, fumigants

Functional Chemicals Business
 Functional Materials Business
 ・ For rising demand for 5G circuit board, devices and semiconductors, expand
  related materials such as epoxy resins, maleimide resins, MEMS materials,
  and semiconductor cleaners

 Color Materials Business
 ・ Focus on expanding inkjet colorants and inks for industrial applications with

 Catalyst Business
 ・ Promote adoption of next term (new and repeat) by applying new products and

 Polatechno Business
