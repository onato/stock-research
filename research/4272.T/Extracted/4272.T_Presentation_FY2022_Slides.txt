      FY 2022 Business Environments and Topics
 Summary of Nippon Kayaku Group
  •   YOY increase in net sales and income                 Net sales hit a record high
  •   Operating income increased due to controlled selling, general and administrative expenses and
      exchange rate impact

 Functional Chemicals Business
  •   Continued strong sales of semiconductor-related epoxy resins, etc., for circuit boards and encapsulations
  •   Strong demand for semiconductors in MEMS materials and cleaners businesses
  •   Consumer inkjet printer colorants remained steady
  •   Industrial inkjet inks performed well in proportion to the recovery in printing demand
  •   Catalysts received orders ahead of schedule, but largely in line with plans
  •   More optical films were used in automobiles than in 2Q because of the semiconductors shortage
  •   X-ray analyzer materials sales were strong, and projector material sales recovered

      FY 2022 Business Environments and Topics
 Pharmaceuticals Business
  •   PORTRAZZA®, a biomedicine for lung cancer treatment, achieved market penetration and saw increased
      adoptions
  •   ALAGLIO®, a photodynamic diagnostics agent, started in-house distribution
  •   PEMETREXED, antimetabolic malignancies (launched in July) saw an increase in new adoptions
  •   FOSAPREPITANT (antiemetic agent) launched in December, BORTEZOMIB (antineoplastic agent) in March

 Safety Systems Business
  •   In addition to the worldwide supply shortage of automotive components, geopolitical risks have
      materialized, resulting in supply chain instability and a slowdown in the pace of demand recovery
  •   Launch of PARASAFE®, a safety device for industrial drones; usage in demonstration trials

 Agrochemicals, Others
  •   In the Agrochemicals business, sales increased, driven by domestic sales of TERONTM and Asahi D-D,
      soil fumigant

 Enlargement and Strengthening of Corporate Governance (G)
   •    Change to a system of 3 independent outside directors from a total of 9 (1/3 of directors are from outside) (June)
   •    New appointment of female Audit & Supervisory Board Members (June)
   •    New restricted stock compensation system for executive directors introduced (June)
   •    Revision of the Basin Policy on Corporate Governance (the amended Corporate Governance Code) and the
        principles to be disclosed are done so in the Corporate Governance Report (November)
   •    Disclosure of Skill Matrix for Board Members, Audit & Supervisory Board Members, and Executive Directors
        (November)
 Compliance (G)
   •    Re-established Nippon Kayaku Group Anti-Bribery Basic Policy and clarified relevant rules (June)
 Assessment with Business Partners (S)
   •    Revision of the CSR Procurement Guidebook (formulation and description of a procurement policy for conflict
        minerals) (November)
   •    Signed up to the UN Global Compact (September)

                              ESG: FY 2023 Topics
Progress on the start of the Mid-term Business Plan (KV25 ) on April 1st, 2022
 Establishment of Sustainable Management Basic Policy
  •   Under our KAYAKU spirit, ensure management transparency and fairness, and practice sustainable
      management that repays the confidence of all stakeholders by contributing to the realization of a
      sustainable environment and society through business activities
 Establishment of Nippon Kayaku Group Human Rights Policy
  •   Established the Nippon Kayaku Group Human Rights Policy based on the UN Guiding Principles on
      Business and Human Rights, with the aim of promoting initiatives to respect human rights across the
      entire Group
  •   Fostering a corporate culture that respects human rights, aiming to be a company that can command
      the trust of all stakeholders and provide happiness and delight
 Endorsement of the recommendation of the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial
 Disclosures “TCFD”
  •   Disclose information on risks and opportunities related to climate change, their financial impact, and
      initiatives to reduce greenhouse gas emissions and build a recycling-oriented society in accordance
      with TCFD recommendations
