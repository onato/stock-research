⚠

Audit Disclaimer of Opinion — Second Consecutive Year — Read Before Proceeding

Grant Thornton New Zealand issued a full DISCLAIMER OF OPINION on the FY2026 (year ended 31 March 2026) audited financial statements — a materially more severe outcome than a standard going-concern emphasis of matter, meaning the auditor could not obtain sufficient evidence to form any opinion on shareholder support or right-of-use asset recoverability. This follows William Buck's disclaimer on FY2025 — two straight years.

AFC survives only on non-binding, discretionary support from its controlling shareholder, NZ Silveray Group Limited (NZSG) — simultaneously the company's largest creditor (NZ$1,170,427 in related-party loans at 10.08%-13.70% interest) and, via related parties, the source of 97% of FY2026 revenue and 99.7% of receivables. Unaudited H1-2026 equity was already negative (-NZ$120,066) before a year-end NZ$780,908 related-party debt-forgiveness injection restored full-year equity to +NZ$206,390.

Revenue collapsed 78% year-on-year to NZ$161,285. The stock is mechanically priced at the NZX minimum tick of NZ$0.001 — a market-structure price floor unchanged since 2017 — roughly 7.4x the probability-weighted intrinsic value of NZ$0.000136/share. This is not a conventional value or income investment; it is a binary, speculative bet on a pre-MOU reverse takeover with "Fast Track Group" completing on favourable terms. See the Model Approach warning above the DCF section.

                Model Approach: Asset/Scenario Shell Valuation, Not a Growth DCF

                A standard perpetuity-growth DCF tripped the sanity check for AFC.NZ and was replaced with an asset/scenario-based shell valuation. AFC has never generated repeatable positive owner free cash flow post-2020 (positive in exactly one of the last seven years, an immaterial FY2023 working-capital blip), revenue has collapsed 78% year-on-year to a seven-year low, and the FY2026 audit carries a full disclaimer of opinion for the second consecutive year — there is no genuine cash-flow stream to project or discount.

                Instead, equity value is modelled as a probability-weighted mix of three scenarios: Bear (55% weight) — insolvency / wind-up once NZSG's discretionary support is withdrawn, ordinary equity holders wiped out (NZ$0.0000/share); Base (35% weight) — shell persists on NZSG life support, valued at tangible book equity plus a modest listed-shell scarcity premium (NZ$0.000193/share); Bull (10% weight) — the pre-MOU Fast Track Group reverse takeover (RTO) completes and the clean listing re-rates toward the top of NZX shell-value precedent (NZ$0.000682/share).

                At the current NZ$0.001 minimum tick, implied Price/Book is approximately 17.7x FY2026 tangible equity. Every scenario — including the optimistic bull case — values the stock below the NZ$0.001 minimum tradeable tick. An income or value investment case does not exist at any currently tradeable price. To the extent AFC.NZ has any speculative appeal, it is purely as an option on the Fast Track Group reverse takeover completing on favourable terms — a binary, unpriced, pre-MOU event with no disclosed structure, exchange ratio, or timeline.

                • Market price (NZ$0.001) is at the NZX minimum tick and has not moved materially since 2017 — it cannot mechanically fall further to reflect deteriorating fundamentals.
                • Implied P/B of ~17.7x at the price floor is far above what any of the three fundamentals-based scenarios support (bear 0x, base ~3.4x, bull ~12.1x book).
