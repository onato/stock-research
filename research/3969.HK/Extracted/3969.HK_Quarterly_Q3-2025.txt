Reasons for selecting this non-GAAP financial indicator

The main reason for the Company to select to exclude the net increase in customer
deposits and interbank deposits from net cash flow from operating activities is that
the net increase in customer deposits and interbank deposits reflects the changes in
deposits taken by the Company’s controlled subsidiary, Finance Company, from its
controlling shareholder, CRSC Group, and its subsidiaries. By excluding the impact
of this item, the figure can more accurately represent the net cash flow from operating
activities generated by the Company’s core businesses, and provide investors with a
clearer understanding of the structure of the Company’s cash inflow from operating
activities.
