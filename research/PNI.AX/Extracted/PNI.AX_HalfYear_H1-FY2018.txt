                        Review of operations

                        Profit attributable to shareholders for the half-year was $8.1 million (5.0 cents per share), compared with $2.8
                        million (2.0 cents per share) for the prior corresponding period. The Group’s continuing operations produced profit
                        attributable to shareholders of $8.1 million (5.0 cents per share), compared to $3.0 million (2.1 cents per share) for
                        the prior corresponding period.

                        While income tax was paid at the Affiliate level, there was no income tax expense at the Group level for both the
                        half-year and the prior corresponding period. Hence profit before tax and profit after tax are the same. The
                        Group’s deferred tax assets were de-recognised at 30 June 2015.
                        A fully franked interim dividend of 4.6 cents has been declared and will be payable on 23 March 2018 to
                        shareholders registered on the record date of 9 March 2018.

                        Funds under management (FUM) increased by 21.9% during the half-year from $26.5 billion at 30 June 2017 to
                        $32.3 billion at 31 December 2017.

                        There was no profit or loss derived from discontinued operations during the period, which relates to the Wilsons
                        Advisory Securities business that was sold on 1 July 2015. In the prior corresponding period discontinued
                        operations incurred a loss of $0.2 million.
                        During the half-year dividends totaling $7.7 million have been paid to shareholders by the Company.
                        The Group continued to have no corporate borrowings at 31 December 2017.
                        During the prior corresponding period, on 25 August 2016 the Group acquired the 24.99% of Pinnacle Investment
                        Management Limited (PIML) that it did not already own, thus becoming a pure listed funds management business
                        benefitting from access to 100% of PIML’s cash flows and increased efficiencies from consolidation of businesses
                        within the Group. The prior period result therefore includes a share of profit attributable to non-controlling
                        interests for the period 1 July 2016 to 25 August 2016 of $0.2 million, with no equivalent amount in the current
                        period.

                        Matters subsequent to the end of the reporting period
                        In the interval between the end of the half year and the date of this directors’ report there has not arisen any item,
                        transaction or event of a material or unusual nature likely, in the opinion of the directors of the Company, to
                        significantly affect the :

                                Group’s operations in future financial years; or

                                Results of those operations in future financial years; or

                                Group’s state of affairs in future financial years.

                        (b) Valuation techniques used to determine fair values

                        The fair value of Australian listed securities and unlisted unit trusts is based on quoted market prices at the end of

                        market price used for unlisted unit trusts is the current exit unit price. These instruments are included in level 1.

                        The fair value of unlisted equity securities and contingent consideration from disposal of discontinued operation is
                        determined using valuation techniques. The Group uses a variety of methods and makes assumptions that are
                        based on market conditions existing at the end of each reporting period. In the circumstances where a valuation
                        technique for these instruments is based on significant unobservable inputs, such instruments are included in level
                        3.

                        The carrying amounts of cash and cash equivalents, trade receivables and payables, loans to entities under joint
                        control, loans to shareholders and other current liabilities are assumed to approximate their fair values due to their
                        short-term nature. The fair value of financial liabilities for disclosure purposes is estimated by discounting the
                        future contractual cash flows at the current market interest rate that is available to the Group for similar financial
                        instruments.
