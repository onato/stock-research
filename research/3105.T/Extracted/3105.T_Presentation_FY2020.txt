                                         What is an automatic operation-compatible simulator?
                                          In the development of automatic driving vehicles, it is
                                         necessary to develop assuming all road conditions.
                                          In the development and evaluation in the real
                                         environment, enormous cost and time are required, and
                                         the utilization of the simulator is very effective for
                                         shortening of the development time and cost reduction.
                                          Especially, the utilization of the simulator becomes
                                         indispensable in order to evaluate reliability and safety
                                         under dangerous situations.
