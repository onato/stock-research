Efforts in FY2019

◆Acquired all shares of NJ Components（July 2019）
  Nisshinbo has taken over business related to coils and
transformers from FDK Corporation and aims to
strengthen the power supply business for xEV, which are
expected to rapidly grow. Nagano Japan Radio plans to
generate sales JPY10.0bn in 2025 with the corporation of
NJ Components.
