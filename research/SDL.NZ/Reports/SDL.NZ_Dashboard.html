Company Overview

Solution Dynamics Limited (SDL) is a New Zealand-headquartered customer communications management (CCM) company operating in New Zealand, North America, and the UK. It provides cloud software platforms, professional services, and managed mailhouse operations helping organisations compose, personalise, and distribute communications across print and digital channels.

The company is transitioning from a predominantly print-and-mailhouse model toward recurring SaaS and AI-enabled digital communications software, though the NZ mailhouse still anchors the business.

Revenue Streams

Software & Technology (~40% H1 FY2026)
International CCM cloud platform — SaaS subscriptions (DMC, Composer, Campaign+), per-document fees, professional services. Clients include Pitney Bowes (US/UK/France/Japan) and UK dental groups. Pure software margin is high.

Digital Print & Document Handling (~13%)
NZ mailhouse — high-speed printing of invoices, statements, and rates notices for councils, utilities, and financial services. Structurally declining due to digital shift.

Outsourced Services / Postage (~48%)
Pass-through of NZ Post bulk postage, paper, envelopes, freight. Revenue inflating from NZ Post price rises (+15% July 2024, +26% July 2025) without proportional earnings uplift.

Competitive Position

Main competitors: OpenText (Exstream), Quadient (Inspire), Precisely (EngageOne), Messagepoint, Pitney Bowes (partner/competitor hybrid)

Moat factors:

  • Deep integration into enterprise billing/data workflows — high switching costs
  • Dominant position in NZ council and utility communications
  • Global distributed print network (Jupiter) across 50+ countries
  • ISO 27001:2022 and in-progress SOC2 certifications
  • Pitney Bowes channel partnership — click-based SaaS growing strongly
  • nGAGE: AI-native dental practice marketing SaaS (early-stage optionality)

Growth Drivers

  • NZ Local Water Done Well reforms — mandatory council water entity separation creates multi-year pipeline of new billing clients
  • nGAGE UK dental market expansion — two corporate dental groups (58+ sites) signed; scalable SaaS product in underserved niche
  • Pitney Bowes click-based SaaS channel growing strongly despite overall international decline
  • NZ market consolidation — major competitor exiting, displaced customers available
  • Digital transition within existing NZ customers — deepening per-customer platform dependency
  • GRI Marketing acquisition (Jan 2025) adds creative services for North American enterprise clients
  • AI-enabled features across DMC, Composer, Campaign+ platforms
  • Australian council expansion via local print partner

Key Risks

  • [HIGH] Customer concentration — top-5 customers = ~46% of revenue; loss of one already proved catastrophic
  • [HIGH] Accelerating physical mail decline — NZ Post up 44% in two years, pushing enterprise clients digital
  • [HIGH] nGAGE execution risk — early-stage, unproven at scale, well-funded competitors possible
  • [HIGH] CEO vacancy — Acting CEO Susie Watts since Jan 2026; Patrick Brand departed due to family medical circumstances
  • [MOD] Pitney Bowes channel dependency — SDL cannot fully control partner arrangements
  • [MOD] FX exposure — ~52% of FY2025 revenue in USD/GBP, no disclosed hedging
  • [MOD] Single-site NZ production facility operational risk
  • [MOD] Micro-cap illiquidity — ~14.7M shares, very thin NZX trading

Bull Case

  • Net cash NZ$8.36M (~NZ$0.57/share) — market near liquidation value; shareholders essentially getting operating business free
  • nGAGE is a genuinely differentiated AI-native SaaS in an underserved UK dental niche — 1,200+ dental corporate groups addressable
  • Local Water Done Well reforms — structural multi-year pipeline of new NZ government billing clients
  • NZ competitor exit creating market share opportunity in a consolidating mailhouse market
  • Pitney Bowes SaaS channel growing independently and profitably
  • Cost base substantially right-sized — SG&A down 26.6% in H1 FY2026; operating leverage from any revenue recovery
  • Active buyback programme (up to NZ$0.5M) signals Board confidence; buybacks below cash backing per share

Bear Case

  • FY2026 collapse demonstrates extreme earnings fragility — a single customer destroyed two years of profit
  • nGAGE unproven: SDL's R&D budget (~NZ$0.9M/yr) is modest; Dentally could build native marketing tools
  • Structural mail decline accelerating beyond SDL's ability to compensate — digital items earn far less per-unit
  • ~351 shareholders on record, very thin daily trading — meaningful entry or exit moves the market
  • Acting CEO during critical reinvention period — execution continuity risk is elevated
  • Value trap risk: if operating cash flow stays near zero, the net-cash buffer erodes over time

Model approach: Solution Dynamics is a net-cash micro-cap in the middle of a structural earnings reset, so the valuation hinges entirely on the NORMALIZED free cash flow of the business AFTER the loss of its largest international (aid-funded NGO) customer — not on the elevated FY2023-FY2025 results that included that customer, and not on the depressed FY2026 trough year distorted by one-off restructuring. We anchor the base case on a normalized ~NZ$2.0M FCF (vs a 9-year median of ~NZ$2.8M and a FY2026 trough near zero). Net cash of ~NZ$8.4M is ~NZ$0.57/share — almost the entire current share price — so the market at NZ$0.58 is effectively assigning close to zero value to the operating business. The large apparent upside (~160% base case) is a function of the market pricing near liquidation value while the model assumes the remaining business survives and stabilizes; this gap reflects genuine survival/execution uncertainty, which is loaded into a deliberately high ~17% WACC rather than into the cash flows. This is a deep-value / asset-backed situation, NOT a multi-bagger growth thesis.
