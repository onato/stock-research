Consolidated Historical Financials (includes divested NZFL through H1-2026)

Structural break at H1-2026: On 1 December 2025 Allied sold NZ Farmers Livestock (NZFL, ~85% of historical consolidated revenue) to Rural Livestock Limited. Every chart below through H1-2026 is TOTAL GROUP (continuing + discontinued) — the dashed marker on time-series charts flags the H1-2026 period as the last one containing NZFL. H1-2026's $17.599M revenue = $1.889M continuing (NZRLM + Parent Operations) + $15.710M discontinued (NZFL, pre-sale stub period). Go-forward continuing revenue is roughly $1.9M per half (~$3.8M/yr), a ~85% step-down from the consolidated base shown here. H1-2026's $3.817M net income / 21.7% net margin / 13.25c EPS are NOT run-rate operating performance — they include a $2.740M one-off income tax benefit and $1.319M of discontinued-operations profit (itself including a $2.110M gain on the NZFL sale); continuing operations alone posted a PRE-TAX LOSS of $0.517M for the half. H1-2024 and H1-2025 interim rows are not present in the source filings/CSV. Pre-FY2020 share counts (105-178m for FY2014-FY2019, 17.855M FY2020, 28.807M from FY2021) span a 10:1 share consolidation effective December 2019 — per-share figures before FY2020 are not directly comparable to later years.
