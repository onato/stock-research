      Regarding the operating environment for the Yokohama Rubber Group in the first three quarters of fiscal
   2024 (January 1, 2024 – September 30, 2024), the environment in Japan was a somewhat mixed bag.
   Conditions remained favorable for the electrical machinery industry, which is benefiting from expanding global
   demand for high-tech machinery. The restaurant, accommodations, and retail industries benefited from
   continued strong inbound demand and progress in passing on price increases, and the
   information/communications and logistics industries saw earnings supported by the trend toward digitalization.
   Meanwhile, some manufacturers, including automakers, were somewhat adversely affected by large typhoons
   that forced them to temporarily suspend operations at some plants. Overall corporate business sentiment,
   however, has remained positive.
      In overseas markets, personal consumption in the United States remained strong, supported by solid
   employment and income environments and the strong stock market’s positive impact on individuals’ assets.
   However, corporate activity, especially at manufacturers, remains in a lengthy adjustment phase owing to the
   tight financial environment. In Europe, a sluggish German economy is weighing on the recovery in the
   eurozone economy, depressing business sentiment. In China, exports are rebounding, but a full-fledged
   economic recovery continues to be hampered by sluggish domestic demand and a continuing real estate
   recession.
      In the above operating environment, the Yokohama Rubber Group continued to pursue the “exploitation” of
   the strengths of its existing businesses and the “exploration” of new value while launching its medium-term
   management plan Yokohama Transformation 2026 (YX2026), which aims to complete the transformation
   begun during the previous medium-term plan. As a result, the Yokohama Rubber Group’s consolidated results
   for the first three quarters of fiscal 2024 included sales revenue of ¥782,906 million (+13.1% YoY), business
   profit of ¥83,411 million (+69.6% YoY), operating profit of ¥85,772 million (+63.8% YoY), and profit
   attributable to owners of the parent totaling ¥60,841 million (+36.5% YoY).
      Tire segment sales revenue totaled ¥701,243 million (+14.4% YoY), accounting for 89.6% of the Yokohama
   Rubber Group’s consolidated sales revenue.
      Original equipment (OE) tire sales revenue was higher than in the same period of the previous year as the
   impact from reductions in domestic production at some Japanese automakers eased, strong sales of vehicle

                                                                 2
    models equipped with YOKOHAMA tires, and outweighed the negative impact from continued weakness in
    Japanese automakers’ sales in China.
       Replacement tire sales revenue also was higher than a year earlier, as increased domestic sales boosted by
    aggressive marketing supplemented overseas sales increases in Europe, India, and other Asian markets, and a
    positive forex impact from the yen’s depreciation.
       The Yokohama Rubber Group’s off-highway tire (OHT) business sales revenue increased year on year, as
    higher sales in Europe, Asia, and the Middle East at the Group’s legacy OHT business under YOHT
    (Yokohama Off-Highway Tires, formerly the Alliance Tire Group or ATG) was supplemented by the addition of
    sales at Y-TWS (formerly Trelleborg Wheel Systems Holding AB or TWS), which was acquired in May 2023.
       MB (Multiple Businesses) segment sales revenue totaled ¥74,828 million (+2.6% YoY), accounting for 9.6%
    of Group consolidated sales revenue.
       The hose & couplings business sales revenue was lower than a year earlier owing to sluggish demand for
    automotive hoses as well as hydraulic hoses for construction equipment and other applications.
       The industrial products business sales revenue increased year on year. Sales of conveyor belts were largely
    flat, but sales of marine products and aerospace products were strong.
       The strong year-on-year growth in Group business profit reflects successful efforts to increase sales volume,
    raise product prices, and improve product MIX supplemented by a full contribution from Y-TWS over the
    entire period and the positive forex impact from the yen’s depreciation.

(2) Consolidated Financial Position

      Total assets as of September 30, 2024, were ¥1,619,718 million, an increase of ¥19,260 million from the end
    of the previous consolidated fiscal year (December 31, 2023). The increase primarily reflects increases in
    inventories and property, plant and equipment.
      Total liabilities as of September 30, 2024, were ¥804,606 million, a decrease of ¥47,057 million from the end
    of the previous consolidated fiscal year. The decrease in liabilities primarily reflects a reduction in interest-
    bearing debt.
      Total equity as of September 30, 2024, came to ¥815,112 million, an increase of ¥66,317 million from the
    end of the previous consolidated fiscal year. The increase primarily reflects the recording of profit attributable
    to owners of the parent.

(1) Outline of Reportable Segments
       The Group’s business segments are organizational units for which the Group is able to obtain discrete
    financial information in order for the Company’s Board of Directors to regularly review performance to
