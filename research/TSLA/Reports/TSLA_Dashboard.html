Model approach: The sanity check flagged the first build (9.5% WACC) for an implied P/E of 417.9x against a 10-year average of 179.3x. Most of that gap is a denominator effect -- SBC-adjusted trailing EPS is a few tens of cents on a 3.3% operating margin, so almost any positive franchise value reads as an extreme multiple -- but part of it was a real model gap: a 9.5% rate built on a 1.05 beta priced an average-risk equity, not a business whose operating income has fallen roughly 4x in three years and whose recovery leans on a regulatory approval (unsupervised autonomy) it does not control. The one-shot fix adds +1.0% for that cyclicality and +1.0% for the regulatory single-point-of-failure risk, taking WACC to 11.5% across all three scenarios -- growth, margins, capex and probability weights were left unchanged. That moves weighted intrinsic value from $158.09 to $111.49 (bear $16.42 / base $81.46 / bull $266.62) against a market price near $390: even the bull case, at the risk-adjusted discount rate, sits roughly a third below where the stock trades. Investment framing: today's price is a bet that the bull path (robotaxi, FSD licensing, Optimus) lands close to certainty, discounted at a rate below what the cyclical core business and its regulatory dependence call for -- this model does not say that optionality is worthless, only that an owner paying today's price is underwriting the bull case plus a sub-10% WACC.
