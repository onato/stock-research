⚠️

This is NOT a standard FCF/DCF valuation. The Bankers Investment Trust is a closed-end investment trust, not an operating company — it has no capex, no stock-based compensation, no working-capital cycle, and its reported net income is dominated by unrealised mark-to-market portfolio revaluation (e.g. FY2021 +£330.6m vs FY2022 -£179.2m). The valuation below uses an NAV-compounding + exit-discount model instead: intrinsic value = today's known NAV per share × (1 + a scenario fair discount/premium to NAV), cross-checked against a 5-year IRR build of dividends plus a discounted exit value.

The base case shows downside (roughly -9% to -10%). That is not a modelling error: BNKR.L shares now trade at essentially PAR to NAV (152.2p vs 152.0p NAV, a ~0.13% discount) versus an 8-year average discount of 7.3% (10.6% over 5 years). The re-rating from a persistent double-digit discount to near-zero has already happened — driven by £185.5m of FY2025 buybacks plus £75m+ more in H1 FY2026 — and a re-rating of that scale cannot mechanically repeat. The base case assumes a mild reversion back toward the long-run average discount, not further re-rating.

Read Bankers as an income + market-return holding (progressive dividend, diversified global equity beta, 0.51% low ongoing charge), not as a value/re-rating opportunity at today's price.
