 5. Fiscal 2025 Forecasts (1)
    Net sales are expected to increase chiefly due to the effect of foreign currency translation in the overseas flour milling business and
    higher shipments in the mesh cloth business despite the effect of cuts in flour prices due to wheat price revisions in the domestic flour
    milling business.

    Operating profit is expected to increase, mainly due to an increase in profit in the overseas flour milling business, processed food
    segment, and prepared dishes and other prepared foods businesses, as well as higher shipments in the mesh cloth business, despite
    a decrease in profit in the domestic flour milling business.
    The forecast for profit attributable to owners of the parent has been revised upward due to a planned reduction in cross-
    shareholdings.
