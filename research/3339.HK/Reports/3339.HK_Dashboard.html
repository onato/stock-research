Model approach: The first build (base IV HK$6.54, +134% at a price in the upper 77% of its 10-year range) tripped scripts/sanity_check.py with diagnosis growth_too_high: the base 10-year revenue CAGR of 4.7% exceeded the FY2017-FY2025 through-cycle CAGR of 2.8%. Fix applied per the runbook (Step 8c.5, items 3 and 4): (1) base growth path capped at a 2.77% 10-year CAGR, keeping the banked FY2026 +15% and flattening to 1% by FY2032, terminal growth 2.5% -> 2.0%; bull trimmed 6.4% -> 5.0% for ordering; bear unchanged (~0%). (2) Exit-multiple caps set from the stock's own 10-year EV/EBITDA range -- bear 3.7x (min), base 6.6x (average), bull 10.9x (max) -- expressed as owner-FCF cap multiples of 5.6x / 9.5x / 14x (bull kept at the tighter 14x already judged). Margins, capex, D&A, tax, working capital, WACC, bridge and weights were deliberately left unchanged. The residual upside after the fix (base IV HK$5.68, +103%) is almost entirely the cash: Lonking holds RMB7,276.6m of cash/time deposits plus RMB900.8m of FVTPL investments against zero borrowings (net of the FY2025 dividend paid 31 July 2026, about HK$2.04/share, ~73% of the share price), and the 10-year historical multiples this check compares against were struck on an enterprise value that already deducted that cash. Crediting the cash at face is the house's deliberate choice, charged through a 25% bear weight and historical-range exit multiples for the operating business rather than through an ad-hoc discount on the cash -- a multi-bagger case built on a cash floor plus a modest, through-cycle operating recovery, not on a re-rating of the machinery business.
