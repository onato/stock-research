Company Overview

Seeka Limited is one of NZ's two largest kiwifruit post-harvest operators, providing integrated supply chain services from orchard to market. Operates 12 packhouse/coolstore facilities across all major NZ growing regions plus Australian operations.

Packed a record 47.1m class 1 trays in 2025. Delivered consecutive record profits in FY2024-FY2025 following weather-related losses in 2022-2023.

Business Model

Highly seasonal fee-for-service business. 70-90% of gross profit in H1 (Jan-Jun) during NZ kiwifruit harvest. Strong operating leverage: FY2024 saw 44% volume increase drive 193% EBITDA improvement.

  • Post-Harvest (packing & coolstorage)60%growing
  • Orcharding (managed & leased)25%growing
  • SeekaFresh Retail8%stable
  • Australia5%growing

Competitive Position

Competitors: EastPack (~30%), Apata, DMS Progrowers, Trevelyan's, Hume, Satara

  • Scale: 12 packhouses, >50m tray capacity across all NZ regions
  • Vertical integration: Grew ~40% of fruit handled in post-harvest
  • Automation: Reemoon technology - 19% cost-per-tray reduction in FY2024
  • Weakness: Can't offer growers equity (vs co-op competitors), Zespri concentration

Growth Drivers

  • Zespri targets 236m trays by 2030 (+10%) - Seeka's capacity already in place
  • SunGold variety shift: 63% of trays, yields growing 7% pa
  • Reemoon automation at Kerikeri & Huka Pak for 2026 season
  • Raukokore orchard development (~70 ha) entering production
  • Australian expansion: $17.2m invested, production up 166%
  • Debt reduction: $172m peak to $100m - reducing interest drag
  • Dividend reinstated: ~$0.55/share trailing 12m distributions

Key Risks

  • HIGH: Weather - EBITDA ranged $26m to $96m in 3 years. No earnings floor.
  • HIGH: Zespri dependency - ~60% of revenue through single-desk system
  • HIGH: Climate change - Cyclone Gabrielle caused $21m loss. Bay of Plenty = 85% of supply
  • MOD: Grower retention vs co-operative competitors offering equity
  • MOD: Debt covenants (3.25x leverage limit) in poor seasons
  • MOD: Northern Hemisphere SunGold competition from Zespri licensing

Bull Case

  • Record earnings with structural underpinning - automation delivered 19% cost-per-tray reduction
  • >50m tray capacity in place - captures industry growth at high incremental margins
  • Net debt down 42% from peak - materially reduces financial risk
  • Dividend ~10.5% yield signals board confidence in sustainability
  • Automation creates durable cost advantage reducing labour dependency

Bear Case

  • FY2023 pre-tax loss of $21m shows profitability collapses in poor seasons
  • Valuation may be at earnings peak - stock pricing normalised record profits
  • Climate change increasing extreme weather frequency - structural Bay of Plenty risk
  • Northern Hemisphere SunGold will eventually moderate per-tray returns
  • Australian expansion ($17.2m) historically loss-making, execution risk
