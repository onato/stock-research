                ⚠ Reading the DCF: Why Intrinsic Value Sits Below the Market Price

                This DCF deliberately diverges from the current $9.08 share price in the base and bear cases. The market appears to price Contact largely on a forward EV/EBITDAF basis (~12.4x FY26 guidance), which is in line with CEN's own FY2023-25 trading history (~12.84x average) — but that methodology does not explicitly deduct the cash currently being consumed by the renewable growth-capex build. A disciplined owner free-cash-flow DCF charges that capex in full as it is spent, and Contact is only mid-way through a multi-billion-dollar programme (Manawa integration, Te Mihi Stage 2, Glenbrook-Ohurua battery, Kōwhai Park & Glorit solar, Tauhara 2 pre-FID). That is the source of the gap — not a modelling error.

                • Base case $6.36 (-29.9%) and Bear case $2.57 (-71.6%) both pass every historical-multiple sanity check cleanly (implied P/E, P/B, EV/EBITDAF and P/S all sit below CEN's own FY22-25 averages).
                • Bull case $11.28 (+24.3%) is the one scenario where the sanity check trips: its implied near-term EV/EBITDAF (14.78x on FY26 guidance) modestly exceeds CEN's own all-time-high multiple (13.49x, FY2023). This is judged proportionate given the bull case's deliberately optimistic, best-case assumption stack (above-consensus electrification demand, full Manawa synergy capture, benign Electricity Authority reform outcome) and its 25% probability weight — it should be read as an optimistic upper bound, not a central expectation.
                • Probability-weighted IV $6.65 (-26.8%), using 25% Bull / 50% Base / 25% Bear, remains firmly in downside territory.
                • This is best understood as a yield-plus-modest-growth bond proxy (dividends 35→39 cps over FY21-25), not a multi-bagger growth story. At $9.08 — top decile of the 10-year $4.66-$9.63 range — the market has already capitalised the growth programme's future earnings, while an owner-FCF view is still paying for the capex that produces them.
                • Model correction (9-Aug-2026): a bug in a prior version of this DCF applied the terminal exit-multiple cap to terminal owner FCF instead of terminal EBITDAF, binding the cap far below the documented 12.5x/13.5x/10.5x multiples. This has been corrected (cap now = min(Gordon growth TV, terminal multiple × Y10 EBITDAF)); base IV moved from $4.99 to $6.36, bull from $8.44 to $11.28, bear from $1.90 to $2.57.
