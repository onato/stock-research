Model approach: Step 8c.5 fix #2 (structural WACC premium), 2026-10-16. Trip: implied P/E 20.7x vs 2x the 10yr average 6.0x; P/B, EV/EBITDA, P/Sales in range. Diagnosis: (1) the P/E denominator is FY2025 EPS, the cycle trough with ~RMB400m of ancillary impairments -- H1 FY2026 alone earned 86% of FY2025's profit, so on base-case FY2026E net income (~RMB1.56bn, consistent with H1 annualised) the pre-fix base IV implied ~10x, not 20.7x; (2) all printed multiples are ~17% high on both sides because an HKD value is divided by CNY per-share figures (ratio-invariant); (3) the genuine residual is that even ~10x normalised is ~2x what the market paid in FY2018-24 and the price is at 0.90 of its 10yr range -- a discount-rate issue (single mine, chairman control, RMB1.35bn connected-party prepayments, free float), not a flows issue, since margins (41% settled vs 50% history) and growth are already below history. Change: WACC 10.0% -> 11.5% in all three scenarios (+1.0pp single-asset/region concentration, +0.5pp governance/free float; cyclicality premium deliberately NOT added because it is already charged in the scenario flows and the 30% bear weight). No driver path changed. Result: base IV HK$2.14 -> 1.77, bull 3.11 -> 2.56, bear 1.11 -> 0.91, weighted 2.03 -> 1.67 (upside +11.9% -> -7.9%). Residual: the script's rule still trips at 17.1x because the denominator is unchanged; clearing it would need an IV below ~HK$1.24 (12x trough EPS), i.e. asserting the shares are worth a third less than the market in the half-year profit doubled. Recorded as a trough-denominator artifact; not iterated further per the one-fix budget.
