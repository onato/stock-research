                          Fundamentals That Support Kaoʼs Value Creation                               The origins of the Kao Way can be traced back to the company’s founder,




                            The Kao Way
                                                                                                       Tomiro Nagase. In 1887, Tomiro Nagase founded Nagase Shoten, a Western




                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                 What Kao Aims for
                                                                                                       sundry goods store. He launched Kao Sekken (Kao Soap) three years later in
                                                                                                       1890. His goal was to create a high-quality facial soap and offer it at an affordable
                                                                                                       price to contribute to cleaner and more comfortable lives for people at the time,
                                                                                                       when the only choices available were poor-quality domestic soap and high-priced
                                                                                                       imported soap. This is the origin of our mission “to strive for the wholehearted
                                                                                                       satisfaction and enrichment of the lives of people.”
