CVC Fund IX + Groupe Bruxelles Lambert, via Respighi BidCo, are offering €51.29/share ex-dividend (€52.00 cum-dividend, ~€10.7bn total) for all shares. Rossini S.àr.l. (CVC's vehicle, 46.82%) has irrevocably committed to tender; CONSOB approved the offer document 8-Jul-2026. Acceptance period: 31-Aug-2026 to 15-Oct-2026. A full squeeze-out needs ≥66.67%. Six of ten directors called the offer fair; four independent directors dissented as inadequate. Current price €52.65 is ABOVE the €51.29 offer — a bet on a bump, not a discount for deal risk. Deal-state weighted IV is €50.47 (−4.1% vs price) across three scenarios: completes at €51.29 (78% weight), bumped to €55.50 (12%), breaks to ~€43.65 (10%). Risk/reward is 4.6:1 against (lose €9.54 on a break to make €2.05 on a bump); a buyer needs ~67% probability of a bump just to break even against a realistic 10% break risk. Conclusion: AVOID at €52.65 — holders should tender or sell above the offer; the DCF section below is the standalone/no-deal anchor, not a live trading signal.
