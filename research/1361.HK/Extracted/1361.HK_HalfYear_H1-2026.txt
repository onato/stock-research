CHAIRMAN’S
  STATEMENT

Dear shareholders,

On behalf of the board (the “Board”) of directors (the “Directors”) of 361 Degrees International Limited (the “Company”), I am
pleased to present the interim results of the Company and its subsidiaries (collectively referred to as the “Group” or “361º Group”)
for the six months ended 30 June 2026 (the “period under review”).

In the first half of 2026, the global economy continued to show signs of fragmentation, with heightened geopolitical tensions
further increased uncertainty. However, China’s economy maintained steady and positive momentum, driven by policy guidance and
endogenous resilience. Domestic demand for sports consumption continued to be unleashed, with the event economy and outdoor
sports emerging as the core industry growth drivers. Driven by the growing popularity of national fitness, consumer demand for
sports goods shifted from meeting basic functionality to professional, scenario-based, high-quality, and value-driven consumption.
This clear structural upgrading trend in the industry has created a favourable operating environment supporting the Group’s sound
operations.

During the period under review, the Group reported a revenue of RMB6.16 billion, representing a year-on-year increase of 8.0%.
Profit attributable to equity holders was RMB926 million, representing a year-on-year increase of 8.0%. In recognition of the
shareholders’ trust and support for the Group, the Board has resolved to declare an interim dividend of 22.2 HK cents per ordinary
share for the period under review, representing a dividend payout ratio of 45%.

The 361º Group has always upheld the brand ethos of “ONE DEGREE BEYOND” ( 多一度熱愛 ) and remained committed to its brand
positioning as a “professional, youthful, and internationalised” brand, consistently delivering high-value mass-market professional
sports products to global consumers. Driven by industry innovation and event partnerships to enhance brand influence, the Group
continued to reinforce the competitive advantages of its core categories while accelerating its presence across diverse emerging
sports segments, steadily broadening the strategic depth for the brand’s long-term development.


Omnichannel Upgrading and Deeper Global Expansion
The Group remains steadfast in its consumer-centric approach and is advancing its omnichannel integration throughout its online-
offline platforms. Through initiatives such as retail scenario innovation and global market expansion, we continue to enhance our
market share and retail operational efficiency.

As of 30 June 2026, the Group had a total of 5,076 361º branded stores in the Chinese mainland. During the period under review,
the Group continued to optimise its store channel structure, resulting in robust growth in retail sales.

The Super Premium Store ( 超品店 ) format continued to expand. As of 30 June 2026, a cumulative total of 187 Super Premium
Stores had been launched nationwide. We had opened one additional Super Premium Store in Cambodia, bringing the total number
of Super Premium Stores to 188. Built on the innovative model of “thousand-square-metre space, comprehensive multi-category
coverage, and one-stop self-service shopping”, the Super Premium Store has established a proven model for channel innovation.

The forerunner of “1+6” new retail omnichannel operating model. 361º is one of the earliest sports brands to achieve full
systematic integration of multi-platform omnichannel operations. In February 2026, 361º entered into a partnership with “JD
Instant Delivery” ( 京東秒送 ), marking the full implementation of its instant retail strategy, under which offline physical stores now
simultaneously operate across six online channels, effectively expanding the store’s service radius and delivery efficiency. At the same
time, we continued to enhance the capabilities of frontline retail staff into versatile talents equipped with full-domain operational
capabilities, thereby enhancing service quality and sales efficiency.

International business continued to deepen. During the period under review, the Group had 1,167 offline points-of-sales (“POSs”)
in the overseas market (excluding the Chinese mainland), covering regions such as the Americas, Europe, and regions alongside the
Belt and Road Initiative, while actively expanding into global markets through cross-border e-commerce platforms and standalone sites.
Overseas retail sales achieved a year-on-year increase of over 80% and overseas cross-border e-commerce sales marked a year-on-year
increase of over 140%, demonstrating significant effectiveness of our internationalisation efforts.

The e-commerce business remained committed to a differentiated exclusive-product strategy. Capitalising on emerging consumer
trends in sports such as tennis, badminton, and pickleball, it achieved simultaneous growth in both revenue and operational
profitability. In addition, ONEWAY, a Finnish professional outdoor brand, achieved a breakthrough in the Greater China market, with
multiple outdoor products receiving positive market feedback.


Empowering the Event Ecosystem, Strengthening the Brand’s Professional
Competitiveness
The Group adheres to a two-way synergy between top-tier international event partnerships and proprietary event operations,
building a professional, normalised, and internationalised brand event ecosystem. This approach has continuously reinforced our
brand’s professional credentials, enhanced global exposure, and deepened consumer mindshare.

As the official partner of the Asian Games for five consecutive editions, event partnership has become a core resource in the Group’s
international brand strategy. In June 2026, we unveiled the sportswear for the torchbearers of the Asian Games Aichi-Nagoya.
Furthermore, the Group continues to enrich its brand event portfolio by serving as a global partner of World Aquatics, supporting the
successful hosting of its events, and acting as the Honorary Partner of the Qingdao Marathon for the third consecutive year. Coupled
with the on-court endorsement and empowerment from our international ambassador lineup, these initiatives have collectively
demonstrated the technological strength and professional pursuit embodied in our products.

In terms of proprietary event operations, the Group continues to iterate and upgrade its mass-participation event system, regularly
operating its own events such as the “3# Track” ( 三號賽道 ), “Light Up” ( 觸地即燃 ), “Women’s Fitness Gym” ( 女子健身局 ), and
the “SWING AS ONE” ( 一拍即合 ), broadening the brand’s reach in the mass sports arena. In April 2026, the new season of “Light
Up” ( 觸 地 即 燃 ) underwent comprehensive optimisation across competition system, visual identity, storytelling, and commercial
conversion, propelling grassroots basketball events into a new phase of ecosystem development.

Dual-Track Synergy to Build a Differentiated Product Portfolio
Guided by the development strategy of “technology-driven, brand-first”, the Group continues to strengthen its foothold in core
segments such as running and basketball, while positioning itself in emerging segments including trail running, women’s sports, and
racquet sports, seizing growth opportunities through agile deployment.

Running – Building a full-scenario product ecosystem. In March 2026, the “Flying Flame 5” ( 飛燃 5) Tokyo-exclusive coloured
edition running shoes made their debut at the Tokyo Marathon, where running brand ambassador GUAN Yousheng ( 管 油 勝 )
achieved outstanding results, fully validating the Group’s product iteration capabilities in professional racing footwear. In the
trail running segment, the “Peak Pierce V1” ( 淩 刺 V1) and “Peak Pierce V2” ( 淩 刺 V2) were launched, receiving widespread
recognition from professional runners. Additionally, the “Titan 4CQT” ( 泰坦 4CQT) running shoes were specifically designed for HYROX
comprehensive fitness training, bridging the product gap between road running and comprehensive training.

Basketball – Ambassador lineup reinforcing international positioning. In February 2026, we partnered with our global brand
ambassador Nikola JOKIĆ to launch the second-generation signature basketball shoes, the “JOKER2 GT”. In May 2026, the flagship
guard shoes, “BIG3 7.0 PRO”, made their debut at the Drew League in the United States, completing overseas on-court validation.
At the 27th China (Jinjiang) International Footwear Expo, we showcased new products, including the “JOKER2”, “JOKER2 GT”,
and “ZEN VIII” ( 禪 8), covering the full spectrum of the lineup from entry-level performance shoes to premium signature models,
solidifying the international positioning of our basketball category.

Expanding into emerging segments to reach younger consumer demographics. During the period under review, the Group
launched the “Wenqing” ( 穩 擎 ) all-round hard-court tennis shoes, the “Match Point” ( 賽 點 ) professional pickleball shoes, and
the “Victory PRO V2”( 制 勝 PRO V2) professional badminton shoes, precisely addressing the professional needs of different sports
scenarios. In women’s sports, we continued to operate the “Women’s Fitness Gym” ( 女 子 健 身 局 ) community and launched
the “I Am Colourful” ( 我 自 多 彩 ) thematic campaign, building a women’s sports ecosystem from products to experiences through
integrated online-offline marketing. The deployment in emerging segments not only drives business growth in the short term but
also builds differentiated competitive advantages for the long term.


Strengthening Professional Resources and Product Technology to Consolidate
the “Youth Sports Expert” ( 青少年運動專家 ) Positioning
361º Kids carries forward the athletic DNA of the core brand, with its brand statement of “CHASING YOUR LOVE!” ( 熱愛吧 , 少年 !)
and a clear positioning as the “Youth Sports Expert”. Its product line covers professional segments, including running, basketball,
football, rope skipping, outdoor activities, and campus life. During the period under review, 361º Kids continued to consolidate its
first-mover advantage in the kids’ sportswear market, maintaining steady and robust growth. As of 30 June 2026, the number of
361º Kids POSs reached 2,202 in the Chinese mainland, anchored by fifth-generation stores and Super Premium Stores ( 超品店 ),
forming a modern, multi-dimensional terminal channel system.

Professional event resource deployment continued to break new ground. 361º Kids is a strategic partner of the International
Jump Rope Union, the exclusive title sponsor of the 2026 Asian Rope Skipping Championship and the 2027 World Jump Rope
Championships, and has served as the official partner of the China National Rope Skipping Team for a long time. At the same
time, we deepened our footprint in football and basketball by partnering with “Beijing Guoan Junior Training” ( 北 京 國 安 少 訓 )
and the “China Football Development Foundation” ( 中 國 足 球 發 展 基 金 會 ), and signed three young basketball players as brand
ambassadors. During the period under review, the 5th “National Rope Skipping Competition” ( 第五屆全國跳繩大賽 ), the “Light
Up-Junior Basketball Game” ( 觸 地 即 燃 少 年 賽 ), the “361º Football Champions Cup” (361º 足 球 冠 軍 杯 ), and a series of other
events were regularly held across multiple cities nationwide, contributing to the steady expansion of the kids’ business.

                      Production

                      Sichuan Province in the PRC, while the remaining is outsourced
                      to a number of high-quality OEMs. For apparel products,
                      our Wuli Industrial Complex in Jinjian, Fujian Province, and
                      self-owned factory in Liuzhou, Guangxi Province, can meet
                      approximately 23.0% of the Group’s production requirements,
                      while the remaining is outsourced to several high-quality OEMs.
                      In late last year, the Group strategically acquired two factories.
                      Besides bolstering in-house production capacity, this move
                      has reduced our reliance on third-party OEMs. This strategic
                      shift has effectively lowered production costs, safeguarded the
                      Group’s proprietary technologies, and laid a solid foundation
                      for our future business expansion.
