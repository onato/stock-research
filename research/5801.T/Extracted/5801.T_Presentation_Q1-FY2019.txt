FY2018 Q1 Highlights of Results
 Operating income totaled JPY 8.5 billion, down JPY 1.9 billion (down
  18%) YoY
    Operating margin declined to 3.5% (down by 1.1 percentage points) YoY
    － While it had achieved high-margin sales in FY17 Q1, infrastructure segment saw a decline
      in operating income due to an increase in depreciation/research expenses

          Other segments remained unchanged from the previous year, as expected

 Net income totaled JPY 3.3 billion, down 3.5 billion (down 52%) YoY

    － In addition to a deterioration in profit/loss in equity method investments, the Company
      reported an extraordinary loss on disposal of non-current assets
