H1 Overview and Forecast of FY2016
FY2016 H1 Overview
  Operating income totaled 13.3 billion yen, up 4.1 billion yen (up 45 %) YoY.
    － Operating income (real change basis) was up 63% YoY due mainly to continued strong
      performance in communications solutions (Infrastructure segment)
      although strong yen gave negative impact.
    － Effects of structural reform in the copper foil business (Functional Products segment)
      was also contributed .
  Net income was up 4.7 billion yen YoY, totaling 6.7 billion yen.
     － The earnings increase was attributable to the growth of operating income, posting of
     extraordinary gains following the sale of fixed assets, and other factors despite losses from
     equity method investments and increased foreign exchange losses caused by the yen’s
     appreciation.
FY2016 Forecast
  The operating income forecast has been revised upward by 1.5 billion yen to 29.0 billion yen
   from 27.5 billion yen. (The net sales forecast has been revised downward due to the impact
   of the strong yen and falling copper price.)              （billion yen）
