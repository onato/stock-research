     Defense equipment

 Outline of defense equipment business：History
    Founded in 1907 in Muroran, Hokkaido, for the domestic production of weapons. Established manufacturing
     technology for high quality, large materials (steel) and produced various artillery and armor plates, etc.




                                           High-quality steel manufacturing
      Muroran Plant（1907）                       and processing technology




                                            Manufacturing technology for
      Hiroshima Plant（1920）                        various machines


    In 1920, the Hiroshima Plant was established. In addition to small- and medium-caliber artillery, it
