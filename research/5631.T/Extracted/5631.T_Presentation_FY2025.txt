 Impact of the Middle East Situation

Direct impact is limited, while we continue to closely monitor indirect impacts




    ✓ Direct impact
        The scale of business in Middle East is very limited. That said, impacts recognized are considered
        in each segment.

                No expansion of direct impact is anticipated at this time.


    ✓ Indirect impact
        Potential concerns include supply constraints for production materials such as lubricants and
        paints, as well as higher transportation and energy costs. We are proceeding with appropriate
        countermeasures.

                As the impact on business performance cannot be reasonably quantified at the moment,
                it has not been factored into the full-year forecast.
