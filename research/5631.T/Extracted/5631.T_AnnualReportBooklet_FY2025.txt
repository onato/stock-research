                                                                                                                                        Based on its Purpose—“Material Revolution, making the world sustainable and prosperous”—JSW Group will contribute to
                                                                                                                                        stakeholders as follows through the development and implementation of industrial machinery and new materials that solve social
                                                                                                                                        issues, and simultaneously ensure thorough organizational crisis management as it works to create social value and sustainably
                                                                                                                                        enhance its corporate value.
