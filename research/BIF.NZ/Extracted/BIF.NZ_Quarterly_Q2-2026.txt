BIF – Update on Senior Managers

7 May 2026

Booster Investment Management Limited (BIML), as manager of the Booster Innovation Fund (the Fund)
provides an update to its Senior Managers.

Following a change in the group structure in December 2025, Allan Yeo (Managing Director of the Booster
group) has, effective 4 May 2026, stepped off the Board as a director of Booster Financial Services
Limited (BIML’s parent company). Allan will continue to be a director of Booster Group Limited, the
group’s ultimate holding company. As a result of this change, Allan is no longer deemed to be a Senior
Manager of BIML.

Following this change, the Directors and Senior Managers for BIML are:

BIML Directors:
   • Dianne Day (Independent Director)
   • Paul Foley (Executive Director)
   • Di Papadopoulos (Chief Executive Officer and Director)
   • John Selby (Independent Director and Chair)
   • Melanie Templeton (Independent Director)

BIML Senior Managers:
   • Nadine Brown (Chief Customer Officer)
   • Mihaela Chitu (Head of Technology Solutions)
   • Natasha Keddie (Head of Finance and Administration)
   • Andrew Hughes (Chief Risk Officer)
   • Simon O’Grady (Chief Investment Officer)
   • Alison Payne (Chief Operating Officer)

About Booster
Booster Investment Management Limited (Booster) is the manager and issuer of the Fund, and part of
the Booster Group which has been helping New Zealanders save since 1998. The group currently
administers superannuation and investment funds of over $8 billion on behalf of more than 200,000 New
Zealanders.
BIF is a managed investment fund that invests in early-stage companies founded on intellectual property
originated or developed in New Zealand.
