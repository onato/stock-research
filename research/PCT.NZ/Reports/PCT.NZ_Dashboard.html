Precinct Properties New Zealand (PCT.NZ)

Premium Auckland & Wellington CBD office REIT · stapled security (PPNZ + PPIL) · FY end 30-Jun · NZD millions

Reading this dashboard as a REIT: Statutory NetIncome and EPS are dominated by unrealised IAS 40 investment-property revaluations (+$254.9m FY2018, -$153.1m FY2023, -$22.1m FY2024, +$11.0m FY2025) and are shown below labelled as such — they are not a cash-flow proxy and no P/E is built around them. The CSV FreeCashFlow line is structurally negative in almost every period because development capex (Commercial Bay, Bowen Campus, Molesworth Street, Downtown Car Park, 256 Queen Street) runs through investing cash flow — that is growth capital funding the development pipeline, not the company burning cash to survive. The real earnings measure is AFFO per stapled security, which is what management guides to and pays the dividend against.

Dividend vs AFFO per Security (Payout Coverage)

Dividend has exceeded AFFO every year since FY2021 — the gap is funded by asset recycling and debt, not by cash generation. H1 FY2026 AFFO/security fell to 2.76c (from 3.23c H1 FY2025) because the Oct/Nov-2025 $325m raise put +16.8% more securities in the count before the capital has started earning — a dilution effect, not operating deterioration.

FFO per Stapled Security

FFO (Funds From Operations) is the broader non-GAAP measure Precinct guides on (FY2026: 7.30-7.50 cps); AFFO deducts maintenance capex and leasing incentives from FFO and is the more conservative, dividend-coverage-relevant figure.

Portfolio Quality & Valuation Drivers

NTA per Security vs Share Price

NTA fell from $1.54 (FY2022) to $1.18 (H1 FY2026) as cap rates expanded. Precinct traded at or above NTA for most of the last decade (avg P/NTA ~1.02x) — unlike diversified NZX REIT peers — making the current 0.87x discount unusually cheap by its own history.

Portfolio Occupancy & WALT

Occupancy and weighted average lease term (WALT) underpin income durability; both are point-in-time disclosures at FY/H1 reporting dates.

Average Portfolio Capitalisation Rate

Cap-rate expansion (values rising here) is the single biggest driver of the FY2023-25 statutory devaluation cycle and of NTA erosion; cap-rate compression is the biggest lever for NTA recovery.

Gearing / LVR

Pro forma gearing fell to 33.7% at 31-Dec-2025 after hotel and 22 Stanley Street sale proceeds repaid debt — part of the capital-partnering/asset-recycling de-gearing strategy, not a balance-sheet distress signal.

DCF Valuation — REIT AFFO-Capitalization / NTA Model

Model approach: This is a blended AFFO-capitalization / NTA-based cost-of-equity model, not a WACC-based owner-FCF DCF. Precinct's reported NetIncome is dominated by unrealised IAS 40 property revaluations (+$254.9m FY2018 to -$153.1m FY2023) and its reported Free Cash Flow is structurally negative from development capex — neither is a usable valuation input, so P/E and EV/EBITDA are deliberately skipped (EBITDA is left NULL in the Metrics CSV by design). Instead this model checked the REIT-meaningful multiples — P/NTA, P/AFFO (AFFO yield) and implied capitalisation rate — against Precinct's own 10-year traded history (FY-end closes vs FY NTA/AFFO). The sanity check PASSED: the base-case blended intrinsic value of ~$0.94 implies 0.80x P/NTA, below every FY-end observation in the 10-year history (0.87x-1.21x) and far below the 1.82x trip threshold, and an implied 14.7x P/AFFO on FY2026e, below the current price's 17.0x TTM and the entire 18.1x-24.7x FY2021-25 traded range. A negative base-case upside (-8.9%) with the price sitting at its 10-year low is read as "fairly valued for an income vehicle with an AFFO-uncovered distribution", not as a model artifact.
