                DCF Valuation — Sum of the Parts

                Valuation approach: Liberty Global is NOT a consolidated-FCF DCF story. Consolidated financials cover only Telenet (Belgium), Wyre (Belgian fiber netco), VM Ireland, and Liberty Corporate & Services — the 50%-owned VMO2 (UK) and VodafoneZiggo (Netherlands, pending full buy-in) JVs are equity-accounted and invisible in consolidated revenue/EBITDA/FCF. Consolidated owner FCF (ex-JV distributions) has been negative-to-breakeven every year FY2023-2025 due to the Wyre/Telenet fiber capex cycle — a genuine trough, not a reason to write off the business. This model uses a sum-of-the-parts approach: (1) a 10-year component-built owner-FCF DCF on consolidated operations only, capturing the capex normalization management itself guides to; (2) the two 50% JV stakes (VMO2, VodafoneZiggo) valued via EV/EBITDA cross-checked against Liberty Global's own equity-method carrying values; (3) the Liberty Growth venture/data-center/media portfolio at management's mark-to-market FMV ($3.4B); less (4) consolidated net debt and the ~$1.16B cash cost of the pending VodafoneZiggo stake buy-in from Vodafone. The ~$1B of stated Ziggo Group synergies and the 2027 Euronext spin-off are bull-case optionality, not underwritten in the base case.
