  2. Current status and prospects of melamine
       Unlikely to secure stable profits in mid-long term, and to expect profit expansion by new investments
        World production capacity is twice of demand (our estimate)
        Domestic production capacity (2 companies including Nissan Chemical) is 3 times of demand (our estimate)
