Management Guidance

Scale vs. Profitability

T&G is a NZ$1.3-1.6bn revenue business earning low single-digit net margins -- absolute profit is charted separately so it isn't swamped by the revenue line

Revenue & YoY Growth

H1 FY2026 revenue (572.3m) is CONTINUING-OPERATIONS ONLY after the 31-Jul-2026 agreement to sell T&G Fresh's NZ, Fiji and Pacific businesses; every FY bar and H1 FY2025 are whole-company. Like-for-like continuing growth was +2.6%, not the ~38% drop the raw H1 bars imply.

Gross / Operating / Net Margin

FY2023 (-3.4% operating margin) is Cyclone Gabrielle: the storm destroyed ~13% of Hawke's Bay planted hectares and drove a NZ$45.6m operating loss.

Net Income & EBITDA (absolute NZ$)

Net income includes NZ$5-6m/yr of non-controlling interests (mainly the Worldwide Fruit UK 50% JV) that never reaches T&G parent shareholders. FY2023 Cyclone Gabrielle and H1 FY2026's -30.5m disposal-loss print are both real but non-recurring shocks, not trend.

Strategic Pivot: Apples & VentureFruit

management's own framing -- branded fruit and plant-variety IP licensing over low-margin produce trading

EBITDA Margin

H1 FY2026 EBITDA margin of ~6.1% is on continuing operations; management's base-case DCF path assumes this climbs to 9% by FY2031 as VentureFruit reaches breakeven and stranded corporate costs are cut.

Operating Cash Flow vs Reported FCF vs Owner FCF

Owner FCF (DCF JSON) deducts full IFRS-16 lease principal from reported FCF and was negative in 6 of the last 7 years -- replanting capex and lease payments consistently exceed operating cash flow. FY2025's reported FCF of 59.3m is a capex-suppressed peak (32.6m vs 48-107m in FY2021-24), which is why the DCF's base_fcf uses the continuing-ops 2.5m figure, not this series.

CapEx (Orchard & Facility Investment)

Capex swings 33-107m/yr with orchard replanting cycles (ENVY plantings) and post-harvest facility investment (Whakatu, Hawke's Bay, Nelson); FY2025's 32.6m is below the historical range and is expected to normalise toward ~3% of revenue.

Balance Sheet & Asset Backing

the DCF's asset leg rests on book value and NTA, not the going-concern earnings alone

NTA per Share (vs NZ$2.40 current price)

Share price NZ$2.40 (4-Sep-2026) is not plotted as a series (no time series exists for it in this dataset) but sits at ~0.68x the latest NTA/share shown here. T&G has closed above 1.0x book only once in ten fiscal year-ends (FY2016). The DCF's asset leg (parent book x exit P/B) is calibrated to this history, not to a re-rating.

Total Debt vs Cash

TotalDebt excludes NZ$165-210m of IFRS-16 lease liabilities (treated as an operating cost in the DCF, not debt). Dec-2025 net debt of ~146m is expected to fall to ~70m once T&G Fresh sale proceeds (~76m) are received and applied to debt, including a NZ$20.8m related-party BayWa loan.

Shareholders' Equity

Equity fell from FY2021's 573.6m peak as Cyclone Gabrielle losses (FY2023) and ongoing thin/negative earnings eroded retained earnings; the 5-year equity CAGR is -0.6%, materially below the +2.0% revenue CAGR over the same period.
Model approach: This valuation is a 50/50 BLEND of two legs: (1) an owner-FCF DCF on the continuing Apples/VentureFruit business, and (2) an asset leg (parent book value per share × a 10-year-history-disciplined exit price-to-book). The blend was applied because the pure earnings DCF alone tripped the model's own sanity check — it implied 0.26x book, below the 10-year trading minimum of 0.42x, effectively valuing NZ$232m of independently-valued land and buildings, and the ENVY/JAZZ orchard estate, at less than any buyer has ever paid. The sliders below move only the going-concern leg; the DCF JSON's non_operating_assets field carries the asset-leg blend term at full (100%) weight even though the model applies it at 50%, so moving a slider shows roughly 2x the true sensitivity of the blended intrinsic value (see inputs.notes, "SLIDER NOTE", in the DCF JSON).
