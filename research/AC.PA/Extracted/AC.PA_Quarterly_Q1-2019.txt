           NEW ACCOUNTING PRINCIPLES
           I M PA C T O F I F R S 1 6 I M P L E M E N TAT I O N


                                                                                                                       R E V E N U E
             Standard distinguishes between:
Before       • Finance leases: the lessee has substantially all of the risks and reward of ownership
             • Operating leases: the lessor has substantially all of the risks and reward of ownership

                                                                                                                       2 0 1 9


             No distinction between operating & finance leases anymore, all recognized on balance sheet                Q 1
New rule     Exemptions: variable leases, short-term leases (<12 months) and “low-value assets” leases                 /




                                                                                                                       A C C O R
             • No cash impact – No effect on credit profile
Impact       • Retained method: Modified retrospective (simplified approach)
             • Present value of lease payments accounted for as lease liability (with recognition of a corresponding
               right-of-use asset) implying increase in debt (c.€1bn)
             • Fixed rent expense in EBITDA replaced by depreciation of right-of-use asset and interests expense
               implying increased EBITDA (€100m+ in 2018)
