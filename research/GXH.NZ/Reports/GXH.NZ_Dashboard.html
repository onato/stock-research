Green Cross Health Limited (GXH.NZ)

Pharmacy Retail (Unichem / Life Pharmacy) — NZX — All figures in NZD millions unless stated — Post-Medical-Divestment (completed 31-Jul-2026)

Read this first: On 31 July 2026 GXH completed the sale of its Medical division ("The Doctors" — 65 clinics, >400,000 enrolled patients) to Tend Health for NZ$270 million. GXH is now a pure-play NZ pharmacy retailer. Every historical series below (FY2018–FY2026) still consolidates the now-sold Medical division, and years before Feb-2023 also include a Community Health division sold at that time. None of these trend lines describe the business that exists today — a reader who extrapolates them will be wrong. GXH also divested the more profitable of its two former divisions: FY2026 segment profit was Pharmacy $22.245m vs Medical $26.042m (Medical grew profit +33% YoY vs Pharmacy's +4%). As of 11-Aug-2026 the Board has announced no decision on how the $270m proceeds will be used — capital allocation, not pharmacy growth, is the central question for this stock now.

                Reported FCF vs Owner FCF (after lease principal)

                Owner FCF = Reported FCF minus IFRS-16 lease principal repayments ($13.752m FY2026, $12.577m FY2025) minus SBC. For a lease-heavy retail chain, reported FCF materially overstates true owner cash flow.

                Lease liabilities ($105.012m) are more than 5x bank borrowings ($19.823m). The leases are the real obligation and must be treated as debt-like when assessing leverage.

                Dividends per Share (cents)

                Only FY2025 (4.5c) and FY2026 (5.75c, being 5.5c final + prior interim shown in the aggregate DPS figure) are disclosed in this dataset. FY2023's $40.1m / 28cps SPECIAL dividend (Community Health sale proceeds) is not in the CSV's DividendPerShare column but is a key precedent for the $270m Medical proceeds.
                Model approach: A conventional revenue/EPS-CAGR-driven DCF is inapplicable here because every historical growth rate blends a Medical division that no longer exists within GXH (sold 31-Jul-2026) and, pre-Feb-2023, a Community Health division sold at that time. Instead this section uses a sum-of-parts model: PV(retained Pharmacy-only owner-FCF DCF) + existing net cash + risk-adjusted net Medical sale proceeds. The exact net proceeds from the $270m gross headline have not yet been disclosed — the $270m must still be adjusted for net debt/working-capital true-up, the Medical division's own transferring lease liabilities, transaction costs and tax leakage on the gain, so a conservative/mid/optimistic range ($199.2m / $223.3m / $252.2m) is modelled instead of a single number. Compounding that uncertainty, the Board has announced no decision on how proceeds will be used (special dividend, pharmacy reinvestment, or a mix) as of 11-Aug-2026, so each scenario also applies a capital-allocation discount (0% bull / 15% base / 35% bear) to the effective proceeds. The base case lands slightly BELOW the current market price because the stock has already re-rated roughly +63% from $1.20 (March 2026 FY-end close) specifically on this divestment news. Read this as a capital-allocation and cash-realisation bet, not a growth-compounding story.
