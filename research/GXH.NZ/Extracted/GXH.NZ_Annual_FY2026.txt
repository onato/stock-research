               MEETING RESULTS ANNOUNCEMENT
14 July 2026

Results of Annual Meeting of Shareholders

At Green Cross Health’s shareholder meeting, held at the Ellerslie Event Centre in Auckland today,
shareholders were asked to vote on three ordinary resolutions and one special resolution. As required by
NZX Listing Rule 6.1, all voting was conducted by a poll.

The ordinary resolutions passed were:
   1. That Catherine Treneman be elected as a Director of Green Cross Health.
   2. That Andrew Bagnall be re-elected as a Director of Green Cross Health.
   3. That the Directors be authorised to fix the remuneration of the Auditor for the ensuing year.

The special resolution passed was:
   4. That the sale of all of the shares in Green Cross Health Medical Limited was approved for all
       purposes, including for the purposes of section 129 of the Companies Act 1993 and NZX Listing
       Rule 5.1.1.
