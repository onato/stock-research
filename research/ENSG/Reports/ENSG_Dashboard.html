Skilled Nursing & Post-Acute Care — Financial Dashboard — Data through Q1 2026

Company Overview

The Ensign Group is a holding company operating skilled nursing facilities, senior living communities, and rehabilitative services across 17 states through independent subsidiaries. As of May 1, 2026, Ensign operates 395 facilities with over 44,000 operational beds and units, following 22 acquisitions completed in Q1 2026 alone. The company uses a decentralized, local-leadership model and owns a growing real estate portfolio through Standard Bearer Healthcare REIT, Inc.

Business Model

Ensign generates revenue primarily from skilled nursing services (~95% of revenue), with Medicare/Medicaid as the dominant payor. The company pursues a dual strategy: acquire underperforming facilities and improve them through local leadership, while building a real estate portfolio via Standard Bearer REIT.
