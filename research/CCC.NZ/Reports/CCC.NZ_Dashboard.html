System-Wide Growth & Network

ⓘ
Read store sales, not reported revenue, as the top-line KPI. Cooks is a franchisor: it books only royalties from franchised stores but full store sales from the small number it operates directly (Dairygold, Tesco Ireland). System-wide network store sales — NZ$95.8m in FY2026, +22.8% — is roughly 7.5x reported revenue (NZ$12.785m) and is the metric management itself uses to describe growth.

System-Wide Store Sales (UK & Ireland)

Source: GroupStoreSalesUKIreland KPI, FY2022–FY2026. This is the real top-line measure of network health — roughly 7.5x reported revenue.

UK & Ireland Store Count

Network reached 105 sites at FY2026 (+18%), 82 in the UK. Target: 300 stores by FY2034.

Revenue & Profitability

⚠
FY2026's 84% reported revenue jump is an accounting/consolidation effect, not organic growth. Company-managed stores (Dairygold, Tesco Ireland) now consolidate FULL store sales as Group revenue rather than a royalty-only franchise fee. Managed Cafes segment revenue rose NZ$0.987m → NZ$5.317m alone. Franchise revenue excluding Dairygold grew a more representative 22.6%. Do not extrapolate the reported-revenue growth rate.

Reported Revenue

FY2026 callout: +84% YoY driven by Dairygold/Tesco Ireland managed-store consolidation, not comparable organic growth. See System-Wide Store Sales chart above for the real growth signal.

Gross & Net Margin

Margin decline (94.2%→86.8% gross, 12.1%→3.2% net FY25→FY26) is a MIX EFFECT from low-margin managed-store revenue displacing high-margin franchise royalties — not operational deterioration.

EBITDA & Net Income

FY2023 includes a NZ$2.385m Triple Two goodwill impairment; FY2024 includes a NZ$3.352m loss on disposal of Triple Two (discontinued operations). FY2018-21 losses reflect a prior business configuration.

Operating Cash Flow vs Free Cash Flow

Reported OCF/FCF is negative or near-zero in most years. FY2026 OCF as-filed includes a one-off NZ$4.147m lease-receipts reclassification (see DCF section) — comparable-basis FY2026 OCF is only NZ$0.975m.

Balance Sheet & Capital Structure

⚠
Shareholders' equity is negative (NZ$-1.897m FY2026). No book-value or price-to-book chart is shown — it would be unusable/misleading for this balance sheet.

Shareholders' Equity

No log toggle: equity is negative in several years and cannot be plotted on a log axis. Equity has been negative every year since FY2024.

Total Debt vs Cash

Bank debt fell from NZ$4.3m to NZ$2.86m in FY2026, a genuine de-risking step. Excludes IFRS16 lease liabilities (~NZ$27.7m), which are offset ~1:1 by matching sublease receivables from franchisees.
