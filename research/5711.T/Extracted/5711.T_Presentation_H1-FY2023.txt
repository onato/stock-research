              Comparison with the Previous Year Results（Factor Analysis）
                               Operating profit increased, while Ordinary profit decreased.
 Operating profit increased by 0.5 billion yen from the previous year due to the depreciation of the yen, mainly in the U.S.
 dollar and euro, an increase in sales of Copper & copper alloy business mainly in Europe and the United States, and an
 increase in production and sales in the Metals business, despite the impact of the exclusion of the Cement business and
 Aluminum business from the scope of consolidation, an increase in energy costs, and a decline in palladium prices.
 Ordinary profit decreased by 15.1 billion yen from the previous year, mainly due to a decline in dividends received from
 the Los Perambles mine and the recording of equity method investment losses from Mitsubishi UBE Cement Corporation.
