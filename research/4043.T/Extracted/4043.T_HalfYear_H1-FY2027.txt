 Net sales
 Net sales came in at ¥178,676 million, up 9.1%, or ¥14,919 million, compared with the
 corresponding period of the previous fiscal year. This was largely due to the inclusion of Tokuyama
 Life Science (TLS) Group in the Company’s scope of consolidation and increased sales of
 semiconductor-related products.

  Cost of sales
  Cost of sales increased 13.0%, or ¥ 13,557 million compared with the corresponding period of
  the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 117,936 million. This was due mainly to the upturn in raw material
  and fuel costs.


  SG&A expenses
  SG&A expenses increased 17.3%, or ¥ 6,949 million compared with the corresponding period of
  the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 47,174 million largely due to the upswing in general and administrative
  expenses following inclusion of the TLS Group in the Company’s scope of consolidation and
  logistics costs.


  Operating profit
  Operating profit decreased 29.2%, or ¥ 5,587 million, compared with the corresponding period of
  the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 13,564 million largely due to the upturn in raw material and fuel costs.

Cement

Despite the efforts to revise domestic sales prices, earnings from cement decreased due to lower
domestic shipment volumes compared with the same period of the previous year, as well as
increased manufacturing costs resulting from higher raw material prices.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 2.5% compared with the corresponding period
of the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 33,219 million and operating profit decreased 12.0% to ¥ 4,255
million. The segment reported lower earnings on higher sales.

Electronic & Advanced Materials

Semiconductor-grade polycrystalline earnings decreased due to a decrease in sales volume
compared with the same period of the previous year and inventory valuation losses.

IC chemical earnings decreased despite the increase in sales volumes of high-purity isopropyl
alcohol for electronic manufacturing. This downturn largely reflected higher raw material costs.

Fumed silica earnings increased. This largely reflected robust sales volumes and successful efforts
to reduce manufacturing costs at Tokuyama Chemicals (Zhejiang) Co., Ltd.

Thermal management material earnings increased. This largely reflected robust sales volumes of
such applications used for semiconductor production equipment.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 9.3% compared with the corresponding period
of the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 46,557 million and operating profit decreased 3.1% to ¥ 6,756
million. The segment reported lower earnings on higher sales.

Life Science

Dental materials earnings increased. This largely reflected robust sales volumes and the impact of
movements in foreign currency exchange rates as a result of the weak yen.

Diagnosis earnings increased. This largely reflected the inclusion of TLS Group in the Company’s
scope of consolidation and the sales growth in clinical laboratory systems at A&T Corporation.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 50.6% compared with the corresponding
period of the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 29,049 million and operating profit increased 32.1% to
¥ 535 million. The segment reported higher earnings on higher sales.

Eco Business

Ion exchange membrane earnings decreased owing to a decrease in membrane and equipment
shipments.

In waste gypsum board recycling, earnings improved, as a result of steady acceptance of waste
gypsum board.

As a result of the above, segment net sales decreased 3.3% compared with the corresponding
period of the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 2,592 million and operating profit decreased 79.0% to ¥ 53
million. The segment reported lower earnings on lower sales.

(3) Explanation concerning Capital Resources and Funding Liquidity
(Analysis of Capital Resources)

The Tokuyama Group recognizes the need to retain a certain level of funds in order to secure the
working capital required to finance its business activities, for priority investments in growth fields
for the purpose of transforming its business portfolio and capital investments for the purpose of
addressing climate change, such as energy saving and measures to reduce GHG emissions, as well
as to promote strategic investments. While the principal method of procuring these funds is to
accumulate cash on hand through the continuous posting of business earnings, the Group will also
pursue other avenues. These include borrowing from financial institutions and the issuing of
unsecured bonds. Furthermore, the Tokuyama Group’s intended capital investment amount for the
consolidated fiscal year under review is ¥ 40,993 million. Plans are in place to utilize cash on hand,
borrowings from financial institutions, and the issuance of unsecured bonds.



(Analysis of Funding Liquidity)

Cash and cash equivalents stood at ¥ 46,070 million as of September 30, 2026. On this basis, the
Tokuyama Group is confident that it maintains more than ample liquidity to promote its business
activities. In addition, Tokuyama has executed revolving credit facility, overdraft, and credit
liquidation agreements with a financial institution. Accounting for these factors, the Company is
more than capable of maintaining a certain level of liquidity should any impediment arise. To
secure liquidity funds in preparation for unforeseen circumstances, we will also set up commitment
lines on an as needed basis.

treatment was finalized during the first quarter of the fiscal year under review.
With this finalization of the provisional accounting treatment, the comparative data included in
the consolidated financial statements for the first quarter of the fiscal year under review reflects a
material revision to the initial allocation of the acquisition cost.
As a result, provisional goodwill totaling ¥60,139 million decreased ¥28,346 million following
finalization of the accounting treatment, to ¥31,792 million. The decrease in goodwill was
primarily attributable to increases of ¥40,649 million in customer-related assets and ¥12,455
million in deferred tax liabilities. In addition, customer-related assets, deferred tax liabilities, and
retained earnings increased ¥39,993 million, ¥12,254 million, and ¥0 million, respectively, while
goodwill decreased ¥27,638 million on a year-on-year consolidated balance sheet basis. There is
no impact on the quarterly consolidated statements of income for the first quarter of the
previous fiscal year. Meanwhile, the amortization period for goodwill is 20 years, while the
amortization period for customer-related assets allocated to items other than goodwill is 31 years.

(Material Subsequent Event)
  (Business Separation)
At a Board of Directors’ meeting held on March 25, 2026, Tokuyama resolved to transfer its
domestic sales business for cement and solidification agents (including shares in certain
consolidated subsidiaries related to the said business) to a wholly owned subsidiary (hereinafter
referred to as “the New Company”) newly established on October 1, 2026 through an absorption-
type split, and subsequently transfer all of the New Company’s outstanding shares to TAIHEIYO
CEMENT CORPORATION. On this basis, the Company entered into a share transfer agreement
with TAIHEIYO CEMENT CORPORATION on the same date as its resolution.
Thereafter, Tokuyama undertook the absorption-type split and share transfer (hereinafter
collectively referred to as “the Transaction”) on October 1, 2026.
The absorption-type split qualifies as a simplified absorption-type split pursuant to Article 784,
Paragraph 2, of Japan’s Companies Act and was undertaken without obtaining approval at the
Company’s General Meeting of Shareholders.


  1. Overview of the Business Separation
   (1) Name of the Separated Entity
        Taiheiyo Cement Corporation
   (2) Content of the Business to be Separated
        Domestic Sales Business for Cement and Solidification Materials
   (3) Main Reasons for the Business Separation
        It aims to further accelerate structural reforms and business transformation,
        strengthen competitiveness, and enhance the allocation of resources to growth
        businesses
   (4) Business Separation Date
        October 1, 2026
   (5) Transfer Price
         37,000 million yen
         * The final transfer amount is expected to be an amount adjusted based on the price at the
         time of execution of the share transfer as stipulated in the share transfer agreement.
   (6) Other
        As far as the cement and solidification agent manufacturing equipment that the Company
        will retain is concerned, following completion of the Transaction, the recoverable amount
        based on future cash flows generated by the asset group after the Transaction is projected
        to fall below the book value. As a result, the Company expects to record an impairment
        loss on non-current assets of approximately ¥12,000 million as an extraordinary loss in
        the third quarter of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027.
        Please note that this amount is an estimate as of the present time and may change in the
        future.
