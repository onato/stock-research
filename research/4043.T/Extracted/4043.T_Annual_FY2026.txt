    My Mission: Developing Seeds Sown and Saplings Planted into
    the “Trunks” That Support Our Future Earnings
I joined Tokuyama in 1989. I would then go on to gain a wide range of experiences, including
working in North America as an engineer at Figaro Engineering Inc., a semiconductor gas
sensor company, before returning to Tokuyama in 2012. After working in planning roles in
such areas as business promotion and corporate planning, I became General Manager of the
Cement Manufacturing Department in 2018. I subsequently oversaw the Eco Business Division
and the Corporate Planning Division as an executive officer and director before becoming the
Company’s Representative Director, President and Executive Officer on April 1, 2026. As General
Manager of the Corporate Planning Division in 2024 and 2025, I led the formulation of Five-Year
Business Plan 2030 (“FBP 2030”). Throughout that process, I was guided by the conviction
that remaining a “good company” for customers, business partners, and employees and being
a “good stock,” an attractive company to shareholders and investors, are not conflicting goals,
and that maximizing corporate value requires us to further enhance our standing as both a “good
company” and a “good stock.” I continue to hold this conviction. Drawing on the experience
and knowledge I have gained across Group companies and business divisions, I am determined
to drive Tokuyama to develop sustainably and devote myself to enhancing both its value as a
company and its appeal as an investment stock.

                                                                                                     created, and I believe that the trust underlying them is the foundation of management. I strive to make
                                                                                                     decisions that maintain capital returns as their fundamental benchmark while taking into account how
                                                                                                     those decisions will affect each stakeholder, especially when deciding on major investments.

                                                                                                     capital strategies to lead the Group toward sustainable growth and higher corporate value.
