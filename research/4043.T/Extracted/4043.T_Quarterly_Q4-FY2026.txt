1. Analysis of Operating Results and Financial Position
  (1) Analysis of Operating Results
  <1> Operating results for the fiscal year under review
  [1] Overview of performance for the fiscal year under review

  Looking at the global economy throughout the fiscal year under review, geopolitical risks escalated
  due to a variety of factors, including the prolonged conflict in Ukraine, and growing uncertainty
  surrounding international trade impacted by such factors as the tariff policies adopted by the U.S.
  This ongoing instability has in fact been exacerbated by crisis conditions in the supply chain for
  raw materials and fuels, including crude oil, owing to the conflict in the Middel East that began in
  February 2026.

  As far as the domestic front is concerned, despite corporate sector capital investment and ongoing
  efforts to lift wages, Japan’s economic recovery has been limited due to the impact of high
  commodity prices and rising interest rates.

  Under these circumstances, the Company has been working on the priority measures of its
  Medium-Term Management Plan 2025: transform business portfolio, contribute to mitigation of
  global warming, and practice socially responsible management.

  In terms of business performance, both net sales and operating profit increased compared with
  the previous fiscal year, on the back of such factors as firm trends in semiconductor-related
  product sales and progress in improving manufacturing costs.

Cost of sales
Cost of sales decreased 4.4%, or ¥ 10,399 million compared with the previous fiscal year, to
¥ 224,530 million. This was mainly due to progress in manufacturing cost improvements.

SG&A expenses
SG&A expenses increased 12.5%, or ¥ 9,753 million compared with the previous fiscal year, to
¥ 87,928 million largely due to the upswing in general and administrative expenses following
inclusion of the TLS Group in the Company’s scope of consolidation.


Operating profit
Operating profit increased 23.5%, or ¥ 7,049 million compared with the previous fiscal year, to
¥ 37,017 million largely on the back of such factors as firm trends in semiconductor-related
product sales and progress in improving manufacturing costs.

Non-operating income/expenses, Ordinary profit
Non-operating income/expenses improved by ¥ 1,565 million compared with the previous fiscal
year owing to a variety of factors, including the increase in foreign exchange gains and share of
profit of entities accounted for using equity method.
As a result of the above, ordinary profit increased 29.1%, or ¥ 8,614 million compared with the
previous consolidated fiscal year, to ¥ 38,203 million.

Extraordinary income/losses, Profit before income taxes, Profit, Profit attributable to
owners of parent
Extraordinary income / losses deteriorated ¥ 3,468 million compared with the previous fiscal year
owing to a variety of factors, including the posting of a provision for contract loss with respect to
non-cancellable long-term power supply contracts with power generation companies, as well as the
absence of the gain on share exchange of affiliated companies posted during the previous fiscal
year.
As a result of the above, profit before income taxes increased 16.4%, or ¥ 5,146 million compared
with the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 36,462 million.

As a result of posting income taxes - deferred of ¥ 8,355 million due to changes in the estimate
of deferred tax assets, profit after deducting income taxes calculated in an appropriate way
decreased 3.2%, or ¥ 742 million compared with the previous fiscal year, to ¥ 22,536 million.

Profit attributable to owners of parent decreased 5.1%, or ¥ 1,182 million compared with the
previous fiscal year, to ¥ 22,205 million.

  Chemicals

  Caustic soda earnings decreased owing to such factors as the decline in export volumes.

  Vinyl chloride monomer and vinyl chloride resin earnings decreased owing to such factors as the

Cement

Despite a decrease in sales volume year-on-year in Japan, cement earnings grew on the back of

such factors as successful efforts to revise domestic sales prices at an appropriate level and progress

in reducing manufacturing costs.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 3.4% compared with the previous fiscal year,

to ¥ 66,881 million and operating profit increased 27.9% to ¥ 9,536million. The segment reported

higher earnings on higher sales.

Electronic & Advanced Materials

Semiconductor-grade polycrystalline earnings increased due to the manufacturing cost
improvements and fluctuations in the product mix.

IC chemical earnings increased. This mainly reflected the increase in sales volumes of high-purity

isopropyl alcohol for electronic manufacturing.

Fumed silica earnings increased. This largely reflected robust sales volumes and successful efforts
to reduce manufacturing costs at Tokuyama Chemicals (Zhejiang) Co., Ltd.

Thermal management material earnings increased. This reflected robust sales volumes of such
applications used for semiconductor production equipment.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 5.3% compared with the previous fiscal year,
to ¥ 91,675 million and operating profit increased 63.6% to ¥ 15,681 million. The segment
reported higher earnings on higher sales.

Life Science

Dental materials earnings grew thanks to an increase in shipments to overseas.

Medical diagnostic systems earnings declined mainly due to an increase in manufacturing costs.

The Company incurred a goodwill amortization expense following inclusion of the TLS Group,
which engages in the in vitro diagnostics and in vitro diagnostic materials businesses, in its scope
of consolidation from the third quarter of the fiscal year under review.

Plastic lens-related materials earnings were in line with the previous fiscal year. While fluctuations
in the product mix had a negative impact, this was largely offset by such factors as the posting of
a reversal of loss on valuation of inventories.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 17.7% compared with the previous fiscal year,
to ¥ 49,387 million and operating profit increased 0.2% to ¥ 7,828 million. The segment reported
higher earnings on higher sales.

Eco Business

Ion exchange membrane earnings decreased owing to the decline in shipments.

In waste gypsum board recycling, earnings increased, as a result of steady acceptance of waste
gypsum board.

As a result of the above, segment net sales increased 17.5% compared with the previous fiscal year,
to ¥ 6,129 million and operating profit amounted to ¥ 655 million (posted ¥ 52 million in the
previous fiscal year). The segment reported higher earnings on higher sales.



<2> Outlook for fiscal 2026

[1] Outlook for operating forecasts for fiscal 2026

In the next fiscal year, we believe that results will be favorably impacted by a variety of factors,
including growth in semiconductor demand, and in particular for cutting-edge products. Having
said this, uncertainty regarding the future remains extremely high due to such risks as supply
disruptions and rising costs stemming from conditions in the Middle East.

Taking into consideration a comprehensive review of prevailing conditions, consolidated
performance forecasts for fiscal 2026 are yet to be determined at this time. Tokuyama will promptly
disclose forecasts going forward when a reasonable assessment can be made.

Following Medium-Term Management Plan 2025, details of which were announced on February
25, 2021, we will continue to focus on the growth areas and work to enhance corporate value under
our next Medium-Term Management Plan covering the period from fiscal 2026 to fiscal 2030.

<3> Procurement of Funds and Liquidity
(Procurement of Funds)
The Tokuyama Group recognizes the need to retain a certain level of funds in order to secure the
working capital required to finance its business activities, for priority investments in growth fields
for the purpose of transforming its business portfolio and capital investments for the purpose of
contributing to the mitigation of global warming, such as rationalization, energy saving, and
measures to reduce GHG emissions, as well as to promote strategic investments. While the
principal method of procuring these funds is to accumulate cash on hand through the continuous
posting of business earnings, the Group will also pursue other avenues. These include borrowing
from financial institutions and the issuing of unsecured bonds.


(Liquidity)
Cash and cash equivalents stood at ¥ 46,466 million as of the end of the fiscal year under review.
On this basis, the Tokuyama Group is confident that it maintains more than ample liquidity to
promote its business activities. In addition, Tokuyama has executed revolving credit facility,
overdraft, and credit liquidation agreements with a financial institution. Accounting for these
factors, the Company is more than capable of maintain a certain level of liquidity should any
impediment arise. To secure liquidity funds in preparation for unforeseen circumstances, we will
also set up commitment lines on an as needed basis.

(3) Achievement of Medium-Term Management Plan 2025
While the Chemicals segment struggled due to deterioration in market conditions, the Cement
segment achieved higher profits, on the back of such factors as successful efforts to revise domestic
sales prices. Although the Electronic & Advanced Materials segment achieved a significant increase
in profit compared with fiscal 2020, the baseline year when formulating the Medium-Term
Management Plan 2025, its performance trended below the initial assumptions due to a temporary
slowdown in the growth of the semiconductor market. The Life Science segment achieved a
significant increase in profit and has maintained solid performance. For the Tokuyama Group as a
whole, due to sluggish performance in the Chemicals and Electronic & Advanced Materials
segments, net sales decreased by 12.6% compared to the plan, and operating profit decreased by
17.7%. As a result, ROE was 8.2%, falling short of the target.

(5) Basic Policy for Profit Distribution and Dividends for Fiscal 2025 and 2026
The Company has positioned the return of profits to its shareholders as one of its most important
management policies. The company will continue to provide stable and continuous returns to
shareholders, based on a comprehensive assessment of factors, including maximizing corporate
value from a medium- to long-term perspective through investment, financing and research and
development for sustainable growth, business performance, financial situation and the cost of
capital. Under this policy, after FY2024, the Company aims for a dividend payout ratio of at least
30% with a target of 3% for the dividend on equity ratio (DOE), which is not easily affected by
performance during any one fiscal year.

The Company's basic policy is to pay dividends from capital surplus twice a year, an interim
dividend and a year-end dividend. The year-end dividend is determined by the General Meeting of
Shareholders and the interim dividend by the Board of Directors. The Articles of Incorporation
stipulate that interim dividends may be paid by a resolution of the Board of Directors in
accordance with Article 454, Paragraph 5 of the Companies Act.

Prior to the application of the aforementioned policy, the Company has decided to pay a year-end
dividend of ¥60 per share for the fiscal year under review. This, together with the interim dividend
of ¥60 per share paid in December 2025, brings the annual dividend to ¥120 per share, an increase
of ¥20 over the previous fiscal year. This in turn brings the dividend payout ratio for the fiscal year
under review to 38.9%. Taking into consideration the uncertain outlook caused by escalating
tensions in the Middle East and the fact that the dividend amount is calculated based on the
assumptions outlined in [1] Outlook for operating forecasts for fiscal 2026” in “<2> Outlook for
fiscal 2026” the dividend amount is yet to be determined at this time.

(Business Combinations, etc.)

At a meeting of the Board of Directors held on April 22, 2025, Tokuyama resolved to acquire all
of the shares of a company established by JSR Corporation (“JSR”) and to include the company
in its scope of consolidation as a subsidiary. Accordingly, the shares were acquired on October 1,
2025.


<1> Overview of business combination
 1. Name and business of acquired company
    Name : JSR-01 Corporation
    * The company’s name was changed to Tokuyama Life Science Corporation on October 1,
      2025.
    Business: In vitro diagnostics business, in vitro diagnostic materials business
            and stock management business


 2. Purpose of business combination
    Under its Medium-Term Management Plan 2025, Tokuyama has identified the “Electronics,”
    “Healthcare,” and “Environment” fields as growth businesses. As a part of efforts to
    transform our business portfolio, our plan is to increase the proportion of consolidated sales
    generated from growth businesses to more than 60% by FY2030. Taking the aforementioned
    into consideration, our intention is to position the newly acquired businesses at the heart of
    our healthcare domain.
    In the Company’s existing diagnosis systems business, our wholly owned subsidiary A&T
    Corporation is working to expand its in vitro diagnostics endeavors and is engaged in the
    research and development of new in vitro diagnostics reagents. In order to further accelerate
    the pace of healthcare domain growth, we recognize the need to generate high levels of
    additional revenue on a sustainable basis by entering new business areas.
    In bringing the aforementioned new businesses into the Tokuyama Group fold and
    complementing our ability to commercialize immunoassay reagents that utilize particles and
    antibodies, we expect to significantly shorten the time required to develop new products and
    to generate synergies with our fundamental technologies. In this manner, we anticipate
    building a highly profitable reagent business within the Group. At the same time, we believe
    that the acquisition of new businesses will enable us to cross-sell both the Company’s and
    A&T’s electrolyte analyzer electrodes, reagents, and other products to our existing customer
    base of hospitals in Japan and Korea as well as to customers in China.
    In a bid to accelerate the pace of business portfolio transformation, we will work diligently
    to develop the in vitro diagnostics business as the cornerstone of the healthcare domain
    centered on the newly acquired businesses going forward.
