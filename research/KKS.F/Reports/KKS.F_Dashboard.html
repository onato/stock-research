DCF Valuation

Kaspi is a deposit-funded lender. This is a discounted distributable-earnings model (attributable net income × payout ratio, discounted at a KZT cost of equity, with a Gordon terminal capped at a 12x exit P/E) — not an owner-FCF DCF. Reported OCF/FCF are loan/deposit working-capital artefacts and are not used. Intrinsic values are computed per share in KZT, then converted to EUR at spot EURKZT (534.26) to match this line's quote currency. The gap between the current EUR 92.80 price and the KZT-CoE-based intrinsic value is driven almost entirely by the discount rate: solving for the rate implied by today's price gives ~15.0% KZT cost of equity (an international investor's blended USD+depreciation rate) versus the house's 19% built from the local KZT curve — see Required Return Table logic below.
