The Q2 2026 margin story

Gross profit rose 17.5% YoY on revenue up only 7.9%, and operating expenses grew just 1.5%. Gross and operating margins expanded sharply while the net margin fell — that divergence is non-operating, and the charts below are arranged so you can see both halves.

Gross vs Operating vs Net Margin

Q2 2026: gross 70.5%, operating 40.2%, net 23.2%. The gap between the operating and net lines in the last quarter is fair-value declines on equity investments, impairments and a 25.5% effective tax rate (14.7% in Q2 2025) — not operating deterioration.

Free Cash Flow vs Net Income

FY2025 FCF included an ~¥8.4bn working-capital inflow equal to roughly 116% of that year's revenue increase — the DCF therefore uses a 35% working-capital capture rate rather than carrying the FY2025 margin forward.

Cash & Equivalents vs Total Debt

The CSV cash column is cash and equivalents only. NetEase's full treasury including time deposits and short-term investments is ~¥167.5bn stated; the DCF haircuts it to ¥150.1bn.
