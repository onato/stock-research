Company Overview

NetEase is a leading Chinese internet and gaming company headquartered in Hangzhou, operating the second-largest gaming business in China behind Tencent. Self-developed games account for roughly 82% of total revenue, with FY2025 gaming revenue of RMB 92.1 billion up 10.2% year-over-year. Beyond gaming, NetEase operates NetEase Cloud Music (HKEX: 9899), AI-driven education platform Youdao (NYSE: DAO), and Yanxuan e-commerce. Dual-listed on NASDAQ (NTES) and HKEX (9999.HK), providing structural insulation against US ADR delisting pressure.

Business Model

Gaming-focused with ~82% of revenue from games and related services. Revenue generated primarily through in-game purchases across a portfolio of long-lived self-developed titles. Strategy centers on 'evergreen' franchises generating recurring revenue for years, reducing dependence on individual launches.

  • Favorable Chinese regulatory environment — NPPA approved 1,771 games in 2025 (up 25% YoY); 2,300+ forecast for 2026

Key Risks

  • [HIGH] Chinese gaming regulations — Dec 2023 draft rules crashed stock 28% in one session; policy can reverse suddenly
  • [HIGH] Gaming revenue concentration — 82% single-industry dependence; failed launches severely impact results
  • [HIGH] US-China geopolitical escalation — HFCAA enforcement revival, ADR delisting risk, IP partnership restrictions
  • [MOD] Non-gaming segment decline — Cloud Music RMB 7.8B (-2.5% YoY), Innovative Businesses RMB 6.8B (-16% YoY)
  • [MOD] International execution risk — Prior Western studio closures (Ouka, Jar of Sparks, Fantastic Pixel Castle, Bad Brain)
  • [MOD] ADR delisting (HFCAA) — HKEX dual listing provides mitigation but not elimination
  • [MOD] Currency exposure — RMB losses of RMB 775.9M in FY2025, RMB 622.1M in Q1 2026

  • Gaming regulatory unpredictability: Dec 2023 incident shows violent overnight policy shifts are possible
  • Live-service competition intensifying: Marvel Rivals vs Overwatch 2, Valorant, Apex Legends with deep marketing budgets
