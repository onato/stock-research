Company Overview

Infratil (NZX/ASX: IFT) is a NZ$16 billion infrastructure investment company that owns and actively manages a portfolio across four sectors: Digital Infrastructure (~57%), Renewable Energy (~22%), Healthcare (~13%), and Airports (~5%). Externally managed by Morrison & Co, targeting 11–15% annualised TSR over a rolling 10-year basis — achieving 18.9% TSR since 1994 inception and 21.0% over 10 years. The company achieved BBB+ (S&P) credit rating in December 2025 and was added to ASX 200 in July 2025.

FY2026 Highlights: Proportionate EBITDAF NZ$989m (+8%), total assets NZ$20,624m (+13%), NAV NZ$16.26/share (+5%), final dividend 13.65 cps. CDC contracted capacity now exceeds 1GW following the landmark 555MW deal.

Business Model

Returns through capital appreciation and distributions from long-term contracted infrastructure. Three-pillar strategy: cash generators (One NZ, Wellington Airport, healthcare), scaling growth (CDC, Longroad), and early-stage platforms (Gurin, Galileo, Mint, Kao Data).

Competitive Position

Competitors: Brookfield Asset Management, Macquarie MIRA, First Sentier / Igneo, AustralianSuper (direct), Morrison & Co other funds

Moat Factors:

  • CDC structural AI data centre advantage: 671MW operating, 572MW under construction, 2.9GW pipeline. 30+ year contracts with hyperscalers/government. Baa2 (Moody's).
  • Long-term contracted revenue: Data centres 10–30 years, renewable PPAs 15–25 years.
  • Morrison & Co origination track record: CDC 35.8% IRR, Longroad 47.3% IRR since entry.
  • Regulatory barriers: One NZ spectrum licences, Wellington Airport natural monopoly, CDC power/planning approvals.
  • BBB+ balance sheet: Saves ~NZ$7–10m p.a. in funding costs; broadens investor access.

Growth Drivers

  • CDC AI supercycle: 555MW contract (30-year, A$680–720m FY27 EBITDAF guided, A$1bn+ FY28, ~A$2bn at full deployment)
  • Longroad US renewable acceleration: 3.5GW operational, 2GW under construction, 2.8GW acquisition pending; US$1bn run-rate EBITDAF target CY29/30
  • Longroad data centre co-location: 4GW+ of grid-connected co-location options at renewable sites
  • Capital recycling: NZ$600m divestments completed; NZ$495m Contact block trade; Qscan sale (NZ$590m independent valuation) underway
  • Kao Data UK expansion: Revenue +28% to £48m; new West London greenfield site acquired for 2029 delivery
  • ASX 200 inclusion + BBB+ rating broadening institutional ownership and valuation discovery

Key Risks

  • HIGH: CDC concentration (43% portfolio) — AI capex cycle disruption, 555MW delivery risk
  • HIGH: Rising corporate net debt NZ$3.2bn (+46% YoY), short 2.7-year average tenor
  • HIGH: Interest rate / discount rate sensitivity on infrastructure asset values
  • MOD: NZ economic weakness affecting One NZ, Wellington Airport, healthcare
  • MOD: Galileo European write-offs (€61.2m, IRR 9.9%), strategy reset risk
  • MOD: Gurin Vanda project: Indonesia export licence still pending
  • MOD: US IRA policy / tariff risk for Longroad; Longroad US$300m equity commitment
  • MOD: FX exposure: 65% offshore (AUD 47%, USD 13%, GBP/EUR 5%)
  • LOW: External management fee structure (net negative NZ$39.4m in FY26)

Bull Case

  • CDC AI supercycle sustained beyond 1GW: ~A$2bn annualised EBITDAF at full deployment; CDC alone could justify multiples of current implied value
  • Dual convergence platform (digital + renewable): Longroad co-location and power synergies create unique moat unavailable to pure-play peers
  • NAV discount narrows: Consensus target NZ$17.21 (~9% above current), with Qscan sale and capital bonds as catalysts
  • 18.9% TSR since 1994 inception, 21.0% over 10 years; FY27 guidance implies step-change to new earnings phase
  • BBB+ rating, multiple capital avenues (capital bonds, divestments, Contact proceeds) improve financial flexibility

Bear Case

  • CDC concentration in an AI cycle: Any hyperscaler capex moderation or 555MW delivery delay has outsized NAV impact
  • FY27 capex NZ$3.8–4.4bn (vs NZ$2.7bn FY26, +53%): FCF constrained for years; equity dilution risk
  • NZ$3.2bn net debt, 2.7-year average tenor: Elevated refinancing risk if rates stay high
  • Longroad IRA policy risk, construction complexity at 2GW p.a. cadence, 2.8GW acquisition pending approvals
  • Multiple sub-portfolio drags: RHCNZ valuation -NZ$73m, Galileo €61.2m write-offs, One NZ essentially flat
