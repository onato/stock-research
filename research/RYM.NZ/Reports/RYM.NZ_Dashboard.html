Revenue & YoY Growth

Statutory Net Income (incl. fair-value movements)

Includes IAS-40 fair-value gains/losses on the $10.9bn investment-property portfolio plus impairments ($243.6m FY2024, $172.9m FY2025). FY2025's -$513.7m and FY2026's -$171.3m are NOT operating losses of that scale -- they reflect muted/negative fair-value movements on a book that is still growing in cash terms. Never used as a P/E input here.

Statutory EPS (cents)

EPS is reported in CENTS, not dollars. Two dilutive raises changed the share base (500m to FY2022, 688m FY2023-24, 1,015.7m FY2025+) -- do not compare FY2021 EPS of 84.6c against FY2026's -16.9c as like-for-like; the denominator more than doubled.

Cash Flow & Deleveraging

The live thesis: falling development CapEx and debt paydown produced the first genuinely positive free cash flow in a decade

Operating Cash Flow, CapEx & Free Cash Flow

Operating Cash Flow is NOT free cash flow for an ORA operator: it is inflated by resident occupation-advance receipts (a financing-like inflow) that must be continuously redeployed into new-unit construction. CapEx collapsed from $535.9m (FY2025) to $221.8m (FY2026) as management paused development -- this, not an operating improvement alone, is what pushed Free Cash Flow to +$112.2m in FY2026.

Total Debt vs Shareholders' Equity

Reported TotalDebt (FY2026 $1,581.0m) differs slightly from management's own 'net interest-bearing debt' KPI ($1,650.8m at H1 FY2026, down from a $2,548m FY2022 peak) due to netting conventions; both tell the same deleveraging story. A new 1.50x Interest Cover Ratio covenant starts Sept-2026.

Dividend per Share

Dividend suspended from FY2023 onward (zero shown/blank thereafter). Management targets a return to sustainable dividends in FY2028 at 20-50% of cash flow from existing operations, conditional on continued execution.

Balance Sheet & Margins

Book value per share (NTA) is the central valuation anchor for this ORA/DMF operator -- see the DCF section below for how it drives the intrinsic value

Shareholders' Equity & Total Assets

Total Assets keep growing (the $10.9bn investment-property portfolio) while Shareholders' Equity has fallen since FY2023 despite two large capital raises, because fair-value losses and impairments have outpaced new capital and retained earnings.

Net Margin (Statutory)

Swings entirely with IAS-40 fair-value movements, not the underlying cost base -- read alongside the Statutory Net Income chart, not as an operating efficiency signal.

Cash & Equivalents

Ryman runs a lean cash balance and manages liquidity through committed bank facilities (refinanced to ~$2bn in Nov-2025, no bank maturities until FY31) rather than holding cash reserves.
