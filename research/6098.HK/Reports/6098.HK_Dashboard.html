Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: The sanity check tripped at the original 10.5% cost of equity: a weighted IV of HK$13.40 implied 112.9x FY2025 EPS against a 47.0x 10yr average (2x ceiling 94x). The fix adds two structural premia on top of the 10.5% base cost of equity (1.8% CGB + 1.25×7.0% ERP), bringing WACC to 15.0% in every scenario: +2.5% for single-counterparty concentration (~49% of managed GFA and ~RMB2.0bn of receivables trace to the distressed, related Country Garden Holdings) and +2.0% for structural single-point-of-failure risk (dual chairmanship, the Feb-2026 SSE sanction, and a RMB1.0bn related-party loan drawn from the cash pile). After the fix, the implied P/E is 86.3x — still above the 47.0x average, but on statutory EPS that is depressed by a RMB969m goodwill impairment and RMB1,088m of acquired-intangible amortisation; on core EPS (excluding those non-cash items) the implied multiple is roughly 11-15x, inside the 10yr range and below the 47x average set in the FY2018-22 growth-premium era. The investment case does not depend on that multiple: market cap at the current price is roughly equal to net cash, so the market is paying close to nothing for operations generating ~RMB2.5bn/yr of core profit — the bear case alone is covered by cash on the balance sheet. The risk to watch is whether that cash stays shareholders' cash: the dividend and buyback-cancellation record says yes, the RMB1.0bn related-party loan to the CGH-linked parent says watch it.
