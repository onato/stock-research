〈Material subsequent events〉
 Terumo Corporation (TSE: 4543) announced that its Board of Directors has decided a two-for-one stock split and an
increase from 1.519 billion to 3.038 billion in the number of authorized shares of the Company's common stock on
February 7, 2019. On April 1, 2019, each shareholder of record at the close on March 31, 2019 will receive one additional
share of common stock for every outstanding share of common stock held.
