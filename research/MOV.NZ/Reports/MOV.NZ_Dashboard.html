MOVe Logistics Group (MOV.NZ)

NZ domestic freight, fuel tankers, warehousing & heavy haulage · FY end 30-Jun · Reports in NZD

Company Overview

MOVe Logistics Group (formerly TIL Logistics Group, rebranded ~2022; NZX Main Board, secondary ASX-listed since a December 2017 reverse listing with Bethunes Investments) is one of New Zealand's largest domestic freight and logistics operators, built from a roll-up of over 150 years of regional trucking and logistics businesses. It operates a nationwide network of ~39 branches, warehouses, depots and crossdocks with 500+ trucks and ~96,000 m² of warehouse space. The company is roughly 18 months into a two-year "Accelerate" cost-out and network-rightsizing turnaround (FY25-FY26), pivoting next to a four-year "New Horizons" roadmap targeting FY28.

Business Model

Revenue is transactional/contract-based freight and logistics fees across five reporting segments, with many multi-year strategic partnerships (e.g. 17-year Z Energy fuel distribution relationship, 500+ retail sites). ~70% of the top 20 clients are retail-sector exposed; one customer contributes >10% of group revenue.
