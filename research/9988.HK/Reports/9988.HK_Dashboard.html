Model approach: FY2026 reported free cash flow of −RMB 46.6bn is a deliberate investment trough, not structural decline — Alibaba deployed roughly RMB 126bn of AI/cloud capex (part of a stated RMB 380bn+ three-year programme) and heavy Taobao Instant Commerce subsidies to compete with Meituan. Anchoring a DCF on this single reported year would systematically undervalue the business. This model instead uses a normalized mid-cycle FCF base of RMB 120,000M (a 10.5% discount to the FY2020–FY2025 average of RMB 134,032M), with scenario-specific growth paths and an exit-multiple cap (7.0x base / 9.5x bull / 4.5x bear, versus normalized EBITDA) that prevents any scenario from implying multiples the market has never historically paid for Alibaba. The sanity check passed: normalized EV/EBITDA of 8.23x and P/B of 1.72x both sit comfortably inside the corrected FY2022–FY2025 historical range (4.62x–9.94x EV/EBITDA in normal years, average ~8.12x; 1.35x–2.30x P/B), computed with the FULL unrestricted investment portfolio (not just cash) netted out of enterprise value in every year — see the revision note below.
ℹ
Revised 2026-07-26 — Net-Debt Correction & BABA Re-Sync: This DCF was rebuilt today to fix a material net-debt error and to re-sync with the same-day corrected BABA model. Net debt flipped from a reported RMB 128,466M (cash-only basis) to a NET CASH position of RMB 260,828M, after correctly netting cash, unrestricted short-term investments, and unrestricted other treasury investments against total debt per the FY2026 20-F MD&A — a swing of roughly RMB 389bn in the equity bridge. Scenario assumptions were re-synced to the corrected BABA model: base FCF growth path 22/18/14/10/7% (was 25/20/15/10/7%), bull FCF base RMB 138,000M with growth 28/23/18/14/10% (was RMB 140,000M, 30/25/20/15/10%), bear FCF base RMB 68,000M with growth 6/5/4/3/2% (was RMB 70,000M, 5/5/3/3/2%), and exit-multiple caps recalibrated to 7.0x/9.5x/4.5x (was 9.0x/12.0x/6.0x) following a corrected, fully-netted historical EV/EBITDA series. Historical normal-year (FY2022–FY2025) EV/EBITDA range is now 4.62x–9.94x (avg ~8.12x), down from a prior cash-only-basis range that overstated every year's multiple. Net effect: base intrinsic value is essentially flat (HK$126.99 vs. HK$127.66 previously, as a less-aggressive base growth path offsets the net-cash uplift), bear intrinsic value rose sharply (HK$58.56 vs. HK$35.42, net cash dominates), and bull intrinsic value fell modestly (HK$182.86 vs. HK$196.54, on the lower bull exit cap). Probability-weighted intrinsic value is HK$123.85 (+12.6% vs. current price), up from HK$121.82 previously.
