Model approach: Reported free cash flow materially overstates owner cash flow for this issuer. Under IFRS 16, lease principal repayments (¥42.8bn in FY2026 and rising) sit in financing rather than operating cash flow, and roughly 24-26% of consolidated profit belongs to non-controlling interests. After charging lease principal, stripping ¥8.1bn of interest and dividend income (net cash is added back to enterprise value separately), and netting out minorities, FY2026 owner free cash flow is ¥22.9bn — about 1.1% of revenue, versus ¥78.0bn reported. Because owner FCF has swung between ¥3.2bn and ¥64.0bn over six years, the DCF is built on a mid-cycle 1.75% owner-FCF margin rather than any single year. The sanity check passed in the conservative direction: implied P/E of 13.7x equals the 10-year average while implied P/B of 0.66x and EV/EBITDA of 2.15x both sit below their 10-year minimums. Framing: this is not a distressed company — the balance sheet holds ¥89bn of net cash — but a low-margin, capital-intensive assembler whose value hinges on the operating margin recovering from 2.6% toward management's 5% ambition. The bull case that assumes exactly that recovery reaches only ¥2,372, barely above the current ¥2,226 price, so the market is already paying for the turnaround.
