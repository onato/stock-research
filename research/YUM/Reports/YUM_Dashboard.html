Model approach: None -- one fix pass considered and declined: the trip is a negative-book-equity artifact, not an overvaluation signal. Shareholders' equity has been negative since the 2016 leveraged recap/Yum China spin, so P/B is meaningless and 'more negative than the historical max' only says the deficit shrank (-7,107 vs -7,926 in FY2018) while the price rose. The multiples that mean something are inside their 10yr ranges: SBC-adjusted P/E 26.5x vs 27.6x average (20.3-41.3), EV/EBITDA 18.7x vs 20.3x average (12.6-27.9), P/S 4.8x vs 5.3x average. The two diagnosis candidates are also artifacts: the -1.3% 'through-cycle revenue CAGR' spans the 2016 Yum China spin-off and the 2016-18 refranchising that halved reported revenue (FY2015 13,105 incl. China -> FY2016 6,356), while post-2020 revenue CAGR is 7.8% and the 6.5% projected sits below it; and a WACC premium would charge risk twice on probability-weighted scenarios (owner-fcf.md section 8). Mid-cycle FCF is not applicable (franchisor royalties are not cyclical and FY0 is already normalized ex-Pizza Hut and ex-special items); the exit multiple already binds at the 20x cap in base and 15x in bear.
