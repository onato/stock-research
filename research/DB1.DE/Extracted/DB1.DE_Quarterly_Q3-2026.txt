Report on Expected Developments

As outlined on pages 73 to 75 of the 2025 Annual Report, the forecast for net
revenue without treasury result is confirmed at around €5.7 billion. However,
based on the performance in the first nine months, we see potential to slightly
exceed this level. EBITDA without treasury result is still expected to be around
€3.1 billion.

Following an initial upward revision at the half-year mark, we are again raising
the forecast for the treasury result – due to the development of key interest
rates and higher cash balances – to more than €0.8 billion (previously: more
than €0.7 billion). Accordingly, for the financial year 2026, we expect total
net revenue of more than €6.5 billion (previously: more than €6.4 billion) and
total EBITDA of around €3.9 billion (previously: more than €3.8 billion).
Growth in operating costs – adjusted for expenses related to the Allfunds trans-
action – is still expected to be around 3 percent.
