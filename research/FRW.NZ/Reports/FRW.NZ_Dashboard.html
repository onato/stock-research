                Verdict: this DCF concludes Freightways is OVERVALUED at the current price. Base case intrinsic value NZ$7.85/share vs a NZ$13.97 market price is -43.8% downside; even the bull case (NZ$12.45) sits -10.9% below market, and the bear case (NZ$4.73) implies -66.1% downside. The model's sanity check (below) confirms this is not a modelling artifact: implied exit multiples remain below Freightways' own 8-10 year trading range even after correcting for lease accounting. Freightways trades at the 92nd percentile of its 10-year price range — the market may be paying for growth, quality and Australian M&A optionality that a plain Gordon-growth perpetuity structurally cannot capture. Read the full valuation philosophy and sanity-check diagnosis below before acting on this figure.
                IFRS16 lease accounting — read before "correcting" net debt back to $589m. This model treats Freightways' depot and vehicle leases as OPERATING (Option A). Lease principal repayments (~$57.7m FY2025, rising to ~$75.6m by FY2030E) are deducted in full from every year of projected owner FCF, and lease interest already sits inside EBIT (post-IFRS16). Because the full economic cost of the leased fleet is already charged through cash flow, the ~$373.7m IFRS16 lease LIABILITY is deliberately excluded from net debt in the equity bridge. Net debt used here = bank borrowings $258.5m − cash $43.3m = $215.2m, not the $588.9m lease-inclusive figure management sometimes quotes. Including the lease liability on top of deducting lease principal from FCF would charge the same obligation twice — that was an error in a prior version of this model, corrected here. Every lease dollar is charged exactly once.
                ⚠ Revenue 3yr/5yr CAGR (13.9%/15.4%) is inflated by the August-2022 Allied Express acquisition and is NOT organic; EPS CAGR is depressed by the FY2024 cyclical trough; equity CAGR is distorted by acquisition scrip. None of the standard CAGR anchors is reliable in isolation — this DCF instead uses a revenue-driven component build (Y1 8.0% decelerating to 4.5% by Y5) calibrated against non-acquisition-year organic growth.
