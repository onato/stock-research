Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: The DCF's sanity check initially tripped: the base case extrapolated LTM owner free cash flow (RMB2,112m), 1.5x the FY2018-25 median, producing an implied P/E of 171.8x against a 10-year average of 30.1x. The model was rebuilt on mid-cycle owner FCF (RMB1,756m, the midpoint of the historical median and LTM), every scenario now carries an EV/EBITDA exit-multiple cap from the stock's own 10-year range, and WACC was raised from 9.0% to 10.0% across all scenarios to reflect the cost of the 9.125% senior notes and HK small/mid-cap risk. After the fix, implied P/B (4.3x), EV/EBITDA (18.1x) and P/Sales (1.8x) sit within historical bounds; implied P/E (130.8x) still runs high only because FY2025 net income (RMB196m) is depressed by heavy interest expense, FX losses and impairments on a highly levered balance sheet — the market price itself implies a similar ~57x multiple on that same depressed earnings base. Read this as a leveraged-recovery and deleveraging thesis (net leverage fell from 3.99x to 2.05x in three periods) rather than a cheap-on-current-earnings thesis.
