FRESENIUS GROUP FIGURES AT A GLANCE
Fresenius is a global healthcare company. Fresenius offers solutions to the social challenges
posed by a growing and aging population and the resulting need for affordable, high-quality healthcare.
Fresenius comprises the Operating Companies Fresenius Kabi and Fresenius Helios as well as
the Investment Company Fresenius Medical Care.
