FRESENIUS GROUP FIGURES AT A GLANCE
Fresenius is a global healthcare group providing products and services for dialysis,
hospitals, and outpatient medical care. In addition, Fresenius focuses on hospital
operations. We also manage projects and provide services for hospitals and other
healthcare facilities.
