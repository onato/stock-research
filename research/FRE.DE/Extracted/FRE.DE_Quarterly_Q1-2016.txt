FRESENIUS GROUP FIGURES AT A GLANCE
Fresenius is a global health care group providing products and services for dialysis,
hospitals, and outpatient medical care. In addition, Fresenius focuses on hospital
operations. We also manage projects and provide services for hospitals and other
health care facilities. In 2015, Group sales were € 28.0 billion. As of March 31, 2016,
more than 220,000 employees have dedicated themselves to the service of health in
about 100 countries worldwide.
