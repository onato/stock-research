Finniss: Resource, Reserve & Production

Lithium-specific drivers -- updated on drilling/reserve releases, not every period

Finniss Mineral Resource & Ore Reserve

JORC Resource/Reserve estimates are only restated when new drilling is released, so this is a step series, not an annual one. The FY2026 annual report cites a further-updated 15.6Mt Ore Reserve across Grants, BP33 and Carlton (33% tonnage increase) not yet captured as a dated data point here.

Spodumene Concentrate Produced

FY2023 is Finniss's only full year of production before the 2024 price-crash suspension. Mining restarted at the Grants open pit in May 2026, first new concentrate was produced in September 2026, and first shipment of new-production spodumene is targeted for the December-2026 quarter -- the tonnes that validate the restart are not yet in this series.

Exploration Spend

Covers Finniss resource-definition drilling and the Blackbeard lithium prospect; spend was cut sharply through the 2024-2026 care-and-maintenance period and is not separately broken out in every filing.
