Collaboration to Develop and Commercialize Relugolix
 Myovant and Pfizer entered into a collaborative development and commercialization agreement on
 relugolix in Dec. 2020

                  Myovant grants Pfizer the right to jointly develop and commercialize relugolix. Myovant/Pfizer will begin
  Outline         co-promoting ORGOVIXTM for advanced prostate cancer in early 2021
                  Myovant records sales revenues. Myovant/Pfizer equally share profits and certain expenses for developing and selling

  Disease Area    Oncology area, Women’s health area
  Territory       North America（U.S., Canada）

                  Upfront $650M, Regulatory milestones for FDA approvals in women’s health $200M
  Consideration
                  Myovant will receive up to $4.2B including sales milestones and above payments

                  Myovant grants Pfizer an exclusive option to commercialize relugolix in oncology outside U.S. and
  Option right    Canada, excluding certain Asian countries
                  Myovant will receive $50M and be entitled to receive double-digit % royalties when the option exercised


  Accounting treatment
       Upfront and regulatory milestones are expected to record deferred revenue on a straight-line basis over a certain
