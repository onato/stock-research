(1) Business Results
    (About the performance indicator of “Core operating profit”)
    With the adoption of International Financial Reporting Standards(IFRS), the Group has set “core operating profit,”
    which shows the Group’  s profitability as its original performance indicator.
    “Core operating profit” is calculated by deducting certain items from operating profit. The deduction items mainly
    include impairment losses, business structure improvement expenses, and changes in fair value of contingent
    consideration.

   Highlights of the Group’s consolidated financial results for the six months of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 are
   as follows:

   ■ Revenue increased by 17.3% year-on-year to 266.5 billion JPY.
   Revenue increased by 39.4 billion JPY year-on-year to 266.5 billion JPY. The increase resulted primarily from expanded
   sales of ORGOVYX® (therapeutic agent for advanced prostate cancer) and GEMTESA® (therapeutic agent for overactive
   bladder) in the U.S., as well as the depreciation of the Japanese yen. These factors more than offset the revenue
   decline resulting from the partial transfer of the Asian business in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year.

   [U.S.]
   Revenue increased by 40.8 billion JPY year-on-year to 197.8 billion JPY. Despite the recording of sales milestone
   revenue from ORGOVYX® in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year, revenue increased primarily owing to
   expanded sales of ORGOVYX® and GEMTESA®, as well as the depreciation of the Japanese yen.

   [Japan]
   Revenue increased by 1.8 billion JPY year-on-year to 44.6 billion JPY. Although the sales partnership for Equa® and
   EquMet® (both therapeutic agents for type 2 diabetes) expired, revenue increased primarily owing to the Company's
   commencement of its own sales of XEPLION® and XEPLION TRI® (both long-acting antipsychotic medications), as well
   as sales growth of TWYMEEG® (therapeutic agent for type 2 diabetes).

   [Others]
   Revenue decreased by 3.2 billion JPY year-on-year to 24.1 billion JPY. Although sales to Europe increased, revenue
   decreased primarily due to the impact of the partial transfer of the Asian business in the corresponding period of the
   previous fiscal year.

   ■ Core operating profit decreased by 56.2% year-on-year to 42.1 billion JPY.
   Although revenue increased, the increase in gross profit was limited, primarily due to changes in the product mix
   resulting from the partial transfer of the Asian business. Selling, general and administrative expenses increased
   primarily due to the increase in sales promotion expenses aimed at expanding revenue in the U.S. Research and
   development expenses also increased due to accelerated clinical development in oncology and the advancement of
   clinical programs in psychiatry and neurology. These factors, together with the absence of the gains on sales of shares
   of subsidiaries recorded in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year following the partial transfer of the Asian
   business, resulted in a significant decline in core operating profit.

(2) Financial Condition
    Total assets increased by 46.5 billion JPY from the previous fiscal year-end to 851.1 billion JPY.
    Non-current assets decreased by 11.6 billion JPY from the previous fiscal year-end, primarily due to a decline in
    goodwill from foreign currency translation, as well as the return of retirement benefit trust assets.
    Current assets increased by 58.1 billion JPY from the previous fiscal year-end, owing to an increase in cash and cash
    equivalents resulting mainly from the public offering conducted during the six months ended September 30, 2026, as
    well as an increase in trade and other receivables driven by strong sales in the U.S.
    Liabilities decreased by 92.2 billion JPY from the previous fiscal year-end to 419.9 billion JPY as a result of decreases
    in borrowings and other current liabilities.
    Total equity increased by 138.6 billion JPY from the previous fiscal year-end to 431.1 billion JPY as a result of
    increases in share capital and capital surplus following the public offering conducted during the six months ended
    September 30, 2026, and an increase in retained earnings following the recording of net profit.
    The ratio of equity attributable to owners of the parent to total assets as of September 30, 2026 was 50.7%.

(3) Cash Flows
    Cash flows provided by operating activities amounted to 16.7 billion JPY, representing a year-on-year decrease of JPY
    1.4 billion. This was primarily due to a decline in net profit, despite the return of retirement benefit trust assets during the
    six months ended September 30, 2026.
    Cash flows provided by investing activities amounted to 0.5 billion JPY, reflecting a year-on-year decrease in net cash
    inflow of 27.1 billion JPY. This was primarily due to a decrease in proceeds from the loss of control of subsidiaries in the
    previous fiscal year.
    Cash flows provided by financing activities amounted to 17.6 billion JPY, reflecting a year-on-year increase in net cash
    inflow of 61.5 billion JPY. This was due to proceeds from the issuance of shares during the six months ended
    September 30, 2026, despite an increase in repayments of borrowings.
    After taking into account the effect of exchange rate changes on cash and cash equivalents, the balance of cash and
    cash equivalents as of September 30, 2026 was 77.8 billion JPY, which represents an increase of 33.4 billion JPY from
    the previous fiscal year-end.

(4) Research and Development Activities
    Research and development expenses for the six months ended September 30, 2026 amounted to 23.8 billion JPY
    (increased by 35.6% year-on-year). Please note that, excluding the business structure improvement expenses recorded
    in the corresponding period of the previous fiscal year, research and development expenses on a core basis would have
    increased by 35.9% year-on-year.
    In the oncology area, a pivotal Phase 2 monotherapy study of enzomenib (product code: DSP-5336) in patients with
    acute myeloid leukemia with NPM1 mutation was initiated in April 2026. In June 2026, enrollment of the number of
    patients required for the interim analysis was completed in a pivotal Phase 2 monotherapy study of enzomenib in
    patients with acute leukemia with KMT2A rearrangement.

 2.Reasons for the Revisions
   In these revised forecasts, the Company has changed its full-year foreign exchange assumption (average exchange
   rate for the period) from 155.00 JPY per USD in the previous forecast to 157.00 JPY per USD.
   Revenue has been revised upward by 25.0 billion JPY from the previous forecast to 565.0 billion JPY, reflecting the
   expectation that sales in the U.S., led by GEMTESA®, will exceed the initial forecast, as well as an increase arising from
   the revised foreign exchange assumption.
   Core operating profit has been revised upward by 14.0 billion JPY from the previous forecast to 105.0 billion JPY,
   reflecting an expected increase in gross profit from higher revenue, while higher selling expenses associated with
   further commercial expansion are also expected.
   Reflecting the increase in core operating profit, operating profit has been revised upward by 14.0 billion JPY from the
   previous forecast to 104.0 billion JPY, and net profit attributable to owners of the parent has been revised upward by
   17.0 billion JPY to 94.0 billion JPY.

(Segment Information)
   The Group has historically managed its business under three reportable segments (Japan, North America, and Asia),
   but beginning with the six months ended September 30, 2026, it manages its business as a single operating segment.
   In the previous fiscal year, the Group transferred assets, including patent rights for the three key products in the U.S.,
   from its wholly-owned subsidiaries, Sumitomo Pharma Switzerland GmbH and Urovant Sciences GmbH, to the
   Company, thereby establishing a structure under which the Company is directly involved in the business operations of
   these products. As a result, the Representative Director, President now makes decisions on the allocation of
   management resources and evaluates performance for the pharmaceutical business as a whole.
   Accordingly, the reportable segments have been consolidated into a single segment, Pharmaceutical Business, and
   disclosures of revenue and performance by the Group's former reportable segments have been omitted.
