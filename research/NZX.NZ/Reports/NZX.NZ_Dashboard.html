NZX Limited (NZX.NZ)

Operator of New Zealand's securities exchange · Markets, Smart (funds management) & Wealth Technologies · Dec FY end · Reporting in NZD (millions)

Company Overview

NZX Limited operates New Zealand's only registered securities exchange, providing listing, trading, clearing, settlement, depository and data services across equity, debt, funds, derivatives (dairy and equity) and energy markets. Beyond exchange operations, NZX owns Smart (formerly Smartshares), New Zealand's leading passive funds manager and KiwiSaver/ETF provider, and NZX Wealth Technologies (NZXWT), a wealth-administration platform used by financial advisers. NZX is a genuinely small-cap business (~NZ$470m market cap) generating roughly NZ$130-145m of annual revenue, and it is simultaneously the country's front-line market regulator (via NZ RegCo) and a listed company subject to that same regulatory framework.

Business Model

Three-segment model: Markets (exchange listing fees, secondary trading/clearing/depository, dairy derivatives, Information Services) is the largest, highest-margin segment (FY2025 EBITDA margin ~64%) but its revenue is flat-to-declining and cyclical; Funds Management (Smart) earns FUM-based fees on ETFs, KiwiSaver and managed funds and has been the fastest-growing segment (+18% revenue FY2025, +19% H1-2026); Wealth Technologies (NZXWT) earns SaaS/platform administration fees from financial advisers based on funds under administration and is growing fastest in percentage terms (+26% H1-2026) off a smaller base.

    Competitive Position

    Competitors: ASX (primary threat), bank/boutique KiwiSaver & managed-fund providers (Milford, Fisher Funds, ANZ Investments, Kiwi Wealth, Simplicity, Booster), QuayStreet Group, FNZ & Class (wealth platforms), private equity/offshore capital markets, SGX (dairy-derivatives partner & global competing venue).

    Market position: Sole operator of NZ's licensed securities exchange - a legal monopoly on domestic listing/trading infrastructure - over a genuinely small, low-liquidity market (~NZ$244b total market cap, ~56% of GDP). ASX's daily traded value in NZ-linked stocks already exceeds total NZX cash-market turnover, and ASX's capital pool is roughly 4x New Zealand's.
