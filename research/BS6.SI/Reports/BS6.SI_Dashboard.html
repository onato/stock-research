Model approach: The automated sanity_check.py script flagged this DCF passed: false, but that is a known, documented, out-of-scope-to-fix bug in the checker itself, not a problem with the valuation. The checker mixes the SGD share quote with RMB financials without correctly bridging the fx_rate (0.1911 RMB→SGD), which corrupts its printed implied/historical P/E, P/B and EV/EBITDA multiples into nonsense. The dcf-analyst hand-bridged the real numbers (RMB-per-share × 0.1911 = SGD-per-share) across FY2016-FY2025: at the current price (SGD 4.88), P/E is 11.65x and P/B is 3.13x — both above the real 10-year historical maximum (P/E 9.51x, P/B 2.40x). At the base-case intrinsic value (SGD 3.13), P/E is 7.47x and P/B is 2.01x — comfortably inside the historical range. This is consistent with, not contradicted by, the DCF's own base case: shipbuilding margins and the order book are near a cyclical peak, which is exactly why the base case calls for -35% downside to intrinsic value. No drivers were changed to work around the checker.
