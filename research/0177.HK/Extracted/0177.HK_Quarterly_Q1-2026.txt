   total operating revenue of approximately RMB4,527,556,000, representing
   a year-on-year decrease of 5.31%. The Company achieved a total operating
   income (excluding construction revenue) of approximately RMB2,981,527,000,
   representing a decrease of 0.09% as compared to the corresponding period of last
   year.

   costs for January to March 2026 totalled approximately RMB2,870,602,000,
   representing a year-on-year decrease of 8.10%, and excluding construction costs,
   the Group’s operating costs was approximately RMB1,324,573,000, representing
   a decrease of approximately 0.14% as compared with the same period of the
   previous year, which was mainly due to year-on-year decrease in cost of oil
   procurement and cost of real estate sales.

   January to March 2026, representing a year-on-year increase of 13.80%.
