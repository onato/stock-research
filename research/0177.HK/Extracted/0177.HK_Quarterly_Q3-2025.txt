   1.   Business Progress

        (1) Business Progress from July to September

            From July to September 2025, various businesses of the Company
            and its subsidiaries (collectively referred to as the “Group”) achieved
            a total operating income of approximately RMB3,575,657,000,
            representing a decrease of 12.76% as compared to the corresponding
            period of last year; the Group achieved a total operating income of
            approximately RMB3,233,007,000 excluding construction revenue,
            representing a decrease of 1.72% as compared to the corresponding
            period of last year. In particular, revenue from toll collection business
            amounted to approximately RMB2,597,177,000, representing a decrease
            of approximately 0.05% year on year; revenue from ancillary business
            amounted to approximately RMB416,440,000, representing a decrease
            of approximately 7.29% year on year, mainly due to the decrease in
            selling price per unit of oil product; revenue from real estate business
            carried-forward realized approximately RMB3,229,000, representing
            a decrease of approximately 55.87% year on year, mainly due to
            the year-on-year decrease in delivery scale of its subsidiaries’ real
            estate projects; revenue from electricity sales realized approximately
            RMB171,631,000, representing a decrease of approximately 5.60%
            year on year, mainly due to the decrease in the amount of electricity
            connected to the offshore wind power projects of YS Energy Company;
            revenue from construction achieved approximately RMB342,650,000,
            representing a year-on-year decrease of approximately 57.64%; and
            revenue from other businesses achieved approximately RMB44,530,000,
            representing a year-on-year decrease of approximately 15.89%, mainly
            due to the adjustments to the settlement model for the subsidiary’s
            advertising operations and the removal of certain advertising billboards.

(2) Business Progress from January to September

    From January to September 2025, the Group accumulated total
    operating income of approximately RMB12,981,379,000, representing
    a decrease of approximately 7.66% as compared to the same period last
    year; the Group accumulated total operating income of approximately
    RMB9,115,681,000 excluding construction revenue, representing a
    decrease of approximately 1.25% as compared to the same period
    last year. Of which, road toll revenue realized approximately
    RMB7,201,489,000, representing a year-on-year increase of
    approximately 1.03%; revenue from ancillary businesses achieved
    approximately RMB1,244,360,000, representing a year-on-year
    decrease of approximately 3.93% as a result of the year-on-year
    decrease in selling price per unit of oil product; revenue from property
    sales carry-forward amounted to approximately RMB22,260,000,
    representing a year-on-year decrease of approximately 83.34% as a
    result of a smaller scale of housing deliveries as compared to that of the
    corresponding period of the previous year; revenue from power sales
    achieved approximately RMB506,852,000, representing a year-on-year
    decrease of approximately 4.35%, which was mainly attributable to
    a year-on-year decrease in the on-grid electricity consumption of
    offshore wind power project of a subsidiary; revenue from construction
    amounted to approximately RMB3,865,699,000, representing a
    year-on-year decrease of approximately 19.92%, which was attributable
    to the year-on-year decrease of investment in construction of roads
    and bridges; other businesses realized revenue of approximately
    RMB140,720,000, representing a decrease of approximately 2.54% as
    compared to the same period last year, which was mainly due to the
    adjustments to the settlement model for the subsidiary’s advertising
    operations and the removal of certain advertising billboards.
