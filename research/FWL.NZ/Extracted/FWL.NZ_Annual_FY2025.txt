Results of FWL Annual Meeting – Revised

At Foley Wines Limited’s Annual Meeting of Shareholders, held in Martinborough on 18 November
2025, shareholders were asked to vote on four ordinary resolutions, all of which were supported by the
Board.

As required by NZX Listing Rule 6.1, all voting was conducted by a poll.

All four resolutions were passed by shareholders.
