During this term of fiscal year 2017, from January 1 to March 31, the US economy seemed revived which is one of the trigger for the
recovery of world economy. In spite of continuing a mild upswing, Europe economy has been concerning about central political
events including prospects for Brexit. In emerging countries, mainly China economic has been concerned with growth slowdown.
The world economy has been going through over not only business hopes but also variety of risk impacts, such as geopolitical risk.
Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend with a corporate profits improving and a variety of healthy economic index.
On the other hand, there are concerns such as world economy downward mainly due to US economic condition. Japan economy has
continuously remained uncertainty.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related services have been
still standing out from IT industry market. Both worldwide and domestic PC shipments, which have been decreasing, remained
underrun in consumer demands continuously. On the other hand, OS replacement and gradual growth in business users lead
slowdown in number of shipping decline rate from the same period in prior year. IT investment shows picking up in proportion to
business recovery with increased interests in cloudization, big data, and IoT.
In the computer security industry, while Advanced Persistent Threat (APT) attack has plagued the entire world continuously, cyber
terrorism targeting specific companies or state institution, etc. which related to some cases of identity theft in both business client
information and personal private information have been also happened one after another and remained high-visibility incidents at
home and abroad. In addition, ransomware became a hot topic in this term continuously.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, cloud related business shows strong growth and APT related business has also maintained its
growth continuously in enterprise business sales. Consumer business has kept its sales well mainly due to renewal business growth.
As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 14,466 million yen (8.3% increase from the same period in the
previous year.)
For North America region, despite of continuation of trend with decrease in number of users in consumer business sales, enterprise
business sales have showed surplus growth mainly due to the contribution of TippingPoint business which has finalized in March
2016. As a result, sales for this period in North America region was 9,702 million yen (40.6% increase from the same period in
previous year.)



                                                                    2
In EMEA region sales, both cloud related business and APT related business showed growth and enterprise business sales have been
leading this region's sales. As the result, in spite of the strong yen impact, net sales in this region were 5,885 million yen (6.4%
increases from the same period in previous year).
In the Asia Pacific region sales, with the large growth of both cloud related business and APT related business, as well as traditional
products sales achieved a great expansion to lead enterprise business sales in this region. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia
Pacific region amounted to 4,163 million yen (9.7% increase from the same period in the previous year).
In the Latin America region sales, especially APT related business and cloud related business showed large growth. In addition,
traditional products sales also showed large expansion. From these facts, enterprise business sales have been leading this region's
sales. As the result, net sales in this region were 1,032 million yen (39.4% increases from the same period in previous year).
As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first Quarter fiscal year end of 2017 came to 35,250 million yen (16.2% increase from
the same period in previous year.)


Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 26,530 million yen (22.8% increase from the same period in previous year) mainly due to
substantial increase in people costs, stock option related expenses, and the acquisitions amortization related to TippingPoint. As a
result, consolidated operating income for this period was 8,720 million yen (0.1% increase from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 7,833 million yen (9.9% decrease from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to increase in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was 5,430
million yen (13.8% decrease from the same period in previous year).


 (2) Qualitative Information on the Consolidated Financial Positions
Cash and bank deposits at the end of this period amounted to 78,672 million yen, a decrease of 5,837 million yen from FY2016
annual closing. Although investment securities increased significantly, total assets at the end of this period were 305,751 million yen,

substantial increase in accounts payable, other, etc.

to the dividends paid and decrease in foreign currency translation adjustments, etc.

    (Business mergers, etc.)
 Significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost included in comparative data


   In its consolidated accounts for the first quarter of the previous fiscal year, the Company used a provisional accounting
treatment to account for the business combination of TippingPoint business conducted on March 8, 2016. However, it was
 determined at the end of the previous fiscal year.
   In line with this final determination, the comparative data in the consolidated financial statements for the first quarter of
 the fiscal year under review reflect a significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost of TippingPoint
 business. The main change was the allocation of JPY11,793 million in customer lists accounted for as intangible fixed assets.
 As a result, the provisional calculation of goodwill related to the TippingPoint business acquisition of JPY 31,743 million was
 reduced by JPY 10,337million to JPY 21,406 million. In addition, JPY 5,073 million was allocated to deferred revenue.
   Owing to the increase in net revenue, reducation in the amount of goodwill and an increase in amortizatino of intangible
fixed assets, the consolidated statement of income for the first quarter of the previous fiscal year has been adjusted as follows;
operating income, ordinary income, and net income before taxes were each increased by JPY 497 million, net income and profit
attributable to owners of parent were each increased by JPY 637 million.
