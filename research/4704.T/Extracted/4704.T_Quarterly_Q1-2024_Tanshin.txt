During this term of fiscal year 2024, from January 1 to March 31, the world economy has been going through with attention to global
monetary policy trends. Associated with it, there were risks including exchange rate fluctuations, the steep rise in fuel import prices
due to the uncertainties in prolonging geopolitical risks, etc. in a prospect.
In the worldwide Information Technology industry, according to the report by 3rd party, the forecasts worldwide IT spending to grow
6.8% to $5 trillion in 2024. The overall for weak IT spending growth rate than previous forecast’s is among CIO's change fatigue,
including GenAI which was risen faster than expected and exploded awareness.
In the cyber security industry, continuously multitude of cyber attacks that target specific national institutions, etc., leaking damage of
sensitive business information, and targeted attacks including crypto assets drain in specific companies, organizations, and ransomware
have been still standing out. Going through concerns such threat changes including new security risks coming from GenAI, the trend
of cyber security consciousness has been required regardless business or individual into a new daily life.
Under such an environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, in the enterprise business, SOC operations security showed substantial growth besides on
demands for Trend Micro Vision One (“Vision One.”)               In spite of the weak in IT infra operation security, cloud operation
securitymade a big contribution in this region sales. In consumer business has been also growing continuously under the sustained
good sales in mobile channel shops, except the weakness in security for PC. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region
amounted to 21,361 million yen (2.1% increase from the same period in the previous year) increase to.
For Americas region, in the enterprise business, SOC operations security showed the highest growth rate in all regions, and Managed
Services security performed well. On the other hand, cloud operation security was weak. In addition, there were an adjustment for
sales that were understated in previous years and a weak yen impact, net sales in this region were 14,388 million yen (7.0% increase
from the same period in the previous year) increase to
In Europe region sales, in the enterprise business, despite IT infra operation security was weak, SOC operations security showed a
significant growth to lead this region sales, and Managed Services security performed well. In addition, there was a weak yen impact,
net sales in this region were 13,679 million yen (24.2% increase from the same period in the previous year) in the highest double-digit
growth rate in all regions.
In the Asia Pacific region, also the enterprise business overall showed growth. Especially IT infra operation security contributed
significantly to this region’s sales. Besides, both SOC operations security and Managed Services security showed growth well.
Locally, Australia, Middle East, and Taiwan area led this region’s sales. In addition of the depreciation of yen impact, as a result, net
sales for this period in Asia Pacific region amounted to 16,501 million yen (23.8% increase from the same period in the previous year)
in a double-digit growth rate.
As a result, the consolidated net sales for this period (this term of fiscal year 2024, from January 1 to March 31,) marked 65,931
million yen (12.3% increase from the same period in previous year.)

 (2) Application of Simplified or Special Accounting Procedures
   (Calculation of income tax expenses)
   Multiply net income before tax for this period (3 months) by effective tax rate, which is rationally calculated based on projected
   annual profit and its taxes taking into consideration of deferred tax accounting. In addition, deferred tax expense is included in
   income taxes.
   However, if the calculation of tax expenses using the effective tax rate results in a markedly unreasonable outcome, tax expenses
   are calculated by using the statutory effective tax rate after adding and subtracting important differences that do not fall under
   temporary differences to and from profit before income taxes.
