During this term of fiscal year 2016, from January 1 to March 31, though the world economy has been showing
moderate recovery in mainly developed countries economy, it still remains uncertain while the trend of US economy
which must be cautious to raise interest rates, economic growth slowdown of emerging countries mainly China, and
showing the demands of the fall in crude prices and a turmoil in the financial markets.       The world economy has
been going through in those concerns.
Under the government's various economic policies, Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend with a
corporate profits improving and a variety of healthy economic index. On the other hand, there are still concerns
such as world economy downward mainly due to China economic impact, and impact of exchange rate fluctuations
after introducing a policy of negative interest rates. The Japan economy has also still remain uncertainty.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related
services have been still standing out from IT industry market continuously. On the other hand, the worldwide PC
shipments remain underrun from the same period in previous year due to local currencies against the U.S. dollar
and an inventory buildup from holiday sales in the fourth quarter of 2015, etc. Either the domestic PC shipments
underrun from the same period in previous year. Meanwhile, domestic cooperate IT investments could be expected
to show positive yearly growth based on moderate recovery trend in Japan economy.
In the computer security industry, while APT attack has plagued the entire world continuously, cyber terrorism
targeting specific companies or state institution, etc. which related to some cases of identity theft in both business
client information and personal private information have been also happened one after another and remained high-
visibility incidents at home and abroad. In addition, ransomware became a hot topic in this term continuously.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:

With regards to sales in Japan region, APT related business shows strong growth and cloud related business has also
maintained its growth continuously in enterprise business sales. Consumer business has kept well due to monthly
billing business, etc. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 13,350 million yen (3.3%
increase from the same period in the previous year.)
For North America region, despite enterprise business sales have showed surplus growth, it performed limitedly. In
regard to the contribution of TippingPoint business acquisition, which has finalized in March 2016, was de-minimis.
On the other hand, consumer business sales have not still performed well with decrease in number of the users. In
addition, with affected by the strong Japanese yen, sales for this period in North America region was 6,606
million yen (4.2% decrease from the same period in previous year.)
In EMEA region sales, traditional products sales showed starting its contribution to whole enterprise business in
this region build on both APT related and cloud related businesses achievements. In spite of affected by the strong
yen, sales for this period in this region was 6,219 million yen (8.3% increase from the same period in previous year).
In the Asia Pacific region sales, though mainly Australia has been leading in this region's sales, both enterprise and
consumer business sales were decreased due to China business transfers in last November and affected by strong
yen. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific region amounted to 2,952 million yen (18.5% decrease from
the same period in the previous year).
In the Latin America region sales, especially cloud related business showed largely growth and enterprise business

 (3) Change of the Accounting Policy, Change of Accounting Estimation and Restatement
     Change of the Accounting Policy
       From the first qurter of this fiscal year, the Comapny has applied the "Revised Accounting Standard
     for Business Combinations" (ASBJ Statement No.21; September 13, 2013), the "Revised Accounting Standard
     for Consolidated Financial Statements" (ASBJ Statement No.22; September 13, 2013) and the "Revised
     Accounting Standard for Business Divestitures" (ASBJ Statement No.7; September 13, 2013). The
     accounting standards reflect a switch in methodology with respect to the posting to capital surplus of
     differences arising from changes in equity in a subsidiary by the parent company in cases which control
     continues, and the posting of acquisition-related expenses as expenses for the consolidated fiscal year
     in which they are incurred. For business combinations occurring after the start of the first quarter,
     the Company has switched to a method in which revisions to the distribution of the cost of acquisition
     due to decisions regarding temporary accounting treatment are reflected in the quarterly consolidated
     financial statements applicable to the quarterly periond in which the date of the business combination
     falls. Additionally, the Company has made changes in the presentation of quarterly net income, etc. and
     the presentation of a switch from minority interests to non-controlling interests. Financial statements
     for the first quarter of the previous fiscal year and for the previous fiscal year have been
     reconfigured to reflect these changes in presentation.
       In the consolidated statement of cash flows, cash flow related to the acquisition or sale of shares
     of subsidiaries not affecting the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from financing
     activities", while cash flows related to expense arising from acquisition of shares of subsidiaries
     affecting the scope of consolidation or expenses arising from acquisition or sales of shares of
     subsidiaries not affecting the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from operating
     activities".
       With respect to application of the Accounting Standards regarding Business Combinations, the
     transitional treatment as prescribed in article 58-2 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business
     Combinations, article 44-5 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Cosnolidated Financial Statement, and
     article 57-4 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business Diventiture has been applied from the
     beginning of this first quarter of the fiscal year, and will continue to be applied going forward.
        The effect to the consolidated financial statements for the first quarter of this fiscal year is
     minor.
