During this term of fiscal year 2016, from January 1 to June 30, though the world economy has been showing moderate recovery in
mainly developed countries economy, uncertainty still remains such as the trend of US economy which require careful consideration to
raise interest rates, the risk of Brexit, economic growth slowdown of emerging countries mainly China, and confusion of the financial
markets caused by crude prices and a turmoil. The world economy has been going through in those concerns.
Under the government's various economic policies, Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend with a corporate profits
improvement and economic index health. On the other hand, there are still concerns such as world economy downward and the
impact after introducing a policy of negative interest rates. The Japan economy has also still remained uncertainty.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related services have been still
standing out from IT industry market. On the other hand, the worldwide PC shipments remain underrun from the same period in
previous year except US performed best in typical PC buying season especially for enterprise and education market.
In the computer security industry, while APT attack has plagued the entire world continuously, cyber terrorism targeting specific
companies or state institution, etc. which related to some cases of identity theft in both business client information and personal private
information have been also happened one after another and remained high-visibility incidents at home and abroad. In addition,
ransomware became a hot topic in this term continuously.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, APT related business showed strong growth and cloud related business has also maintained its
growth continuously in enterprise business sales. Consumer business has showed slightly increase caused by keeping the number of
users.   As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 26,681 million yen (3.2% increase from the same period in
the previous year.)
For North America region, consumer business sales have not still performed well with decrease in number of the users. On the other
hand, enterprise business sales have showed surplus growth with mainly cloud business. In addition, TippingPoint business has been
also contributive. In spite of the strong yen, sales for this period in North America region was 14,600 million yen (1.9 % increase
from the same period in previous year.)
In EMEA region, despite the strong Japanese yen, traditional product sales showed starting its contribution to whole enterprise
business established from both APT related and cloud related businesses achievements. As the result, sales for this period in this
region was 11,998 million yen (3.5% increase from the same period in previous year).
In the Asia Pacific region, though Enterprise business showed both APT related and cloud related businesses’ highly growth in local
currency with mainly led by Australia, China business transfers in last November affected. In addition to strong yen, both enterprise
and consumer business sales decreased. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific region amounted to 6,067 million yen
(18.1% decrease from the same period in the previous year).

In the Latin America region, cloud related business showed especially large growth in enterprise business sales. As the results, in
spite of the strong yen impact, net sales in this region were 1,571 million yen (5.1% increase from the same period in previous year)
which mainly led by Brazil.


As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first half of fiscal year 2016 marked 60,919 million yen (0.4% increase from the same
period in previous year.)
Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 46,813 million yen (3.6% increase from the same period in previous year.) Although
mainly stock option related expense decreased, Amortization of goodwill increased. As a result, consolidated operating income for
this period was 14,106 million yen (9.0% decrease from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 12,902 million yen (22.1% decrease from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to substantial increase in Foreign exchange loss, etc. and the consolidated net income for this period was 8,612 million yen
(17.5% decrease from the same period in previous year).

 (3) Change of the Accounting Policy, Change of Accounting Estimation and Restatement
   (Change of the Accounting Policy)
   (Change in accounting standared for business combinations etc)
     From the first quarter of this fiscal year, the Comapny has applied the "Revised Accounting Standard for Business
   Combinations" (ASBJ Statement No.21; September 13, 2013), the "Revised Accounting Standard for Consolidated Financial
   Statements" (ASBJ Statement No.22; September 13, 2013) and the "Revised Accounting Standard for Business Divestitures"
   (ASBJ Statement No.7; September 13, 2013). The accounting standards reflect a switch in methodology with respect to the
   posting to capital surplus of differences arising from changes in equity in a subsidiary by the parent company in cases which
   control continues, and the posting of acquisition-related expenses as expenses for the consolidated fiscal year in which they are
   incurred. For business combinations occurring after the start of the first quarter, the Company has switched to a method in which
   revisions to the distribution of the cost of acquisition due to decisions regarding temporary accounting treatment are reflected in
   the quarterly consolidated financial statements applicable to the quarterly period in which the date of the business combination
   falls. Additionally, the Company has made changes in the presentation of quarterly net income, etc. and the presentation of a
   switch from minority interests to non-controlling interests. Financial statements for the first quarter of the previous fiscal year and
   for the previous fiscal year have been reconfigured to reflect these changes in presentation.
     In the consolidated statement of cash flows, cash flow related to the acquisition or sale of shares of subsidiaries not affecting
   the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from financing activities", while cash flows related to expense arising
   from acquisition of shares of subsidiaries affecting the scope of consolidation or expenses arising from acquisition or sales of
   shares of subsidiaries not affecting the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from operating activities".
     With respect to application of the Accounting Standards regarding Business Combinations, the transitional treatment as
   prescribed in article 58-2 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business Combinations, article 44-5 (4) of the Accounting Standard
   for Consolidated Financial Statement, and article 57-4 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business Divestiture has been applied
   from the beginning of this first quarter of the fiscal year, and will continue to be applied going forward.
     The effect to the consolidated financial statements of this fiscal year is minor.


   (Change in depreciation method)
          In accordance with an amendment of Japan's Corporation Tax Act, the Company has applied the "Practical Solution on
       a change in depreciation method due to Tax Reform 2016" (Practical Issues Task Force No.32 issued on June 17, 2016)
       from the second quarter of this fiscal year and has changed its depreciation method for facilities attached to buildings and
       structures acquired on or after April 1, 2016, from the declining balance method to the straignt-line method.
           The effect of this change on the consolidated financial statements of this fiscal year is minor.


 (4) Additional information
   (Influence of changing income tax rate)
   Partial Amendment of the Income Tax Act, etc. is announced on March 31, 2016 and income tax rate was to be lowered from
   consolidated fiscal year starting on or after April 1, 2016. The income tax has increased 389 million yen by this change.
