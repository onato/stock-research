During this term of fiscal year 2020, from January 1 to March 31, the world economy which various economic activities are limited
caused by COVID-19 pandemic has been going through toward the end of the year. It is projected to 3% contraction in 2020 in IMF
world economic outlook. The global recession seems to be inevitable.
Though Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend until the beginning of 2020, it has going through the future prospects
look increasingly bleak caused by COVID-19 pandemic mentioned above including voluntarily refrain from economic activities
government called, and the world economic impacts, etc.
In the worldwide Information Technology industry, corporates’ interests in new fields including IoT Internet of Things, AI, and fully
introducing 5G networks, etc. have been leading IT spending. In addition, cloud computing and related services seems to be expected
increase more demands from both home and abroad in IT industry market for explosion in both work from home and new channels of
communication under the COVID-19 pandemic.
In the cyber security industry, multitude of cyber terrorism that targets specific national institutions, etc., a spate of identity theft in
business client information, and targeted attacks including cryptocurrency drain in specific companies, organizations have been still
coming across occasionally. Moreover, there was a domestic issued has revealed that a job-hunting site to provide a wide range of
employment information and services to registered users had sold job-seeker-data without the clients’ consent. After such a recent
high-visibility incident, it has been required showing more rigorous approach to information management to the collection-side. From
now on, various threats are predicted to increase in more sophisticated attacking, as like targeted to IoT devices and its environment,
unauthorized access in cashless payment, and regardless both of individual and business in changes to day-to-day life since COVID-19
pandemic, etc.
 Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, both enterprise and consumer business were going well. The sales in mobile channel shop has
been leading to total consumer business. In Enterprise business, strategic products line mainly cloud related business, and network
security related business for large enterprise, showed large growth. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to
17,392 million yen (10.5% increase from the same period in the previous year) with a double-digit growth rate.
 For North America region, in enterprise business, despite cloud related business showed large growth, network security related and
other business has been sluggish this period. As the result, sales for this period in North America region was 8,877 million yen (0.8%
increase from the same period in previous year) slight increased to.
In Europe region sales, though strategic products line performed well mainly due to cloud related business showed growth, traditional
products sales were in slackness. In addition, there was a big strong yen impact, net sales in this region were 7,427 million yen (0.4%
decreases from the same period in previous year) slight decreased to.
In the Asia Pacific region, strategic products line mainly cloud related business and network security related business showed

substantial growth. Middle East area and Australia area have led this region sales. As a result, in spite of strong yen impact, net sales
for this period in Asia Pacific region amounted to 7,245 million yen (14.9% increase from the same period in the previous year) with
showing the highest double-digit growth rate in all region.
In the Latin America region sales, traditional products sales and network security related business maintain the sales in entire this
region. However, there was a big strong yen impact, net sales in this region were 1,182 million yen (1.3% increases from the same
period in previous year) slight increased to.
As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first Quarter fiscal year end of 2020 came to 42,125 million yen (6.7% increases from the
same period in previous year.)
 In terms of our Y2020Q.1 earnings results, we recognize COVID-19 pandemic impact has been limited. On the other hand, its social
impact has been estimated to be continued and it is very difficult to predict when this pandemic will be over.
the Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 32,006 million yen (6.1% increase from the same period in previous year.) This
increase were mainly increase in getting volume public cloud service usage belonging to our SaaS business, people costs due to staff
up, and significant increase in outsource fee to consignee for good sales of consumer business at mobile channel shop, etc. As a result,
consolidated operating income for this period was 10,119 million yen (8.7% increase from the same period in previous year) increased
to.
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 12,034 million yen (24.0% increase from the same period in previous year) and
the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was 8,861 million yen (28.4% increase from the same period in
previous year.)


 Operating income based on Pre-GAAP (revenue before adjusting deferred revenue, etc.) serves as the important annual management
indicators for our company. It for this period was 9,010 million yen, a decrease of 150 million yen (1.6% decrease from the same
period in previous year.)   This decrease was caused by Pre-GAAP growth in excess of cost of sales and operating expenses growth
including people costs increase mainly due to prior speculative staff up and public cloud service usage volume increase for future
strengthening of our SaaS business.

 (2) Application of Simplified or Special Accounting Procedures
   (Calculation of income tax expenses)
   Multiply net income before tax for this period (3 months) by effective tax rate, which is rationally calculated based on projected
   annual profit and its taxes taking into consideration of deferred tax accounting. In addition, deferred tax expense is included in
   income taxes.
   However, if the calculation of tax expenses using the effective tax rate results in a markedly unreasonable outcome, tax expenses
   are calculated by using the statutory effective tax rate after adding and subtracting important differences that do not fall under
   temporary differences to and from profit before income taxes.

     (Additional information)
     (Transfer of AsiaInfo Security Limited's shares)
     On October 8, 2019, Trend Micro has reached a share transfer agreement with Great Media Security Limited to transfer all shares
     of Asia Info Security Limited,which is an equity-method.


     Though the shareholders' right is transferred, the settlement of the consideration is done by three installment payment by
     October 2021. The consideration will be Approximately 585 million RMB (approximately 8.7 billion yen / 1 RMB=15 JPY) .


     Asia Info Security Limited's share is transferred to Great Media Security Limited. However, Trend Micro has the right to recover
     the share rights in case of default . Thus it is interpreted as such Trend Micro keeps the share investment as it is. The book value
     will be transferred to investment securities, and the gain on sale will be recognized in October 2021, after all consideration is
     received.
