During this term of fiscal year 2016, from January 1 to September 30, the world economy has continuously remained still remained
uncertainty. With regards to the US economy, Fed has been holding off on interest rates this year against prediction. Despite Europe
economy is recently showing a sign of pick up, it has still continued to be almost flat. Emerging countries, mainly China, show
continued moderate economic growth slowdown.
Under the government's various economic policies, Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend. On the other hand, there
are still concerns such as world economy downward and the appreciation of the yen. The Japan economy has been going through in
those world economic concerns.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related services have been
still standing out from IT industry market. On the other hand, the worldwide PC shipments remain underrun from the same period in
previous year. It appears to be caused by weak back-to-school demand in US and weak PC consumer demand in emerging markets.
In domestic market, there is an increase in enterprises which have a plan to raise IT investment including IoT and Artificial
Intelligence (AI) as a new items.
In the computer security industry, while APT attack has plagued the entire world continuously, cyber terrorism targeting specific
companies or state institution, etc. which related to some cases of identity theft in both business client information and personal
private information have been also happened one after another and remained high-visibility incidents at home and abroad. In
addition, ransomware has been also a hot topic continuously.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


Notice: In Europe and Asia Pacific region, both net sales and its growth rate from same period in previous have been reflected to new
region structure in this result due to changes in-house management system.


With regards to sales in Japan region, APT related business showed strong growth and cloud related business has also maintained its
growth continuously in enterprise business sales. Consumer business has remained strong by keeping the number of users. As a
result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 40,279 million yen (3.9% increase from the same period in the previous
year) which is the highest net sales growth among the 5 sales regions.
For North America region, consumer business sales have not still performed well due to decrease in number of the users. On the
other hand, TippingPoint business has been contributive in enterprise business sales. Thus, in spite of the strong yen, sales for this
period in North America region was 23,051 million yen (5.9 % increase from the same period in previous year.)
In Europe region, though enterprise business maintained strong with APT related businesses highly contribution in local currency,
strong yen impact affected. As the result, net sales for this period in this region was 15,310 million yen (1.3% decrease from the same

period in previous year.)
In the Asia Pacific region, the enterprise business has been substantial in local currency with mainly led by Australia, though business
transfers occurred in China last November and strong yen impact affected. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific region
amounted to 11,115 million yen (16.0% decrease from the same period in the previous year).
In the Latin America region, both APT related business and cloud related business showed large growth in enterprise business sales
especially lead by Brazil. As the results, in spite of the strong yen impact, net sales in this region were 2,488 million yen (6.4%
increase from the same period in previous year.)


As a result, the consolidated net sales for this period (this term of fiscal year 2016, from January 1 to September 30,) marked 92,244
million yen (0.7% increase from the same period in previous year), showing revenue growth in all region.
Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 69,514 million yen (3.0% increase from the same period in previous year.) Although
mainly stock option related expense decreased, amortization of goodwill and people costs increased. As a result, consolidated
operating income for this period was 22,730 million yen (5.8% decrease from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 21,566 million yen (20.4% decrease from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to substantial increase in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the consolidated net income for this period was 13,713 million
yen (20.0% decrease from the same period in previous year).

 (3) Change of the Accounting Policy, Change of Accounting Estimation and Restatement
   (Change of the Accounting Policy)
   (Change in accounting standard for business combinations etc)
     From the first quarter of this fiscal year, the Company has applied the "Revised Accounting Standard for Business
   Combinations" (ASBJ Statement No.21; September 13, 2013), the "Revised Accounting Standard for Consolidated Financial
   Statements" (ASBJ Statement No.22; September 13, 2013) and the "Revised Accounting Standard for Business
   Divestitures" (ASBJ Statement No.7; September 13, 2013). The accounting standards reflect a switch in methodology with
   respect to the posting to capital surplus of differences arising from changes in equity in a subsidiary by the parent company in
   cases which control continues, and the posting of acquisition-related expenses as expenses for the consolidated fiscal year in
   which they are incurred. For business combinations occurring after the start of the first quarter, the Company has switched to a
   method in which revisions to the distribution of the cost of acquisition due to decisions regarding temporary accounting
   treatment are reflected in the quarterly consolidated financial statements applicable to the quarterly period in which the date of
   the business combination falls. Additionally, the Company has made changes in the presentation of quarterly net income, etc.
   and the presentation of a switch from minority interests to non-controlling interests. Financial statements for the third quarter of
   the previous fiscal year and for the previous fiscal year have been reconfigured to reflect these changes in presentation.
     In the consolidated statement of cash flows, cash flow related to the acquisition or sale of shares of subsidiaries not affecting
   the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from financing activities", while cash flows related to expense arising
   from acquisition of shares of subsidiaries affecting the scope of consolidation or expenses arising from acquisition or sales of
   shares of subsidiaries not affecting the scope of consolidation are classified into "Cash flows from operating activities".
     With respect to application of the Accounting Standards regarding Business Combinations, the transitional treatment as
   prescribed in article 58-2 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business Combinations, article 44-5 (4) of the Accounting Standard
   for Consolidated Financial Statement, and article 57-4 (4) of the Accounting Standard for Business Divestiture has been applied
   from the beginning of this first quarter of the fiscal year, and will continue to be applied going forward.
     The effect to the consolidated financial statements of this fiscal year is minor.


   (Change in depreciation method)
     In accordance with an amendment of Japan's Corporation Tax Act, the Company has applied the "Practical Solution on
    a change in depreciation method due to Tax Reform 2016" (Practical Issues Task Force No.32 issued on June 17, 2016)
    from the second quarter of this fiscal year and has changed its depreciation method for facilities attached to buildings and
    structures acquired on or after April 1, 2016, from the declining balance method to the straignt-line method.
      The effect of this change on the consolidated financial statements of this fiscal year is minor.


 (4) Additional Information
   (Influence of changing income tax rate)
   Partial Amendment of the Income Tax Act, etc. is announced on March 31, 2016 and income tax rate was to be lowered from
   consolidated fiscal year starting on or after April 1, 2016. The income tax has increased 633 million yen by this change.
