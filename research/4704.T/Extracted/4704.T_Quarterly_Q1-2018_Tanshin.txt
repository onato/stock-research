During this term of fiscal year 2018, from January 1 to March 31, even though the world economy stayed mostly firm, it has been
going through various risks rising mainly U.S.-China trade frictions and geopolitical risks.
Though Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend continuously with improved mainly corporate profits and a variety of
healthy economic indices, it is still going through over concerns mentioned above world economic risks and growing geopolitical
risks.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related services have been
still attracted from both home and abroad in IT industry market. In addition, corporates interests in new fields including IoT
 Internet of Things and AI (Artificial Intelligence), etc. have been leading IT spending. Worldwide IT spending will be estimated to
reach into $3.68 trillion by the end of 2018 that marks a 4.5% increase from the year prior.
The cyber security industry comes across multitude of cyber terrorism that targets specific companies, organizations, and even state
institution, etc., and a spate of identity theft in both business client information and personal private information occasionally. In
future, increasing in more sophisticated attacking, the industry will be required to adopt a techniques to minimize the risk of various
threats as like ransomware, targeted to digital currency , IoT devices and environment, social or political cyber attacking, etc.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, consumer business has kept roughly the same level as the same period in the previous year.
Enterprise business shows a steady performance of traditional products sales. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region
amounted to 15,174 million yen (4.9% increase from the same period in the previous year.)
For North America region, consumer business sales have showed growth to recover in a local currency. On the other hand, enterprise
business will booked only domestic TippingPoint business sales, which used to be booked including any other regions sales, since
this fiscal year. Though this fact has brought about negative impact on this region sales, enterprise business sales has showed steady
performance in a local currency. However, there is a strong yen impact, sales for this period in North America region was 9,675
million yen (0.3% decrease from the same period in previous year.)
In Europe region sales, both cloud related business and APT related business showed substantial growth. In addition, TippingPoint
business has contributed this region sales. Including this contribution, enterprise business sales have a strong showing. As the result,
net sales in this region were 6,835 million yen (16.1% increases from the same period in previous year).
In the Asia Pacific region sales, mainly Australia and Middle East area leads the sales in this region, both cloud related business and
APT related business showed significantly growth. Moreover, there was an effect of TippingPoint business to this region sales. As a
result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific region amounted to 5,270 million yen (26.6% increase from the same period in the
previous year) which is the highest net sales growth among the 5 sales regions.
In the Latin America region sales, though traditional products sales were not going well in mainly Mexico, APT related business

showed growth. In addition, from these facts, enterprise business sales have been leading this region's sales. As the result, net sales
in this region were 1,035 million yen (0.3% increases from the same period in previous year).
As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first Quarter fiscal year end of 2018 came to 37,993 million yen (7.8% increase from the
same period in previous year.)


Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 28,689 million yen (8.1% increase from the same period in previous year) mainly due to
increase in people costs, and getting volume public cloud service usage, etc. As a result, consolidated operating income for this
period was 9,303 million yen (6.7% increase from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 9,148 million yen (16.8% increase from the same period in previous year) due
to decrease in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was 6,585 million yen
(21.3% increase from the same period in previous year).


 (2) Qualitative Information on the Consolidated Financial Positions
Cash and bank deposits at the end of this period amounted to 95,237 million yen, an increase of 9,339 million yen from FY2017
annual closing. Total assets at the end of this period were 307,551 million yen, 23,605 million yen decrease from FY2017 annual
closing mainly due to a substantial decrease in marketable securities, investments securities, and note receivable, etc.
Total liabilities at the end of this period were 149,918 million yen, 4,161million yen decrease from FY2017 annual closing due to a
decrease in mainly deferred revenue, accrued expenses and accrued bonus, etc.
As a result, total net assets at the end of this period were 157,633 million yen, 19,444 million yen decrease from FY2017 annual
closing. This decrease is due to the dividends paid and decreasing in foreign currency translation adjustments, etc.
