During this term of fiscal year 2017, from January 1 to June 30, the world economy has been in a recovery trend in both Europe
economy, which dominated by Euro zone with a variety of healthy economic indices, and emerging economies, mainly China which
was better than expected. On the other hand, though the US economy has been showing moderate growth, there are some risks
associated with the indication of implementation of protectionist policy. The world economy has been still going through over variety
of risk concerns mentioned above.
Though Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend continuously with improved corporate profits and a variety of healthy
economic indices, it has still going through over concerns such as world economic risks and growing geopolitical risks.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, corporates interest in cloud computing and big data have been increasing in IT
industry market as well as new fields including IoT Internet of Things and AI (Artificial Intelligence), etc. Both worldwide and
domestic PC shipments remained underrun with decrease in user demands and price rise impacts due to component shortage. In spite
of such background, some regions and venders downturn rate in PC shipping has shrunk from the same period in prior year.
In the computer security industry, cyber terrorism who targets specific companies or state institution, etc., identity theft in both
business client information and personal private information, and ransomware have been hot topic in this field continuously. All of
them have remained to be reported as high-visibility incidents all over the world.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, cloud related business showed strong growth and APT (Advanced Persistent Threat) related
business has also maintained its growth continuously in enterprise business sales. Consumer business has kept its sales slight increase
mainly due to renewal business growth. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 28,958 million yen (8.4%
increase from the same period in the previous year.)
For North America region, despite of continuation of trend with decrease in number of users in consumer business sales, enterprise
business sales have showed surplus growth mainly due to the contribution of TippingPoint business. As a result, sales for this period
in North America region was 19,615 million yen (24.9% increase from the same period in previous year.) with a double-digit growth
rate.
In Europe region sales, in spite of the strong yen impact, enterprise business sales have been leading this region's sales mainly due to
cloud related business growth. As the result, net sales in this region were 11,351 million yen (4.6% increases from the same period in
previous year).


                                                                    2
In the Asia Pacific region sales, with the growth of both APT related business and cloud related business as well as traditional typed
security products sales achieved a great expansion to lead the sales in this region. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific
region amounted to 8,600 million yen (10.3% increase from the same period in the previous year) with a double-digit growth rate.
In the Latin America region sales, especially APT related business showed large growth. Accordingly, traditional typed security
products sales showed growth. From this fact, enterprise business sales have been leading this region's sales. As the result, net sales
in this region were 2,112 million yen (32.0% increases from the same period in previous year) with a double-digit growth rate.
As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first half of fiscal year 2017 marked 70,639 million yen (12.7% increase from the same
period in previous year.) showing revenue growth in all region.


Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 54,352 million yen (16.4% increase from the same period in previous year) mainly due to
increase in both people costs and stock option related expenses especially stock-based remuneration with stock price movement. As a
result, consolidated operating income for this period was 16,286 million yen (1.9% increase from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 15,772 million yen (6.7% increase from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to decrease in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was
11,375 million yen (3.0% increase from the same period in previous year) without gain on sale of affiliated company securities
recorded in the same period in previous year.

    (Business mergers, etc.)
 Significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost included in comparative data


   In its consolidated accounts for the second quarter of the previous fiscal year, the Company used a provisional accounting
treatment to account for the business combination of TippingPoint business conducted on March 8, 2016. However, it was
 determined at the end of the previous fiscal year.
   In line with this final determination, the comparative data in the consolidated financial statements for the second quarter of
 the fiscal year under review reflect a significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost of TippingPoint
 business. The main change was the allocation of JPY11,793 million in customer lists accounted for as intangible fixed assets.
 As a result, the provisional calculation of goodwill related to the TippingPoint business acquisition of JPY 31,743 million was
 reduced by JPY 10,337million to JPY 21,406 million. In addition, JPY 5,076 million was allocated to deferred revenue.
   Owing to the increase in net revenue, reducation in the amount of goodwill and an increase in amortizatino of intangible
fixed assets, the consolidated statement of income for the second quarter of the previous fiscal year has been adjusted as follows;
operating income, ordinary income, and net income before taxes were each increased by JPY 1,881 million, net income and profit
attributable to owners of parent were each increased by JPY 2,431 million.
