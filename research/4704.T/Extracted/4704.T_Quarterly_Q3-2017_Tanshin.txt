During this term of fiscal year 2017, from January 1 to September 30, the world economy stayed persistent: US economy has shown
steady economic indices, Europe economy, which is dominated by Euro zone, has been in a recovery trend, as well as emerging
economies, mainly China, maintained steady growth.
Though Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend continuously with improved corporate profits and a variety of healthy
economic indices, it is still going through over concerns mentioned above world economic risks and growing geopolitical risks.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, corporates interests have been increasing in new fields including IoT Internet
of Things and AI (Artificial Intelligence), etc. The worldwide PC shipments remained underrun with decrease in user demands and
price rise impacts due to component shortage and inventory adjustment in certain regions. In spite of such background, demands in
some developed countries, emerging countries, as well as corporates within Japanese showed some recovery. As a result, diminution
of venders downturn rate in PC shipping has shrunk compared with which is primarily projected.
In the computer security industry, cyber terrorism that targets specific companies or state institution, etc., identity theft in both
business client information and personal private information, and ransomware, all of them have been major threats in this field
continuously. All of them have remained to be reported as high-visibility incidents all over the world.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, mainly cloud related business showed strong growth in enterprise business sales. Consumer
business has kept its sales slight increase mainly due to renewal business growth. As a result, net sales for this period in Japan region
amounted to 43,459 million yen (7.7% increase from the same period in the previous year.)
For North America region, despite of continuation of trend with decrease in number of users in consumer business sales, enterprise
business sales have showed surplus growth mainly due to the contribution of TippingPoint business. As a result, sales for this period
in North America region was 30,065 million yen (21.4% increase from the same period in previous year.) with a double-digit growth
rate.
In Europe region sales, enterprise business sales have been leading this region's sales mainly due to cloud related business growth. As
the result, net sales in this region were 17,330 million yen (9.5% increases from the same period in previous year).
In the Asia Pacific region sales, with the strong growth of both APT related business and cloud related business as well as traditional
typed security products sales achieved a great expansion to lead the sales in this region. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia

Pacific region amounted to 13,738 million yen (19.3% increase from the same period in the previous year) with a double-digit growth
rate.
In the Latin America region sales, which is led by Mexico, mainly traditional typed security products sales showed growth. As the
result, net sales in this region were 3,188 million yen (25.7% increases from the same period in previous year) with a double-digit
growth rate.
As a result, the consolidated net sales for this period (this term of fiscal year 2017, from January 1 to September 30,) marked 107,782
million yen (13.5% increase from the same period in previous year), showing revenue growth in all region.


Cost of sales and operating expenses totaled 79,853 million yen (15.0% increase from the same period in previous year.) mainly due
to increase in both people costs and stock option related expenses especially stock-based remuneration with stock price movement.
As a result, consolidated operating income for this period was 27,929 million yen (9.4% increase from the same period in previous
year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 28,145 million yen (15.5% increase from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to decrease in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was
20,515 million yen (17.5% increase from the same period in previous year).

    (Business mergers, etc.)
 Significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost included in comparative data


   In its consolidated accounts for the third quarter of the previous fiscal year, the Company used a provisional accounting
treatment to account for the business combination of TippingPoint business conducted on March 8, 2016. However, it was
 determined at the end of the previous fiscal year.
   In line with this final determination, the comparative data in the consolidated financial statements for the third quarter of
 the fiscal year under review reflect a significant change in the original allocation of the acquisition cost of TippingPoint
 business. The main change was the allocation of JPY11,793 million in customer lists accounted for as intangible fixed assets.
 As a result, the provisional calculation of goodwill related to the TippingPoint business acquisition of JPY 31,743 million was
 reduced by JPY 10,337million to JPY 21,406 million. In addition, JPY 5,076 million was allocated to deferred revenue.
   Owing to the increase in net revenue, reducation in the amount of goodwill and an increase in amortization of intangible
fixed assets, the consolidated statement of income for the third quarter of the previous fiscal year has been adjusted as follows;
operating income, ordinary income, and net income before taxes were each increased by JPY 2,808 million, net income and profit
attributable to owners of parent were each increased by JPY 3,743 million.
