During this term of fiscal year 2018, from January 1 to June 30, even though the world economy stayed mostly firm, it has been going
through various risks rising mainly U.S.-China trade frictions, the impact of a Fed interest rate hike, and geopolitical risks seen in
crude oil price hikes, etc.
Though Japan economy has been on a moderate recovery trend continuously with improved mainly corporate profits and a variety of
healthy economic indices, it is still going through over concerns mentioned above world economic risks and growing geopolitical risks.
In the worldwide Information Technology Industry, cloud computing including server virtualization and related services have been still
attracted from both home and abroad in IT industry market. In addition, corporates’ interests in new fields including IoT （Internet of
Things） and AI (Artificial Intelligence), etc. have been leading IT spending. Worldwide IT spending will be estimated to reach into
$3.7 trillion by the end of 2018 that marks a 6.2% increase from the year prior.
In the cyber security industry, the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) became enforceable beginning 25 May 2018. On the
other hand, multitude of cyber terrorism that targets specific companies, organizations, and even state institution, etc., and a spate of
identity theft in both business client information and personal private information have been still coming across occasionally.       In
addition, the data scandal that personally identifiable information of the SNS users suffered unauthorized use by third party revealed.
This recent high-visibility incident required showing more rigorous approach to information management to the collection-side.        In
future, increasing in more sophisticated attacking, the industry will be required to adopt a techniques to minimize the risk of various
threats as like ransomware, targeted to digital currency , IoT devices and environment, social or political cyber attacking, etc.
Under such environment, our group business conditions are as follows:


With regards to sales in Japan region, consumer business has kept roughly the same level in the number of users as the same period in
the previous year. Enterprise business shows a steady performance of Unified Threat Management (UTM) products sales. As a
result, net sales for this period in Japan region amounted to 30,919 million yen (6.8% increase from the same period in the previous
year.)
For North America region, consumer business sales have showed growth to recover in a local currency. On the other hand, enterprise
business has booked only domestic TippingPoint business sales, which used to be booked including any other regions’ sales, since this
fiscal year. Though this fact has brought about negative impact on this region sales, enterprise business sales has showed steady
performance due to mainly cloud related business. As the result, though there was a strong yen impact, sales for this period in North
America region was 20,017 million yen (2.0% increase from the same period in previous year.)
 In Europe region sales, enterprise business has been going well. Both cloud related business and network security related business
including TippingPoint business contribution showed substantial growth. In addition, traditional products sales have also been
growing. In addition, there was a weak yen impact. As the result, net sales in this region were 13,647 million yen (20.2% increases
from the same period in previous year).
 In the Asia Pacific region sales, not only mainly Australia and Middle East area, but also South-Eastern Asia where showed

substantial growth leads the sales in this region due to a significant growth in network security related business including especially
cloud related business, and TippingPoint business effects to this region sales. As a result, net sales for this period in Asia Pacific
region amounted to 10,899 million yen (26.7% increase from the same period in the previous year) which is the highest net sales
growth among the 5 sales regions.
In the Latin America region sales, though traditional products sales recovered modestly in a second half of this period, across the
enterprise business in this region was not going well in mainly Brazil. In spite of slightly increase in local currency, there was a
strong yen impact. As the result, net sales in this region were 2,006 million yen (5.0% decreases from the same period in previous
year).
As a result, the consolidated net sales for the first half of fiscal year 2018 marked 77,490 million yen (9.7% increase from the same
period in previous year.)


Cost of sales and operating expenses increased totaled 59,638 million yen (9.7% increase from the same period in previous year.) In
spite of a decrease in virtual share bonus plan, this increase was mainly due to increase in people costs, hardware purchase caused by
network security related business sales growth, and getting volume public cloud service usage, etc. As a result, consolidated
operating income for this period was 17,851 million yen (9.6% increase from the same period in previous year).
The consolidated ordinary income for this period was 18,242 million yen (15.7% increase from the same period in previous year)
mainly due to decrease in foreign exchange loss, etc. and the net income attributable to owners of the parent for this period was 13,468
million yen (18.4% increase from the same period in previous year).


 (2) Qualitative Information on the Consolidated Financial Positions

This decrease is due to the dividends paid and decreasing in foreign currency translation adjustments, etc.

 (2) Application of Simplified or Special Accounting Procedures
   (Calculation of income tax expenses)
   Multiply net income before tax for this period (6 months) by effective tax rate, which is rationally calculated based on projected
   annual profit and its taxes taking into consideration of deferred tax accounting. In addition, deferred tax expense is included in
   income taxes.
   However,if the calculation of tax expenses using the effective tax rate results in a markedly unreasonable outcome,tax expenses
   are calculated by using the statutory effective tax rate after adding and subtracting important differences that do not fall under
   temporary differences to and from profit before income taxes.
