Volume Drivers

South Port's revenue is largely volume-linked. Cargo tonnage, Tiwai smelter volume and container throughput are the operating drivers behind every line in the financials below.

Total Cargo Volume (Bulk + Container + Tiwai)

FY2026's 3.96m tonnes includes ~16,000t of one-off wind-farm project cargo and a wind-throw log surge; management guides FY2027 volumes broadly flat, not a continuation of the FY26 growth rate.

Bulk vs Container vs Tiwai Volume

Only FY2025-FY2026 (plus H1 FY2026) carry the full bulk/container/Tiwai breakdown in the disclosed data; earlier years show total tonnage only.

Container Volume (TEU)

FY2026's record 62,000 TEU (+18.5%) was supported by the new MSC Eagle Service and an enhanced Trans-Tasman Southern Loop; only the last two fiscal years are disclosed.

Growth & Profitability

Revenue & YoY Growth

EBITDA Margin

10-year range 38.0%-43.5%; FY2026's 41.6% sits above the disclosed NZ port-sector average (~38%) but below SPN's own peak.

Reported vs Normalised NPAT

FY2024 reported NPAT ($7.376m) was cut by a one-off $2.3m deferred-tax expense from the March-2024 removal of building tax depreciation; normalised NPAT was $9.956m that year.

Cash Flow, Balance Sheet & Dividend

A high-payout income stock: dividend yield and coverage matter more here than growth.

Operating Cash Flow, CapEx & Free Cash Flow

FCF is lumpy because capex is lumpy ($2.5m FY2017 vs $24.4m FY2022 channel-deepening peak). FY2026's $14.2m is 2.2x the 8-year median on a raw-dollar basis; the DCF rebuilds Year-1 FCF mid-cycle rather than carrying FY2026 forward.

Total Debt vs Cash

Net debt/EBITDA fell to 0.6x (from 1.0x FY25) with $19m of covenant headroom; management targets a long-term gearing ceiling of <2.0x.

Dividend per Share

Never cut in the 10-year window (including the FY2024 trough): 23.0c (FY2015) -> 29.0c (FY2026). FY2026 payout ratio was 47% of NPAT, covered ~1.8x by owner FCF.
