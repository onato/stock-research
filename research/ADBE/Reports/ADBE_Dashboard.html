Reported FCF vs Owner FCF (after SBC)

TTM basis in the DCF (9.71%, including cash taxes on equity-award settlement) runs slightly lower than the raw CSV SBC-only ratio shown here, and is declining -- the opposite of the usual SaaS dilution warning.

Subscription Base & ARR

ARR was redefined in FY2026 -- the two series below are never the same line

ARR: Digital Media (legacy) vs Total Adobe (current)

Digital Media ARR ($19.20bn FY2025) is the metric Adobe reported through FY2025. Total Adobe ARR ($25.20bn FY2025, $27.10bn Q2-FY2026) is the broader FY2026 replacement, disclosed alongside Digital Media ARR only at the FY2024/FY2025 bridge points. Chaining them (19.2 -> 26.06) would fabricate ~36% growth; the company's own reported growth is ~10-11%.

Segment Revenue (through FY2025)

Adobe moved to a single reportable segment from FY2026 (Semrush folded into Digital Experience through Q1, then unified) -- the series stops at FY2025 because the company itself stopped disclosing the split, not because either business shrank.

Subscription Revenue vs Total Revenue

Subscription has been ~96-97% of total revenue for the full disclosed history -- the gap between the two lines is the small, structurally declining non-subscription (publishing/advertising) tail.

Capital Structure & Balance Sheet

Buyback-funded share count decline against a modest net-debt position

Shares Outstanding

485.5m (FY2020) -> 402.5m (Q2-FY2026), a 17% reduction, funded by $11.28bn of FY2025 repurchases against $50bn of authorizations running to 2028/2030 on a ~$116bn market cap. The DCF holds the count flat going forward rather than projecting further reduction, since SBC dilution is already fully expensed in the flows.

Cash & Equivalents vs Total Debt

Net debt was $1,726m as of Q2-FY2026 (debt exceeds cash) -- modest for the scale of cash generation, and small enough that interest income is left in the DCF's flows rather than stripped out.

Net Income vs Reported FCF

FCF has run consistently above net income for a decade -- D&A and working-capital timing exceed the (real, but non-cash) SBC charge sitting inside net income. See the Reported vs Owner FCF chart for the cash cost SBC represents once it is added back out.
