FY Jan – Dec 2024 Business Topics
   During FY2024, Fine Carbon’s performance grew, reflecting a recovery in demand for memory semiconductors,
    and the Company also saw continued steady performance in Carbon Black, resulting in increases in net sales and
    operating income for these two segments. On the other hand, Smelting and Lining continued to see decreased
    demand for relining of aluminum smelting furnaces, customer inventory adjustments, and price decrease due to
    intensifying competition. Similarly, Graphite Electrodes continue to be affected by sluggish steel production and
    intensifying competition with the Asian rivals. Consequently, net sales and operating income declined significantly
    for both segments, resulting in an operating loss. As a result, consolidated net sales and operating income
    declined, with operating income falling below the announced forecast. EBITDA, a key measure of cash-based
    earning capacity, declined, but EBITDA margin maintained in the upper 10% range.
   Due to ongoing business structural reforms in Graphite Electrodes and Smelting and Lining, impairment losses,
    etc. on goodwill and fixed assets were recorded as extraordinary losses for 2024, resulting in a net loss.
   In Fine Carbon, additional shares were acquired in its subsidiary, Tokai Carbon Korea, and its equity method
    affiliate, MWI, became a wholly-owned subsidiary.
   While the net D/E ratio slightly increased due to a decrease in equity capital, financial soundness and liquidity
    remained strong.
   The year-end dividend is 15 yen per share, as previously announced, maintaining the annual dividend at 30 yen,
    including the interim dividend.
   For the fiscal year ending December 31, 2025, operating income is projected to increase owing to reductions in
    depreciation and amortization due to impairment losses in 2024, as well as the fixed cost saving effects.
    Extraordinary losses have already peaked, and the dividend for 2025 is projected to be maintained at 30 yen.

   FY Jan – Dec 2025 Consolidated Earnings Forecast
 Net sales are projected to decrease by 2.6%, driven by lower sales for Smelting and Lining, Graphite Electrodes,

    and Industrial Furnaces and Related Products, outweighing the higher sales for Carbon Black and Fine Carbon.
 Operating income is expected to increase by 20.2%. While operating income for Carbon Black (increase in

    depreciation and amortization in the Thai plant expected) and Fine Carbon (slower growth of power
    semiconductors expected) is projected to be lower, improvement for Smelting and Lining and Graphite
    Electrodes is expected, mainly due to reductions in depreciation and amortization and fixed costs.
 Dividend forecast is maintained at ¥30 per share; payout ratio of 58%.
