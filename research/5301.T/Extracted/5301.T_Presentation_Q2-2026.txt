  Consolidated Earnings Forecast FY2026
◼ Full-year forecasts announced in May were further revised upward (net sales: +19.5% YoY, operating income: +35.4% YoY).

  This reflects the bottoming-out of operations in the tire and automotive sectors alongside price adjustments to pass through
  cost increase caused by Middle East disruptions in Carbon Black, increased sales volume of SiC and specialty graphite
  products driven by semiconductor market expansion in Fine Carbon, and a recovering trend of the graphite electrode market
  mainly in the U.S.
◼ Driven by an extraordinary gain on the sale of cross-shareholdings, net income attributable to owners of the parent company

  is expected to reach ¥22.0 billion (+9.6% YoY), an increase of ¥10.0 billion from the previous forecast.
