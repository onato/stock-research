 2       Description of business
         The Tokai Carbon Group (the Company and its affiliates) engages in business operations categorized into the following six
         reportable segments: Graphite Electrodes business, Carbon Black business, Fine Carbon business, Smelting and Lining business,
         Industrial Furnaces and Related Products business, and Other Operations.
         There was no change in the principal business of the reportable segments during the first six months of the fiscal year under
         review. Nor was there any change in major affiliates.

 2         Management’s analysis of financial position, operating results, and cash flows
           Forward-looking statements in the document are based on the judgments of the Group as of the end of the consolidated quarter

     (1)     Financial position and operating results
             During the first six months of the fiscal year under review (January 1, 2021 to June 30, 2021), the global economy as a whole
             continued to recover from the downturn caused by the spread of the novel coronavirus (COVID-19). However, as opposed to
             the solid recovery in the United States, where vaccination is progressing, the recovery is slower in countries such as Japan and
             emerging and developing countries, where the progress of vaccination has been relatively slow, and disparities among countries
             and regions have become visible. In addition, in some countries and regions, the so-called Delta variant has spread, and
             subsequent waves of infections due to the resumption of economic activities have been observed. Thus, the forecast for the
             global economy still remains uncertain.
             Under these circumstances, the Tokai Carbon Group formulated and disclosed the medium-term management plan "T-2023"
             this May, and has been conducting business activities following three basic policies: "Returning core businesses to a path of
             growth," "Optimizing the business portfolio," and "Strengthening the consolidated governance structure." In recent business
             results, demand for steel, automobiles, semiconductors, and aluminum, which are industries directly related to the Company,
             has been generally on a recovery track, and the Company is directing its focus toward production and sales activities in order
             to capitalize on these sales opportunities.

             As a result, net sales for the first six months of the fiscal year under review grew 25.2% year on year to 117,380 million yen.
             Operating income increased 88.1% year on year to 10,945 million yen. Ordinary income leaped 106.2% year on year to 10,646
             million yen. Quarterly net income attributable to owners of the parent company jumped 164.4% to 5,708 million yen.

             Results by business segment were as follows:

     Graphite Electrodes
         Global crude steel production is recovering steadily. Despite increasing demand and signs of improving market conditions, the
         graphite electrode market remained weak compared with the level a year ago.
         As a result, net sales for this business were down 14.1% year on year to 17,986 million yen, and operating income fell 91.9%
         year on year to 123 million yen.

     Carbon Black
         Despite a temporary decline in sales volume at production bases in the United States when they were affected by a severe cold
         wave in the southern United States, all of the production bases recorded a year on year increase in sales volume with sharply
         recovering auto production in China and the global recovery of demand in the tire and auto parts industries. Operating income
         also went up year on year due to an improvement in the cost of sales ratio accompanying an increase in the operating ratio and
         adjustments in selling prices due to an increase in the price of raw oil.
         As a result, net sales for this business increased 41.8% year on year to 46,264 million yen, while operating income was 4,353
         million yen (operating loss of 108 million yen in the previous fiscal year).

     Fine Carbon
          Production and shipments for semiconductors and photovoltaic power generation remained firm. Sales to general industries
          also began to recover. Shipments of solid SiC (silicon carbide) products, which are high value-added products, have increased
          on the back of robust global demand.
          As a result, net sales for this business rose 20.2% year on year to 18,677 million yen and operating income increased 30.8%
          year on year to 4,557 million yen.

Smelting and Lining
    Although automobile production has recently stalled due to a shortage of semiconductors, it has recovered compared with the
    same period last year. The demand for aluminum remained firm accompanying an increase in the market price on the back of
    economic recovery from the COVID-19 pandemic and financial supports by governments. On July 17, 2020, the Company
    added Tokai Carbon Savoie International SAS (the former Carbone Savoie International SAS), a French carbon and graphite
    product manufacturer, and its group companies to this segment as consolidated subsidiaries.
    As a result, net sales for this business increased 69.7% year on year to 21,893 million yen, while operating income after
    adjustment for amortization related to valuation differences arising from purchase price allocation and goodwill amortization
    was 302 million yen (operating loss of 238 million yen in the previous fiscal year).

Industrial Furnaces and Related Products
     Net sales of industrial furnaces decreased from the previous fiscal year as sales for energy-related industry applications, which
     are a major source of demand, concentrate in the second quarter and thereafter. Meanwhile, net sales of heating elements and
     other products increased from the previous fiscal year due to strong demand from the electronic components industry and the
     Chinese domestic market.
     As a result, net sales for this business rose 2.3% year on year to 6,539 million yen and operating income increased 17.7% year
     on year to 1,953 million yen.

Other Operations
    Friction materials
    Net sales of friction materials increased 38.7% year on year to 4,404 million yen, because of increasing demand for construction
    and agricultural machinery, motorcycle, and electromagnetic applications in parallel with the recovery of the global economy.
    Anode materials
    Sales volume decreased from the previous fiscal year as competition intensified with rising new players entering the anode
    materials market.
    As a result, net sales of anode materials fell 26.2% year on year to 1,547 million yen.
    Others
    Net sales for real estate leasing and other businesses fell 5.3% year on year to 67 million yen.
    As a result of the foregoing, net sales for Other Operations increased 12.6% year on year to 6,019 million yen and operating
    income rose 48.9% year on year to 348 million yen.

     Total assets as of the end of the second quarter under review increased by 41,899 million yen from the end of the previous
     fiscal year to 501,609 million yen, due to increases in cash, deposits, tangible fixed assets, etc. Liabilities grew by 22,036
     million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year to 256,930 million yen, due to an increase in interest-bearing liabilities
     such as bonds payable. Net assets were up 19,862 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year to 244,678 million yen,
     due mainly to an increase in foreign currency translation adjustments.
     As a result, the Group’s capital-to-asset ratio decreased 0.1 percentage points year on year to 43.7%.

Opinion
We have conducted a quarterly review of the quarterly consolidated financial statements of Tokai Carbon Co., Ltd. for the second quarter
(April 1, 2021 to June 30, 2021) as well as the first six months (January 1, 2021 to June 30, 2021) of the fiscal year from January 1, 2021
through December 31, 2021, provided in “IV. Financial Statements” in the Company’s Quarterly Securities Report; that is, the quarterly
consolidated balance sheets, quarterly consolidated statements of income, quarterly consolidated statements of comprehensive income,
quarterly consolidated statements of cash flows, and related notes thereof, in accordance with Article 193-2, Paragraph 1 of the Financial
Instruments and Exchange Act of Japan.

In our quarterly review, in all material respects, we did not find any matters that lead us to believe that these quarterly consolidated
financial statements do not properly present the financial position of Tokai Carbon Co., Ltd. and its consolidated subsidiaries as of June
30, 2021, and their operating results as well as cash flows for the first six months ended on that day, in accordance with the standard for
preparation of quarterly consolidated financial statements generally accepted as fair and valid in Japan.

Basis for the Audit Opinion
We conducted our quarterly review in accordance with the quarterly review standards that are generally accepted as fair and valid in
Japan. Our responsibility under quarterly review standards is described in “Auditor's Responsibility for Conducting the Quarterly Review
of Quarterly Consolidated Financial Statements.” We are independent of the Company and its consolidated subsidiaries and fulfill our
other ethical responsibilities as an auditor in accordance with provisions of the Code of Professional Ethics in Japan. We believe that we
have obtained evidence to provide a basis for our opinion.

Other matters
The quarterly review of quarterly consolidated financial statements for the second quarter, as well as first six months of the fiscal year
ended December 31, 2020, and the audit of consolidated financial statements for the previous fiscal year were conducted by the former
auditors. The former auditors expressed an unqualified opinion on the said quarterly consolidated financial statements on August 13,
2020, and on the said consolidated financial statements on March 30, 2021.
