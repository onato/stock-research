2     Management’s Analysis of Financial Position, Operating Results, and Cash Flows
      The forward-looking statements contained herein are based on the judgments of the Group as of the end of the total period of the
      Quarterly Consolidated Financial Statements.

    (1) Financial position and operating results
           During the first three months of 2022 (from January 1, 2022 to March 31, 2022), while the balance between anti-infection
           measures and economic activities has progressed in countries around the world and the economy has continued to recover
           mainly in developed countries, uncertainty over the future of the global economy has further increased due to factors such as
           confusion in the supply chain and soaring raw material and energy costs due to the re-spread of the novel coronavirus (COVID-
           19) by new variants and economic and financial sanctions against Russia following the Ukraine crisis.

           Under these circumstances, in February this year, Tokai Carbon Group disclosed the rolling medium-term management plan
           “T-2024” for the three-year period from 2022 to 2024. Based on the three basic policies of “returning core businesses to a path
           of growth,” “optimizing the business portfolio (selection and concentration),” and “strengthening the consolidated governance
           structure,” we aim to achieve quantitative targets of 356 billion yen in net sales, 57 billion yen in operating income, 16% in
           ROS, and 91 billion yen in EBITDA by 2024.
           Focusing on our core businesses of graphite electrodes and carbon black, we are working to secure appropriate profits by
           passing through the higher raw material and capital investment costs to the selling prices. At the same time, we are expanding
           our production capacity for the growing future demand. Also, along with our selection and concentration strategy, in February
           2022 we completed the transfer of Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) Co., Ltd., our Chinese carbon black plant. On top, we initiated and
           started activity of both Sustainability Committee and Carbon Neutral Committee from January 2022, one to deliberate
           important issues related to sustainability and other to function as a control tower for handling carbon neutral issues.

           As a result, net sales for the first three months of 2022 increased 28.8% year on year to 69,512 million yen. Operating income
           increased 94.7% year on year to 7,938 million yen. Ordinary income increased 110.2% year on year to 8,406 million yen. Net
           income attributable to owners of the parent company increased 105.5% year on year to 4,044 million yen.

           Results by business segment were as follows:

       Graphite Electrodes

           Demand for electrodes increased due to the recovery in the global steel market, and receiving active inquiries mainly in Europe
           and the United States. Operational rates and sales volume are recovering and sales prices rebounded from the previous year’s
           bottom.

           As a result, net sales for the Graphite Electrodes business increased 55.3% year on year to 12,362 million yen, while operating
           income was 1,258 million yen (operating loss of 223 million in the previous year).

       Carbon Black

           The replacement tires significantly recovered mainly in Europe and United States, while tires for new vehicles was affected
           by shortages of semiconductors and other materials. Sales and operating income increased due to measures such as passing on
           the increase in raw oil prices to sales prices, despite the overall sales volume decreased year on year due to the sale of the
           China-based business Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) Co., Ltd. .

           As a result, net sales for the Carbon Black business increased 36.3% year on year to 29,430 million yen, while operating
           income increased 68.8% year on year to 3,049 million yen.

       Fine Carbon

           Production and shipments for semiconductors and solar power generation remained strong, and shipments for general

                                                                    -3-
       industries also turned to recovery. Sales of high-value-added products in solid SiC (silicon carbide) products remained strong
       due to continued global demand.

       As a result, net sales for the Fine Carbon business increased 23.9% year on year to 10,935 million yen, while operating income
       increased by 35.7% year on year to 2,912 million yen.

   Smelting and Lining

       Primary aluminum metal prices stayed at historically high levels, and sales of cathodes for aluminum electrolysis, particularly
       in emerging countries, remained firm. Furnace linings demand facing a slight downturn but carbon electrode remained strong
       backed by the brisk demand for metallic silicon.

       As a result, net sales for the Smelting and Lining business increased 7.8% year on year to 11,605 million yen, while operating
       income increased 57.4% year on year to 67 million yen.

   Industrial Furnaces and Related Products

       Capital investment was strong in the IT-related industries, which are the main customers of the industrial furnaces. Sales of
       heating element and other products to the electronic component industry also remained strong.

       As a result, net sales for the Industrial Furnaces and Related Products business increased 27.3% year on year to 2,523 million
       yen, while operating income increased by 46.3% year on year to 763 million yen.

   Other Operations
      Friction materials
      Along with the recovery of the global economy, demand for construction machinery, agricultural machinery, motorcycles and
      electromagnetic equipment for each use was strong.
      As a result, net sales of friction materials increased 7.3% year on year to 2,290 million yen.

       Anode materials
       Competition intensified due to the rise of emerging markets in anode materials, and sales volume decreased year on year.
       As a result, net sales of anode materials decreased 50.7% year on year to 330 million yen.

       Other
       Net sales from real estate leasing and other businesses increased 0.5% year on year to 34 million yen.
       As a result, net sales for Other Operations decreased 6.5% year on year to 2,655 million yen, while operating income increased
       45.7% year on year to 201million yen.

       Total assets as of the end of the first quarter under review (March 31, 2022) increased by 14,126 million yen from the end of
       the previous fiscal year to 526,630 million yen due to increases in inventories and tangible fixed assets. Liabilities decreased
       by 1,856 million yen from the end of previous fiscal year to 254,076 million yen due to decreases in income taxes payable and
       interest-bearing liabilities despite increases in deferred tax liabilities. Net assets increased by 15,982 million yen compared to
       the end of previous fiscal year to 272,553 million yen due to an increase in foreign currency translation adjustments.
       As a result, the Group’s equity ratio was 46.1%, an increase of 1.4 points from the end of previous fiscal year.

  (Changes in accounting policy)
      (Application of accounting Standards for revenue recognition)
      Accounting Standard for Revenue Recognition (ASBJ Statement No. 29, March 31, 2020, hereinafter referred to as, “Revenue
      Recognition Accounting Standard”) etc. has been applied from the beginning of the first three month of the consolidated period.
      With the application of the Revenue Recognition Accounting Standard, revenue is recognized at the amount expected to be
      received in exchange for the promised goods or services when control of the goods or services is transferred to the customer.
      Moreover, revenue is recognized at the time of shipment if the period from the time of shipment to the time when control over

(Business combination, etc.)
   Business separation
       On November 15, 2021, the Company entered into an agreement to transfer all of interests in Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) Co.,
       Ltd. (“Tokai Carbon (Tianjin)”), a wholly owned subsidiary of the Company, to Cabot (China) Limited, a subsidiary of
       Cabot Corporation (“Cabot”). On February 28, 2022, the Company transferred all equity interests. As a result, Tokai Carbon
       (Tianjin) is excluded from the scope of consolidation.

    1. Outline of the business separation
       (1) Name of the successor company
           Cabot (China) Limited


       (2) Name and business of the separated subsidiary
           Company name                       Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) Co., Ltd.
           Description of business            Manufacture and sale of carbon black


       (3) Main reasons for the business separation
           Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) was established as a joint venture in Tianjin Economic-Technological Development Area in 2004
           responding to the growing demand of carbon black in China. In 2013, Tokai Carbon acquired all of the shares owned by
           the joint venture partner making Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) a wholly owned subsidiary. Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) has been
           satisfying the demands of tire and industrial rubber parts manufacturers by providing high-end carbon black, however the
           business environment surrounding Tokai Carbon (Tianjin) is becoming increasingly severe year by year such as the
           intensifying market competition and strict environmental regulations governing the operations. In light of these
           circumstances, we have decided to sell the business of Tokai Carbon (Tianjin), after considering the redistribution of our
           resources from various perspectives in line with our basic policy of optimizing our business portfolio in our medium-
           term management plan T-2023.


       (4) Date of business separation
           February 28, 2022 (deemed sale date: January 1, 2022)


       (5) Legal form of business separation
           Transfer of investments in exchange for cash


    2. Implemented accounting method
       (1) Amount of gain or loss on transfer
           Loss on sale of equity invested in associates 1,167 Million yen (provisional)
           Among them, 1,137 million yen is included in provision for loss on sale of equity invested in associates in the previous
           fiscal year. In the future, the amount of loss on sale may change due to adjustments in the sale price based on the equity
           transfer agreement.
