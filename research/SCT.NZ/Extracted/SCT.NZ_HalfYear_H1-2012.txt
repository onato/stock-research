Goodwill arose in the acquisition of QMT because the consideration paid for the combination effectively included amounts in relation
to the benefit of expected synergies, revenue growth, the cost and length of time involved in establishing a business in China and
future market development of the business. These benefits are not recognised separately from goodwill as the future economic
benefits arising from them cannot be reliably measured and they do not meet the definition of identifiable intangible assets.
