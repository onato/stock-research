                ℹ️ Turnaround Valuation Approach & Share Count Note

                This is a cyclical turnaround / balance-sheet-repair story, not a growth story. FY2024 (NPAT -$80.4m) and FY2025 (NPAT -$104.8m) were catastrophic loss years driven by $53.9m of impairments and $15.1m of inventory net-realisable-value provisions during an industry-wide Manuka honey oversupply crisis. FY2022/23 were post-COVID China-boom cycle peaks that are not repeatable absent a structural rebalancing of industry supply. H1-2026 (six months to Dec-2025) returned to profit: revenue $118.0m, NPAT $4.6m. The DCF is built bottom-up from a mid-cycle earnings base — not peak, not trough — anchored to management's FY2026 normalised EBIT guidance of ~$15.5m (haircut to a 6.0% Year-1 EBIT margin for the company's own repeatedly-flagged "execution risk"), ramping gradually toward a 9.0% terminal margin that remains below the FY2022/23 cycle-peak range of 9.6–10.2%. Crucially, the strong reported FCF of the last two periods is stripped of the one-off inventory-destocking release before being used as a going-forward base (see the FCF Quality chart below). WACC is stacked to 17.5% to reflect NZX micro-cap illiquidity, extreme historical cyclicality, China/single-market concentration, agricultural/weather exposure and the company's own recent going-concern/covenant-waiver history. The base case ($0.73) sitting just below the current price ($0.755, -3.8%) means the market already prices in a reasonable central-case recovery — this is a sanity-check PASS, not a red flag. Note also that the Entry Price ($0.845) sits ABOVE both current price and base intrinsic value: this is the expected, mathematically consistent result of a base-case WACC (17.5%) that is higher than the 15% IRR hurdle used to compute entry price, and is explained further in the DCF section below — it is not a bug.

                Share count discontinuity: the historical Metrics CSV reports 70.5m shares outstanding through H1-2026 (Dec-2025), which was accurate at the time. A $40.5m recapitalisation completed 18-May-2026 ($30m rights offer at $0.65/share + $10.5m F&N Ventures strategic placement) added 60.75m new shares, taking the count to ~131.3m and reducing net debt from ~$48.7m (Dec-2025) to an estimated ~$11.2m post-raise. All per-share and market-cap figures on this dashboard use the post-raise 131.3m share count and current price of NZ$0.755 (verified 10-Aug-2026); historical per-share time series in the charts below (e.g. EPS) still reflect the pre-raise share count as reported for each period and are not restated.
