                DCF Valuation — Scenario-Weighted Path to Breakeven

                ⚠ This is NOT a standard discounted-FCF model. Me Today has never generated positive free cash flow or a continuing-operations profit in any period on record (accumulated losses $54.5m), and the share count is not a continuous series (1-for-5 consolidation Apr-2020, 1-for-100 consolidation Jan-2024, then a near-doubling via the Nov-2025 rights issue) — a Gordon-growth terminal value on today's negative FCF, or a per-share CAGR across those breaks, would be arithmetically meaningless. Instead this is a scenario-weighted path-to-breakeven model: continuing-operations revenue and EBITDA margin are projected to the point EBITDA turns durably positive under each scenario (referencing management's own twice-raised, twice-reaffirmed FY2026 guidance), owner FCF is built from that path, the 95.522m warrants are modelled conditionally (they exercise — cash in, shares out — only in scenarios where price clears the $0.06 strike ahead of each window; otherwise they expire worthless and a discounted placement bridges the shortfall), and Year-5 EBITDA is capitalised at an exit EV/EBITDA multiple (Gordon perpetuity is unusable this close to zero) discounted at a scenario-specific cost of equity of 18.5%–26% — well above a normal WACC, built up from illiquidity, pre-profitability, concentration and governance/execution premiums. P/E and EV/EBITDA sanity anchors are undefined (both are negative in every historical period); P/Sales (~0.66x EV/Sales on FY26 guided revenue) and P/Book (~1.61x, book value only turned positive in H1-2026 on the one-off King Honey gain) are used instead.
