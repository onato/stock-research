                                                                                  1.6. NOTES TO THE CONDENSED CONSOLIDATED FINANCIAL STATEMENTS




Highlights of the first half of 2026                                                                                                                  1.
On February 26, 2026, TP announced changes to its governance: Mr. Daniel Julien (Chief Executive Officer and Director) and Mr. Thomas
Mackenbrock and Mr. Olivier Rigaudy (Deputy Chief Executive Officers) stepped down from their respective roles effective March 15, 2026, and
the Board of Directors decided to appoint Mr. Jorge Amar as Chief Executive Officer and co-opted him as a director, effective March 16, 2026.
This cooptation, as other directors coopted , was ratified by the Shareholders' Meeting held on May 21, 2026.
In addition, on May 28, 2026, Teleperformance SE initiated a bond issue in two tranches, one with a maturity of six years and a coupon of
4.75% in the amount of €700 million redeemable on March 28, 2032, the other with a maturity of nine years and a coupon of 5.375% in the
amount of €500 million redeemable on January 28, 2035.
The net proceeds of the issue were intended to finance the early redemption of two existing bond issues, the first of €500 million with
a coupon of 0.25% maturing in November 2027, and the second of €700 million with a coupon of 5.25% maturing in November 2028.
The balance will be used for the Group’s general corporate purposes, in line with its financial policies.
This operation extends the average maturity of TP’s debt, strengthening its financial flexibility and optimizing its debt structure, in the context
of the implementation of the Group’s strategic plan.
Finally, during the first half of 2026, in the context of the Future Forward strategy, the Group launched restructuring plans, primarily in Europe,
MEA & Asia-Pacific, as part of its transformation driven by artificial intelligence. A restructuring charge of €109 million was recognized during
the first half of 2026.

2.2.1. Highlights
On February 26, 2026, TP announced changes to its governance: Mr. Daniel Julien (Chief Executive Officer and Director) and Mr. Thomas

This cooptation, as other directors coopted, was ratified by the Shareholders' Meeting held on May 21, 2026.
In addition, on May 28, 2026, Teleperformance SE initiated a bond issue in two tranches, one with a maturity of six years and a coupon of
4.75% in the amount of €700 million redeemable on March 28, 2032, the other with a maturity of nine years and a coupon of 5.375% in the
amount of €500 million redeemable on January 28, 2035.
The net proceeds of the issue were intended to finance the early redemption of two existing bond issues, the first of €500 million with
a coupon of 0.25% maturing in November 2027, and the second of €700 million with a coupon of 5.25% maturing in November 2028.
The balance will be used for the Group’s general corporate purposes, in line with its financial policies.
This operation extends the average maturity of TP’s debt, strengthening its financial flexibility and optimizing its debt structure, in the context
of the implementation of the Group’s strategic plan.
Finally, during the first half of 2026, in the context of the Future Forward strategy, the Group launched restructuring plans, primarily in Europe,
MEA & Asia-Pacific, as part of its transformation driven by artificial intelligence. A restructuring charge of €109 million was recognized during
the first half of 2026.
