2021 first-half highlights
Key facts and figures

• Record growth in H1 2021 revenue and earnings
   • +36.8% like-for-like revenue growth
   • +89.5% increase in recurring EBITA and a margin of 14.0%, above
     pre-Covid levels
   • Net profit – Group share quadrupled to €255 million
   • Net free cash flow up +73.4% to €333 million

• Key developments and responsible transformation
   • Accelerating market digitalization
   • Consolidation of a hybrid business model:
       o   TP Cloud Campus platform in 52 countries today vs. 32 at end-2020
       o   Nearly 240,000 employees working from home

• LFL revenue growth accelerated sharply in Q2 vs. Q1, resulting from a very favorable base of comparison,
