         Acquisition of ZP Better Together
         On February 5, 2025, the Group finalized the acquisition of the entire share capital of ZP Better Together, a leader in the provision of
         language solutions and technology platforms to deaf and hard of hearing individuals. The acquisition, announced on November 26, 2024, has
         been approved by the relevant regulatory authorities. This acquisition, which enhances LanguageLine Solutions’ portfolio through the
         addition of key services, is in line with the Group’s development strategy aiming to strengthen its Specialized Services business.
         The acquisition price, which amounted to US$500.9 million, is final and was entirely financed through debt. Transaction costs amounted
         to €1.2 million and have been expensed as other operating expenses.
