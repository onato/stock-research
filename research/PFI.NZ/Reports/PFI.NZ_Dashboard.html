Valuation Model Approach — REIT AFFO/NTA Blend (sanity check passed)

PFI is valued as a REIT, not with an owner-FCF DCF: statutory EPS/NetIncome is dominated by non-cash IAS-40 fair-value revaluation noise, so the model instead capitalizes AFFO per share (already post-interest) at an 8.6% cost of equity, blended 50/50 with an NTA-based leg (PV of interim dividends + exit NTA at a justified P/NTA). Net debt is never subtracted from either leg — doing so would double-count leverage already embedded in a post-interest cash flow. The sanity check passed (no overvaluation trip): every implied multiple at the base intrinsic value sits below its historical average. Base IV of NZ$2.05 versus a NZ$2.37 price implies the market is pricing PFI at roughly a 7.3–7.5% required return, below our 8.6% hurdle. Read plainly: PFI looks fully priced to modestly overvalued at a disciplined required return — a quality hold for income, not a bargain buy at this price.
