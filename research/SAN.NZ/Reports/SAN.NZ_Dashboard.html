Annual Revenue & YoY Growth

Gross Margin

Both FY and half-year points are shown on the FY/interim toggle. FY: 15.8% (FY2013) to 26.5% (FY2025). H1 FY2026: 33.7%, up from 28.2% in H1 FY2025 -- three consecutive halves of expansion.

Operating & Net Margin

Cash Flow & Earnings Quality

The DCF's central argument: FY2025's record cash flow does not repeat at the same rate as it converts into working capital

Operating Cash Flow vs Net Income

FY2025 OCF was $135.3m against NPAT of $63.7m (strong conversion, partly a $22m cash-tax underpayment now unwinding). H1 FY2026 OCF collapsed to $13.7m against NPAT of $42.4m as earnings were absorbed into frozen mussel inventory -- the single most important risk signal in this dashboard. Toggle to H1/H2 view to see the H1 FY2026 print directly.

Free Cash Flow vs Owner FCF (Base Case)

Reported FCF (from the CSV) goes negative in several early years, so this chart cannot use a log scale. It swings from near-zero or negative through most of FY2013-FY2024 to a peak of $112.9m in FY2025. The DCF strips out SBC and lease payments to get owner FCF of $96.2m for FY2025 -- 6.4x the FY2016-FY2025 median (~$15m) -- and treats it as a peak: the model's year-1 (FY2026E) owner FCF is $74.2m, fading to a $41-50m mid-cycle by the outer projection years as margins, capex and working capital normalise (see the DCF Valuation section below for the full path).

CapEx

FY2025 capex of $22.5m (3.9% of revenue) and H1 FY2026's $6.7m are deferral-level lows against a 10-year average near 8% -- the DCF normalizes capex up to 6% of revenue by year 5 given the aging fleet's five-year dry-dock cycle.

Balance Sheet & Capital

Quota (carried at historic cost, not fair value) is the durable underlying asset; net debt has been cut sharply since FY2024

Total Debt vs Shareholders' Equity

Shareholders' equity (~$740m at FY2025, ~$780m at H1 FY2026) includes roughly $486m of fishing-quota intangibles carried at HISTORIC COST under NZ IFRS, not fair or replacement value -- book equity, and any P/B-style comparison drawn from it, understates the true asset backing. Net debt fell from $185.5m (FY2024-end) to $93.4m (FY2025-end) before rising to $102.1m at H1 FY2026 on the mussel inventory build.

EPS (cents)

EPS is reported in the CSV in CENTS (FY2025 = 68.1, meaning NZ$0.681), not dollars -- do not read the raw number as a dollar figure. EPS more than tripled from 21.1c (FY2024) to 68.1c (FY2025) on the same profit surge described in the Net Income and Gross Margin charts. The dividend (DividendPerShare column, in whole NZ dollars, e.g. 0.05 = 5.0 cents) has been held flat at 5.0 cents per half through FY2025 and the HY26 interim as the Board prioritises debt paydown over payout growth -- roughly 7% of EPS at the FY2025 run rate.

EBITDA

FY2025 EBITDA of $141.0m is a record, but includes non-cash biological fair-value gains on the salmon book; the DCF strips these out for its projected margins (see Cash Flow section).
