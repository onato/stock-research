OVERVIEW
EBITDA for the period improved significantly by 18.1% to $33.9m ($28.7m prior year) on similar revenue,
mainly due to lower fuel prices and changes in product and species mix. Net profit after tax, however,
decreased by 18.3% to $9.6m this period ($11.7m prior year), primarily due to impairments of $6.8m (pre-tax)
of our fleet and our mussel processing facility in Christchurch.
