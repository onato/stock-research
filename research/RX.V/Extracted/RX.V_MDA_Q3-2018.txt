Legacy Business
Protect-It®
The Company manufactures and markets
Protect-It®, a bio-friendly non-toxic product.
Protect-It® is a non-chemical, food-safe grain
insecticide. The patented formula contains
a natural mineral called diatomaceous earth.
Protect-It® was developed through collaborative research between
the Winnipeg-based Cereal Research Centre of Agriculture and
Agri-Food Canada. Protect-It® is used as a preventative treatment
against insect infestations in stored grains. It is registered for use in
Canada and the U.S. The Legacy Business provides an additional
source of stable cash flows for the Company allowing it to focus
on its strategic areas of growth in the pharmaceutical market.
