                    BioSyent is a Canadian specialty pharmaceutical

                    company focused on sourcing, acquiring or in-licensing

                    and further developing innovative pharmaceutical

                    and other healthcare products that improve the lives

                    term growth through portfolio diversification while

                    maintaining profitability.
