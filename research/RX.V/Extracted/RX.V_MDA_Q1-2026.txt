pre-launch stage, (iv) launch stage, (v) growth stage, and (vi)
maintenance and lifecycle management which could include

Product Pipeline
The Company is committed to expanding its pharmaceutical and
oral health product portfolio and accelerating its product pipeline
with a focus on innovative products that are unique. Although
some of these products are launched in markets outside of Canada,
they may require additional investment before the Company seeks
approval from Health Canada for the Canadian market.
