Growth & Profitability

Revenue & YoY Growth

FY2022 and earlier include the Automotive and Powder Metallurgy divisions, demerged as Dowlais Group in April 2023; Melrose has traded as an Aerospace-only pure-play since FY2023. On a like-for-like Aerospace-only basis the FY2023 annual report restated FY2022 revenue to £2,954m (vs £7,537m as reported here) — the FY2022→FY2023 drop in this chart is the divestiture, not an operating collapse.

Margins

Gross Profit, Operating Income & Net Income

FY2022 operating income (-£236m) covers the pre-demerger group including Automotive/Powder Metallurgy. Restated Aerospace-only, FY2022 operating income was -£270m and adjusted operating profit was £147m — compare FY2023 onward (Aerospace-only) to that restated figure, not to the as-reported FY2022 bar.
