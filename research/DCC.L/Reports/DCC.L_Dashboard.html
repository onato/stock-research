Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: DCC.L is subject to a pending scheme of arrangement: a recommended cash acquisition by Dragon Bidco (KKR / Energy Capital Partners), Rule 2.7 announced 27 Jul 2026 at 6,525p cash plus up to ~125p of Nexora-linked contingent consideration (now estimated ~42p following the 9 Oct 2026 Nexora sale, taking expected forward consideration to ~6,567p). Shareholders approved the scheme on 18 Sep 2026 (78.09% in favour); it still needs Irish High Court sanction and remaining regulatory clearances, with completion expected Q1 2027. While the scheme is live, a holder cannot realize the standalone intrinsic value below — only the deal consideration. The DCF's Prob-Weighted IV (≈7,272p) and Intrinsic Value (Base) (≈7,611p) are a standalone fair-value-of-the-business reference point (did the board accept a fair price?), not a number to compare against the current share price. The relevant comparison for a holder today is the deal-weighted value in the DCF JSON's takeover block — deal_weighted_iv ≈6,522p (92% probability of completion × PV of ~6,567p expected consideration, plus 8% probability of a break at the standalone weighted IV) — versus the current price, implying roughly a 5.8% annualised return if the deal completes on schedule.
