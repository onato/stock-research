NOTIFICATION OF CHANGE OF COMPANY NAME
Further to shareholder approval at the Annual G eneral Meeting of the Company held on 16 July 2026, the Company
is pleased to announce that its change of name from DCC plc to DCC Energy plc was registered at the Companies
Registration Office today, 17 July 2026, and is now effective.

Trading in the Company’s shares will commence under the new name DCC Energy plc with effect from 2              0 July 2026.
The Company’s TIDM code ‘(DCC)’, ISIN and Sedol will remain unchanged.

ABOUT DCC         ENERGY PLC
DCC Energy plc is a   leader in multi - energy sales and distribution in Europe and the US.

We serve millions of customers across the commercial & industrial          , public and domestic sectors. We deliver mainly
off- grid energy solutions, led by liquid gas, and operate services stations and fleet services.       We supply the secure,
cleaner and competitive energy our customers need, supporting industrial processes, heating homes, and keeping
transport moving. We do this while supporting customers through the transition with the energy and services they
need next.

Headquartered in Dublin, DCC Energy is listed on the London Stock Exchange and is a constituent of the FTSE 100.      In
our financial year ended 31 March 202 6, DCC Energy generated revenues of £15.4 billion and adjusted operating
profit of £634.0 million . DCC Energy has an excellent record, delivering compound annual growth of      14% inadjusted
operating profit and unbroken dividend growth      of 13% while maintaining high returns on capital employed over 3    2
years as a public company.
