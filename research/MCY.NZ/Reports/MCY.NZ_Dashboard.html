Earnings & Cash Flow — Annual (FY2017–FY2025)

EBITDAF is Mercury's headline operating metric (earnings before interest, tax, depreciation, fair-value changes and disposal gains/losses) and is what management guides to and is judged against. Statutory Net Income / EPS is dominated by non-cash fair-value swings on unhedged electricity derivatives and carbon units — e.g. FY2025 NetIncome was just NZ$1m (EPS $0.07) versus FY2024's NZ$290m, and H1‑2025 was a NZ$67m statutory LOSS despite EBITDAF of $418m. Treat the Net Income chart as noise, not a performance signal.
