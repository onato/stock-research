Financial Summary and Topics
Financial Results for FY2024
  ▌   In Consolidated financial results for FY2024, Net sales increased by 7.6% YoY to 1,177.4 billion yen, reaching a record high due to the increase in Completed construction contracts
      and Real estate sales, but Operating profit decreased by 1.2% YoY to 84.7 billion yen due to the increase of SG&A expense and Ordinary profit increased by 0.1% YoY to 83.4 billion
      yen.

  ▌   ROE in FY2024 decreased by 5.0pt YoY to 6.6% due to Extraordinary loss posted FY2024 3Q.

  ▌   Orders Received (non-consolidated) in FY2024 increased by 9.3% YoY to 586.6 billion yen, a record high, due to an increase in orders received from Private-sector condominiums.




Forecast for FY2025

  ▌   In Consolidated financial results forecast for FY2025, Net sales are expected to increase by 4.5% YoY to 1,230.0 billion yen, and Ordinary profit is expected to increase by 1.9% YoY
      to 85.0 billion yen due to an increase in Real estate sales and an improvement in profit ratio of completed construction contracts.

  ▌   Orders Received (non-consolidated) forecast in FY2025 is expected to reach a record high for the third consecutive term, amounting to 620.0 billion yen (5.7% increase YoY).

  ▌   ROE in FY2025 is expected to be 10.2%, recovering to above 10%.



Shareholders’ return and Capital policy

  ▌   The annual dividend per share for FY2024 is planned to be 85 yen as initially forecasted. and the dividend payout ratio is planned to be 67.4%

  ▌   The forecast for the annual dividend per share for FY2025 is planned to be 90 yen, an increase of 5 yen YoY, the dividend payout ratio is expected to be 44.6%.

  ▌   We announced the acquisition of own shares of 20.0 billion yen to improve capital efficiency and enhance shareholders return today. The total payout ratio is expected to be approx.
      80%.
