Summary of Financial Results
      In Consolidated financial results for FY2023 1Q-3Q, Net Sales increased 8.8% year-on-year to 768.9 billion yen due to an
       increase in sales of Construction contracts and Real estate sale. Ordinary Income increased 9.9% year-on-year to 60.5 billion yen
       due to an increase in the gross profit of Real estate sale, etc.
      Orders Received (non-consolidated) increased 27.5% year-on-year to 312.1 billion yen mainly due to an increase in orders
