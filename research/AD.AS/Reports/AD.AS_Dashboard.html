Growth & Margin

Revenue growth is steady but slow; the entire investment case rides on a ~3-4.4% operating margin, so a few tenths of a point matter more than the top line. FY2016 is a merger stub (Delhaize consolidated only from 24-Jul-2016, ~5 months) — exclude it from any growth read. FY2016/2020/2026 are 53-week years.

Revenue & YoY Growth

FY2016→FY2017 (+26.6%) is the Delhaize merger consolidating for a full year, not organic growth.

Operating Margin: Reported vs Underlying

Underlying margin strips restructuring, impairments and one-offs (e.g. FY2020's Giant Food pension settlement) — it is the company's own guided measure and sits slightly above reported margin in most years.

Net Margin

Gross Margin

Gaps are FY2016, FY2019, FY2020 and FY2023: cost of revenue was not disclosed in a comparable form in the source filings for those years — the chart skips them rather than plotting zero.

Segments, Online & Comparable Sales

The US and Europe segments are run as near-independent businesses (different banners, currencies and margin structures); online and like-for-like comparable sales are the growth engines inside a mature store base. Segment and online figures are disclosed annually only.

Revenue by Segment: US vs Europe

FY2025: US €53.1bn (57.5% of group) vs Europe €39.3bn (42.5%) — Europe grew faster in FY2025 (Profi/Romania + FX) while US comps have been the softer segment recently.

Segment Operating Margin: US vs Europe

Online Sales & Share of Group Revenue

Online sales reached €10.3bn in FY2025 (11.1% of group revenue), up from €2.0bn (4.0%) in FY2016 — mostly grocery e-commerce and meal-kit/on-demand delivery, not a marketplace GMV figure.

Comparable Sales Growth (Like-for-Like)

Like-for-like sales at constant store count and currency — the volume/price measure a grocer discloses instead of same-store units. FY2020 and FY2022 spikes are COVID pantry-loading and inflation pass-through respectively, not durable growth.

Cash Generation & Shareholder Returns

The equity story is per-share compounding: a stable free cash flow stream funds both the dividend and a buyback that has cut the share count by 30% since FY2016.

Net Income vs Free Cash Flow vs Owner FCF

Reported FCF already deducts lease principal repayments and net interest — it is NOT OCF minus capex (FY2025: reported €2,600m vs OCF−capex €4,425m). Owner FCF is the DCF's own build (reported FCF + interest paid − interest received − SBC), used to keep every year on one consistent basis back to FY2017; FY2016 is excluded as a merger stub.

Shares Outstanding

1,272m (FY2016) → 887m (FY2025), a ~30% reduction funded by ~€1bn/yr of buybacks — the main lever behind EPS growth outpacing net income growth.
