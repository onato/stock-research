3 Operation Performance of the First Quarter
As at the end of the reporting period, total assets of the Group amounted to RMB3,712.344
billion, representing an increase of RMB231.252 billion or 6.64% as compared to that at the end
of last year, of which total loans and advances to customers amounted to RMB2,013.215 billion,
representing an increase of RMB90.504 billion or 4.71% as compared to that at the end of last
year. Total liabilities amounted to RMB3,499.134 billion, representing an increase of RMB225.377
billion or 6.88% as compared to that at the end of last year, of which customer deposits amounted
to RMB2,154.561 billion, representing an increase of RMB111.095 billion or 5.44% as compared to
that at the end of last year.


During the reporting period, operating income of the Group amounted to RMB17.201 billion,
representing an increase of RMB66 million or 0.39% period-on-period, of which, net interest income
was RMB11.825 billion, representing a decrease of RMB156 million or 1.30% period-on-period, net
interest margin was 1.60%, representing a decrease of 0.16 percentage point period-on-period,
and net non-interest income was RMB5.376 billion, representing an increase of RMB222 million
or 4.31% period-on-period. Cost-to-income ratio was 25.73%, representing a decrease of 1.33
percentage points period-on-period. Net profit attributable to shareholders of the Bank amounted
to RMB5.992 billion, representing an increase of RMB43 million or 0.72% period-on-period.


As at the end of the reporting period, the capital adequacy ratio of the Group was 11.24%,
representing a decrease of 0.88 percentage point as compared to that at the end of last year. The
tier-one capital adequacy ratio was 9.40%, representing a decrease of 0.20 percentage point as
compared to that at the end of last year, and the core tier-one capital adequacy ratio was 8.26%,
representing a decrease of 0.14 percentage point as compared to that at the end of last year.


As at the end of the reporting period, the non-performing loan balance of the Group was
RMB27.268 billion, representing an increase of RMB1.231 billion or 4.73% as compared to that at
the end of last year; the non-performing loan ratio was 1.36%, flat as compared to that at the end
of last year; allowance to non-performing loans ratio was 155.63%, representing an increase of 0.26
percentage point as compared to that at the end of last year; the allowance to total loans ratio was
2.12%, representing an increase of 0.01 percentage point as compared to that at the end of last
year.
