FY26 Third Quarter Results Summary

Vital Healthcare Property Trust (Vital) provides the following third quarter (unaudited) results summary.

Commenting on Vital’s Q3 results, Chris Adams, Vital CEO said:

“Vital’s results, following the 31 December 2025 internalisation of management, are tracking slightly ahead of our
expectations, in part due to the strength of the Australian dollar where 69% of Vital’s properties (by value) are
located. Importantly, Vital’s underlying business continues to perform well and in line with expectations.”

About Vital (NZX code VHP):

Vital Healthcare Property Trust is an NZX-listed fund that invests in high-quality healthcare properties in New Zealand
and Australia including private hospitals (~80%* of portfolio value), ambulatory care facilities (~16%* of portfolio
value) and life sciences (~4%* of portfolio value).

Vital is the leading specialist listed landlord of healthcare property in Australasia. For more information, please visit our
website: www.vhpt.co.nz
* All figures are as at 31 March 2026, NZD/AUD exchange rate of 0.8330.
