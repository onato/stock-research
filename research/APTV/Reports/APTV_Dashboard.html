Model approach: One fix pass applied per Step 8c.5: raised WACC (base 10.5%->12.5%, bull 9.5%->11.0%, bear 12.0%->13.5%) with an explicit structural premium for extreme automotive-production cyclicality (+1.25pp) and concentrated China geographic/regulatory exposure (+0.75pp), and tightened the terminal-value exit-multiple cap (base 14x->12x, bull 16x->14x, bear 11x->9x EV/EBITDA, all now at or below the 10yr EV/EBITDA average of 13.2x). This lowered weighted_iv from .02 to .63 (-25%) and brought EV/EBITDA (8.53x) and P/Sales (0.56x) further inside the historical range. The P/E trip (273.4x vs a 128.8x ceiling) DID NOT resolve and further WACC/cap tightening will not resolve it: the checker computes implied P/E as (latest_FY_net_income - latest_FY_SBC) / shares using core_metrics FY2025, which is the LAST WHOLE-COMPANY (pre-spin) reported year -- FY2025 net income was M, almost entirely offset by SBC of M alone in this arithmetic (before even reaching the real driver, which is that FY2025 GAAP net income was itself crushed by ~M of Versigent separation-announcement costs disclosed in the qualitative analysis), giving an adjusted-EPS denominator of just /bin/zsh.19. Since the P/E ceiling is 2x the 10yr average (128.8x), any intrinsic value above roughly .50/share trips this rule against that denominator regardless of how conservative the DCF drivers are -- this is a denominator/data-availability mismatch (continuing-operations net income for FY2025 is not yet parsed into core_metrics as a separate RevenueContinuing/EBITDAContinuingBeforeSignificant row), not evidence the valuation is too high. EV/EBITDA and P/Sales, which are not distorted by the one-off-charge-and-spin-off double effect on net income, both sit comfortably inside the historical range post-fix and corroborate that the DCF value itself is reasonable. Recommend a future data task backfills post-spin continuing-operations FY2025 net income into core_metrics so this check can grade APTV on a comparable basis; until then this P/E trip on this specific ticker should be read as expected and disclosed, not iterated on further.
