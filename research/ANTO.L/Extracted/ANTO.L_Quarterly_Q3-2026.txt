                                                                                                     NEWS RELEASE, 15 OCTOBER 2026

                                             Q3 2026 PRODUCTION REPORT
                               7% INCREASE IN QUARTERLY COPPER PRODUCTION
             2027 PRODUCTION GUIDANCE OF 630,000–690,000 TONNES OF COPPER
Antofagasta plc CEO, Iván Arriagada, said: “During the quarter, we delivered a 7% increase in production to 151,900
tonnes, with Los Pelambres completing the drawdown of inventories accumulated during the prior quarter, and
demonstrating resilience following the severe weather event in July. Looking ahead, we have announced 2027
copper production guidance of 630,000–690,000 tonnes, reflecting higher grades at Los Pelambres and lower grades
at Centinela Concentrates.
“Construction activities across our major growth projects at Centinela and Los Pelambres are progressing well, and
we have entered the final phases of each schedule, ahead of construction and commissioning activities completing
in 2027. These projects will deliver significant growth and strengthen the resilience of our portfolio, and we remain
focused on completing each project in line with expectations.
“The copper market continues to demonstrate strong fundamentals. Looking ahead, the combination of continued
investment in energy infrastructure and digitalisation, together with supply constraints, provides a supportive
backdrop, helping to reinforce the long-term outlook.”

                                                                 HIGHLIGHTS
PRODUCTION

     •    Copper production in Q3 2026 was 151,900 tonnes, 7% higher on a quarter-on-quarter basis, reflecting
          higher output at Los Pelambres. Year-to-date copper production of 436,900 tonnes was 8% lower as a result
          of lower grades at Centinela Concentrates and lower recoveries at Centinela Cathodes.
     •    Gold production in Q3 2026 was 44,300 ounces, 4% lower than the previous quarter, following lower gold
          grades at Centinela Concentrates. Year-to-date gold production of 137,100 ounces was 5% lower as a result
          of lower gold grades at Centinela Concentrates and Los Pelambres.
     •    Molybdenum production in Q3 2026 was 2,900 tonnes, 6% below the level achieved in the prior period,
          with lower recoveries and grades at Los Pelambres, partially offset by higher grades recoveries at Centinela
          Concentrates. Year-to-date molybdenum production was 9,000 tonnes, 21% lower than the first nine
          months of 2025, with this movement principally related to lower molybdenum grades at Los Pelambres.

CASH COSTS

     •    Cash costs before by-product credits in Q3 2026 were $2.73/lb, representing a 7% decrease quarter-on-
          quarter, largely driven by higher production at Los Pelambres, the settlement of a three-year labour
          agreement at Centinela in the prior period and lower treatment charges. In the first nine months of 2026,
          cash costs before by-product credits were 20% higher at $2.81/lb, principally driven by the lower level of
          production and higher unit costs for key consumables, such as diesel and sulphuric acid

     •     By-product credits in Q3 2026 were $1.35/lb, representing a 15% decrease quarter-on-quarter, with lower
           output of by-products and lower realised prices for gold. By-product credits in the first nine months of 2026
           were 38% higher year-on-year at $1.53/lb, following higher by-product realised prices, partially offset by
           lower by-product output.
     •     Net cash costs in Q3 2026 were $1.38/lb, 1% higher on a quarter-on-quarter basis, with lower underlying
           costs offset by lower by-product credits. Net cash costs in the first nine months of the year were 3% higher
           at $1.28/lb, with higher underlying cash costs largely offset by stronger by-product credits. As inflationary
           pressures persist across the global economy, the Group remains focused on supply chain resilience,
           disciplined cost control, operational excellence and project execution, alongside the benefit derived from
           by-product credits.

SAFETY AND SUSTAINABILITY

     •     The Group’s strong health and safety track record continued in the third quarter of 2026, with no fatalities
           and the Group-level lost-time injury frequency rate remaining below 1.0. The Group has now completed
           over 5 years without a fatal accident at any of its sites and projects.

GROWTH AND DEVELOPMENT PROJECTS

     •     Our major growth projects continue to advance towards the completion of commissioning next year, in line
           with the schedule for each project.
     •     Centinela Second Concentrator Project: Key activities during Q3 2026 included the initial operation of the
           primary crusher and the overland conveyor as part of pre-commissioning activities. During the period, the
           overland conveyor reached its nominal operating speed during testing and integrated no-load tests were
           completed across key elements of the material handling circuit.
     •     Los Pelambres’ Growth Enabling Projects:
               o Concentrate pipeline: Progress continued on both the upper and lower sections of the pipeline
                   route, including tunnel sections and civil works, as well as pipeline installation, lining and testing.
                   In the lower section, the first hydraulic tests commenced, while preparations for hydrostatic testing
                   continued in the upper section.
               o Desalination plant expansion: Pre-commissioning activities continued across the main systems, in
                   parallel with the installation of cabling in the electrical room and equipment in the pumping station.
     •     Zaldívar Water Supply: Camp construction works continued during Q3 2026, in parallel with the receipt of
           key pipeline construction materials and initial civil works associated with the high-voltage transmission line.

FULL YEAR (FY) 2026 GUIDANCE

     •     Production guidance for 2026 remains unchanged. Group copper production for the full year is expected to
           be in the range of 625,000–655,000 tonnes.
     •     Group-level guidance for cash costs before by-product credits has been raised to $2.60–2.70/lb, reflecting
           global diesel prices remaining at elevated levels. The previous guidance range was $2.40–2.60/lb.
     •     Net cash cost guidance remains unchanged at $1.15–1.35/lb.
     •     Capital expenditure guidance is unchanged at $3.4 billion.1

FY 2027 GUIDANCE

     •     Total Group copper production in 2027 is expected to be in the range of 630,000–690,000 tonnes, with
           higher copper grades at Los Pelambres, partially offset by lower copper grades at Centinela Concentrates.
     •     In line with previous years, the Group will provide its 2027 guidance for cash costs and capital expenditure
           in the Q4 2026 Production Report, due for release in January 2027.

MINING OPERATIONS
Los Pelambres
Copper production during Q3 2026 rose by 17% quarter-on-quarter to 79,300 tonnes, reflecting higher recoveries
and the drawdown of approximately 7,000 tonnes of inventories accumulated during the prior period. These factors
were offset in part by lower throughput, which related to the July 2026 weather event and the related temporary
pause in operations. Copper grades and throughput at Los Pelambres are both expected to increase in the fourth
quarter.
Year-to-date copper production of 213,100 tonnes was 1% lower than in the prior-year period, reflecting a balance
of higher recoveries and lower ore throughput rates.
In respect of by-products, output of molybdenum was 2,000 tonnes in Q3 2026, 13% lower on a quarter-on-quarter
basis, with lower molybdenum grades and recoveries during the period. Gold production rose by 14% during the
quarter, reflecting the drawdown of inventories accumulated during the previous quarter, partially offset by lower
gold grades and recoveries.
Copper sales in Q3 2026 were 90,700 tonnes, approximately 10,000 tonnes ahead of production, as the Group
loaded material carried over from previous periods. Year-to-date sales in the first nine months of 2026 were broadly
in line with production.
Cash costs before by-product credits of $2.41/lb were 7% lower than the prior quarter in Q3 2026, with this
movement related to the higher level of copper production, depreciation of the Chilean peso and lower treatment
charges. Year-to-date cash costs before by-product credits of $2.53/lb were 13% higher year-on-year, reflecting
lower copper production, higher prices for key consumables such as diesel, explosives and electricity, and
appreciation of the Chilean peso, which was partially offset by lower treatment charges.
Net cash costs in Q3 2026 of $1.01/lb were 26% higher quarter-on-quarter, mainly due to lower molybdenum
production, partially offset by lower underlying costs and higher realised pricing for molybdenum. Year-to-date net
cash costs of $0.86/lb were 2% lower due to higher realised pricing for gold and molybdenum, partially offset by
lower by-product output and higher underlying cash costs.

Centinela
Total copper production was 46,800 tonnes in Q3 2026, 3% below the prior quarter, following lower production at
Centinela Concentrates. Total year-to-date copper production was 143,900 tonnes, 19% below the first nine months
of 2025, reflecting a lower contribution from both Centinela Concentrates and Centinela Cathodes, in line with the
mine plan.
At Centinela Concentrates, copper production in Q3 2026 fell by 5% quarter-on-quarter to 32,300 tonnes,
principally reflecting lower grades, in line with the mine plan, and lower recoveries. Year-to-date copper production
of 102,000 tonnes was 19% below the same period in 2025, reflecting the same factors outlined above.
At Centinela Cathodes, copper production in Q3 2026 was broadly in line quarter-on-quarter at 14,500 tonnes, with
higher recoveries offsetting lower grades. Year-to-date copper production of 41,900 tonnes was 19% lower year-
on-year following lower recoveries, offset by higher grades.
At Centinela Concentrates, gold production was 31,000 ounces in Q3 2026, representing a 10% decrease quarter-
on-quarter following lower gold grades and recoveries. Molybdenum production rose by 13% to 900 tonnes during
the quarter, corresponding to higher molybdenum grades and recoveries.
Copper sales in Q3 2026 at both Centinela Concentrates and Centinela Cathodes were higher than production,
reflecting sales of additional material carried over from previous periods. Consequently, year-to-date sales in the
first nine months of 2026 broadly matched production.
Cash costs before by-product credits were $2.97/lb during Q3 2026, representing a level 7% lower quarter-on-
quarter, principally reflecting the settlement of a three-year labour agreement in the prior period, in addition to
lower treatment charges and depreciation of the Chilean peso. Year-to-date cash costs before by-product credits
were $2.96/lb, 32% higher than the prior year period, reflecting the lower level of production and higher unit costs
for key consumables such as diesel and sulphuric acid, and the appreciation of the Chilean peso, partially offset by
lower treatment charges.
Net cash costs were $0.96/lb in Q3 2026, 9% lower on a quarter-on-quarter basis, as a result of higher output of
molybdenum, offset by lower gold prices. Net cash costs in the first nine months of 2026 were $0.78/lb, 11% below
the prior year period, with higher by-product credits partially offset by an increase in underlying cash costs before
by-product credits.

higher copper grades and ore throughput, partially offset by lower recoveries due to a campaign of lower
permeability. Year-to-date copper production of 55,800 tonnes was 4% below the same period in 2025, reflecting

lower sulphuric acid costs and depreciation of the Chilean peso. Year-to-date cash costs of $2.98/lb were 9% higher
year-on-year, principally reflecting lower recoveries and higher unit costs for key consumables such as diesel,
electricity and sulphuric acid and the appreciation of the Chilean peso.

The Transport Division (FCAB) transported a total of 1.4 million tonnes during Q3 2026, 3% lower quarter-on-
quarter, reflecting decreases in both rail and road transportation.

the north of Chile and a lower level of sulphuric acid demand by customers. Year-to-date tonnages for rail and road
transport were 10% and 15% lower on a year-on-year basis, which relates to lower levels of customer activity.
