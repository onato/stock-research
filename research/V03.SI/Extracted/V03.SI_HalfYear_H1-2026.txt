  For the six months ended 30 June 2026 (1H 2026), the Group registered revenue of S$1,354.7 million (1H 2025: S$1,261.9

  Measurement Instrumentation, Networking & Communication and Semiconductor Related Equipment technology domains
  across multiple end markets, including those supporting AI-related infrastructure, partially offset by decline in the Lifestyle
  Consumer technology domain.
