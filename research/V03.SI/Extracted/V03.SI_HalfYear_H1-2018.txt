PERFORMANCE REVIEW
For the quarter ended 30 June 2018, the Group generated revenue of $$952.3 million. Quarter-on-quarter, revenue for 2Q 2018 increased by 11.2%. On
a year-on-year basis, revenue declined by 6.0%.       For the six months ended 30 June 2018, the Group recorded revenue of $$1.8 billion, a slight decline of
2.6% year-on-year. In US dollar terms, revenue for 1H 2018 would have grown by 3.4%.


The Group recorded profit before tax (PBT) of S$115.2 million for the quarter ended 30 June 2018, an increase of 36.3% year-on-year. For 1H 2018, the
Group recorded PBT of $$213.8 million, an increase of 48.0% year-on-year. In line with the higher PBT, the Group reported income tax expense of
$$17.3 million for the reported quarter and $$32.2 million for 1H 2018. Net profit (profit attributable to owners of the Company) rose 40.2% year-on-year
to $$97.9 million for the reported quarter. For the six months ended 30 June 2018, net profit rose 53.3% year-on-year to $$181.6 million. The Group’s
initiatives to leverage its core capabilities in engineering, advanced manufacturing to deliver deep value creation and drive operational excellence across
the value-chain contributed to the improvement in profitability. The Group registered net margin of 10.3% for the reported quarter (2Q 2017: 6.9%) and
10.0% for 1H 2018 (1H 2017: 6.4%).


Diluted Earnings Per Share for the reported quarter was 33.6 cents (2Q 2017: 24.4 cents) and 62.4 cents for the first half of 2018 (1H 2017: 41.6 cents).
