Reading this dashboard: HFL.NZ is a closed-end investment trust, not an operating company. There is no revenue in the operating-company sense, no EBITDA, no capex, and no free cash flow — those fields are intentionally blank in the underlying data and omitted from every chart below. The metrics that matter are NAV per share, dividend per share & cover, and premium/discount to NAV. The central fact this dashboard is built to show: bare NAV per share fell from 337.76p (FY2016) to 223.32p (FY2025) — about −4.5%/yr — while the dividend was raised every single year from 20.00p to 24.90p. NAV total return (dividends reinvested) was about +6.1%/yr over the same 10 years. Both facts are true simultaneously.
