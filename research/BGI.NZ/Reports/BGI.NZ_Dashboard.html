Dead Ticker — This is Not a 30x Return

BGI.NZ is a legacy/frozen Yahoo Finance symbol. The company renamed from Blackwell Global Holdings Limited to RTO Limited and its NZX ticker changed BGI → RTO effective 1 May 2024. Yahoo's BGI.NZ quote is frozen at NZ$0.004 as of its last trade timestamp, 30 April 2024, on the pre-consolidation share count. The live, currently-quoted instrument is RTO.NZ, last close NZ$0.119 (7 Aug 2026).

The apparent ~30x jump from $0.004 to $0.119 is not a real return — it is an artifact of a 73.6259:1 share consolidation completed 30 August 2024 (673,759,059 shares → 10,000,026 shares). Any screener or valuation referencing "BGI.NZ" should redirect to RTO.NZ and treat the price discontinuity as a share-count artifact.

🛑

Going Concern — Material Uncertainty

Auditor William Buck (signed 24 June 2026) issued an unmodified opinion with an explicit Material Uncertainty Relating to Going Concern paragraph. Survival depends on a $200,000 related-party shareholder loan from majority owner Blackwell Global Group Limited, entered into after the 31 March 2026 balance date and expected to be advanced in July 2026.

Cash at FY2026 year-end was just $114,903 against an annual cash burn of ~$173,632 — under 8 months of self-funded runway before the pending loan. There is no operating business, no revenue-generating asset, and no committed timeline for a reverse takeover (RTO) transaction.

Model Approach — No Conventional DCF Was Run (sanity check: FAILED)

RTO Limited has no operating business, no revenue, and no positive owner free cash flow in any year since FY2023. A discounted-cash-flow model would either extrapolate a business that no longer exists or compound an unrepeatable cash-burn trajectory into a meaningless negative valuation. This dashboard instead shows a probability-weighted asset/scenario valuation (bear / base / bull), anchored on FY2026 net tangible assets (NTA) plus a scenario-dependent listed-shell scarcity premium for RTO optionality.

The market is not agreeing with this model. At NZ$0.119, RTO.NZ trades at roughly 6.4x FY2026 NTA, while the probability-weighted intrinsic value of NZ$0.0432 implies only about 2.3x NTA. Of the three scenarios, only the low-probability (8%) bull case ($0.25) clears the current price — both bear ($0.002, 60% weight) and base ($0.0687, 32% weight) sit well below it.

  • Unlike a mechanically tick-floored shell, RTO.NZ trades freely and has persistently commanded a 4.5x–6.9x P/NTA premium over the last two years — genuine, if illiquid, price discovery of RTO optionality.
  • This model's central estimate is deliberately conservative, treating years of unconverted "discussions with several potential acquisition targets" (repeated across at least three consecutive annual reports) as evidence a deal is uncertain rather than imminent.
  • A rational buyer at today's price is making a largely binary bet on bull-case-or-better RTO success — not investing in a business with a demonstrable path to that value.
