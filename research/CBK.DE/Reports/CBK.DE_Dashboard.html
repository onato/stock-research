Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: The multiples sanity check tripped: the original probability-weighted value of €36.25 implied 1.41x book, against a 10-year maximum of 0.72x (FY2024). One fix pass was applied. The cost of equity got an explicit +0.75pt premium for the pending UniCredit control decision and residual credit-cycle risk (base 10.75%, bear 11.75%, bull 10.25%). Earnings growth after the FY2028 Momentum plan, and terminal growth, were capped at the 2.4% through-cycle income CAGR. The weighted value fell to €31.87 (bear €22.12 / base €32.42 / bull €40.53), implying 1.24x book and 3.4x sales. That still exceeds the 1.08x trip line, and it is kept there on purpose. The historical band (0.23–0.72x) was set while Commerzbank earned 0–8% on tangible equity, below its cost of capital, and ends before the 2025 re-rating driven by UniCredit's approach and the Momentum upgrade. Forcing a pass would mean assuming returns near the bear case. The investment framing is income plus modest growth, not a re-rating story. At €36.27 the stock trades about 14% above the weighted value and about 70% above the €21.53 entry price at a 15% hurdle. The price already assumes about 14.5% sustained returns on tangible equity, close to the full 15% Momentum target. Upside from here depends mostly on UniCredit paying a control premium, not on standalone value.
