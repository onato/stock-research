## Thesis

A parent-controlled keiretsu supplier priced as if its balance sheet were captive forever, inside an environment — TSE governance reform, cross-shareholding unwind, the 2025 Toyota Industries take-private precedent — that is systematically forcing value realisation at exactly this kind of company. Tokai Rika has already done most of its own unwinding: a Nov-2023 board decision to hold no policy shares as a rule, six more listed names sold out entirely in FY26 (¥9.4bn proceeds, ¥5.9bn gains), just ¥2.1bn of listed policy holdings left, a DOE 3% dividend floor, treasury cancellation. The bridge (cash, deposits, verified cash-like securities, investment securities, pension-surplus credit, less debt) is **¥139.8bn ≈ 54% of the market cap**, leaving the operating business at **~2.0x EV/EBITDA**. Any take-private/buy-in premium is deliberately **excluded** and held as free option value on top. Toyota Motor holds **34.4% ex-treasury — a >1/3 special-resolution blocking stake**; Toyota-related sales ~74%.

Realisation rests on structural pressure plus the company's own cadence; with the cross-holding unwind nearly complete, **the DOE 3% floor is what pays you to wait**. An activist filing would flip to an upside catalyst — re-check quarterly.

## The bear case, stated fairly

Five consecutive years of ICFR material weakness (FY Mar-21→Mar-26; FY21–25 restated, corrections filed May-26) with remediation unproven — even granting the restatement left operating profit essentially untouched (FY23/24 identical, FY25 −0.5%), control risk at this concentration is not free. The bZ-shock is reported fact, not hypothesis: Nikkei (19-Mar-26) documented keiretsu suppliers losing orders as Toyota/Nissan adopt Chinese parts, and switch/HMI content is precisely what decontenting strips. FY27 guidance is a real step-down (OP −16%, net −32%) on Middle East and raw-material drag. A 34% parent can entrench as easily as rescue: special resolutions are blocked, any endgame needs Toyota's consent, and Toyota can sit forever. The easy balance-sheet catalysts are also mostly spent — cross-holding sales can no longer fund returns at scale. And a decade of ≤3.1x EV/EBITDA says the market may keep treating the cash as captive. Bear path: ¥2,476 (−19%).
