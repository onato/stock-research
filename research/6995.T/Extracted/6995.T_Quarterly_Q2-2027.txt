 Financial Results and Position
１．
 （１） Consolidated Financial Results for FY2027 First Half
           For the six months ended September 30, 2026, consolidated net sales increased by 18,252 million yen or
        5.8%, year on year, to 333,075 million yen. Consolidated operating income decreased by 3,783 million yen or
        17.7%, year on year, to 17,562 million yen. Consolidated ordinary income decreased by 7,534 million yen or
        29.9%, year on year, to 17,646 million yen. Profit attributable to owners of parent decreased by 9,529 million
        yen or 49.0%, year on year, to 9,934 million yen.


           Results by segment were as follows.
           (Japan)
              Net sales increased by 15,201 million yen or 9.6% year on year, to 173,182 million yen due to an increase
           in client production volume. Operating income decreased by 903 million yen or 71.1% year on year, to 367
           million yen due to higher raw material costs and increased fixed costs.

           (North Americas)
             Net sales increased by 4,710 million yen or 5.4% year on year, to 91,858 million yen due to the impact of
           yen appreciation. Operating income decreased by 1,724 million yen or 27.9% year on year, to 4,456 million
           yen due to a decrease in client production volume.

           (Asia)
             Net sales increased by 1,454 million yen or 1.5% year on year, to 97,514 million yen due to the impact of
           yen appreciation. Operating income decreased by 612 million yen or 5.0% year on year, to 11,543 million yen
           due to a decrease in client production volume.

           (Others)
             Net sales increased by 1,048 million yen or 4.4% year on year, to 24,735 million yen. Operating income
           decreased by 188 million yen or 9.7% year on year, to 1,759 million yen.

   （２） Consolidated Financial Position for FY2027 First Half
         Assets at the end of the second quarter in consolidated accounts were 548,850 million yen, an increase by
       516 million yen from the end of previous year.
         Liabilities at the end of the second quarter in consolidated accounts were 176,535 million yen, a decrease
       by 277 million yen from the end of previous year due to a decrease in accrued bonuses to employees.
         Equity at the end of the second quarter in consolidated accounts was 372,314 million yen, an increase by
       793 million yen from the end of previous year due to an increase in retained earnings.

   （３） Explanation for Forecast of Consolidated Financial Results
         We have not revised our consolidated financial results forecasts for the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027,
       which was announced on May 15, 2026.

（３）Notes to Semi-Annual Consolidated Financial Statements
   (Note to Special Accounting Methods in Preparation for Semi-Annual Consolidated Financial Statements)
      Regarding tax expenses, we reasonably estimate the effective tax rate after applying tax effect accounting to
   income before income taxes for the consolidated fiscal year, and calculate tax expenses by multiplying income
   before income taxes for the interim period by the estimated effective tax rate.
      However, if the calculation of tax expenses using the estimated effective tax rate results in a significantly
   unreasonable outcome, the statutory effective tax rate is used.
      In addition, deferred tax expenses are included in income taxes.

(Significant Subsequent Events)
   (The Repurchase and Cancellation of Treasury Shares)
   At a meeting of the Board of Directors held on October 28, 2026, the Company resolved to repurchase its
own shares pursuant to Article 156 of the Companies Act, as applied pursuant to Article 165, Paragraph 3
thereof. The Company also resolved to cancel treasury shares pursuant to the provisions of Article 178 of
the Companies Act. The details are as follows.

   1．Reason for share repurchase and cancellation of treasury shares
     Under the medium-term business plan (Tokai Rika Vision 2030), the Company will flexibly
   repurchase treasury shares for the purpose of enhancing shareholder returns. Based on this policy, the
   Company will repurchase treasury shares and cancel all repurchased treasury shares.

   2．Details of matters related to share repurchase
   (1) Share categoryCommon Shares                  Common Shares
   (2) Total number of shares to be repurchased      Up to 2,600,000 shares (2.9% of the total number of
                                                     issued shares (excluding treasury shares))
                                                     *The Company has introduced a share-based
                                                     remuneration system for employees. The 1,560,800
                                                     shares of the Company’s stock held under the
                                                     system are not included in the number of treasury
                                                     shares stated above.
   (3) Total amount of share repurchase cost         Up to 8,600,000,000 yen
   (4) Date of share repurchase                      From October 29, 2026 to December 31, 2026
   (5) Method of share repurchase                    Purchase through Tokyo Stock Exchange Off-Auction
                                                     Own Share Repurchase Trading (ToSTNeT-3)

   3．Details of matters related to cancellation
   (1) Share category                                Common Shares
   (2) Total number of shares to be cancelled        All repurchased shares mentioned in Section 2
   (3) Scheduled date of cancellation                The last day of the month immediately following the
                                                     month in which the share repurchase is completed
                                                     (if such last day is not a bank business day, the
                                                     immediately preceding bank business day).
