Summary
1. FY2025 2 nd Quarter Financial Results
●Sales and profits decreased year on year due to a decrease in production
 volume at major customers, etc.

2. FY2025 Financial Forecast
●As cumulative performance up to the 2 nd quarter has exceeded
 our budget plan, we have revised the annual forecast upward.

3. Shareholder returns：Change of dividend policy
●Regarding our new dividend policy, we have changed our target to
 “Dividend On Shareholders’ Equity: 3％” while also taking
 “dividend payout ratio and dividend yield” into consideration.
  Based on this change in dividend policy, we have decided to increase
  the dividend per share from ¥70 announced at the beginning of
  this fiscal year to ¥90. (Annual dividend increase +¥20)               2/24
