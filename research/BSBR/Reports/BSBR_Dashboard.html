Model approach: none -- trip diagnosed as a checker currency/per-unit artifact, not a valuation error. Both sides of every multiple divide a USD (or doubly FX-converted) per-ADS price by BRL per-SHARE fundamentals (1 ADS = 2 shares): the checker's history says BSBR traded at 0.05-0.17x book and 0.4-1.7x earnings for a decade, which is impossible for a bank that has traded around 1-2x book. Applying a WACC premium or growth cap against that reference would have distorted a model whose currency-consistent multiples sit inside or below BSBR's own range. The diagnosis candidate (wacc_too_low, 'regulatory single point of failure') is already priced: country risk in the 15% BRL cost of equity, IoC reform listed as a key risk, bear weight 30%.
