(I)   Operations

In the first quarter of 2026, faced with a critical window period for industrial transformation and
upgrading, as well as external environmental challenges, the Company resolutely upheld its mission
and responsibility as the “national team” in energy and power construction. Focusing on the core task
of safeguarding national energy security and advancing the construction of a new-type power system,
the Company maintained sound operation and development, continuously enhanced its core functions,
and further consolidated the foundation for high-quality development.

1.    Focusing on expanding core business markets, with significant improvements in the quality
      and efficiency of core business development.

The Company has deepened its engagement in the core sectors of energy, power and water resources,
firmly undertaken the strategic mission of ensuring national energy and water security, and concentrated
its efforts on expanding high-value core business markets. In the first quarter, the value of contracts
secured in the Company’s core business sectors accounted for 80% of the total. The energy and power
business maintained a leading position in the industry. The Company signed contracts for a number
of projects, including the Guangdong Yangjiang Sanshandao No. Three Offshore Wind Farm, the
conventional island and BOP construction and installation works for Units 3 and 4 of the Guangdong
Taishan Nuclear Power Plant, the Turpan Valley Peak Smart Computing Center and its supporting wind
power facilities, and the EPC general contracting for the 2×600MW Coal-fired Power Environmental
Protection Replacement Project of Guangzhou Zhujiang Power Plant. These projects cover offshore
wind power, nuclear power, integrated energy solutions for smart computing centers, and coal-fired
power plant upgrading and renovation. Among them, domestic power transmission and transformation,
energy storage and hydrogen energy businesses increased by 40.25%, 111.53% and 161.20% year-
on-year, respectively. The water conservancy business accelerated its revitalisation, with the value of
newly signed domestic contracts rising 263.6% year-on-year in the first quarter. Major projects secured
include the second phase of the key embankment reinforcement project in the Dongting Lake area
of Hunan Province and supporting projects for the Three Gorges Water Transport New Channel. The
Company actively promoted national key water conservancy projects such as the Yarlung Zangbo River
Downstream Hydropower Project and the River Diversion Project from Yangtze River to Hanjiang
River, continuously consolidating its dominant position in the water conservancy sector.




                                                 - 10 -
2.   Focused on breakthroughs in emerging fields and created a demonstration benchmark for
     green and new quality productive forces.

Focusing on the goal of building a new energy system, the Company accelerated the cultivation of
strategic emerging industries such as new energy, new energy storage and hydrogen energy, forming
a development pattern of “technological breakthroughs + project implementation + model innovation”.
In the first quarter, the amount of new contracts signed for domestic new energy and comprehensive
smart energy businesses accounted for 46% of the total amount of domestic contracts. In terms of
energy storage, following the successful operation of the world’s first set of 300MW compressed air
energy storage power station “Energy Storage No. 1” in Yingcheng, Hubei Province, the 300MW
artificial cavern compressed air energy storage new technology demonstration project in Jiuquan,
Gansu Province, has entered the commissioning stage. The Company has successfully mastered the
key technical pathways for both natural salt caverns and artificial caverns as underground gas storage
reservoirs, and achieved 100% localization of key core equipment for 300MW compressed air energy
storage and deep underground space utilization product technologies, achieving a globally leading
position in long-duration energy storage technology and engineering capabilities. In terms of hydrogen
energy, the Jilin Songyuan Green Hydrogen-Ammonia-Alcohol Integration Project Phase I has been
successfully put into operation, successfully tackling the world-class challenge of reconciling new
energy volatility with chemical stability, and creating four world records and four internationally leading
technologies. It has been selected as a green and low-carbon advanced technology demonstration project
by the National Development and Reform Commission, as well as one of the first batch of green liquid
fuel technology research and industrialization pilot projects and hydrogen energy pilot projects by the
National Energy Administration, providing a replicable “Energy China Solution” for large-scale green
hydrogen consumption and green chemical industry. In terms of integrated development, the Company
actively promoted major transportation and energy integrated projects such as the Electrified Dongting
Lake Basin and the Electrified Canal Corridor, hosted a sub-forum at the Boao Forum, promoted the
construction of a new green, smart, economical and safe transportation energy system, and continuously
established new benchmarks for integrated development of “energy+”, providing innovative pathways
for flexible regulation and diversified accommodation and consumption of the new power system.

3.   Adhered to the core drive of scientific and technological innovation and activated new impetus
     for digital and intelligent transformation and development.

The Company has deeply implemented the innovation-driven development strategy. At the scientific and
technological innovation work conference held in February 2026, the Company designated scientific
and technological innovation as the “top priority project”. By coordinating the breakthrough of key core
technologies with a comprehensive digital and intelligent transformation, the Company has injected
strong impetus into building a high-level scientific and technological innovation enterprise. In the first
quarter, in terms of core technology research and breakthroughs, the Company obtained 487 patent
authorizations. The National Yarlung Zangbo River Downstream Hydropower Technology Innovation
Center, in which the Company is a key participant, was approved by the Ministry of Science and
Technology. The high-altitude wind power capturing umbrella was selected as one of the top ten national
key technologies of central enterprises for 2025. The “Large-capacity Solid-liquid Hybrid Lithium-ion
Battery Energy Storage System” and the “Large-scale Green Power-Hydrogen-Ammonia Integrated

                                                   - 11 -
Flexible Synthesis Technology and Equipment” led by the Company were selected as part of the top
ten scientific and technological innovation achievements in the energy industry by the National Energy
Administration in 2025. In terms of digital and intelligent transformation, the Company promoted the
integrated development of the four elements, i.e. “computing power, algorithms, data and scenarios”,
and launched the “Rongguang (融光)” large model. Guided by scenarios, driven by data, supported by
technology, and with security as the bottom line, the Company focused on implementing five major
projects, including the integration of management digitalization and intelligence, innovation in industrial
digitalization and intelligence, leapfrog development of the digital-intelligent industrialization, activation
of data value and upgrading of digital and intelligent technologies, strived to achieve intelligent upgrade
of core businesses, breakthroughs in key areas of characteristic tracks, and comprehensive reinforcement
of regulatory penetration, accelerated the creation of an artificial intelligence platform and products
with the characteristics of Energy China, and sped up the empowerment of intelligent dispatching, lean
operation and maintenance, and safety supervision in the new power system.

4.    Deepened green cooperation along the “One Belt and One Road” and served the global green
      and low-carbon transformation.

The Company has deeply integrated into the high-quality development pattern of jointly building the
“One Belt and One Road”, focusing on the global demand for green and low-carbon transformation.
Supported by the core businesses of energy, power and water conservancy, the Company comprehensively
expanded the space for green cooperation overseas. In the first quarter, the Company achieved the amount
of newly signed overseas contracts of RMB91.39 billion, with energy, power and water conservancy
business accounting for over 80%. The Company signed contracts for a batch of high-quality projects,
including the Egypt Benban Nefertiti 1,000 MWh Energy Storage Project, the South Africa Noxholme
475MW Photovoltaic Project and the DRC Nzilo II Hydropower Station etc., and deeply participated
in the formulation of regulations and standards for the power industry in Vietnam. The China-Laos
500kV Grid Interconnection Project, in which the Company participated in the construction, has been
fully completed. The Uzbekistan Tashkent Yad Energy Storage Power Station, which was invested
and constructed by the Company, has achieved full capacity grid connection. The projects such as
the Cambodia Kracheh cement project, the Uzbekistan Navoi photovoltaic energy storage project
and Uzbekistan phase III energy storage project are all advancing in an orderly manner. Through the
integrated operation of “investment, construction and operation”, the Company’s overseas asset layout and
operational efficiency have steadily improved. In addition, the “China-Ethiopia Civil Engineering Green
Construction and Intelligent Operation and Maintenance ‘One Belt and One Road’ Joint Laboratory” for
which the Company participated in application, has been successfully approved, further consolidating
its technical leading position in green construction field and continuously contributing “Energy China
Solutions” to the green energy cooperation along the “One Belt and One Road”.
