III. OTHER REMINDER

(I)   Operations

The Company closely followed the new national energy security strategy and the goal of “Dual Carbon”,
focused on its two core businesses, i.e. energy and power, and water conservancy, and developed
related diversified businesses such as green transportation, green buildings, artificial intelligence,
new materials, civil explosives, ecological environmental protection, high-end equipment, urban
development and operation, and industrial finance in a coordinated manner, to fully promote the high-
quality development of the Company. In the first three quarters of 2025, the amount of newly signed
contracts, operating revenue and total profit of the Company were RMB992.775 billion, RMB323.544
billion and RMB8.507 billion respectively, representing a year-on-year increase of 0.40%, 9.62% and
0.09% respectively. The overall operation showed a favorable situation of “stronger main businesses,
better structure and greater resilience”.

1.    Main business advantages have been continuously consolidated, and the leading position has
      become more prominent.

The Company has always adhered to its main responsibilities and main businesses in energy and power,
and water conservancy, deeply integrated into major national strategies, and continuously consolidated
its leading position in the industry through planning guidance, high-end planning and major project
construction. During the reporting period, the Company took the lead or participated in eight major
national projects, including the “15th Five-Year Plan” power development plan and energy technology
innovation, etc., successfully held the first “International Conference on Innovation and Cooperative
Development of Energy and Power Engineering” and the “High-level Forum on New Energy System”,
and took the lead in establishing a branch of the Global Sustainable Transport Innovation Alliance,
thereby further enhancing the industry discourse power and influence of the Company. The Company
fully participated in the construction of major national projects such as the Yarlung Zangbo River
downstream hydropower project, the Xinjiang Dashixia Water Conservancy Hub, the Guangdong-Tibet
DC Project, the desert-gobi-wasteland new energy base and the water diversion project from the Yangtze
River to the Han River. The market share in key areas of energy and power remained at a high level.
In the first three quarters, the operating revenue and total profit from the Company’s advantageous
businesses such as energy, power and water conservancy increased by 16.76% and 18.42% respectively
year-on-year, both accounting for over 75% of the total profit of the Company, fully playing the role
of a “cornerstone” for performance. In particular, the traditional energy business maintained strong
momentum, with operating revenue and total profit increasing by 22.68% and 26.62% respectively
year-on-year; the new energy business maintained rapid growth, with operating revenue and total
profit increasing by 10.62% and 3.95% respectively year-on-year. The stable structure and growth
resilience of the main businesses were further demonstrated.




                                                - 11 -
2.   Scientific and technological innovation has achieved fruitful results, and industrial upgrading
     has been comprehensively accelerated.

The Company adhered to scientific and technological innovation as the core driving force for high-
quality development, and carried out key core technology research on the construction of a new energy
system and a new power system, systematically created a source of original technology and accelerated
the cultivation of strategic emerging industries focusing on cutting-edge directions such as new energy
storage, hydrogen energy, solar thermal power generation, controlled nuclear fusion and deep-sea
energy. Technological research and development capabilities continued to be strengthened. In
the first three quarters, the R&D expenses of the Company increased by 7.72% year-on-year, and the
Company has been granted 1,496 patents, representing a year-on-year increase of 12.90%, including
498 invention patents; 2 projects were selected as national green and low-carbon advanced technology
demonstration projects, 3 platforms were selected as pilot verification platforms for central enterprises,
and 4 achievements entered the final review stage for the National Science and Technology Progress
Award. Major demonstration projects were rapidly implemented. Hubei Yingcheng Demonstration
Project, the world’s first 300MW non-combustion compressed air energy storage power station, which
was independently developed and constructed by the Company, achieved full-capacity grid connection
in January 2025 and entered commercial operation, with many indicators such as energy storage scale
and conversion efficiency setting world records; Gansu Jiuquan 300MW artificial chamber compressed
air energy storage demonstration project has entered the commissioning stage, and is expected to
be put into operation by the end of the year; Zhongnengjian Songyuan Hydrogen Energy Industrial
Park, the world’s largest green hydro-aminol integration new technology demonstration project under
construction, will be put into operation by end of 2025 and was selected as the first batch of green liquid
fuel technology research and industrialization pilot projects of the National Energy Administration;
Xiamen 5GWh electrochemical energy storage battery project has been steadily advanced; the 2 million
tons/year coal gangue high-value utilization new technology demonstration project in Datong, Shanxi
Province has been ignited and entered trial operation on 30 September 2025, with an aim to creating
a full-chain demonstration of “industrial solid waste - green building materials - product application”.
The contribution of strategic emerging industries continued to increase. In the first three quarters,
the Company’s operating income from strategic emerging industries increased by 16.75% year-on-year,
accounting for 38.81%, the formation of new quality productive forces accelerated, and the industrial
structure continued to upgrade to high-end.




                                                   - 12 -
3.    The international competitive advantages continued to expand, and the joint construction of
      the “One Belt and One Road” has achieved remarkable results.

The Company firmly implemented the international priority development strategy, continued to optimize
the global market layout, deeply participated in the energy and infrastructure cooperation under the
“One Belt and One Road” initiative, and achieved both scale and quality improvement in international
operations. The global brand influence continued to grow. The Company frequently attended
international high-end events such as the International Infrastructure Investment and Construction
Summit Forum, China - Central Asia Summit, China - ASEAN Expo and World Expo, systematically
shared Chinese technology, Chinese standards and energy engineering solutions with its influence in the
global energy and power industry continued to increase. International operating performance grew
rapidly. In the first three quarters of 2025, the Company’s international business achieved double-digit
growth in newly signed contract amount, operating income and total profit, with year-on-year increase
of 12.67%, 21.21% and 21.29%, respectively. Among them, the amount of new contracts signed for
overseas new energy and comprehensive smart energy businesses increased by 58.7% year-on-year,
becoming the main engine driving overseas growth. Remarkable results have been achieved in the
“One Belt and One Road” markets. The newly signed contract amount of the Company in countries
along the route accounted for 90.33% of the total overseas amount, representing a year-on-year increase
of 8.64%. The Company signed a number of key projects such as the Saudi Arabia PIF Phase V Shaqra
1GW Wind Power Project, Saudi Arabia PIF Phase V Starah 2GW Wind Power Project, Saudi Arabia
PIF Phase VI Khulis 2GW Photovoltaic Project and the 450MW gas-fired power station in Garinjia,
Kano State, Nigeria, further consolidating its regional market advantages and brand position.

4.    Lean operations enhanced quality and efficiency, strengthened development foundations.

The Company comprehensively promoted lean management and digital transformation, reinforced
operational control, and continuously improved development quality and risk resilience. Project
management and supply chain synergistically created efficiencies. The Company issued multiple
documents on lean management and penetrative supervision for projects, established a categorized and
graded control mechanism, and enhanced our ability to carry on our contracts and create efficiencies.
Our supply chain management received an A-level rating from the State-owned Assets Supervision and
Administration Commission of the State Council. Our operating revenue from ongoing projects increased
by 13% year-on-year. Digital transformation has been systematically advanced. The Company
fully implemented the three-year action plan for artificial intelligence, accelerated the construction
of computing infrastructure and an AI platform, improved the intelligent and penetrative regulatory
system, deepened the application of technologies such as BIM design and intelligent construction,
promoted the digital upgrade of traditional industries, and fully released the value of data resources.
Investment and operations returns steadily increased. The Company strengthened lean management
and control throughout the entire lifecycle of investment projects. In the first three quarters, our operating
revenue from investment and operations business achieved a year-on-year increase of 24.93%, with
asset operation efficiency continuously optimized. Key operational metrics continued to improve.
Through the systematic implementation of the “1+5” special governance initiatives and the “five major
special actions”, our key efficiency indicators, such as the operating cash flow rate and total employee
productivity, have steadily increased, and administrative expenses have been effectively reduced.
