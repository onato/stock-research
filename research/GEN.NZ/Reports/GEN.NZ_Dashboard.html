Growth & Profitability

Operating Income & NPAT

Operating income (net interest + fee + advisory revenue) is climbing on loan-book growth, while NPAT has flattened and fell 2.9% in FY2026 — costs are outrunning revenue.

Return on Equity (ROE)

ROE on average equity has declined for four straight years: 11.9% (FY2023) → 9.0% (FY2026), against a 16.75% cost of equity.

Net Margin & Cost-to-Income Proxy

Operating margin (proxy for 1−cost/income) and net margin, both compressing as personnel and ECL costs scale with the loan book.

Book Value per Share (Consolidation-Adjusted)

1-for-4 share consolidation executed 2-Aug-2024. Pre-FY2025 shares outstanding are restated ÷4 here so the series is comparable; raw as-reported CSV figures for FY2019-FY2024 are ~4x the post-consolidation basis and must never be plotted alongside FY2025-FY2026 without this adjustment.

Balance Sheet & Capital

Loan Receivables Proxy & Total Assets

Total assets NZ$283.7m (+30% YoY); loan receivables NZ$242.5m (+60.5% YoY, from NZ$151.1m) drove almost all of the growth.

Equity vs Total Assets (Capital Constraint)

FY2026: equity +7% vs assets +30% — the balance sheet is scaling far faster than the capital base that must support it under the 8% trust-deed minimum capital ratio.

Funding Base: Term Deposits & Other Liabilities

These are customer term deposits — the raw material funding the loan book — not financing debt. TotalDebt in the CSV is correctly 0; General Capital does not carry a "debt-free" badge because it carries NZ$252m of deposit liabilities.

Dividend per Share

Dividends only began FY2025 under the new (Jul-2025) policy of paying up to 40% of NPAT, fully imputed. FY2026 DPS NZ$0.0118 = ~4.5% gross yield at NZ$0.26.
