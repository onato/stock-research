No priority-order cause (peak-earnings extrapolation, WACC understatement, aggressive growth) diagnosed this trip since the other three multiples already passed cleanly before any fix. Applied Step 8c.5 item 4 (tighten the exit-multiple cap) as the closest defensible lever regardless: base-case terminal_cap_multiple lowered 18x->16x and base WACC raised 8.0%->8.3%, which pulled weighted_iv from .05 to .84 (upside -0.8% -> -5.8%).
