1. Results of Operations and Financial Overview
  (1) Quarterly Results of Operations Overview

        During the first half of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027 (April 1, 2026 to
     September 30, 2026), the global economy progressed in a manner actualizing the real GDP
     growth rate in the 3% range indicated by the International Monetary Fund this month in its
     World Economic Outlook Report for this fiscal year, despite facing various challenges. In
     addition to trade frictions with no end or resolution in sight and geopolitical divisions and
     turmoil, inflationary pressures continued unabated and interest rates rose in response.
     Nevertheless, we believe that the global economy has managed to hold up thanks to the
     continuing spectacular expansion of AI-related investment, which has induced investment
     across various industries, and the unremitting efforts made by companies.
        Under these circumstances, we have also maintained close communication with our
     customers, continued to provide a reliable supply of products with required quality, and
     conducted sales with agility. As a result, the operating income increased by 27%, the
     ordinary income increased by 20% in the July-September of the current fiscal year
     compared with the same period of the previous year. We will focus even more on business
     growth and improved operating performance. To that end, we will accelerate the
     development of products that are valuable to our customers and actively continue capital
     investments with a medium- to long-term perspective in order to meet the needs and
     demands of the customers and markets in a timely manner.
        The business results for the first half of the fiscal year ending March 31, 2027, are as
