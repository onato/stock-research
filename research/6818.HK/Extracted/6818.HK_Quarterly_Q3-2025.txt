ii.     Strategy Implementation

During the reporting period, the Bank proactively served the real economy and national strategies,
expanded key business areas, strengthened capability building, and continuously improved core
competitiveness, in a bid to promote high-quality and sustainable development.




                                                 2
First, the Bank made solid strides in serving the real economy with a focus on serving the five
target areas of finance, including technology finance, green finance, inclusive finance, pension
finance and digital finance. Leveraging its strengths in featured operation, the Bank rolled out an array
of special supportive measures such as devising separate credit plans, giving preferential funds transfer
pricing (FTP) basis points to key areas and weak links, and offering capital cost discounts for major
projects, realizing steady growth in loans to key sectors including technology, green finance, inclusive
finance, medium- to long-term manufacturing, strategic emerging industries and private enterprises.
In technology finance, the Bank continued to optimize the “1+16+100” specialized and dedicated
organizational system for technology finance, and provided tech firms with diversified financial services
with a supporting system featuring excellent services, products, ecosystems, industry research and digital
intelligence. In green finance, the Bank continuously advanced system construction, product innovation
and service upgrading, and improved the product spectrum of green finance themed by “green loans as
the base + various green finance featured products + industry-specific solutions”. In inclusive finance,
the Bank deepened and implemented the MSE (micro and small enterprises) financing coordination
mechanism, continuously refined its “3+1+N” online product series including credit products, guarantee
products and mortgage products, and constantly enhanced its capabilities of online business expansion
and risk management for inclusive finance. In pension finance, the Bank launched a brand named “He
Guang Yi Xiang” (Sunshine Elderly Comfort), built two features including comprehensive services
that combine finance with medical treatment, elderly care, healthcare and tourism, and collaborative
services that integrate banking, insurance and elderly care, set up more pension finance service centers,
actively implemented relevant re-lending policies to support consumer finance and elderly care, and
provided all-round comprehensive financial services for elderly care service institutions. As at the end
of the reporting period, the Bank offered over 900 pension finance products on a cumulative basis, and
its online special zone of pension finance served customers for 2.882 million person-times. In digital
finance, the Bank fully leveraged advanced technologies such as big data, cloud computing and AI to
accelerate the whole-process transformation of key businesses towards online operation, mobile access,
intelligent processing and ecosystem integration with a focus on building scenarios such as fee payment
and financing. By getting deeply rooted in serving the real economy, the Bank steadily scaled up its
digital finance business and continuously advanced high-quality development.




                                                    3
Second, the Bank optimized asset & liability management to enhance integrated management
efficiency. In terms of assets, the Bank refined asset allocation structure, focused on key areas and
competition fields, strengthened industry research, and refined marketing paradigms to bolster advantages
in featured and specialized competitions. In terms of liabilities, the Bank adhered to a dynamic quantity-
quality balancing strategy. For corporate deposits, the Bank deepened integrated operation, expanded
high-frequency transaction scenarios, strengthened the collaboration of corporate banking and retail
banking to advance agency payroll services, and continuously improved its capabilities in acquiring
customers in batches and obtaining source funds. For retail deposits, the Bank refined deposit structure
to enhance deposit quality through four growth drivers including source fund introduction, sales-led
growth, customer group management and business traffic retention, and took multiple measures to
advance the high-quality development of deposits with respect to quantity, quality and profitability.

Third, the Bank consolidated customer base by continuously deepening customer group integrated
management. In terms of corporate customer group management, the Bank practiced a stratified,
grouped and tiered operation approach by formulating differentiated marketing strategies for strategic,
potential and basic customer groups. In retail customer group management, the Bank strengthened
batch customer acquisition and business coordination through collaboration between corporate banking
and retail banking, upgraded the grouped customer management model, optimized online and offline
integrated channel service capabilities, and promoted the empowerment of digital tools and the building
of professional teams to elevate the quality and efficiency of customer comprehensive services.

Fourth, the Bank insisted on breaking new ground while upholding fundamental principles with
a focus on developing business features. The Bank developed the technology finance business called
“Sunshine Tech Innovation”, and established a full-life-cycle product system covering equity, loan,
bond, custody and private banking products by focusing on key industries and deepening scenario-based
comprehensive services. The Bank rigorously developed the “Sunshine Wealth” business based on
two product series such as “Sunshine Wealth Management” and “Sunshine Gold Selection”, launched
themed wealth management products including pension finance, inclusive finance, green finance and
tech innovation, and innovatively launched the “7+” Fixed Income Plus wealth management product
system. As at the end of the reporting period, the Bank realized an AUM of RMB1.89 trillion for wealth
management products. The Bank continued to maintain the industry-leading position of the “Cloud Fee
Payment” platform, made efforts to build its digital finance scenario platform, and launched featured
products such as Enterprise Administrative Management Service Platform (“Xin Yue Tong”). The
Bank continuously optimized its “Sunshine Transaction Banking” comprehensive financial ecosystem
services, constructed the “Sunshine Transaction +” comprehensive service system, and pooled resources
to build the supply chain finance product system to satisfy enterprises’ needs in digital transformation
and scenario-based fund settlement. As at the end of the reporting period, the number of supply chain
finance customers reached 19,928. The Bank continued to promote the “Sunshine Investment Banking”

business, implemented the strategy of “integration of commercial banking, investment banking and
private banking”, and enhanced comprehensive financing service capabilities. During the first three
quarters of 2025, the Bank underwrote RMB316,541 million of bonds and granted RMB23,925 million
of M&A loans. The Bank rigorously advanced its “Sunshine Financial Market” business, adhered to
the main development direction of “stable allocation, active trading, increased agency business and
strong risk control”, and optimized and strengthened its core investment capabilities to stimulate market

risk control. The Bank strengthened internal control and compliance management, and optimized
the money laundering risk management system. The Bank deepened credit-granting industry research
to proactively empower business development. The Bank continued to enhance loan concentration
management, regularly carried out penetrative risk monitoring for large-amount credit customers, and
advanced the mandatory early-warning response mechanism to resolutely forestall new NPLs. The
Bank strengthened the prevention and control of financial risks in key areas such as local government
debts, real estate and credit cards. The Bank advanced professional and refined transformation for the
management of special assets, continuously stepping up efforts to dispose of non-performing assets.

Sixth, the Bank accelerated digital transformation to empower business development. The Bank
emphasized the role of digital means in driving business development, kept empowering scenario-
based ecosystems with digital technology and established a digital scenario-based work mechanism
featuring “value stratification, pattern classification and Head Office-branch coordination” to improve
the efficiency of customer conversion via scenario traffic. The Bank built a new pattern of digital
management, leveraged data elements and digital technologies to transform traditional productivity,
systematically deployed the innovative application of AI, and actively engaged in research on large
model technology application in key business areas to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of
operation and management.

vi.   Operation Analysis

As at the end of the reporting period, total assets of the Group reached RMB7,217,717 million,
representing an increase of 3.72% over the end of the previous year. Among them, total loans and
advances stood at RMB4,026,943 million, representing an increase of 2.37% over the end of the
previous year. Total liabilities registered RMB6,612,858 million, representing an increase of 3.83%
over the end of the previous year. Among them, the balance of deposits was RMB4,184,694 million,
representing an increase of 3.69% over the end of the previous year.

From January to September 2025, the Group realized a net profit of RMB37,278 million, representing
a year-on-year decrease of 3.40%. The Group’s operating income registered RMB94,337 million,
representing a year-on-year decrease of 7.95%. As for the subcategories, net interest income was
RMB68,881 million, representing a year-on-year decrease of 5.11%. Net fee and commission income
stood at RMB15,502 million, representing a year-on-year increase of 2.18%. Return on weighted average
equity was 8.83%, representing a year-on-year decrease of 0.83 percentage point.

From January to September 2025, the Group incurred operating expenses of RMB29,005 million,
representing a year-on-year decrease of 4.47%. The impairment losses on credit assets stood at
RMB18,838 million, representing a year-on-year decrease of 23.41%.

As at the end of the reporting period, the Group’s balance of non-performing loans (NPLs) registered
RMB50,712 million, up RMB1,460 million over the end of the previous year. The NPL ratio was
1.26%, up 0.01 percentage point over the end of the previous year. The provision coverage ratio was
168.92%, down 11.67 percentage points over the end of the previous year.

As at the end of the reporting period, the Group’s CAR, Tier 1 CAR and common equity Tier 1 CAR
stood at 13.65%, 11.70% and 9.65% respectively, all of which met the regulatory requirements. The
leverage ratio was 7.20%, representing a decrease of 0.08 percentage point over the end of the previous
year.

V.    OTHER MATTERS

i.    Issuance of perpetual capital bonds

As approved by National Financial Regulatory Administration and the People’s Bank of China, the
Bank successfully issued RMB40 billion of perpetual capital bonds on 18 September 2025. The initial
coupon rate is set at 2.29% for the first five years, which will be reset every five years thereafter.
The issuer retains a conditional call option on every interest payment date starting from the fifth year.
All the net proceeds, after deducting relevant issuance expenses, were used to replenish the Bank’s
Additional Tier 1 Capital.

ii.   Increased holdings of the Bank’s shares by the controlling shareholder

China Everbright Group Ltd. planned to increase its holdings of the Bank’s A shares so as to give
full play to the role as the Bank’s controlling shareholder, convey confidence in the Bank’s future
development, reinforce the recognition of the Bank’s long-term investment value in the capital market
and support the Bank’s high-quality development. As at 11 September 2025, China Everbright Group
Ltd. increased its shareholdings of the Bank by 13,970,000 A shares totaling RMB51,660,999 by
centralized bidding via the Shanghai Stock Exchange system, accounting for 0.02% of the Bank’s total
share capital. For more details, please refer to the Announcement on the Progress of the Shareholding
Increase Plan of the Controlling Shareholder disclosed by the Bank on 11 September 2025.
