                  Overview
                  In Q3 2026, net revenue reached €738.1 million, up 23% YoY, or 22% on a constant currency basis,
                  in line with our full-year guidance.
                  Processed volume totaled €451.1 billion in the third quarter, up 30% YoY as we continued to outperform
                  our market. The number of transacting terminals across the platform reached 878k, up 25% YoY.
                  This strong quarter reflects continued expansion of wallet share with existing customers and a healthy
                  cohort of new enterprise merchants across regions and pillars, as well as the first-time consolidation of
                  Talon.One and Orb.



                  Business Updates
                  Digital
                  • Digital net revenue reached €387.4 million, up 16% YoY, or 14% on a constant currency basis.
                  • Processed volume grew 28% YoY.
                  • Our growth in Digital was driven by wallet share gains with our largest customers, with strong
                    performance in travel & mobility and content & subscriptions. Adyen's tiered pricing structure
                    incentivizes our customers to bring more volume to our platform.

                  Unified Commerce
                  • Unified Commerce net revenue reached €250.5 million, up 29% YoY, or 27% on a constant currency
                    basis.
                  • Processed volume grew 22% YoY.
                  • Unified Commerce results reflect our wallet share gains and scaling with global customers amid a
                    subdued retail environment and revenue contribution from Talon.One.
                  • We now support 499 customers processing across channels at scale.1

                  Platforms
                  • Platforms net revenue reached €100.2 million, up 46% YoY, or 44% on a constant currency basis.
                  • Processed volume increased 55% YoY.
                  • We now have 46 platform customers processing over €1 billion annually, up from 37 last quarter and
                    31 a year ago. Active business customers2 reached 312k, up 48% YoY.
                  • Adyen is growing rapidly across the SaaS segment, gaining notable share in financial services and
                    insurance. Our Platforms offering allows us to provide payments and financial products to smaller
                    merchants, which will be a key driver of future growth.

                  2

                  Talon.One and Orb
                  Customer interest in the combined offering across payments, loyalty, billing, and financial products has
                  been substantially stronger and broader than anticipated. To capitalize on the momentum, we are
                  intentionally accelerating our planned investment behind the Talon.One and Orb teams.
                  Orb’s usage-based billing platform gives us an early entry point with high-growth, AI-native businesses.
                  Since Orb joined Adyen, we won the payments volumes of several of their customers, including Route
                  and Wasabi Technologies. Meanwhile, with Talon.One, merchants are excited by the unique
                  opportunity to combine industry-first in-store and online loyalty to drive higher customer engagement.
                  The combination of these value propositions with the power of Adyen's platform creates a significant
                  growth opportunity.



                  Team
                  Reflecting the momentum in the business, our recent hiring has been focused on specialized technical
                  and go-to-market roles, particularly in the US. To attract and retain high-caliber talent, our
                  compensation structure increasingly emphasizes equity-based incentives.
                  During the third quarter we added 177 net-new team members on an underlying basis, and 399 team
                  members through the acquisitions of Talon.One and Orb, which brought us to a total of 5,596 FTEs.
                  We now target 1,000-1,100 net-new FTEs in 2026, including Talon.One and Orb. We continue to target
                  550-650 net-new FTEs on an underlying basis in 2026.

                  Financial Objectives
                  Adyen’s opportunity remains substantial, and we continue to build the business guided by decisions
                  that benefit us over the long term.
                  Net revenue growth: We expect 2026 net revenue growth of 21% to 23% YoY on a constant currency
                  basis.
                  EBITDA margin: Strong merchant interest in Adyen's joint value proposition across payments, loyalty,
                  billing, and financial products gives us the confidence to accelerate investments in our team, platform,
                  and products to capture the large opportunity we see ahead. As a result, we now expect EBITDA
                  margin of 50% in 2026. To retain flexibility, we are discontinuing our medium-term EBITDA objective.
                  We remain confident in the inherent operating leverage of our business model over time.
                  Capital expenditures: We expect CapEx to be 7% of net revenue this year. We expect CapEx to return
                  towards historical levels post-2026.
