Adyen is the complete financial operating system for modern
commerce — from payments to loyalty, billing, and money
movement.



The recent acquisitions of Talon.One and Orb reflect our commitment to addressing merchants’
structural challenges beyond the transaction. Together, they extend our platform and move us deeper
into our customers’ operations. Both acquisitions build on the real-time behavioral insights of our
Dynamic Identification layer. As more merchant workflows consolidate on Adyen, our intelligence
becomes richer, our products become more valuable, and our customer relationships become
increasingly durable.

Our product updates this first half of the year show how we broaden our reach to create more value for
customers. We introduced Adyen Agentic, enabling merchants to integrate once and participate across
the rapidly evolving ecosystem of Al commerce. We also officially launched Intelligent Money
Movement, bringing enterprise payments, liquidity management, and payouts together on a single
platform.

We're growing faster than the market because our platform continues to solve more of our customers’
most critical challenges. We'll continue to outpace the market as the scope of what we solve increases
across the commerce lifecycle. Our structural differentiation enables us to deepen our role within our
merchants’ operations, expand Adyen's long-term growth opportunity, and cement our position as one
of the world's leading fintech companies.
Our competitive advantage
Long-term architectural choices made years ago continue to strengthen our commercial edge. Every
interaction processed on our unified platform feeds the intelligence embedded across our products.
This compounding, structural advantage is built on three foundational layers.


Asingle, global technology platform
Our single platform allows us to develop and make our products available to customers across regions,
channels, and industry segments. Customers benefit from faster access to new products and
optimizations while we scale efficiently by avoiding duplication and minimizing maintenance overhead.


Global banking licenses
Our own licensed infrastructure grants direct access to payment networks, giving us control over funds
and enabling us to scale advanced monetization and treasury products globally. The resulting
regulatory and technical autonomy allow us to move money with the speed, efficiency, and flexibility
modern commerce demands.


Dynamic Identification
Our unique dataset powers the proactive intelligence embedded across our platform. Because our
diversified product lines are connected, every transaction strengthens this intelligence, continuously
resolving historical blind spots and improving how we optimize payment performance, manage risk, and
create value for our merchants.
Built for the full commerce lifecycle
We address complexities that span the entire commerce journey — before, at, and after the transaction
— evolving beyond our historical discussions centered on optimizing the moment of transaction. We
help merchants deepen relationships with shoppers, maximize revenue, and manage money movement
across their entire operations.

Expanding where we play accelerates our growth on two fronts. First, it enables us to win in fast-
evolving verticals and drive growth across a more diverse set of stakeholders. Recent wins like OpenAl
show our ability to solve the most complex operational and financial challenges in these emerging
business models. Second, it allows us to capture a greater share of wallet with existing customers. As
we solve a broader set of challenges, we become deeply embedded in our customers’ businesses and
increasingly central to how they scale.

Merchants trust us with a larger share of their operations over time. Our typical share of wallet increases
from under 20% in years 3-7 to over 40% after year 12. Longstanding partners like Google, Microsoft,
Spotify, and Uber continue to expand their usage even after a decade on our platform, highlighting the
significant runway within our base. Newer partnerships follow this exact same trajectory. For
example, Toast! recently expanded their relationship with Adyen into the U.S.

This combined momentum yields healthier, broader-based growth across our portfolio, with 300
merchants now accounting for 60% of our growth, down from more than 70% three years ago.

Deepening our customer relationships before the transaction
We're uniquely positioned to create value early in the shopper journey. By unifying promotions, rewards,
and product discovery with payment data on a single platform, we help our merchants deliver more
relevant experiences from the very first interaction.

The acquisitions of Talon.One? and Orb? allow us to solve the complexities of a commerce landscape
that is becoming increasingly dynamic, with pricing, promotions, and monetization evolving
continuously in response to consumer behavior. Recognizing this shift, we made the deliberate decision
to bring these solutions onto our platform. Both companies give merchants the ability to influence two
fundamental drivers of revenue in real time: pricing and purchasing behavior. These determine how
much customers buy, how often they return, and ultimately, how merchants grow.

Talon.One expands our role before the transaction. Loyalty has been a strategic focus for us for several
years, and Talon.One built a world-leading enterprise loyalty and promotions platform. They power
incentive programs for over 300 global brands, many of which are already Adyen customers.
Merchants struggle to bridge online and offline loyalty because in-store transactions traditionally live in
asilo. Integrating Talon.One’s promotion engine directly into our architecture will bridge this gap and
enable personalized rewards and offers in real time. By combining the global reach of more than 838K
transacting terminals with our unified payments data, we're able to unlock truly omnichannel loyalty
experiences that standalone systems cannot replicate.

Our acquisition of Orb extends this strategy beyond customer engagement into monetization. Al is
transforming how software is consumed, making usage-based billing the default pricing model for many
modern businesses. Traditionally, companies relied on fragmented systems for usage metering, billing,
and payments, creating operational complexity. By integrating Orb with our payment infrastructure, we
replace this fragmentation with a unified monetization engine spanning the entire revenue lifecycle —
from usage tracking to settlement. Beyond basic billing, Orb enables merchants to continuously
experiment with, optimize, and evolve their pricing strategies using real-time data. For software and Al
companies, this eliminates underlying friction and turns billing into a strategic growth driver.

Adyen Agentic is another way in which we expand our platform beyond payments. Two modules of the
product suite extend our role earlier in the commerce journey: Agentic Feed structures and distributes
real-time catalog, pricing, and availability data into conversational commerce environments so Al
agents have accurate inventory. Agentic Cart then hooks the agent's selection back into existing
checkout, tax, fulfillment, and order management systems to build a dynamic, purchase-ready order.
Because this architecture is Al platform-agnostic, merchants can integrate once and participate across
the entire ecosystem. Early participants in Adyen Agentic include strategic partners American Express,
Mastercard, Salesforce, and Visa, as well as enterprise retailers ESW, Scheels, Sézane, and
SharkNinja.4

Together, these capabilities, from loyalty to dynamic billing and agentic commerce, fundamentally
expand our role. By engaging customers earlier, we are creating new revenue opportunities for them
and, in turn, for Adyen. This opens the door to new partnerships, strengthens existing customer
relationships, increases stickiness, and creates new opportunities to expand our share of wallet before
a transaction even begins.

Leading with trust at the moment of transaction
For the past two decades, we've been helping merchants optimize payment performance. It's our
foundation, and we lead in this space by improving conversion, reducing fraud, and lowering costs for
our merchants across a wide range of industries.

In H1, we continued solving increasingly complex payment challenges across sectors. Luxury retailer
Tod's Group® unified more than 100 stores and 35 e-commerce markets onto our single platform in
under three months, replacing fragmented acquiring relationships with one global integration. In
sporting events, HYROX,° the global fitness racing series, achieved a 95.1% authorization rate under
peak loads while enabling new markets to go live in just three days. In the public sector, GOV.UK Pay’
migrated from a competitor to us, selecting our flexible infrastructure to standardize payments across
approximately 1,000 public services, drive operational efficiency, and support future innovation.

As commerce becomes increasingly global and fraud more sophisticated, payment performance is no
longer determined by processing transactions alone. Every authorization requires balancing conversion,
fraud prevention, and payment costs in real time. This is where Dynamic Identification gives us an
unreplicable advantage. Rather than relying on static credentials or retrospective reporting, it
continuously applies intelligence from across our unified platform to optimize every payment decision
as it happens.

One of the clearest outcomes of this intelligence is higher conversion. Dynamic Identification
determines the optimal way to process every transaction by intelligently selecting payment methods,

The same intelligence transforms how merchants manage risk. Because we see the entire payment

blocking to active revenue preservation. For one leading North American sports league, we identified a
customer who claimed refunds for more than 70% of their purchases over several years — an abuse
pattern that traditional controls had missed. By recognizing behavior across the entire customer journey
rather than evaluating transactions in isolation, the merchant was able to stop policy abuse without
adding friction for legitimate customers.

Payment performance also depends on how transactions move through local financial infrastructure.
Our direct connections to domestic payment rails enable merchants to process payments more
efficiently, improve authorization rates, and reduce costs while operating locally at global scale. We
recently obtained a Category II payments license from the Central Bank of the UAE,’ giving us full
control over local settlements. This delivers a more locally aligned payment experience for merchants.
Earlier this year, we also received approval for direct access to France's domestic interbank clearing
system,’ strengthening the infrastructure that supports future product innovation and local payment
capabilities.

The moment of transaction will only become more important as commerce becomes increasingly
automated. As Al agents begin making purchases on behalf of consumers, trust must be established
instantly, without a human in the loop. The payment module within the Adyen Agentic product suite is
built to enable merchants to accept machine-to-machine payments while maintaining the same high
standards of authentication, fraud prevention, and payment performance that defined our platform for
the past twenty years.

Owning the monetization and treasury stack after the transaction
For large global enterprises, complexity often increases after the transaction. While customer
payments happen in seconds, the movement of money across banks, entities, and markets remains
fragmented, delaying access to capital and creating operational friction. This means our opportunity
extends beyond processing payments to managing money.

That's why we officially launched Intelligent Money Movement (IMM)" in the first half of 2026, bringing
the end-to-end flow of funds together on our single platform. By connecting these workflows on
infrastructure we own and operate, we give businesses real-time visibility over liquidity, automate
treasury operations, and put capital to work sooner — without the delays and complexity of fragmented
banking relationships. Companies including Etsy, Expedia, and Vinted use these capabilities to simplify
treasury operations, accelerate the movement of funds, and support global growth.

The value of IMM becomes especially clear in industries with complex fund flows. For online travel
businesses, payments often take four or more days to move from the customer to the platform and
ultimately to suppliers. We are working with some of the world's largest travel platforms to reduce that
process to near real time, giving them instant access to funds and greater control over working capital.

We also capture post-transaction value through our embedded financial products, enabled by our
banking licenses. By offering native business accounts, card issuing, and capital within a single
integration, we enable platforms and enterprises to consolidate their financial operations in one place.

On April 23 2026, Adyen announced that it entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the shares of Talon.One
GmbH, through a share purchase. The closing of the transaction occurred on July 1 2026. As from this date (acquisition
date), Talon.One will be consolidated into the Adyen Group.

The acquired entity consists of the following:


                                          PARTICIPATION
COMPANY              DOMICILE             PERCENTAGE           CORE ACTIVITIES



Talon.One GmbH       Germany              100%                 Loyalty and incentive software solutions (Services/Software-as-a-Service)




Talon.One develops and operates a real-time incentives engine and loyalty platform for global brands and merchants. The
acquisition addresses a key customer need within the Group’s Unified Commerce strategy by connecting online and in-
store shopper interactions with real-time decisioning capabilities. The transaction is expected to expand the Group's role
from optimizing individual payments to improving the overall economics of transactions across its global merchant
network.


The preliminary fair value of consideration for the transaction is EUR 750 million, which is subject to adjustment during the
measurement period upon finalization of the Purchase Price Allocation (PPA). Of the purchase price, EUR 69.8 million has
been issued as Adyen shares.


17.2 Acquisition of Orb

On June 112026, Adyen announced that it entered into a definitive agreement to acquire 100% of the shares of enterprise
billing platform Orb through a reverse triangular merger. The closing of the transaction occurred on July 12026. As from
this date (acquisition date), Orb will be consolidated into the Adyen Group.


The acquired entity consists of the following:


                                          PARTICIPATION
COMPANY              DOMICILE             PERCENTAGE           CORE ACTIVITIES



Orb Inc.             USA                  100%                 Enterprise billing and monetization infrastructure




Orb provides an infrastructure engine that tracks real-time usage data and processes complex billing and pricing contracts
for global enterprise clients. The acquisition addresses a strategic entry point into high-growth digital businesses and
usage-based pricing models, particularly within the evolving Al sector. By connecting Orb’s billing logic directly with the
Group's real-time payments platform and financial health signals, the transaction is intended to close the loop between
automated billing and transaction success execution.


The preliminary fair value of consideration for the transaction is USD 335 million, which is subject to adjustment during the
measurement period upon finalization of the PPA. Of the purchase price, USD 33.2 million has been issued as Adyen
shares.
Together, Talon.One and Orb are expected to add approximately 1 percentage point to net revenue growth for 2026, with

commenced on 1 July 2026, following the acquisitions, therefore the fair value of net assets, goodwill and the composition
of the fair value of consideration are not yet available.


Goodwill arising from the acquisitions is expected to primarily reflect strategic synergies from integrating Talon.One’s real-
time enterprise loyalty platform and Orb’s usage-based billing engine directly into Adyen's payment architecture,
extending platform capabilities into pre-transaction engagement and dynamic monetization.
