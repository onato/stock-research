Executive Summary


   First-Half Overview

 ◆ Sales and profit increases exceed the targets by mainly a product mix enhancement strategy
   focused on strengths
    ・Sales increase of 3.9% due to factors including maximization of Asahi Super Dry, Mitsuya Cider, and other strong brand assets
    ・Operating income increase of 18.3% due to the impact of the sales increase and product mix enhancement as well as implementation of
     profit structure reforms in all businesses

 ◆ Structural reform of businesses in Oceania and strengthening of the business foundation
   in China and Southeast Asia
    ・In Oceania, businesses in New Zealand under-performed targets, but progress made with structural reforms in the businesses in Australia
    ・Improved profitability from China and strengthening of the business foundation in Southeast Asia as a result of the Etika acquisition



  Full-Year Business Strategy
 ◆ Revise full year targets upward and expand brand investment to reinforce strengths
    ・Revise annual performance targets upward, mainly in the Alcohol Beverages and Soft Drinks Businesses, and aim to increase
     operating income by 8.1%.
    ・ Focus investment on reinforcing strong brand assets in each business in pursuit of long-term stable growth.

 ◆ Achieve the targets in the medium-term management plan by steadily improving business
   performance and implementing an optimal capital strategy
    ・Achieve sales and profit increases in all businesses and aim for net income of ¥69.0 billion (a record high for a 14th consecutive year).
     ・Continue an optimal capital policy to achieve the KPIs in the medium-term management plan (ROE and EPS increases).
