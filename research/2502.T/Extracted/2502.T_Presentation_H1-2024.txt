 Executive Summary

         ◆ Overview of H1 2024
              ➢ Achieved 3.8% growth in revenue and 6.2% growth in Core Operating Profit
                (constant currency basis)
                 ・While profit from our business in Oceania declined, overall profit outstripped our plan following strong
                  performances in Japan and Europe
                 ・Unit sales prices continued to improve on the back of determined premiumization strategies which included
                  appropriate pricing strategies and an expansion of global brands

         ◆ Future Direction
              ➢ FY2024: Aim to achieve 2.7% revenue and 4.3% Core Operating Profit growth
                (constant currency basis)
                 ・We will invest in initiatives that will further improve our competitive advantage while also accelerating the
                  reform of our earnings structures, enabling us to maintain our initial plan for Core Operating Profit while also
                  considering various risks
                 ・In addition to expanding shareholder returns based on sound finances, we will actively engage in dialogue
                  with capital markets to help improve corporate value

Sustainability: Environment
⚫ Formulated a new roadmap to help attain Asahi Group Environmental Vision 2050
⚫ Earned SBTi approval of net zero CO2 emissions targets across the value chain by 2040


  We formulated a roadmap to help achieve the Asahi Group             Roadmap: Climate Change
  Environmental Vision 2050, outlining our strategies, target
  milestones, and initiatives for ensuring desired progress in four
  pillar areas: Climate Change, Packaging, Agricultural Raw
  Materials, and Water Resources.


  On June 28, we earned Science Based Targets initiative (SBTi)
  approval of net zero CO2 emissions targets across the value
  chain by 2040 including FLAG from SBTi to ensure our targets
  align with the Paris Agreement.

Sustainability: Social

⚫ Launched a new global reporting system

  In May 2024, we launched a new Speak Up reporting system to help
  us recognize and respond to any compliance issues or human rights
  violations at an early stage. The system functions in the local
  language of all the countries in which we operate and is designed to
  make it easier and more comfortable for any stakeholder to submit a
  report 24/7/365




                  ⚫ Joint research with University of Tsukuba to help eradicate inappropriate drinking

                     Conducted joint research with University of Tsukuba on reducing alcohol intake through
                     the provision of non-alcohol beverages. The research results on the use of non-alcohol
                     beverages to suppress alcohol intake were shared with medical organizations and free
                     samples offered
