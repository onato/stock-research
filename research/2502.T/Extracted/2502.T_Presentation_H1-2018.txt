  performance of on-premise market.

  retail price hike.
【Sales Volume of Asahi Breweries】
 ・H1: Finished below target with -7.5% YoY decline in total volume due to the market contraction more than expected in
  each category.

                 newly launched products in competitors.

  at achieving the initial target in H2.
