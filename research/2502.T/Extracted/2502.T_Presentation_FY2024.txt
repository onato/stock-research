 Executive Summary
       ◆ 2024 Overview

               ➢ Slightly-below-anticipated Core Operating profit: +3.7% YoY overall. While profit declined in the Oceania region due
                 to a tough business environment, profit improved higher than anticipated in Japan and Europe.

               ➢ Unit sales prices improved on appropriate pricing strategies and premiumization. Launched Asahi Global
                 Procurement Pte. Ltd. (AGRPO) to drive global procurement. Restructured regional earnings structures

               ➢ Increased cash flow generation and fulfilled guidelines for financial soundness. Increased dividend payout ratio to
                 over 40% one year ahead of schedule
                     *AGPRO: Asahi Global Procurement Pte. Ltd.(Organization promoting global procurement)

       ◆ 2025 Direction

               ➢ Set a minimum increase in Core Operating Profit of 3.2% in initial plan after factoring in items such as continued
                 growth investment and the risk of a potential shrinking in demand. Aim to increase that measure beyond the 3.2%
                 minimum

               ➢ Promote management that is conscious of capital costs and stock prices based on the Revised Key Indicator
                 Guidelines and Financial Policy compiled following repeated dialogue with capital markets

               ➢ Further evolve governance as a global company by transitioning to a Company with a Nominating Committee, etc.,
                 and pursuing other measures
