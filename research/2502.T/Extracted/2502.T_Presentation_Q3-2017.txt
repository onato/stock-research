Financial Results Highlights for Q3 2017

◆ All Businesses achieved YoY growth in core operating profit and decent progress above the targets,
 despite unseasonable weather in summer season.
 ・Alcohol Beverages : YoY growth and following target in core OP driven by cost reduction and streamlining advertising
                       and promotion expenses, despite YoY decline in revenue due to unseasonable weather.
 ・Soft Drinks : YoY growth and above targets both in revenue and core OP driven by sales increases of Calpis, sales
               channel and product mix improvements and reduction of advertising and promotion expenses.
 ・Food : YoY growth and above targets both in revenue and core OP driven by continuous favorable sales of core
          brands, promotion of high added value products and reduction of manufacturing costs.
 ・Overseas : YoY growth and above targets both in revenue and core OP due to firm results of Europe business,
              mitigating shortfall of Southeast Asia business.
◆ Aiming at exceeding annual targets by strengthening core brands power and streamlining various
 expenses in 3 domestic Businesses, in addition to pursuing growth above targets in Overseas
 Business centered on Europe business.
