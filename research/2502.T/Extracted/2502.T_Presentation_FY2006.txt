   Overview of the Second Medium-Term Management Plan

Results ■ Creation of a new foundation for growth for the group
         ・Expanded business areas in the beverage business through M&As in China and South Korea, and in the chilled
         drinks and food & healthcare businesses.

         ■ Earnings structure reforms that exceeded plans
         ・ Increased marginal profit and enhanced efficiency in fixed costs in the alcoholic beverages business; promoted
         structural reforms in the soft drinks business.
         ・ Reorganized production and distribution at alcoholic beverage producing subsidiaries; embarked on
         construction of optimum production and distribution structures.

         ■ Steady growth in net income ⇒ Strengthened financial position and enhanced shareholder returns
         ・ Introduced asset impairment accounting and promoted restructuring of businesses; reduced financial obligations;
         Returned high level of profits to shareholders.


Issues    ■ Objectives under the growth strategy for alcoholic beverages business unachieved
          ・ Core beer operations lost ground in the market; earnings contribution from other alcoholic beverages missed target.

          ■ Lower-than-projected earnings contributions from M&As
          ・ Earnings contributions from acquired business interests still only limited (large contributions made by Tingyi-Asahi-
          Itochu Beverages Holding Co. Ltd, an equity method subsidiary).
          ⇒ Overall quantitative targets unachieved

Alcoholic Beverages Business Strategy
(Earnings Structure Reform and Organizational Strengthening)

Earnings Structure Reform
◆ Enhance marginal profit
・ Invest to promote greater efficiency; build optimum production and
distribution structures (concentrate operations in Ibaraki Brewery, etc.)

◆ Streamline fixed expenses
・ Promote further streamlining of fixed expenses across production, distribution, sales, and headquarters.

◆ Investment and greater efficiency in advertising and sales promotion
・ Expand brand investments: 20th anniversary of "Super Dry" in 2007, rejuvenation of happoshu brands.
・ Promote greater efficiency that takes cost-effectiveness into account through activities including a review
of terms and conditions of trade.
                                                Target for earnings structure reform (2009 vs. 2006) :
                                                                                                ¥10.0-15.0 bn
Organization Strengthening

◆ Strengthen Product Development Frameworks
・ Give absolute priority to the customer's perspective; review product development frameworks with a
view to bolstering development capabilities across the organization.

◆ Redevelop sales structures
・ Establish framework for sales from all group companies to large sales chains; further bolster
commercial sales frameworks.

 Soft Drinks Business Strategy
                     Expand business foundations around Asahi Soft Drinks
                            as the 2nd pillar of the group's business

 Asahi Soft Drinks
◆ "Growth Strategy," "Structural Reform," and "Take on Challenges in New Areas"
・ Solidify the 3 key brands, and develop brands in the focal areas.
・ Develop products utilizing functional materials in growth areas; open up new channels.
・ Promote optimum production and distribution structures through group cooperation and technology
  development; cut unnecessary costs in all areas.


 LB
◆ As the core company in the chilled drinks business, strengthen overall production
and sales foundations
・ Integrate the chilled drinks business of Asahi Soft Drinks; make capital investments aimed at increasing
  production and efficiency.
・ Strengthen product lineup and expand sales channels using resources available within the group.


 Strategic M&As/Alliances
・ Consider and execute M&As and alliances that complement and supplement the strengths and
  weaknesses in the existing infrastructure (vending machine channels, brands, etc.)
・ Search for M&A opportunities in order to strengthen products, brands, and the value chain in the chilled

Food and Healthcare Business Strategy

Enhance value of existing brands and establish a growth path
                by utilizing group synergies

Asahi Food and Healthcare
◆ Strengthen business foundations through "Growth Strategies," "Structural Reform,"
and "Ensuring Safety and Confidence"
・ Strengthen brands in existing business areas, and develop and cultivate high value added products.
・ Reform CoGS, sales promotion, and fixed expenses; enhance product and organizational quality.

Wakodo and Sunwell
・ Wakodo: Enhance brand strengths to make the company the top producer in the baby care sector; develop
products by utilizing R&D materials; carry out full-fledged entries into the food business for young children
and elderly people, and consider and execute business expansions overseas.
・ Sunwell: Increase numbers of sales outlets and expand sales channels; focus on product development
through group synergies.


Strategic M&As/Alliances
・ Consider and execute new investments aimed at further expansions into growth areas.
・ Search for M&A opportunities with the aim of strengthening products, brands, and R&D materials.

  Overseas Business Strategy
               Strengthen business foundations and improve profitability
                    through growth of both Asahi's and local brands

Overseas Alcoholic Beverages Business
◆ Expand business foundations and improve profitability in the Asian region with a
focus on China
・ Strive for dynamic growth of all existing beer brewers in China; ensure the growth
and improved profitability of Beijing Beer Asahi Co., Ltd.
・ Firmly establish "Super Dry" as a premium brand in the Asian region.

Overseas Soft Drinks Business
◆ Expand the foundations of the beverages business throughout all of Asia
・ Haitai Beverage Co., Ltd.: Develop a stable path for growth and improved profitability through support for
the enhancement of company management structures.
・ Tingyi-Asahi-Itochu Beverages Holding Co. Ltd.: Strengthen managerial support with a view towards
achieving dynamic growth in order to take the No. 1 spot in the Chinese beverages market.

Strategic M&As/Alliances
・ Consider and undertake acquisitions of new growth foundations for the alcoholic and soft drinks
businesses in East and Southeast Asia.
・ Search for opportunities to advance into the food and health care business with a focus on the Asian
region.

Efforts aimed at Strengthening Operating Foundations


 Organizational reform and HR development

 ・ Further reinforce business activities based on the "customer's perspective"
   across the group.
 ・ Pursue reforms to acquire an organizational culture that would establish
   the DNA for taking on new challenges.
 ・ Implement systems to develop/assign personnel to managerial/key positions
   in accordance with the needs of expanding businesses.


Introduce anti-takeover measures and reform directorship structure
 ・ Introduce a pre-warning type anti-hostile takeover measure with a view to the smooth execution
 of the Third Medium-Term Management Plan.
 ・ Shorten term of office for executives and directors from 2 years to 1 year.


Strengthen management position
 ・ Use ROIC (Return on Invested Capital) to evaluate business results and
 investments/withdrawals, and promote business portfolio management.
 ・ Enhance corporate governance by strengthening internal control and risk management systems.

Strategies by Business Area

Domestic Alcohol Business
 ◆ Aim to bolster the competitiveness of beer and beer-type beverages with brand
 investment in the "Asahi Super Dry" brand as a key area
 ・ Engage in comprehensive marketing activities for "Asahi Super Dry" in its 20th anniversary.
 ・ Rejuvenate the happoshu lineup and strengthen new-genre brands that meet diverse market needs.
 ・ Strengthen core brands and unique product offerings in the "other alcoholic beverages" segment;
 implement further reforms to earnings structure.



Group Business
 ◆ Bolster growth foundations by strengthening key brands and creating group
 synergies
 ・ Expand investment centered around strengthening the key brands of Asahi Soft Drinks and the
 foundations in the chilled beverages business of the LB companies.
 ・ Food and healthcare companies: Strengthen core brands, and develop and cultivate diverse brands that
 exhibit synergies.


Overseas Business
 ◆ Enhance growth foundations and improved profitability in Asia
 ・ Bolster production capacity of Beijing Beer Asahi Co., Ltd. and Hangzhou Xihu Beer Asahi Co., Ltd.
 and strengthen growth foundations for Super Dry throughout all of Asia.
 ・ Improve profitability of Haitai Beverage Co., Ltd. by strengthening business management; support
 aggressive investment and new product development by Tingyi-Asahi-Itochu Beverages Holding Co.
 Ltd.
