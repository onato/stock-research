・H1: Finished above target with JPY6.9 billion year-on-year increase due to strong performance from the domestic businesses,
 despite lower sales from Overseas business due to foreign exchange impact (negative JPY17.7 billion)

 (negative JPY17.8 billion)

 Soft Drinks and Food Businesses

 non-operating income / loss because of special factors
