  Executive Summary
Overview for Q1 2023
  ➢ Achieved 7.9% revenue growth and 24.3% core operating profit growth
    (constant currency basis)
    • Achieved top-line growth in all businesses due to the effect of price revisions and
      continued premiumization
    • Profit growth exceeded the plan, mainly in Japan, even with continued significant
      cost increases
