Executive Summary



 ◆ Under Medium-Term Management Plan 2012, the Asahi Group improved profitability in existing
     businesses and expanded a new foundation for growth. However, issues remain, principally
     the inability to achieve the profit targets in the soft drinks and food businesses.


 ◆ The Company has revised Long-Term Vision 2015 in light of results outlined in the overview
   to date and changes in the business environment and stakeholder needs and has newly
   established Long-Term Vision 2020.


 ◆ The Company has established Medium-Term Management Plan 2015, an action plan for
   realization of Long-Term Vision 2020.
    ・ Based on the practice of value and network management, the Group will aim for long-term,
      stable growth through value creation that leverages strengths and expansion of networks in
      Japan and overseas.
    ・ The Group has made improvement in ROE and EPS its key performance indicators and
       seeks corporate value enhancement through capital efficiency improvement by means
       including enhancement of shareholder returns in addition to sales and profit growth.
