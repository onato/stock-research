Executive Summary

      First-Half Overview
 ◆ Sales and profit increases were achieved in all businesses through value and network management.
   ・Sales increase of 9.9% due to factors including the consolidation of Calpis, the impact of yen deprecation, and
    maintenance of sales volumes of beer-type beverages
   ・Operating income increase of 12.6%, above the planned level, due mainly to the impact of higher sales from
    the Soft Drinks Business and cost control
 ◆ Despite under-performance of targets by businesses in Oceania, strengthening of the business base of
     the Overseas Business through network expansion in Southeast Asia
   ・Under-performance of targets by businesses in Oceania due to continuing structural changes in the alcoholic
    beverage market in Australia
   ・Expansion of the growth foundation in Southeast Asia leveraging strong brands and the existing network

      Full-Year Business Strategy
 ◆ Steadily achieve the original performance targets in the first year of Medium-Term Management Plan 2015.
  ・Achieve the original target of an 8.8% operating income increase, with the Soft Drinks Business compensating
   for a downward revision of theforecast by businesses in Oceania.
   ・Achieve the original net income target of ¥65.5 billion and pursue steady improvement in ROE and EPS.

 ◆ Construction of a growth foundation through expansion of Group synergies / Steady implementation
     of the capital strategy
   ・Integrate the Calpis sales division into Asahi Soft Drinks and accelerate creation of growth and cost synergies.
   ・Achieve the KPI (ROE and EPS increases) in the medium-term management plan and steadily implement and continue
    the capital strategy.
