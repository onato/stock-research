   Financial Results Highlights for Q3 2019
     ◆ -3.1% YoY decline in core OP for Q3 mainly due to the negative impacts of the unseasonable
      weather in Soft Drinks Business. (-0.4% YoY decline in core OP excluding forex impacts)
        ・Alcohol Beverages : YoY decline and below target in core OP due to the shortfall in sales volume of beer-type, despite
                              YoY growth both in revenue and core OP in other than beer-type beverages.
        ・Soft Drinks : YoY decline and below target in core OP due to the significant sales volume decrease because of the
                       unseasonable weather in July and the negative impacts of cost increase along with the sluggish sales.
        ・Food : YoY increase and above target in core OP due to the revenue increase of main brands and mix improvements,
                 with revenue in line with target.
        ・Overseas : YoY growth but below target in core OP mainly due to the negative forex impacts, with the continuous
                     favorable performances in Europe and Oceania businesses in the situation excluding forex impacts.
     ◆ Annual target in core OP revised downward by JPY9.5 billion from the target announced on August
      1 due to the negative impacts of the unseasonable weather in high season and yen appreciation.
