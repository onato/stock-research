 Executive Summary
      ◆ Overview of H1 2025

               ➢ Core Operating Profit (constant currency basis) decreased by 3.2%, but total profit exceeded our plan,
                    driven by steadily increasing profits in Japan & East Asia* and Europe segments, along with a smaller-than-
                    expected profit decline in the Asia Pacific* segment

               ➢ Unit sales prices improved on optimal pricing strategies and premium strategies. Promoted global
                    procurement through Asahi Global Procurement Pte. Ltd. (AGRPO) and cost management across the region

      ◆ Future Direction

               ➢ Aim to steadily achieve our upgraded Core Operating Profit target (constant currency basis: +4.1%) by
                    continuing to invest in growth and accelerating the earning structure reforms, while strengthening our growth
                    platforms for fiscal 2026 onward

               ➢ Execute initiatives that is conscious of capital cost and stock price, such as share buybacks, as we work to
                    achieve the Key Indicator Guidelines and Financial Policy through 2030
                                              * Business segments updated as of April 2025 (business transfers between Regional Headquarters). See slide 14 for more detail.
