Outlook
•   We remain confident in our 15%+ long‐term organic EBITDA growth target
•   Annual performance remains dependent on timing of projects and contracts
•   Currently there is significant uncertainty for UK energy retailers. Government
    intervention and price regulation have compounded Brexit concerns. Customers
    are adopting a cautious approach to new projects in our pipeline
•   Increased failures amongst smaller independent UK retailers
•   Australian energy price volatility and government reviews also introducing
    investment uncertainty for customers
•   We will update the outlook with the half year results
