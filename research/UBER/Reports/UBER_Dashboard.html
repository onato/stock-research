Net Income vs Operating Income vs FCF

Net income swings on non-operating items unrelated to the business: a ~$6.4bn FY2024 deferred-tax release, a $1.6bn Q4-2025 equity mark-to-market hit ($296m net income on $1.77bn operating income), and a Q2-2026 'other income' of +$1,342m that includes ~$1.1bn from an equity-securities remeasurement tied to the pending Delivery Hero stake. Operating income and FCF track the underlying business; net income does not.

EBITDA (FY basis)

Interim EBITDA is not reliably disclosed for every recent quarter, so this chart is FY-only. FY2025 EBITDA of $8,730m rose from $2,782m in FY2023 as the platform scaled past fixed technology and G&A costs.

Capital Structure & Balance Sheet

The Delivery Hero bridge facility is the key forward risk: a levered balance sheet ahead of a 2H-2027 close

Cash & Equivalents vs Total Debt

Debt rose from $10,521m (FY2025) to $12,723m and cash fell from $7,105m to $4,870m by Q2-2026 -- the balance-sheet impact of pre-funding the ~EUR14.2bn Delivery Hero acquisition bridge. Net debt is now $7,853m against a near-net-cash position a year ago.

Shares Outstanding & Stock-Based Comp

Shares fell from 2,150.5m (FY2024) to 2,119.7m (FY2025) to 2,042.6m (Q2-2026) as the $20bn buyback authorization (announced Q2-2025, $3bn repurchased in Q1-2026 alone) outpaces SBC-driven issuance.

Operating Cash Flow vs Capex

Capex is small and shrinking relative to operating cash flow ($135m capex against $5,213m H1-2026 OCF, ~2.6%) -- Uber's asset-light marketplace model means almost all operating cash converts to free cash flow, unlike capital-intensive AV or logistics operators.
