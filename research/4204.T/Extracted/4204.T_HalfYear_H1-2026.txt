I. Qualitative Information and Financial Review
  1. Consolidated Business Results (April 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026)                                      ★：Record high
    (1) Group Overview
       Net Sales: ★701.7 billion yen (+11.4%), Operating Profit: ★60.4 billion yen (+33.0%), Ordinary Profit: ★61.4
       billion yen (+25.5%), Interim Profit Attributable to Owners of the Parent: 34.7 billion yen (+9.7%)
        In the first half of fiscal 2026, the Group addressed surging prices and procurement constraints for raw materials
      and parts due to the deteriorating situation in the Middle East, while also responding decisively to pre-emptive
      demand and the trend toward building up market inventories caused by product supply concerns. Furthermore, the
      Group steadily captured demand in growth markets such as the Electronics field, resulting in net sales that
      increased significantly year-on-year and setting a new record high for the first half of the fiscal year for the second
      consecutive year.
        Operating profit increased significantly year-on-year, driven by rapid sales price improvements in response to
      surging raw material and parts costs, as well as progress in expanding sales of high value-added products. Ordinary
      profit also saw a substantial increase, boosted by the rise in operating profit. Both operating profit and ordinary profit
      reached new record highs for the first half of the fiscal year. Interim profit attributable to owners of the parent
      increased, driven by the factors mentioned above, despite the recognition of extraordinary losses.


    (2) Overview by Business Segment
      [Housing Company]
      Net Sales: ★278.7 billion yen (+7.8%), Operating Profit: 16.7 billion yen (+3.0%)
         In the first half of fiscal 2026, although the number of new houses sold declined due to a downturn in the new
      housing market, the Company as a whole posted higher sales and profit driven by growth in the Housing
      Renovation and Residential businesses.
         In the Housing business, although the number of houses sold decreased due to the deteriorating market
      conditions, sales increased as the average unit price rose, driven by expanded sales of high-end products such as
      apartment buildings. The number of orders received was 98% of the same period of the previous year, while the
      order value was 102% of that period.
         In the Housing Renovation business, sales increased as growth was seen in exterior wall painting and kitchen
      (LDK remodeling) projects, and the demand for air conditioner replacements was steadily captured.
         In the Residential business, profit increased significantly due to steady growth in the number of rental units under
      management and the number of properties purchased and resold, as well as an increase in condominium sales.
         To drive further growth, the Company actively pursued M&A opportunities in its strategic focus areas of Housing,
      Residential, and Overseas business, including the execution of share transfer agreements with Noble Home and
      Mirai Toshi Kaihatsu in Japan and Ausbuild Group in Australia.


      [Urban Infrastructure and Environmental Products Company]
      Net Sales: ★123.2 billion yen (+10.0%), Operating Profit: ★13.1 billion yen (+63.3%)
         In the first half of fiscal 2026, both sales and profit increased as a result of securing spreads and increased
      shipments driven by a trend toward building up market inventories, while also overcoming difficulties in procuring
      raw materials caused by the deteriorating situation in the Middle East. Both net sales and operating profit
      significantly surpassed previous record highs, and the operating profit margin exceeded 10%.
         In the Pipe Systems field, shipments of pipe products for housing in Japan increased due to a trend toward
      building up market inventories, resulting in net sales that increased significantly year-on-year.
         In the Buildings and Infrastructures Composite Materials field, although the domestic housing and non-housing
      markets remained sluggish, net sales increased year-on-year due to higher sales of prioritized products including
      fire protection and non-combustible materials, as well as the growing adoption of synthetic lumber (FFU) railway
      sleepers in Europe.
         In the Infrastructure Renovation field, while we successfully captured pipeline renewal demand resulting from a
      nationwide special priority survey on pipeline renewal, net sales decreased year-on-year due to delays in certain
      overseas projects.


      [High Performance Plastics Company]
      Net Sales: ★264.1 billion yen (+18.2%), Operating Profit: ★38.3 billion yen (+35.1%)
        In the first half of fiscal 2026, the Electronics, Mobility, and Industrial fields (renamed the Social Infrastructure

    year-on-year due to the capture of pre-emptive demand from Europe and the United States.
      In the Pharmaceutical Sciences business, net sales decreased year-on-year due to delays in certain projects.



2. Overview of Interim Financial Position
    Status of assets, liabilities, and net assets
       Consolidated total assets as of the end of the first half of fiscal 2026 were 1,505,547 million yen, an increase of
    77,613 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal year.
    (Assets)
       Current assets were 766,769 million yen, an increase of 45,071 million yen from the end of the previous fiscal
    year. The main factors were an increase of 33,450 million yen in total inventories, an increase of 14,040 million yen
    in cash and deposits, and an increase of 3,765 million yen in accounts receivable - trade.
       Fixed assets were 738,777 million yen, an increase of 32,542 million yen.
    (Liabilities)
       Total liabilities were 610,526 million yen, an increase of 63,718 million yen as a result of an increase of 45,764
    million yen in long-term borrowings, 10,000 million yen from the issuance of commercial papers, and 7,951 million
    yen in short-term borrowings, among other factors, despite a decrease of 10,182 million yen in the current portion
    of bonds payable.
    (Net Assets)
       Net assets stood at 895,021 million yen at the end of the first half of fiscal 2026, an increase of 13,895 million
    yen. The main factors were the recording of 34,791 million yen in Interim profit attributable to owners of the parent,
    16,203 million yen in dividends paid, and a decrease of 4,363 million yen in the foreign currency translation
    adjustment.

4. Revision of Dividend Forecast
      SEKISUI CHEMICAL positions increasing corporate value and actively returning profits to shareholders as priority
    management objectives. The Group announced the following policies regarding shareholder returns in its Medium-
    term Management Plan, “Accelerate 2028,” and has strengthened and clarified its commitment to shareholder
    returns.
    ・Dividend payout ratio: 40% or higher
    ・DOE: 3.5% or higher
    ・Total return ratio: 50% or higher if the net D/E ratio is less than 0.5; implement additional returns as appropriate,
    taking into account the investment progress under the Medium-term Management Plan, cash position, and stock
    price
    ・Cancellation of treasury shares: Hold treasury shares to the minimum necessary
    ・Progressive dividends

      Regarding dividends for fiscal 2026, after comprehensively considering the aforementioned policy, we are revising
    the dividend for the end of the second quarter to 41 yen, an increase of 1 yen from the previous forecast announced
    on April 28, 2026. As a result, the annual dividend for fiscal 2026 is expected to be 82 yen, an increase of 2 yen.

  (Calculation of Tax Expense)
  Tax expenses are calculated by reasonably estimating the effective tax rate after applying tax effect accounting to
  profit before income taxes for the fiscal year, including the current interim consolidated accounting period, and
  multiplying profit before income taxes (interim) by the estimated effective tax rate. However, if the calculation of tax
  expenses using such estimated effective tax rate would significantly lack rationality, tax expenses are calculated using
  the statutory tax rate. Income taxes - deferred are included in income taxes.

  ※1. Specific product replacement expenses
    Specific product replacement expenses represent expenses recognized for the replacement of certain battery storage
  systems previously sold by the Group that are expected to require replacement due to identified defects.
  ※2. Business restructuring expenses
    Business restructuring expenses represent expenses associated with the withdrawal from the stationary battery storage
  business. The principal component of such expenses is inventory valuation losses.
