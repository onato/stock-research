Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: Sanity check flagged implied P/E 1.6x (vs a 10yr average of 0.4x) and P/B 0.21x (vs a 10yr max of 0.10x). Investigation traced this to a BRL/USD currency-direction bug in the sanity checker's own historical-multiple calculation (scripts/sanity_check.py), not a defect in the valuation: it converts the USD ADR price the wrong way before comparing against BRL financials, understating every historical multiple by roughly the square of the FX rate. No change was made to the DCF model. Recomputed by hand and denominated consistently in BRL, the base case implies about 7.3x trailing recurring earnings and 1.1x book value -- both inside Bradesco's normal historical range (see implied_multiples_after_fix) -- so the valuation itself is not the problem; the checker's currency handling is. Fixing that checker is a separate, cross-ticker change out of scope for this run.
