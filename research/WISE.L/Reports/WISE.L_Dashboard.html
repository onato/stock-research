Growth & Profitability

Key signals (data to Q1 FY2027, quarter to 30 Jun 2026):
  • Growth is re-accelerating. Net revenue grew 19% in FY2026 and 25% YoY in Q1 FY2027 ($714m); cross-border volume grew 31% and 26%.
  • The margin reset is the big change. Operating margin fell from 36.6% (FY2024) to 23.6% (FY2026) as opex rose to fund compliance, servicing and price cuts. Guidance is an income-before-tax margin at the top of 20-25%; the mid-30s margins are not expected to return.
  • Take rate is falling by design, and more slowly. 0.67% (FY2024) → 0.58% → 0.52% → 0.50% in Q1 FY2027, only −2bps YoY. Volume growth is outrunning the price cuts.
  • About a third of revenue is interest on customer money. Gross interest income was $806m, 32% of FY2026 net revenue. The yield on year-end balances fell from 3.6% to 2.7% as rates fell, but balances grew 36%. Further rate cuts are the main earnings sensitivity.
  • Revenue per customer is flat. About $132 per active customer in FY2026 vs $135-138 in the two years before. Growth now comes from more customers, not from earning more from each one.
  • The balance sheet is mostly customer money. Total assets ($33bn) and reported operating cash flow ($7.6bn) move with customer balances. Read Corporate cash ($1.87bn) and owner FCF instead.
  • Regulatory overhang: Belgian criminal AML probe, the OCC charter denial (Jul-2026) and US securities class actions (class period 11 May - 23 Jul 2026). None of these shows up in the charts yet.

Customers, Volume & Holdings

FY bars are the full-year figures Wise reports: customers active at any point in the year, volume for the year, holdings at year-end. The Q view shows quarterly figures, and customers there are those active in the quarter, so FY customer counts run higher than any single quarter. Pre-FY2025 volumes and balances were reported in GBP and are converted at the same per-year rate used for that year's revenue (FY22 x1.36, FY23 x1.19, FY24 x1.2576).

Cross-Border Volume & YoY Growth

Volume grew 31% in FY2026, faster than revenue (19%), because the take rate was cut.

Cross-Border Take Rate

Down 17bps since FY2024, the price cuts Wise funds from scale. In Q1 FY2027 it fell only 2bps YoY, so the decline is slowing.

Active Customers

Customer Holdings: Balances + Assets Under Custody

Holdings rose 40% in FY2026 to $39bn, and 31% YoY to $41.2bn in Q1 FY2027. Balances earn the interest income; assets under custody earn fees.

Interest Income on Customer Balances

Interest is about a third of revenue. The yield is already falling with rates, and balance growth has so far covered it.

Interest Yield on Customer Balances

3.6% (FY2024) to 2.7% (FY2026) as central banks cut. Year-end balances overstate the average in a growth year, so the true yield is somewhat higher.

Net Revenue per Active Customer

Flat at about $132-138 since FY2024: growth now comes from adding customers, not from earning more from each one.
