Swire Pacific is a Hong Kong-based international conglomerate
with a diversified portfolio of market leading businesses.
The Company has a long history in Greater China, where the
name Swire or 太古 has been established for over 150 years.
Our strategy is focused on Greater China and South East Asia, where we seek to grow our core Property,
Beverages and Aviation divisions. We are targeting new areas of growth, such as healthcare.
