1)     Pharmaceuticals
     Revenue for the period under review totaled ¥237,117 million (up 13.2%), with business profit of ¥50,443 million (up 53.9%).

     Main products
     ● Four global products
        The Company positions the long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA, the antipsychotic agent REXULTI*1/RXULTI*2, the V2-
      receptor antagonist Samsca/JINARC*3/JYNARQUE*4, and the anti-cancer agent LONSURF as its four global products. The sales of those
      products totaled ¥110,139 million (up 36.1%).

        *1: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in the U.S.
        *2: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in Europe
        *3: Brand name for autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (“ADPKD”) treatment in multiple regions outside Japan
        *4: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in the U.S.

      ・ Antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA
          Sales of ABILIFY MAINTENA was ¥30,421 million (up 32.1%) reflecting the continuous contribution of expanding prescriptions as a
       treatment for bipolar disorder, an indication was approved in the U.S. in 2017, in addition to promotion of the product’s efficacy as a
       treatment for schizophrenia in global markets and growing awareness of the drug’s ease of use.

                                                                         -2-
      ・ Antipsychotic agent REXULTI/RXULTI
         Antipsychotic agent REXULTI has been highly evaluated in the U.S. for its efficacy and safety as a new treatment option for adjunctive
       therapy in major depressive disorder and a treatment for schizophrenia, and its sales has increased significantly. In Japan, where the agent is
       sold for the treatment of schizophrenia, the number of prescriptions has sharply increased since the limit on the number of prescription days
       was removed in May 2019. RXULTI has been launched sequentially in several European countries since April 2019. As a result, sales of
       REXULTI/RXULTI totaled ¥27,811 million (up 45.6%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist Samsca
          In Japan, it was highly recommended in various guidelines and its prescriptions increased for the treatments of cardiac edema and hepatic
       edema. The number of prescriptions has also increased as a treatment for ADPKD, an intractable kidney disease, due to efforts to raise
       awareness of the disease as well as promote appropriate use of Samsca pursuant to the treatment guidelines. As a result, sales of Samsca
       totaled ¥22,273 million (up 13.4%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist JINARC/JYNARQUE
         In the U.S., the number of prescriptions for ADPKD has since increased smoothly in the country as efforts to raise awareness of the disease
       and provide information about clinical data helped more people recognize the disease and the product. In addition, the number of patient
       users has been increasing in European markets. As a result, sales significantly increased to ¥20,104 million (up 84.7%).

      ・ Anti-cancer agent LONSURF
          In Japan, in addition to the increased number of prescriptions for the treatment of colorectal cancer, the number of prescriptions also
       increased for the treatment of previously treated metastatic gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma, approved in August 2019,
       leading to increased sales. In the U.S., our enhanced activities to provide information led to a strong increase in sales. In Europe, sales
       increased from steady prescription sales. As a result, sales of LONSURF totaled ¥9,529 million (up 15.0%).

2)     Nutraceuticals
     Revenue for the period under review totaled ¥70,597 million (down 3.2%), with business profit of ¥6,175 million (down 6.0%).

 Main products
   The Company positions POCARI SWEAT, Nature Made and the Nutrition & Santé SAS brand as its three major brands. The sales of those
 brands totaled ¥42,009 million (down 6.8%). Total sales of its three nurture brands, the Daiya Foods Inc. brand, EQUELLE and BODY MAINTÉ,
 were ¥6,306 million (up 30.2%).

     ● Three major brands
        Due to increased demand as a health drink, sales volume of POCARI SWEAT, an electrolyte supplement drink, increased in countries
      including Indonesia, Philippines, and Vietnam. However, this was not enough to cover the reversal of the increased demand experienced in
      Japan last year due to influenza, leading to an overall decline in sales for the brand compared with the prior year period.
        Sales of Nature Made, supplements of Pharmavite LLC, decreased due to factors such as intensifying competition in certain categories in
      the U.S.
        At Nutrition & Santé SAS brand, which is active in over 40 countries, mainly in Europe, sales increased on a local-currency basis due to
      increased demand for nutrition and health food products, but yen-based sales slightly decreased due to the impact of foreign exchange rates.

     ● Three nurture brands
         Sales of the plant-based foods of the Daiya Foods Inc. brand increased compared with the prior year period due to the growth of cheese
      alternatives and other products in North America.
         Sales of EQUELLE, a food and supplement brand containing equol that supports women’s health and beauty, grew steadily due to increased
      recognition of the product through the wide ranging spread of information on the product.
         As for BODY MAINTÉ, a protective beverage brand containing the plant-based lactic-acid bacteria B240*1, sales volume increased for both
      BODY MAINTÉ Jelly and BODY MAINTÉ CONDITIONING DRINK, due to increased needs for both immune system and health management,
      leading to a large increase in sales.

        *1: Lactobacillus pentosus ONRICb0240: Strain isolated by Tokyo University of Agriculture, for which Otsuka Pharmaceutical has
            confirmed efficacy.

3)    Consumer products
   Sales volume for mineral water products increased, with main contributors being mainstay brand CRYSTAL GEYSER’s direct shipping to
consumers and its 700-milliliter bottles, the largest size for personal use. Sales volume of MATCH, a carbonated electrolyte drink containing
vitamins, increased in January and February due to marketing strategies including retail-focused campaign planning. However, due to decreased
demand starting in March, sales volume decreased compared with the prior year period. As a result, revenue for the period under review totaled
¥7,505 million (up 6.5%). Business profit was ¥2,315 million (up 41.2%) mainly due to an increase in share of profit from investment accounted
for using the equity method.

4)     Others
   Sales in the specialty chemical business for the period under review decreased compared with the prior year period due to a fall in shipments of
hydrazine hydrate. Sales in the fine chemical business increased compared with the prior year period mainly due to revenue from an active
pharmaceutical ingredient-related business supplying cefixime to overseas licensees, which was acquired in June 2019.
   In the transportation and warehousing business, sales increased compared with the prior year period mainly due to the growth in handling volume
of the Group products and increase in new customers spurred by the “common distribution platform” in line with external customers.
   As a result, revenue for the period under review totaled ¥33,032 million (down 0.3%). Business profit was ¥2,570 million (up 32.8%) mainly
due to an increase in share of profit from investment accounted for using the equity method.

Impact of COVID-19 on business operations and operating results
    In light of each country’s orders or official requests to stay at home, the Group’s response to the spread of the novel coronavirus disease
 (COVID-19) has been to perform daily health checks for employees in manufacturing operations, to apply an in-principle work-at-home policy
 for employees in other roles, and to strive to continue business activities while ensuring the safety of employees.
    For the three month period ended March 31, 2020, the impact from the spread of COVID-19 was minor. In terms of the supply of products,
 the current system is able to continue to provide stable supply. The impact on business operations and operating results for the six month period
 ending June 30, 2020 and thereafter is currently under review, taking into account the scenario where the societal impact from the spread of
 COVID-19 is prolonged.

    The current situation and potential management risks for the Group have been identified as follows.

 Sales
 • In the pharmaceutical business, official requests to stay at home and urban blockages have led to a decrease in outpatient numbers in
     hospitals but there is an upward trend for long-term prescriptions for patients with chronic diseases. If the current situation were to be
     prolonged or become more serious, there may be further decline in the number of new prescriptions and decline in prescriptions such as
     injections as part of hospital treatment due to restrictions on visits to health care facilities and the voluntary restraint on efforts to raise
     awareness of diseases, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.
 • In non-pharmaceutical operations, on one hand, demand for some products is increasing owing to heightened health awareness and
     increases in consumption at home and online shopping. On the other hand, a loss of consumption opportunities is being observed due to
     the limits on outdoor activities resulting from people refraining from leaving their homes. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be
     prolonged or become more serious, there may be further decline in the capture of new customers and loss of consumption opportunities
     due to voluntary restraint and other restrictions on sales activities, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.

 Manufacturing
 • With regard to the procurement of raw materials, the purchasing channels overall are secure, although delays are occurring for some
   import items. Manufacturing had been temporarily suspended for some line items due to employees staying at home, but all
   manufacturing has now been restored. The manufacturing operations are adequately staffed and proceeding smoothly. If the spread of
   COVID-19 were to be prolonged or become more serious causing stoppages in the procurement of raw materials, or if a cluster of
   infections or other similar cases occurred at a manufacturing plant, the supply of certain products may stop.

 Research and Development
 • Plans to launch clinical trial facilities and register patients have been put on hold for some products in development undergoing clinical
    trials. If the current situation were to be prolonged or become more serious, progress of clinical trials or the timing of applications for
    manufacturing and marketing approval may be delayed.
 • In research activities, delays are occurring in some activities due to official directives for research staff to stay at home, etc. If the current
    situation were to be prolonged or become more serious causing delay in research activities, the timeline for new product
    commercialization in the medium to long term may be delayed.

    As stated above, many factors can be foreseen as potentially affecting the Group’s business as the result of the spread of COVID-19. Looking
 forward, the Group will continue its efforts to secure the safety of the Group’s employees and continue business activities under the corporate
 philosophy of “Otsuka-people creating new products for better health worldwide.”

     Operating Segments
      1) Overview of reportable segments
          The Group’s reportable segments are the constituent units of the Group for which separate financial information is available and
         which are subject to periodic reviews by the Board of Directors in order to make decisions on allocation of business resources and to
         evaluate the business performances of the respective segments.
          As the Group’s holding company, the Company directs the Group’s strategic planning, monitors group operations and provides
         various services to its group companies. Business activities are conducted by the Group’s subsidiaries and associates.
          Centering on the healthcare business, the Group operates activities inside and outside Japan relating to the following four reportable
         segments: “Pharmaceuticals,” “Nutraceuticals,” “Consumer products” and “Others” businesses.
          The Group defines the reportable segments as follows:
          “Pharmaceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of prescription drugs and intravenous solutions.
          “Nutraceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of functional beverages, over-the-counter drugs and nutritional supplements.
          “Consumer products” comprises manufacturing and sales of mineral water, soft beverages and food products.
          “Others” encompasses logistics, warehousing, manufacturing and sales of chemical products, evaluation systems for LED displays
         and spectroanalysis devices.
