1)     Pharmaceuticals
     Revenue for the period under review totaled ¥471,773 million (up 7.6%), with business profit of ¥102,701 million (up 20.9%).

     Main products
     ● Four global products
        The Company positions the long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA, the antipsychotic agent REXULTI*1/RXULTI*2, the V2-
      receptor antagonist Samsca/JINARC*3/JYNARQUE*4 and the anti-cancer agent LONSURF as its four global products. The sales of those
      products totaled ¥218,549 million (up 26.2%).

        *1: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in the U.S.
        *2: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in Europe
        *3: Brand name for autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (“ADPKD”) treatment in multiple regions outside Japan
        *4: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in the U.S.

      ・ Antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA
          Sales of ABILIFY MAINTENA were ¥58,939 million (up 21.3%) reflecting the continuous contribution of expanding prescriptions as a
       treatment for bipolar disorder, an indication approved in the U.S. in 2017, in addition to promotion of the product’s efficacy as a treatment
       for schizophrenia in global markets and growing awareness of the drug’s ease of use.

      ・ Antipsychotic agent REXULTI/RXULTI
          Antipsychotic agent REXULTI has been highly evaluated in the U.S. for its efficacy and safety as a new treatment option for adjunctive
       therapy in major depressive disorder and a treatment for schizophrenia, and its sales have increased. In Japan, where the agent is sold for the
       treatment of schizophrenia, the number of prescriptions, particularly for the acute phase of treatment for schizophrenia in adults, have sharply
       increased since the limit on the number of prescription days was removed in May 2019. RXULTI has been launched sequentially in several
       European countries since April 2019. As a result, sales of REXULTI/RXULTI totaled ¥54,413 million (up 33.0%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist Samsca
         In Japan, despite the effect of drug price reduction from repricing measures for market expansion in association with the NHI price
       revisions in April, prescriptions continue to expand for treatments of cardiac edema and hepatic edema and also ADPKD, an intractable
       kidney disease. As a result, sales of Samsca totaled ¥44,208 million (up 3.6%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist JINARC/JYNARQUE
          In the U.S., the number of prescriptions for ADPKD has increased smoothly as efforts to raise awareness of the disease and provide
       information about clinical data helped more people recognize the disease and the product. In addition, the number of patient users has been
       increasing in European markets. As a result, sales of JINARC/JYNARQUE totaled ¥40,146 million (up 65.6%).

      ・ Anti-cancer agent LONSURF
          In Japan, in addition to the increased number of prescriptions for the treatment of colorectal cancer, the number of prescriptions also
       increased for the treatment of previously treated metastatic gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma, approved in August 2019.
       In the US, since at-home care and the use of oral anti-cancer agents*1*2 are recommended due to the spread of COVID-19, sales increased
       with strengthened information provision activities. In Europe, sales increased due to steady prescription sales and an increased number of
       country approvals. As a result, sales of LONSURF totaled ¥20,840 million (up 24.9%).

        *2: ASCO. COVID-19 Patient Care Information, Cancer Treatment and Supportive Care.

Impact of COVID-19 on business operations and operating results
    In light of each country’s orders or official requests to stay at home, the Group’s response to the spread of COVID-19 has been to perform
 daily health checks for employees in manufacturing operations, to apply an in-principle work-at-home policy for employees in other roles and
 to strive to continue business activities while ensuring the safety of employees.
    For the six-month period ended June 30, 2020, the four global products of the pharmaceutical business (ABILIFY MAINTENA,
 REXULTI/RXULTI, Samsca/JINARC/JYNARQUE and LONSURF) grew steadily. However, some products in the pharmaceutical business and
 other businesses were affected by the restrictions on going out due to the spread of COVID-19. In terms of the supply of products, the current
 system is able to continue to provide stable supply. Regarding the impact on business operations and operating results for the nine month period
 ending September 30, 2020 and thereafter, we will continue to pay close attention to the impact on business and business performance,
 assuming that the situation will be prolonged while the impact of the spread of COVID-19 is unclear.

   The current situation and potential management risks for the Group have been identified as follows.

 Sales
 • In the pharmaceutical business, official requests to stay at home and urban blockages have led to a decrease in outpatient numbers in
     hospitals but there is an upward trend for long-term prescriptions for patients with chronic diseases. If the current situation were to be
     prolonged or become more serious, there may be further decline in the number of new prescriptions and decline in prescriptions such as
     injections as part of hospital treatment due to restrictions on visits to health care facilities and the voluntary restraint on efforts to raise
     awareness of diseases, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.
 • In non-pharmaceutical operations, on one hand, demand for some products is increasing owing to heightened health awareness and
     increases in consumption at home and online shopping. On the other hand, a loss of consumption opportunities is being observed due to
     the limits on outdoor activities resulting from people refraining from leaving their homes. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be prolonged
     or become more serious, there may be further decline in the capture of new customers and loss of consumption opportunities due to
     voluntary restraint and other restrictions on sales activities, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.

 Manufacturing
 • With regard to the procurement of raw materials, the purchasing channels overall are secure, although delays are occurring for some import
   items. Manufacturing had been temporarily suspended for some line items due to employees staying at home, but all manufacturing has
   now been restored. The manufacturing operations are adequately staffed and proceeding smoothly. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be
   prolonged or become more serious causing stoppages in the procurement of raw materials, or if a cluster of infections or other similar cases
   occurred at a manufacturing plant, the supply of certain products may stop.

 Research and Development
 • Plans to launch clinical trial facilities and register patients had been put on hold for some products in development undergoing clinical
    trials, but they are gradually reopening. If the interruption or delay of patient registration become more serious, progress of clinical trials
    or the timing of applications for manufacturing and marketing approval may be delayed.
 • In research activities, delays are occurring in some activities due to official directives for research staff to stay at home, etc. If the current
    situation were to be prolonged or become more serious causing delay in research activities, the timeline for new product commercialization
    in the medium to long term may be delayed.

    As stated above, many factors can be foreseen as potentially affecting the Group’s business as the result of the spread of COVID-19. Looking
 forward, the Group will continue its efforts to secure the safety of the Group’s employees and continue business activities under the corporate
 philosophy of “Otsuka-people creating new products for better health worldwide.”

   In the nutraceutical business, the Group draws on its knowledge in the pharmaceutical business to constantly conduct research and
development of world-class products centering on functional foods and beverages that support the maintenance and improvement of day-to-
day well-being. In the SOYJOY brand of soy snack bars, which deliver a convenient way to consume the nutrition of whole soybeans, SOYJOY
Peanuts was renewed and launched in February 2020. While retaining the whole soybean flour and the satisfying mouthfeel of chunky peanuts,
it contains one-fourth less sugar*1. Furthermore, in the cosmedics*2 field, which considers skin health using ideas and technologies developed
independently by Otsuka Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd., through our skincare series InnerSignal, in March 2020, InnerSignal Rejuvenate ONE was
launched, which was a time-delayed absorption, medicinal, all-in-one gel-type cream that combines skin-whitening*3 properties with the
properties of a beauty lotion, moisturizer and cream.
   Moreover, the results of our research on the inhibitory effect of near-infrared (IRA) radiation on human epidermal cell proliferation and
potential mechanisms*4, and on the relationship between hair problems and capacity to produce equol*5 were published in Journals in March
2020, and April 2020, respectively.

      1) Overview of reportable segments
          The Group’s reportable segments are the constituent units of the Group for which separate financial information is available and
