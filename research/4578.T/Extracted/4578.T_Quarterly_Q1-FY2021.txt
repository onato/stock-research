     Based on the concept of total health care, the Group has been promoting corporate activities to maintain and promote health, and diagnose
   and treat disease. Now is the time to demonstrate the Group’s true value as a total healthcare provider, and harness the opportunities presented
   by a growing awareness of health in the new normal in order to continue to move toward the realization of sustainable growth.

      For the three-month period ended March 31, 2021, the Group recorded consolidated revenue of ¥336,789 million (down 0.8% over the same
   period of the previous fiscal year), about the same level as the previous fiscal year, as the four global products ABILIFY MAINTENA,
   REXULTI/RXULTI, JINARC/JYNARQUE, and LONSURF, and the nutraceutical business covered a decrease in sales due to the expiration of
   co-agreements for SPRYCEL and E Keppra.
      In the pharmaceutical business, business performance was driven by sales growth of the four global products, and INQOVI launched in
   North America last year, despite an impact from the above expiration of co-agreements and the launch of a generic version of Samsca in the
   U.S. In the nutraceutical business, although beverage consumption was affected by fewer occasions to go out, sales of Nature Made, the Daiya
   Foods Inc. brand, and EQUELLE grew in line with a rising awareness toward self-management of health and revenue grew.
      Meanwhile, business profit before research and development expenses was ¥97,511 million (down 6.9%), reflecting a decrease in gross
   profit due to the product mix and special factors in accounting and so forth, as well as an increase in advertising expenses for proactive
   marketing activities for the new normal. Research and development expenses were ¥48,603 million (down 10.5%) due to a decrease in
   development expenses related to vadadustat, centanafadine, and fremanezumab, among others. As a result, business profit was ¥48,907 million
   (down 3.1%) and operating profit was ¥50,092 million (down 7.1%); however, earnings performed solidly excluding the above-mentioned
   special factors.
      With exchange rate fluctuations and so forth, the Group recorded profit for the period of ¥45,735 million (up 18.7%), and profit attributable
   to owners of the Company of ¥44,682 million (up 18.5%).

1)   Pharmaceuticals
  Revenue for the three-month period ended March 31, 2021 totaled ¥225,322 million (down 5.0%), with business profit of ¥44,696 million (down
11.4%).

     Main products
     ● Four global products
        The Company positions the long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA, the antipsychotic agent REXULTI*1/RXULTI*2, the V2-
      receptor antagonist Samsca/JINARC*3/JYNARQUE*4 and the anti-cancer agent LONSURF as its four global products. The sales of those
      products totaled ¥114,918 million (up 4.3%).

        *1: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in the U.S.
        *2: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in Europe
        *3: Brand name for autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (“ADPKD”) treatment in multiple regions outside Japan
        *4: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in the U.S.

      ・ Long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA
          In the U.S., sales decreased, despite steady growth in prescriptions, mainly due to the impact of a fall back after inventory increases carried
       out by wholesalers and drugstores in the same period of the previous fiscal year due to concerns about the spread of COVID-19 and the
       impact of an exchange rate fluctuation. In Japan, sales increased despite restrictions on information provision activities, mainly due to the
       addition of an indication for the suppression of recurrence and relapse of mood episodes in bipolar I disorder in September 2020. The number
       of prescriptions also continues to expand steadily in Europe. As a result, sales of ABILIFY MAINTENA totaled ¥30,858 million (up 1.4%).

      ・ Antipsychotic agent REXULTI/RXULTI
          In the U.S., where antipsychotic agent REXULTI is sold as adjunctive therapy in major depressive disorder and a treatment for
       schizophrenia, the agent has seen growth in prescriptions as a new treatment option for both disorders; however, sales remained level year
       on year due to the impact of an exchange rate fluctuation. In Japan, where the agent is sold for the treatment of schizophrenia, the number
       of prescriptions for new patients has increased. As a result, sales of REXULTI/RXULTI were level year on year at ¥28,108 million.

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist Samsca
          In Japan, prescriptions continued to expand for treatment of fluid retention in patients with heart failure and hepatic cirrhosis, and ADPKD,
       through online information provision activities and so forth. However, there was a negative impact due to a significant drug price reduction
       from repricing measures for market expansion in association with the NHI price revisions in April 2020. As a result, sales of Samsca remained
       level year on year. In the U.S., where the drug is sold as a treatment for hyponatremia, generics were launched after the expiry of the exclusive
       sales period. As a result, sales of Samsca totaled ¥20,787 million (down 6.7%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist JINARC/JYNARQUE
         In the U.S., the number of prescriptions for ADPKD has increased smoothly, mainly due to efforts to raise awareness of the disease and
       provide information. As a result, sales of JINARC/JYNARQUE totaled ¥23,905 million (up 18.9%).

      ・ Anti-cancer agent LONSURF
          In the U.S., sales increased since at-home care and the use of oral anti-cancer agents*5, 6 are recommended due to the spread of COVID-
       19. In Europe, sales increased due to steady prescription sales and an increased number of country approvals. In Japan, prescriptions were
       steady, and sales increased slightly. As a result, sales of LONSURF totaled ¥11,260 million (up 18.2%).

2)    Nutraceuticals
   Revenue in the nutraceutical business for the three-month period ended March 31, 2021 totaled ¥76,854 million (up 8.9%), with business profit
of ¥9,010 million (up 45.9%).

 Main products
   The Company positions POCARI SWEAT, Nature Made and the Nutrition & Santé SAS brand as its three major brands. The sales of those
 brands totaled ¥47,475 million (up 13.0%). Total sales of its three nurture brands, the Daiya Foods Inc. brand, EQUELLE and BODY MAINTÉ,
 were ¥6,808 million (up 9.0%).

     ● Three major brands
         Sales of POCARI SWEAT, an electrolyte supplement drink, decreased year on year as a result of decreased consumer activities compared
      with the same period of the previous fiscal year due to the spread of COVID-19 and fewer occasions for drinking POCARI SWEAT related to
      colds and influenza. However, sales have started to recover since the end of March. In this brand, regarding new health issues arising under
      the new normal, there were ongoing efforts to provide information and actively educate in various ways for preventive measures against
      heatstroke and the importance of hydration and replenishment of electrolytes (ions) for health management.
         Amid a growing awareness of physical conditioning among consumers, consumers once again recognized the trustworthiness of the brand
      and quality of Nature Made supplements by Pharmavite LLC, and their sales increased dramatically atop an increase in demand, primarily for
      vitamin D and C, particularly in the mainstay North American market.
         The Nutrition & Santé SAS brand, which sells health food products mainly in Europe, saw sales increase under stringent restrictions,
      including lockdowns, mainly due to progress on establishing systems for the new normal and an increase in e-commerce sales.

Impact of COVID-19 on business operations and operating results

   The current situation and potential management risks for the Group have been identified as follows:

 Sales
 • In the pharmaceutical business, self-restraint from going out have led to a decrease in outpatient numbers in hospitals, but there is an
     upward trend for long-term prescriptions for patients with chronic diseases. If the current situation were to be prolonged or become more
     serious, there may be further decline in the number of new prescriptions due to restrictions on visits to health care facilities and the
     voluntary restraint on efforts to raise awareness of diseases and decline in prescriptions such as injections as part of hospital treatment,
     which may affect revenue.
 • In the non-pharmaceutical businesses, demand for some products is increasing owing to heightened health awareness and increases in at-
     home consumption and online shopping. On the other hand, a decline of consumption opportunities is being observed due to the limits on
     outdoor activities resulting from people refraining from going out If the spread of COVID-19 were to be prolonged or become more serious,
     there may be further decline in the capture of new customers due to self-restraint and other restrictions on sales activities and loss of
     consumption opportunities, which may affect revenue.

 Manufacturing
 • With regard to the procurement of raw materials, the purchasing channels overall are secure, although delays are occurring for some import
   items. Manufacturing had been temporarily suspended for some line items due to employees staying at home, but manufacturing has been
   mostly restored. The manufacturing operations are adequately staffed and proceeding smoothly. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be
   prolonged or become more serious causing stoppages in the procurement of raw materials, or if a cluster of infections or other similar cases
   occurred at a manufacturing plant, delays in the supply of some products should also be considered.

 Research and Development
 • Launching clinical trial facilities and registering patients had been put on hold for some products in development undergoing clinical trials,
    but they have already resumed. If the situation concerning the interruption or delay of patient registration were to become more serious,
    schedule of clinical trials or development strategy such as the timing of applications for manufacturing and marketing approval may be
    amended.
 • If the current situation were to be prolonged or become more serious, due to stay-at-home order etc, research activities may slow down
    and the timeline for new product launches in the medium to long term may be delayed.

   As stated above, he Group anticipates a wide range of impacts on the Group’s business due to the spread of COVID-19. The Group will
 continue to fully recognize management risks in advance and take all possible measures to improve corporate value and achieve the Third
 Medium-Term Management Plan.

    Operating Segments
     1) Overview of reportable segments
         The Group’s reportable segments are the constituent units of the Group for which separate financial information is available and
        which are subject to periodic reviews by the Board of Directors in order to make decisions on allocation of business resources and to
        evaluate the business performance of the respective segments.
         The Company directs the Group’s strategic planning, monitors group operations and provides various services to its group companies.
        Business activities are conducted by the Group’s subsidiaries and associates.
         Centering on the healthcare business, the Group operates activities inside and outside Japan relating to the following four reportable
        segments: “Pharmaceuticals,” “Nutraceuticals,” “Consumer products” and “Others” businesses.
         The Group defines the reportable segments as follows:
         “Pharmaceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of prescription drugs and intravenous solutions.
         “Nutraceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of functional beverages, over-the-counter drugs and nutritional supplements.
         “Consumer products” comprises manufacturing and sales of mineral water, soft beverages and food products.
         “Others” encompasses logistics, warehousing, manufacturing and sales of chemical products, evaluation systems for LED displays
        and spectroanalysis devices.
