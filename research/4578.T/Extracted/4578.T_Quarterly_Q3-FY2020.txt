     Based on the concept of total health care, the Group has been promoting corporate activities to maintain and promote health and diagnose
   and treat disease. Now is the time to demonstrate the Group’s true value as a total healthcare provider, and harness the opportunities presented
   by a growing awareness of health in the new normal in order to continue to move toward the realization of sustainable growth.

      For the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020, the Group recorded consolidated revenue of ¥1,066,959 million (up 3.7% over the
   same period of the previous fiscal year) although the Group’s business activities were affected to a certain extent by the spread of COVID-19.
   The revenue in the pharmaceutical business increased primarily driven by sales growth of the four global products (ABILIFY MAINTENA,
   REXULTI/RXULTI, Samsca/JINARC/JYNARQUE and LONSURF) although intravenous solutions and some other therapeutic businesses were
   affected by decreases in the number of patient visits and operations and in bed occupancy rate. In the nutraceutical business, although beverage
   consumption was affected by fewer occasions to go out and unfavorable weather in Japan, sales of Nature Made, the Daiya Foods Inc. brand
   and EQUELLE grew in line with a rising awareness toward self-management of health, and revenue was maintained at the same level as that
   for the same period of the previous fiscal year even under the condition of the spread of COVID-19. Business profit before research and
   development expenses was ¥346,723 million (up 11.8%) as a result of further cost efficiency improvement. The Group recorded research and
   development expenses of ¥158,059 million (up 2.7%) due to the increase of development expenses for REXULTI/RXULTI, futibatinib,
   centanafadine and VIS649, which resulted in business profit of ¥188,663 million (up 20.7%).
      Impairment losses for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 were ¥25,102 million, due to impairment losses of intangible assets
   (in-process research and development) related to guadecitabine and vadadustat.
      The Group also recorded operating profit of ¥171,695 million (up 10.2%), profit for the period of ¥130,598 million (up 13.7%) and profit
   attributable to owners of the Company of ¥127,766 million (up 14.6%).

1)   Pharmaceuticals
  Revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 totaled ¥715,124 million (up 6.3%), with business profit of ¥163,562 million (up
23.4%).

     Main products
     ● Four global products
        The Company positions the long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA, the antipsychotic agent REXULTI*1/RXULTI*2, the V2-
      receptor antagonist Samsca/JINARC*3/JYNARQUE*4 and the anti-cancer agent LONSURF as its four global products. The sales of those
      products totaled ¥325,520 million (up 20.1%).

        *1: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in the U.S.
        *2: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in Europe
        *3: Brand name for autosomal dominant polycystic kidney disease (“ADPKD”) treatment in multiple regions outside Japan
        *4: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in the U.S.

      ・ Long acting antipsychotic agent ABILIFY MAINTENA
          ABILIFY MAINTENA has continuously expanded prescriptions due to promotion of the product’s efficacy as a treatment for schizophrenia
       in global markets, growing awareness of the drug’s ease of use, and the approval of an additional indication as a treatment for bipolar disorder
       in the U.S. in 2017, and it contributed to sales. In Japan, an additional indication for the suppression of recurrence and relapse of mood
       episodes in bipolar I disorder was approved in September 2020. As a result, sales of ABILIFY MAINTENA totaled ¥87,908 million (up
       17.4%).

      ・ Antipsychotic agent REXULTI/RXULTI
          Antipsychotic agent REXULTI has been highly evaluated in the U.S. for its efficacy and safety as a new treatment option for adjunctive
       therapy in major depressive disorder and a treatment for schizophrenia, and its sales have increased. In Japan, where the agent is sold for the
       treatment of schizophrenia, the number of prescriptions, particularly for the acute phase of treatment for schizophrenia in adults, have sharply
       increased since the limit on the number of prescription days was removed in May 2019. RXULTI has been launched sequentially in several
       European countries since April 2019. As a result, sales of REXULTI/RXULTI totaled ¥79,397 million (up 23.7%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist Samsca
          In Japan, prescriptions continue to expand for treatments of cardiac edema and hepatic edema, as well as ADPKD, an intractable kidney
       disease. However, due to downward pricing measures for market expansion based on NHI price revisions in April, sales of Samsca totaled
       ¥66,152 million (down 1.6%).

      ・ V2-receptor antagonist JINARC/JYNARQUE
          In the U.S., the number of prescriptions for ADPKD has increased smoothly as efforts to raise awareness of the disease and provide
       information about clinical data helped more people recognize the disease and the product. In addition, the number of prescriptions has been
       increasing in European markets.
          As a result, sales of JINARC/JYNARQUE totaled ¥61,016 million (up 51.6%).

      ・ Anti-cancer agent LONSURF
          In Japan, in addition to the increased number of prescriptions for the treatment of colorectal cancer, the number of prescriptions also
       increased for the treatment of previously treated metastatic gastric or gastroesophageal junction adenocarcinoma, approved in August 2019.
       In the U.S., sales increased since at-home care and the use of oral anti-cancer agents *1, 2 are recommended due to the spread of COVID-19.
       In Europe, sales increased due to steady prescription sales and an increased number of country approvals. As a result, sales of LONSURF
       totaled ¥31,046 million (up 26.4%).

        *1: Pelin Cinar et al., Safety at the Time of the COVID-19 Pandemic: How to Keep our Oncology Patients and Healthcare Workers Safe. J
            Natl Compr Canc Netw, Apr 15, 2020; 1-6.
        *2: ASCO. COVID-19 Patient Care Information, Cancer Treatment and Supportive Care.
            https://www.asco.org/asco-coronavirus-resources/care-individuals-cancer-during-covid-19/cancer-treatment-supportive-care. Updated

  Revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 totaled ¥252,767 million (down 0.2%), with business profit of ¥36,952 million

 Main products
   The Company positions POCARI SWEAT, Nature Made and the Nutrition & Santé SAS brand as its three major brands. The sales of those
 brands totaled ¥152,542 million (down 2.3%). Total sales of its three nurture brands, the Daiya Foods Inc. brand, EQUELLE and BODY MAINTÉ,
 were ¥20,189 million (up 27.7%).

     ● Three major brands
         In Japan, the overall beverage market for January-September declined year on year due to lower consumer activity caused by the spread of
      COVID-19, as well as the continuation of weather even more unfavorable than the previous year during the summer months, the peak season
      for beverages*3. The decline was particularly large in the sports drink category*4, with a decrease in sales for the POCARI SWEAT electrolyte
      supplement drink brand as a whole in the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020. However, the situation at present is showing a trend
      of recovery in sales, which has been brought about by providing information in response to new health issues arising under the new normal,
      and by implementing various supportive measures regarding activities and physical conditioning for athletes amid restrictions placed on club
      activities at school and suspension of national championships.
         Sales of Nature Made, supplements by Pharmavite LLC, increased amid a growing awareness of physical conditioning and also owing to

3)     Consumer products
   In mineral water products, sales volume declined overall for the mainstay brand CRYSTAL GEYSER, particularly for the vending machine
distribution channel due to fewer occasions to go out due to the spread of COVID-19, etc., although the sales volume of the 700-milliliter bottle,
the largest size for personal use, remained strong. Sales volume of MATCH, a carbonated electrolyte drink containing vitamins, decreased due to
the impact of unfavorable weather, fewer occasions to go out, etc.
   As a result, revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 totaled ¥24,105 million (down 6.4%) with business profit of ¥9,252
million (up 15.3%) owing to an increase in share of profit from investment accounted for using the equity method and increased cost efficiency in
response to the new normal.

4)     Others
   Sales in the specialty chemical business for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 decreased compared with the prior year period
due to a fall in shipments of foaming agents, titanates, compound materials, etc. due to the impact of the spread of COVID-19 on the automobile
and housing industries. Sales in the fine chemical business increased compared with the prior year period mainly due to sales from an active
pharmaceutical ingredient-related business supplying cefixime to overseas licensees, which was acquired in June 2019.
   In the transportation and warehousing business, both the handling volume of business partner’s products and sales slightly decreased due to the
effect of the spread of COVID-19 despite an increase in the number of new external customers in line with the promotion of a “common distribution
platform.”
   As a result, revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020 totaled ¥104,038 million (down 2.3%). Business profit was ¥10,758
million (up 28.3%) mainly due to an increase in share of profit from investment accounted for using the equity method.

Impact of COVID-19 on business operations and operating results
   The Group’s response to the spread of COVID-19 has been to perform daily health checks for employees in manufacturing operations, to
 apply work-at-home policy for employees in other roles and to strive to continue business activities while ensuring the safety of employees.
   For the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020, the four global products of the pharmaceutical business (ABILIFY MAINTENA,
 REXULTI/RXULTI, Samsca/JINARC/JYNARQUE and LONSURF) grew steadily. However, some products in the pharmaceutical business and
 other businesses were affected by the restrictions on going out due to the spread of COVID-19. In terms of the supply of products, the current
 system is able to continue to provide stable supply. Regarding the impact on business operations and operating results for the fiscal year ending
 December 31, 2020, we will continue to pay close attention to the impact on business and business results, assuming that the situation will be
 prolonged while the impact of the spread of COVID-19 is unclear.

    The current situation and potential management risks for the Group have been identified as follows:

 Sales
 • In the pharmaceutical business, official requests to stay at home and urban lockdowns have led to a decrease in outpatient numbers in
     hospitals but there is an upward trend for long-term prescriptions for patients with chronic diseases. If the current situation were to be
     prolonged or become more serious, there may be further decline in the number of new prescriptions and decline in prescriptions such as
     injections as part of hospital treatment due to restrictions on visits to health care facilities and the voluntary restraint on efforts to raise
     awareness of diseases, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.
 • In the non-pharmaceutical businesses, on one hand, demand for some products is increasing owing to heightened health awareness and
     increases in at-home consumption and online shopping. On the other hand, a loss of consumption opportunities is being observed due to
     the limits on outdoor activities resulting from people refraining from leaving their homes. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be prolonged
     or become more serious, there may be further decline in the capture of new customers and loss of consumption opportunities due to
     voluntary restraint and other restrictions on sales activities, which may affect revenue for the full fiscal year.

 Manufacturing
 • With regard to the procurement of raw materials, the purchasing channels overall are secure, although delays are occurring for some import
   items. Manufacturing had been temporarily suspended for some line items due to employees staying at home, but all manufacturing has
   been restored. The manufacturing operations are adequately staffed and proceeding smoothly. If the spread of COVID-19 were to be
   prolonged or become more serious causing stoppages in the procurement of raw materials, or if a cluster of infections or other similar cases
   occurred at a manufacturing plant, the supply of certain products may stop.

 Research and Development
 • Plans to launch clinical trial facilities and register patients had been put on hold for some products in development undergoing clinical
    trials, but they are gradually resuming. If the situation concerning the interruption or delay of patient registration were to become more
    serious, progress of clinical trials or the timing of applications for manufacturing and marketing approval may be delayed or development
    strategy may be amended.
 • In research activities, delays are occurring in some activities due to stay-at-home order, etc. If the current situation were to be prolonged
    or become more serious and cause delays in research activities, the timeline for new product commercialization in the medium to long
    term may be delayed.

    As stated above, many factors can be foreseen as potentially affecting the Group’s business as the result of the spread of COVID-19. Looking
 forward, the Group will continue its efforts to secure the safety of the Group’s employees and continue business activities under the corporate
 philosophy of “Otsuka-people creating new products for better health worldwide.”

    Operating Segments
     1) Overview of reportable segments
         The Group’s reportable segments are the constituent units of the Group for which separate financial information is available and
        which are subject to periodic reviews by the Board of Directors in order to make decisions on allocation of business resources and to
        evaluate the business performance of the respective segments.
         The Company directs the Group’s strategic planning, monitors group operations and provides various services to its group companies.
        Business activities are conducted by the Group’s subsidiaries and associates.
         Centering on the healthcare business, the Group operates activities inside and outside Japan relating to the following four reportable
        segments: “Pharmaceuticals,” “Nutraceuticals,” “Consumer products” and “Others” businesses.
         The Group defines the reportable segments as follows:
         “Pharmaceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of prescription drugs and intravenous solutions.
         “Nutraceuticals” comprises manufacturing and sales of functional beverages, over-the-counter drugs and nutritional supplements.
         “Consumer products” comprises manufacturing and sales of mineral water, soft beverages and food products.

Impairment of Assets
 FY2020 (From January 1, 2020 to September 30, 2020)
   The Group recorded impairment losses of ¥25,102 million for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2020.
   In the pharmaceutical business, the Group decided to halt the development of guadecitabine, a drug candidate for the treatment of acute
  myeloid leukemia, myelodysplastic syndromes and ovarian cancer, based on a comprehensive review of the results of the Phase III trial
  for acute myeloid leukemia and myelodysplastic syndromes. As a result the Group recognized impairment losses of ¥10,723 million of in-
  process research and development relating to guadecitabine.
   In addition, based on the results of the Phase III trial in non-dialysis patients and subsequent re-evaluation of the business plan of
  vadadustat, a drug candidate for the treatment of renal anemia, the Group recognized impairment losses of ¥14,050 million as the difference
  between the recoverable amount measured by value in use and the book value of the in-process research and development.
