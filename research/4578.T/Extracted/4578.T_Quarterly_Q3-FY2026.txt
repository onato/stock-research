         The Otsuka Group identifies unmet or potential needs and challenges related to health and proposes new perspectives and behavioral
      transformation on health to society in order to address them. As people’s health needs evolve toward well-being, which encompasses
      physical, mental, and social aspects, we create and provide products and services across a wide range of business areas supporting
      health—from disease treatment to diagnosis, prevention, and health maintenance and promotion. Furthermore, we aim to be a company
      that not only creates and provides such products and services but also offers new choices and appropriate information for daily life,
      collaborates with local communities, and delivers value that supports each individual’s health and way of life.

         Revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026 totaled ¥2,042,439 million (up 12.3% over the same period of the
      previous fiscal year), as all operating segments recorded increased revenue. The main factors behind this growth were increases in the
      two core products of antipsychotic agent REXULTI/RXULTI and anti-cancer agent LONSURF, which have been designated the growth
      drivers under the Fourth Medium-Term Management Plan, as well as sales growth of the long-acting injection ABILIFY
      MAINTENA/ABILIFY ASIMTUFII and the anti-APRIL antibody VOYXACT launched in 2025, the increase in royalty income, and the
      new IV solution business in the U.S. launched in May 2025, among others, in the pharmaceutical business. In the nutraceutical
      business, revenue increased due to growth in all three designated growth driver categories by social issues.
         Business profit before research and development expenses was ¥697,201 million (up 13.3%). The main factor was the increase in
      gross profit due to increased revenue of main products in the pharmaceutical business and growth in royalty income.
         Research and development expenses totaled ¥260,316 million (up 4.7%). Development expenses related to clinical development
      increased, primarily driven by repinatrabit, in development for the treatment of Phenylketonuria, TSND-201, in development for the
      treatment of post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD), futibatinib, in development for biliary tract cancer, and ARC-02, an antibody-drug
      conjugate (ADC), for which clinical trials have been newly initiated. On the other hand, development expenses for centanafadine and
      ulotaront, etc. decreased, resulting in expenses being at the same level as the same period of the previous fiscal year when excluding the
      impact of exchange rates.
         Against the backdrop of steady revenue growth, business profit totaled ¥436,884 million (up 19.2%) and operating profit totaled
      ¥423,733 million (up 4.0%).
         Profit for the period was ¥326,463 million (up 8.3%), and profit attributable to owners of the Company was ¥322,105 million (up
      8.2%).

     1)   Pharmaceuticals
            Revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026 totaled ¥1,460,575 million (up 14.2%), with business profit
          of ¥389,601 million (up 21.4%).

          Main products
           ・ Antipsychotic agent REXULTI*1/RXULTI*2
               In the U.S., prescriptions grew mainly due to ongoing activities to raise awareness of major depressive disorder and
             agitation associated with dementia due to Alzheimer’s disease and enhancement of detailing activities. In Japan, in
             addition to the indication for the treatment of schizophrenia, depression, and depressive states, prescriptions grew due to
             the continued provision of information related to agitation associated with dementia due to Alzheimer’s disease*3. As a
             result, sales of REXULTI/RXULTI totaled ¥296,484 million (up 21.4%).
                   *1: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent outside Europe
                   *2: Brand name for the antipsychotic agent in Europe
                   *3: In the Japanese package insert, the indications and effects are described as “excessive motor activity or physically/verbally aggressive
                       behavior due to rapid changes in mood, irritability, and/or outbursts associated with dementia due to Alzheimer’s disease.”


            ・ Anti-cancer agent LONSURF
               In the U.S., Europe and Japan, the number of prescriptions for treatment in combination with bevacizumab for
             colorectal cancer increased as awareness grew. Sales of LONSURF totaled ¥90,031 million (up 15.4%).

            ・ Aripiprazole long-acting injection (1 month) ABILIFY MAINTENA
               In the U.S. and Japan, continued detailing activities for bipolar I disorder and schizophrenia led to sales of ¥186,532
             million (up 11.4%).

            ・ Aripiprazole long-acting injection (2 months) ABILIFY ASIMTUFII
               In the U.S. and Europe, sales increased significantly atop growth in prescriptions as a result of the progress made in
             the switch from the aripiprazole long-acting injection (1 month) ABILIFY MAINTENA, etc., in addition to detailing
             activities regarding the efficacy of the product. As a result, sales of ABILIFY ASIMTUFII totaled ¥42,939 million (up
             66.6%).

            ・ V2-receptor antagonist Samsca/JINARC*4/JYNARQUE*5
               In the U.S., the penetration of generics following the end of the exclusive sales period as treatment for autosomal
             dominant polycystic kidney disease (ADPKD) led to a significant decrease in sales. Sales of
             Samsca/JINARC/JYNARQUE totaled ¥125,133 million (down 31.1%).
                   *4: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in multiple regions outside Japan
                   *5: Brand name for ADPKD treatment in the U.S.

2)   Nutraceuticals
       Revenue for the nine-month period ended September 30, 2026 totaled ¥460,631 million (up 6.9%), with business profit of
     ¥61,478 million (up 3.9%).

     <Three categories by social issues>
       ・ For Climate & Environmental Risk
           Sales volume of POCARI SWEAT, an electrolyte supplement drink, was lower than the previous year in Japan mainly
        due to the impact of unfavorable weather conditions from July to September. Nevertheless, we continued efforts to
        educate and support consumers about hydration and replenishment of electrolytes for heat acclimatization and heatstroke
        prevention even before the heat intensified. On the other hand, overseas, our efforts to improve brand value contributed
        to an increase in sales volume in certain areas, particularly in Indonesia, the Philippines and Vietnam, leading to an
        overall increase in sales over the same period of the previous fiscal year. Sales of the Nutrition & Santé SAS, which sells
        health food products mainly in Europe, increased mainly due to steady performance in the main products such as Gerblé.
        As a result, revenue for this category totaled ¥163,542 million (up 3.6%).

       ・ For Women’s Health
          In Japan, for EQUELLE, a food and supplement brand containing equol, the acquisition of new customers and the
        expansion of regular customers contributed to increased sales, driven by the expansion of product trial opportunities and
        other initiatives. As a result, revenue for this category totaled ¥47,759 million (up 6.0%).

       ・ For Healthier Life
          Nature Made supplements by Pharmavite LLC are created and expanded around three core value pillars - Science,
        Quality, and Innovation. In the U.S., sales of main products continued to perform favorably, and the market share also
        expanded. Revenue for this category, including the new product /zeroz, which focuses on oxygen and was launched by
        Otsuka Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. (“Otsuka Pharmaceutical”) in Japan in March 2026 totaled ¥193,383 million (up
        14.2%).

       [Products comprising the category]
       For Climate & Environmental Risk: POCARI SWEAT, OS-1, Daiya, the Nutrition & Santé SAS brand
       For Women’s Health: EQUELLE, Bonafide, Uqora, Cosmedics*6 (InnerSignal, Sakuraé)
       For Healthier Life: Nature Made, MegaFood, Calorie Mate, /zeroz
       *6: Cosmedics: a word coined by cosmetics + medicine = Otsuka’s unique concept for skin health products

   and other payables decreased by ¥25,753 million, and bonds and borrowings decreased by ¥32,185 million mainly due to the redemption

   liabilities decreased by ¥5,814 million.

 c. Equity

   December 31, 2025. Treasury shares increased by ¥46,567 million compared with the end of the previous fiscal year due to a share
   buyback implemented to improve capital efficiency and return profits to shareholders. On the other hand, retained earnings increased by
   ¥234,651 million, mainly due to the recording of profit attributable to owners of the Company.

         1) Overview of reportable segments
               The Group’s reportable segments are the constituent units of the Group for which separate financial information is available

            associates.

    Acquisition of Transcend and TSND-201
       Otsuka Pharmaceutical, a consolidated subsidiary of the Company, entered into an agreement on March 27, 2026 (U.S. time), under
    which it would make Transcend Therapeutics, Inc. (hereinafter “Transcend”) a wholly owned subsidiary (hereinafter the “Acquisition”)
    through its wholly owned subsidiary Otsuka America, Inc. After completing the necessary legal procedures, the Acquisition was
    finalized on June 12, 2026 (U.S. time). Based on the terms of the agreement, Otsuka Pharmaceutical paid a total of USD 700 million to
    Transcend shareholders as consideration for the acquisition of all of its issued shares. In the future, there is a possibility of paying an
    additional amount of up to USD 525 million, depending on revenue milestones related to assets. As a result of the Acquisition, TSND-
    201 (methylone), which is a rapid-acting neuroplastogen—an agent that induces rapid and durable neural plasticity in the brain—has
    been added to the Group’s pipeline. TSND-201 is being developed as a potential treatment for post-traumatic stress disorder (PTSD)
    and other psychiatric conditions.
       As a result of conducting a concentration test in accordance with the provisions of IFRS 3 “Business Combinations” for the above
    transaction, the Group concluded that substantially all of the fair value of the gross assets acquired was concentrated in a single
    identifiable asset, and also determined that the integrated set of activities and assets acquired did not qualify as a business. For these
    reasons, the Group recorded this transaction as an asset acquisition. As a result, in the condensed interim consolidated statement of
    financial position, an amount of ¥114,279 million, which is part of the upfront payment for the asset acquisition, is recorded as
    intangible assets (in-process research and development).
