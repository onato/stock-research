   3.    Business Review

        In the first quarter of 2026, the world economy experienced generally stable growth, and China’s
   economy achieved a good start, further demonstrating its development resilience and vitality, with the
   gross domestic product (“GDP”) increasing by 5.0% as compared with that in the same period of last year.
   Affected by factors such as the situation in the Middle East, the international crude oil market fluctuated
   drastically and the average international crude oil price increased slightly on a year-on-year basis. The
   average futures price of North Sea Brent crude oil was US$78.38 per barrel, representing an increase of
   4.5% as compared with US$74.98 per barrel for the same period of last year. The average futures price of
   U.S. West Texas Intermediate crude oil was US$72.67 per barrel, representing an increase of 1.8% as
   compared with US$71.42 per barrel for the same period of last year. Domestic consumption of refined oil
   products increased slightly, while the domestic consumption in natural gas basically remained stable.

        The Group proactively responded to changes in the international oil and gas market environment,
   strengthened production and operation organization, and achieved positive results in oil and gas
   exploration and development. Refining and chemical production facilities maintained safe, stable, and

products increased on a year-on-year basis. New energy businesses such as wind and solar power
generation developed rapidly, and the development of bio-manufacturing, fine chemicals, and new
materials businesses accelerated. The profit attributable to owners of the Company continued to grow, and
the financial position remained healthy and sound. In the first quarter of 2026, the average realized price

oil and chemical products.

4.    Other Matters

     China Issued Import Tax Preferential Policies for the Exploration, Development, and
Utilization of Energy Resources during the "15th Five-Year Plan" Period

    On February 14, 2026, the Ministry of Finance, the General Administration of Customs, and the State
Taxation Administration issued the “Notice on Import Tax Preferential Policies for the Exploration,
Development and Utilization of Energy Resources during the 15th Five-Year Plan Period” (Cai Guan Shui
[2026] No. 16) (《關于“十五五”期間能源資源勘探開發利用進口稅收優惠政策的通知》（財關稅
〔2026〕16 號）), which stipulated that from January 1, 2026 to 31 December 2030, for the natural gas
(including pipeline natural gas and LNG) imported through the construction projects of cross-border
natural gas pipelines and imported LNG receiving, storage and transportation equipment that have been
examined and approved by the National Development and Reform Commission (“NDRC”), as well as the
expansion projects of imported LNG receiving, storage and transportation equipment that have been
approved by the provincial people’s government, the import value-added tax will be refunded at a certain
percentage. The specific refund ratios are as follows: (1) for imported natural gas under long-term trade
contracts signed before the end of 2014 and confirmed by the NDRC, the import value-added tax will be
refunded at a rate of 70%; (2) for other natural gas, if the import price is higher than the reference
benchmark value, the import value-added tax will be refunded according to 80% of the inverted ratio of
the import price and the reference benchmark value. The calculation formula for the inverted ratio is:
inverted ratio = (import price - reference benchmark value) / import price, and the relevant calculation
takes one quarter as a cycle.

     This event will not affect the continuity of the business and the stability of management of the Group
and is conducive to the sustainable and healthy development of the Group.
