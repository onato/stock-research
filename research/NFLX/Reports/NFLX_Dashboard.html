Data caveats — read before interpreting the charts below:
  • 10-for-1 forward stock split took effect November 17, 2025. All share counts, EPS, and per-share figures throughout this dashboard (including restated history) are on the current post-split basis.
  • Q1-2026 net income ($5,282.8M) and free cash flow ($5,094.1M) include a one-off $2.8B cash termination fee from Paramount Skydance (paid on Warner Bros. Discovery's behalf after WBD chose Paramount's competing bid). This is NOT organic profitability — charts touching quarterly net income/margin/FCF flag it explicitly. Q2-2026 FCF ($1,525M) was in turn depressed by cash taxes paid on that fee.
  • Netflix stopped disclosing paid memberships starting Q1-2025 (last reported figure: 301.6M at FY2024) and is cutting its engagement report to annual cadence starting 2027. No subscriber count chart is shown here because the underlying data series is no longer published.
  • FY2018–FY2021 free cash flow is genuinely, deeply negative (FY2018 −$2,854M, FY2019 −$3,140M, FY2021 −$132M) — the content-investment build years. This is correct data, not an error, and the FCF chart cannot use a log scale because of it.
  • Q4-2021/2022/2023 EPS is derived (full-year minus H1 minus Q3, using FY weighted-average shares) rather than a filed figure. Q4-2022's near-zero net income ($55.3M, 0.7% margin) is real — a euro-debt FX remeasurement loss — not a data error.

                Valuation Philosophy & Key Judgment Calls

                The base case ($56.90) sits 23% below the current price of $74.14 — only the Bull scenario ($84.94, +14.6%) implies upside. This is an honest, conservative model: the sanity check passed with no auto-fix applied, because the implied exit P/E at fair value (22.5x) sits below both the current trading multiple (29.3x) and the FY2021+ historical average (~41x). In other words, reaching $56.90 requires the market to compress Netflix's multiple further from here — not expand it — consistent with a company navigating confirmed growth deceleration (16% → 13% → 11.7% guided) rather than one still re-rating higher.
