Macroeconomic Environment

Key Factors
•   Recent tariff measures imposed by the U.S. Administration
    under Section 122 of the Trade Act of 1974, following the U.S.
    Supreme Court’s invalidation of IEEPA-based reciprocal tariffs
    in February 2026, indicate a continued shift toward more
    restrictive trade policies, which may increase costs and create
    uncertainty for international trade.


•   Ongoing tensions in the Middle East may continue to affect
    crude oil-based material costs and logistics expenses.


•   Strong demand for components used in AI infrastructure has
    continued to absorb semiconductor manufacturing capacity,
    which may affect the cost and lead time of conventional
    component supplies
