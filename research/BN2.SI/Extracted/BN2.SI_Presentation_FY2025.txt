 Macroeconomic Environment
Key Factors

 Series of announcements by the United States since
  2 April 2025 regarding reciprocal tariffs on its trading
  partners, including China and Vietnam.

 While subsequent measures have led to a partial de-
  escalation, tariff levels have yet to return to those
  prior to 2 April 2025.

 The geopolitical climate remains fluid and uncertain,
  and the global economy appears to be entering a
  new era of elevated trade tariffs.

 These developments have triggered significant
  volatility in global markets, with rising concerns over
  supply chain disruptions and recessionary pressures.
