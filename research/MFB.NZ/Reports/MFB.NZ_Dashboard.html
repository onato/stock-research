LIVE STRATEGIC REVIEW — No Outcome, No Bid (as of 13-Aug-2026)

On 28-Apr-2026 the Board announced a price-sensitive review of "ownership, capital structure and strategic options," appointing Cameron Partners Limited as financial adviser — explicitly because "the company's recent performance has yet to be reflected in the share price." The stock jumped roughly 17% ($0.21 → $0.28) that week, its best week in nearly a year.

As of 13-Aug-2026 the review "remains in its preliminary stages," there is no transaction, no bid, and no timeline, and the Board has explicitly flagged that remaining NZX-listed is one live option. That means a partial takeover-speculation premium is already embedded in the current $0.29 price. The DCF base case below is a STANDALONE valuation with zero takeover premium; the bear case explicitly models the review concluding with no transaction and the speculation premium unwinding.

📊
Model Approach: Why FY2022 Is Excluded as an Anchor

FY2022 EBITDA of $34.2m was a verified COVID-lockdown demand spike — roughly 2.1x the current run-rate (FY26: $16.4m) — and is deliberately excluded from growth, margin and exit-multiple anchoring anywhere in this model. Growth is instead anchored to the FY2024→FY2026 stabilisation CAGR of 2.43%, fading to a 1.5% terminal rate given a mature, competitively squeezed single-country category.

WACC of 17.0% is stacked: NZ small-cap base 11.5% + micro-cap illiquidity 2.25% + single-country/category concentration 1.25% + no-moat/high-churn 1.75%. Terminal value is bound by a 5.0x EV/owner-FCF cap (base case) in every scenario tested, anchored to the post-COVID (FY23-26) EV/EBITDA trading range of 2.76x–3.91x. The model's sanity check against 10 years of trading multiples PASSED — every multiple implied by the base intrinsic value falls inside or immediately adjacent to the post-COVID historical range.
