Growth & Margins

Revenue reaccelerated after a flat FY2018–FY2023 stretch (~€34–38bn); Q3 is structurally the biggest quarter on Northern-hemisphere flu shipments, so read quarterly bars YoY, never QoQ.

Revenue & YoY Growth

Gross Margin

Stepped up from ~70% (through FY2021) to 76-77% (FY2022-2025) on the EUROAPI spin-off and Opella divestiture, then to 79-81% in recent quarters as Dupixent's mix weight grows.

Operating Margin

FY rows are IFRS operating margin, which is noisy: FY2020's 39.2% includes the Regeneron stake disposal gain; FY2019's 8.4% is depressed by one-offs. Quarterly rows carry Sanofi's non-GAAP business operating margin instead (the cleaner series management guides on) — Q2-2026 was 28.4%, +3.7pp YoY. Q3 quarters run structurally highest on flu-vaccine seasonality (35.6-35.7% in Q3-2024/2025); always compare a quarter YoY, never to the adjacent quarter.

Earnings & Cash Generation

EPS basis changes between annual and quarterly rows — see the EPS chart note before comparing across the FY/quarter toggle.

EPS: IFRS (Annual) vs Business (Quarterly)

CAUTION: FY rows are IFRS basic EPS, distorted by disposal gains — FY2020 €9.82 (Regeneron stake sale) and FY2025 €6.40 (Opella gain) both overstate the operating trend; FY2019's €2.24 is a genuine trough. Quarterly rows are business EPS (Sanofi's non-GAAP measure, the one management guides on) — FY2025 business EPS was €7.83 vs €6.40 IFRS. These are NOT one continuous comparable series; do not read a straight line across the FY/quarter toggle.

Net Income vs Free Cash Flow

Net income here is IFRS and carries the same disposal noise as the EPS chart (FY2020, FY2025). FreeCashFlow is statutory OCF less capex; FY2025 was €7,212m vs the company's own broader ‘free cash flow’ definition (pre-restructuring/acquisitions/disposals) of €9.9bn.

Reported FCF vs Owner FCF (DCF Basis)

Single FY2025 snapshot from the DCF JSON, not a CSV time series. Owner FCF (€6,899m) is the DCF's base and sits below both reported FCF (€7,212m) and the company's own broader FCF definition (€9,900m, which excludes restructuring/M&A/disposal cash flows the DCF treats as real costs).

Shareholders' Equity

Fell to €71,376m in FY2025 from €77,507m despite a profitable year — roughly €12bn was spent on BD/M&A (Blueprint Medicines, Vigil Neuroscience) and €5bn on buybacks, both charged directly against equity.

Capital Allocation

A shrinking share count and a 31-year dividend growth streak are both part of the equity story, not just the income statement.

Shares Outstanding

Declining from 1,263m (FY2023) to 1,196.6m (Q2-2026) on buybacks (€5bn completed 2025, €1bn 2026 tranche completed by end-April), roughly 2-3%/year of dilution offset.

Dividend Per Share

€2.85 (FY2015) to €3.92 (FY2025) — a 31-year consecutive increase streak, with a 32nd expected for FY2026.

Stock-Based Compensation

SBC is only ~0.7% of revenue (€313m FY2025) — nearly an SBC-zero name by tech standards, which is why the DCF's house/street valuation band is unusually narrow (~€4.7/share wide).
