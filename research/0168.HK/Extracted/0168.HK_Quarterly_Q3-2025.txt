During the reporting period, the Company adhered to its strategic focus and accelerated high-quality
development under the guidance of the Board. By leveraging the strengths of Tsingtao Beer’s brand, quality
and distribution network, it also optimized product structure adjustments, continuously innovated and refined
marketing operations, and actively expanded domestic and international markets. Maintaining a multi-
channel approach, the Company solidified its position in mainstream distribution channels through refined
operations, accelerated the development of emerging channels, and continuously strengthened its online
business. Through innovative marketing models and multi-scenario marketing practices, it enhanced consumer
experiences, fostered interactive engagement, and drove product consumption expansion and upgrades,
thereby achieving sustained profitability improvements through multiple measures. In the first three quarters
of 2025, the Company achieved operating revenue of RMB29.37 billion, a year-on-year increase of 1.4%;
net profit attributable to shareholders of RMB5.27 billion, a year-on-year increase of 5.7%; and cumulative
product sales of 6.894 million kiloliters, a year-on-year increase of 1.6%. Among these, the flagship brand
Tsingtao Beer achieved sales of 3.99 million kiloliters, a year-on-year increase of 4.1%, while mid-to-high-
end and premium products achieved sales of 2.935 million kiloliters, a year-on-year increase of 5.6%.
