Basic Policy on Profit Distribution and Information on Dividends for the Current Fiscal Year and the Next Fiscal Year
The Company regards the payment of dividends to shareholders as one of its top management priorities.
 For the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025, despite a decline in profits compared to the previous year, the Company has decided to
pay an annual dividend of 150 yen per share, in line with its policy of stable dividends and enhanced shareholder returns.
 For the fiscal year ending March 31, 2026, the Company expects to pay an annual dividend of 159 yen per share and will continue to
work on further enhancing shareholder returns. Details regarding the shareholder return policy for the next and subsequent fiscal years
will be announced in the Midterm Plan 2027 (FY2025-FY2027), which is scheduled to be disclosed on May 20, 2025.
