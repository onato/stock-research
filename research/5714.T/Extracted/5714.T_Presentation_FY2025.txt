      DOWA's Aim is True Circularity


Circularity has entered an era in which quality matters.
Today, with "recycling" becoming increasingly widespread, we aim to achieve a more integrated, long-term true circularity.
By employing new ideas and more advanced technology while coordinating with industry and society as a whole,
we pursue the quality in circularity.

    Revalidating our Optimal Capital Structure
     We have begun revalidating our optimal capital structure, taking into account our business characteristics,
                    in order to achieve the ROE target set out in the Medium-Term Plan 2027


                                                           ＜Background to initiating the review＞
                           Our company operates in an industry where market conditions can have a significant impact on management
                                                 (e.g., profit volatility, sharp increases/decreases in working capital).
 As we have continuously implemented measures to enhance profitability across each business, our shareholders’ equity has reached a certain level of adequacy.



                                          ＜Methodology for revalidating the optimal capital structure＞
Process 1: Assess debt financing capacity (debt capacity)
• Conduct a self-assessment on the premise of maintaining our credit rating (single-A).
