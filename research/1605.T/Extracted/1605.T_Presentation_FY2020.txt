Business Development Strategy
- Towards a Net Zero Carbon Society by 2050 (1)


   Basic Policy on Management
   INPEX will proactively engage in energy structure reforms towards the realization of a net zero
   carbon society by 2050, while responding to the growing energy demands of Japan and the world and
   fulfilling its responsibility for the development and stable supply of energy over the long-term.



   1.    Stable supply of energy
          The company will continue to position its upstream
           business as a core business and will work to fulfill its
           two social responsibilities of providing a stable
           supply of energy and responding to climate change
           by strengthening its upstream business and making it
           cleaner.
          INPEX will accelerate its shift to natural gas and
           continue enhancing initiatives to expand its Global
           Gas Value Chain in Japan and growing markets in
           Asia, while promoting carbon neutral LNG.
