New Zealand King Salmon Investments Limited (NZK.NZ)

Premium King (Chinook) salmon aquaculture · Marlborough Sounds, NZ · FY end 30 September (changed twice) · Reporting in NZD millions

Fiscal calendar changed twice — read the charts accordingly. NZK's balance date moved from 30 June to 31 January in 2021 (a 7-month stub, "FY2021-7mo", covering Jul–Jan) and then from 31 January to 30 September in 2025 (an 8-month stub, "FY2025-8mo", covering Feb–Sep 2025). The current fiscal year (FY2026) runs 1 October to 30 September. Stub periods are shown with a hatched/lighter bar style on annual charts — they are shorter windows, not a collapse in trading. No year-over-year growth figure on this dashboard compares a stub against a full year, or a June-basis year against a January-basis year; where that comparison would be misleading the figure is marked "n/m" (not meaningful). Annual and half-year series are always kept on separate charts. The share count also stepped from ~139m to ~541m at the FY2022/FY2023 dilutive recapitalization — per-share figures before and after that point are not comparable.
