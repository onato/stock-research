Linde Reports Third-Quarter 2026 Results

Third-Quarter Highlights
       ➢ Sales $9.4 billion, up 9% YoY, underlying sales up 5%
       ➢ Operating profit $2.2 billion, adjusted operating profit $2.8 billion, up 8%
       ➢ Operating profit margin 22.9%; adjusted operating profit margin 29.4%
       ➢ EPS $3.66, down 11%; adjusted EPS $4.59, up 9% YoY
       ➢ Third-quarter operating cash flow of $3.2 billion, up 8% YoY
       ➢ Full-year 2026 adjusted EPS guidance of $17.82 - $17.92 representing 8% to 9% growth
       ➢ Project backlog $11 billion

Woking, UK, October 29, 2026 – Linde plc (Nasdaq: LIN) today reported third-quarter 2026 net income of $1,692 million and diluted
earnings per share of $3.66, down 12% and 11%, respectively. Excluding Linde AG purchase accounting impacts and cost reduction
program and other charges, adjusted net income was $2,122 million, up 7% versus prior year. Adjusted diluted earnings per share was
$4.59, 9% above prior year.

Linde’s sales for the third quarter were $9,404 million, up 9% versus prior year including 1% favorable currency impact. Compared to
prior year, underlying sales increased 5% from 3% price attainment and 2% volumes primarily in the electronics, chemicals & energy,
and manufacturing end markets.

Third-quarter operating profit was $2,150 million. Adjusted operating profit of $2,763 million was up 8% versus prior year resulting in an
adjusted operating profit margin of 29.4%. Operating margin was 30 basis points lower than the previous year but flat when excluding
the effects of cost pass-through.

Third-quarter operating cash flow of $3,169 million increased 8% versus prior year. After capital expenditures of $1,392 million, free
cash flow was $1,777 million. During the quarter, the company returned $1,685 million to shareholders through dividends and stock
repurchases, net of issuances.

Commenting on the financial results and business outlook, Chief Executive Officer Sanjiv Lamba said, “Linde’s financial performance
continued to improve in the third quarter, with record sales of $9.4 billion and record EPS of $4.59, alongside strong operating cash flow
of $3.2 billion. During the quarter we took further actions to improve productivity and strengthen performance across our businesses,
while maintaining our goal to deliver industry-leading results.”

Lamba continued, “Customer proposal activity remains robust, particularly across electronics, supporting our confidence in continued
backlog growth. Regardless of the economic environment, we remain focused on operational excellence, disciplined capital
deployment and securing high-quality projects that will compound shareholder value over the long term.”

For the fourth quarter of 2026, Linde expects adjusted diluted earnings per share in the range of $4.40 to $4.50, up 5% to 7% versus
prior-year quarter, with no expected impact from foreign currency translation.

For the full year 2026, the company expects adjusted diluted earnings per share to be in the range of $17.82 to $17.92, up 8% to 9%
when assuming favorable currency of 2% versus prior year. Full-year capital expenditures are expected to be in the range of $5.5 billion
to $6.0 billion to support growth and maintenance requirements including the $7.9 billion contractual sale of gas project backlog.

Americas sales of $3,601 million were up 11% versus prior year. Compared with third quarter 2025, underlying sales increased 9%,
driven by 6% higher pricing and 3% higher volumes, primarily in the electronics, chemicals & energy, and metals & mining end markets.
Operating profit of $1,263 million was 35.1% of sales, 50 basis points above prior year.

APAC (Asia Pacific) sales of $1,950 million were up 12% versus prior year. Compared with third quarter 2025, underlying sales increased
8%, driven by 3% higher pricing and 5% volumes, primarily in the electronics and chemicals & energy end markets and project start-
ups. Operating profit of $534 million was 27.4% of sales, 70 basis points below prior year or 40 basis points when excluding the effects
of cost pass-through.

EMEA (Europe, Middle East & Africa) sales of $2,327 million were up 7% versus prior year. Compared with third quarter 2025, underlying
sales increased 3%, driven by 2% higher pricing and 1% higher volumes primarily in the healthcare and food & beverage end markets.
Operating profit of $817 million was 35.1% of sales, 80 basis points below prior year or flat when excluding the effects of cost pass-
through.

Linde Engineering sales were $582 million, up 12% versus prior year, and operating profit was $107 million or 18.4% of sales. Order
intake for the quarter was $499 million and third-party sale of equipment backlog was $2.9 billion.

About Linde
Linde is a leading global industrial gases and engineering company with 2025 sales of $34 billion. We live our mission of making our
world more productive every day by providing high-quality solutions, technologies and services which are making our customers more
successful and helping to sustain, decarbonize and protect our planet. Linde serves a variety of end markets such as chemicals &
energy, food & beverage, electronics, healthcare, manufacturing, metals and mining. Linde’s industrial gases and technologies are used
in countless applications, enabling space exploration and launch technologies, delivering ultra-high-purity and specialty gases for
semiconductor manufacturing, providing life-saving medical oxygen and enabling clean hydrogen production and carbon capture to
reduce greenhouse gas emissions. Linde also delivers state-of-the-art gas processing solutions to support customer growth, efficiency
improvements and emissions reductions.

For more information about the company and its products and services, please visit www.linde.com
