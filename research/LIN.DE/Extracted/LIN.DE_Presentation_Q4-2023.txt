     Inflation Reduction Act (IRA) Overview

      IRA was passed into law August 2022, in part to promote US decarbonization projects
       US tax code sections 45Q and 45V provide tax credits for carbon capture and low carbon intensity H2 projects, respectively
       The US Treasury recently issued proposed regulations for 45V


                            Section 45Q (pre-existing)                                                                                   Section 45V (new)
       Tax credit increased to $85/ton for CO2 capture &                                                        Tax credit provides up to $3/kg for low carbon intensity
        sequestration (CCS) – e.g. “blue” hydrogen production(1)                                                  hydrogen – e.g. “green” hydrogen production (1)

       Final regs issued on base credit under pre-IRA law in                                                    Proposed regs. on base credit ($0.60/kg) for electrolysis:
        2021                                                                                                           ‒ Incremental: power to be produced by new facilities
                                                                                                                       ‒ Time matched: power used within one hour of being produced
                                                                                                                       ‒ Regionality: power must be produced in the same region


                                     Views on Blue H2                                                                                   Views on Green H2
       Preferred option for production at scale                                                                 Lacks scale - primarily for merchant H2 market
          ‒ low technical risk                                                                                         ‒ Electrolyzers unproven at gigawatt scale
          ‒ lowest overall cost                                                                                        ‒ High overall cost
          ‒ most reliable and proven solution today                                                                    ‒ Requires abundant and cost-effective renewable power
       Most large opportunities in blue H2 projects                                                             Nascent market
          ‒ Linde awarded first world-scale blue H2 project (OCI)
          ‒ Clean energy project pipeline $8B - $10B, including the US
