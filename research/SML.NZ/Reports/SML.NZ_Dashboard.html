Collapse & Recovery: Margins and Profitability

Gross margin fell from ~18-19% (FY2018-20, a2 Milk exclusivity era) to negative in H1 FY2026; EBITDA and net income both went negative in FY2021 and again FY2024/H1 FY2026

Gross Margin

H1 FY2026 gross profit was actually negative (-$1.4m on $777.6m revenue) -- the company lost money before overheads.

EBITDA (FY vs H1, not comparable)

FY bars are full years; H1 bars are six months to 31 Jan and cover only half the year -- do not compare an H1 bar directly to an FY bar.

Net Income

Losses in FY2021, FY2023-FY2025 and H1 FY2026 span the a2 exclusivity loss, the 2023-24 solvency crisis, and the ongoing recovery.

Revenue & YoY Growth

Revenue growth is largely milk-price and mix driven (record $10.16/kgMS FY2025), not a reliable read on the underlying recovery.

Leverage & Solvency

Going-concern qualified: net debt, cash and equity are the metrics that decide whether SML.NZ survives its refinancing, not revenue growth

Total Debt vs Cash

Net debt = Total Debt minus Cash (the gap between the two bars). DCF net debt is a separate FY2026 pro-forma ESTIMATE of $125m after the 1-Apr-2026 Abbott sale and seasonal working-capital unwind -- not the raw 31-Jan-2026 reported figures shown here.

Shareholders' Equity

Two dilutive rescues built this line back up: NZ$200m raised Nov-2020 and ~NZ$212m net raised Oct-2024 against ~NZ$216m of cumulative FY2021-FY2025 net losses -- equity growth here is raise-funded, not organic.

Shares Outstanding

Sept-2024 recapitalisation nearly tripled the share count from 218.6m to 603.2m at NZ$0.60/0.43 -- any per-share comparison across this line is invalid.

Cash Flow

Reported FCF is dominated by capex cycles (Pokeno/Dunsandel build) and working-capital swings, not underlying cash generation -- see the DCF's owner-FCF component build

Operating Cash Flow vs Free Cash Flow

FY2022 and FY2025 FCF (+$140m, +$142m) were both working-capital releases, not repeatable earnings power -- the 10-year median owner FCF is NEGATIVE ($27.1m).

Capital Expenditure

FY2019's -$320m capex was the Pokeno (North Island) plant build, sold to Abbott 1-Apr-2026 for ~NZ$307m.

Per-Share Metrics

The Sept-2024 recapitalisation nearly tripled the share count -- treat every pre/post line here as two different regimes, not one continuous series

EPS (cents)

EPS is reported in CENTS. The Sept-2024 share count jump (218.6m to 603.2m) mechanically depresses post-2024 EPS versus pre-2024 EPS for any given profit level -- this is dilution, not a change in earning power.
