Model approach: The multiples sanity check tripped on implied GAAP P/E (100x vs a 47x ten-year average, itself inflated by trough-GAAP FY2023-25 earnings). GAAP EPS is not an earnings measure this DCF can be reconciled to: $7.4B of non-cash Allergan amortisation, $6.5B of Skyrizi royalty-liability remeasurement and $5.0B of expensed IPR&D take FY2025 GAAP EPS to $2.37 against roughly $10.60 adjusted. The fix applied was to lower the terminal cap multiples (base 18x→16x, bull 20x→18x, bear 14x→12x), since the terminal year sits past the 2033 Skyrizi/Rinvoq loss-of-exclusivity and the method caps a slower post-LOE pharma at 12-15x. Implied P/E after the fix is 92.1x (within the 94.4x bound); implied P/S is 4.94x against a 4.82x historical average. Read this as an income-plus-modest-growth name carrying a large debt load from the Allergan deal, not a multi-bagger — AbbVie's current price capitalises adjusted earnings that exclude the Boehringer royalty on Skyrizi and treat pipeline acquisitions as free.
