Aβ Confirmatory Testing is Not a Limiting Factor in the U.S.

Increase in Aβ Confirmatory Testing*1
   - Aβ PET testing
       Estimated number of tests performed was approx. 40,000 cases from January to
       September 2024 (predicted to reach approx. 60,000 cases in CY2024), approximately four
       times that of the same period of previous year.
   - In October 2023, the upper limit on the number of Aβ PET tests reimbursed by the Centers for
     Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) was lifted. This change is an opportunity for an
     increase in Aβ PET testing.
   - Aβ CSF testing
         Estimated number of tests performed was approx. 10,000 cases from January to
         September 2024. The number is approx. twice that of the same period of the previous year.
   - The usage of BBM*2 for pre-screening purposes has increased approximately fivefold, which
     may have contributed to a higher positive rate in Aβ Confirmatory Testing and improved
     efficiency of Confirmatory Testing.

 *1: Figures are based on external research (US AD Chart Study, Wave 2023, 2024) *2: Blood-based Biomarker   8

                                                                                      SC-AI   *1

                                                (Potential new formulation and administration regimen)
                                                                                   - Key factor 1 -
• IV-Maintenance treatment
    Monthly administration of 10mg/kg can reduce the number of infusions by half. This is expected to reduce the need for
    infusion visits, and the manpower and costs involved with infusions. (Approval anticipated in Q4 FY2024)

• SC-AI Maintenance treatment
    The weekly dose of 360mg allows self-administration or administration by caregivers at home/site of care. Terumo‘s SC-AI has
    the advantages of reducing pain with a tapered needle, improved safety with a plastic syringe, prevention of drug clumping due
    to its silicone oil-free formulation, and the convenience of being able to administer within 15 seconds (1 hour for IV infusion).
    There are also fewer administration reactions. It is expected to greatly contribute to streamlining the AD diagnosis and
    treatment pathway.
    (Rolling submission completed as a new BLA*2, and approval anticipated in the first half of FY2025)

• SC-AI Initiation treatment                                                                                        Tapered Needle

    All doses from the first dose onwards will be covered by SC-AI. This may
    eliminate the need for patient visits for administration, the time for infusion
    and manpower. It may also significantly shorten and streamline
    the diagnosis and treatment pathway, taking a major step forward toward
    the universalization and standardization of AD diagnosis and treatment.
    (Aiming for approval in FY2025)

                                   Situation in Japan

• AD diagnosis and treatment pathway has been standardized and implemented nationwide
  through OUG (Optimal Clinical Use Guidelines) framework and mandatory all case surveillance.

• ARIA*1 incidence is within the range described in the Package Insert.

• Currently, approximately 800 follow-up facilities (Dementia disease medical centers and medical
  institutions with dementia specialists) have agreed to treat patients who have been receiving
  LEQEMBI for more than six months and initiated administration sequentially. Collaborative
  promotion activities, such as meetings that connect physicians who initially introduce LEQEMBI to
  patients, with physicians at follow-up facilities in each medical region are also progressing. This is
  contributing to advance the standardization and universalization of the pathway.

• Plan to initiate DTC*2 campaign on TV starting on November 15 to raise public awareness about MCI
  and promote early consultation and diagnosis.

                                                                                      Conclusion
  The Alzheimer’s disease (AD) diagnosis and treatment pathway is being created by LEQEMBI. In the United States, the first country in
the world to launch LEQEMBI, the issue regarding reimbursement of Aβ Confirmatory Testing has been resolved, the utilization of BBM *1
has increased and we are seeing a large demand being created. This has led to an increase in patients waiting for the treatment, making
it important to secure infusion capacity and effectively deploy medical staff. We know the great passion and enthusiasm of the relevant
medical professionals who are working diligently with us. In FY2026, we expect wide usage of BBM for Aβ Confirmatory Testing in
the medical community and coverage of LEQEMBI SC-AI*2 as an option for the entire treatment period, from initiation to maintenance.
Through these efforts, PCP*3 will play a crucial role in AD diagnosis and treatment, and the AD diagnosis and treatment pathway is
expected to be further simplified. LEQEMBI treatment will become a much more common and popular medical practice.
  In Japan, the initial introduction facilities are expanding, and simultaneously, at the follow-up facilities which cover the patients after
six months treatment, PCPs as well as local dementia medical centers have started to participate in the treatment. The generalization of
AD diagnosis and treatment is progressing at a faster pace. We believe sales has entered the expansion phase. In China, market
introduction based on BBM, AD Digital Platform, and the Self-Pay Market are progressing steadily, and we expect rapid increase in
demand.
 Regarding regulatory status, we believe the EMA re-examination process is in its final stages. Opinions advocating LEQEMBI approval
from European patient organizations and leading AD experts have been shared. Approval by the UK authority (MHRA*4) has already
been granted.
 We are aiming to successfully tackle the two major AD pathologies, Aβ and Tau and continue with a robust pipeline for this purpose.
MTBR*5-Tau antibody (E2814*6) has confirmed its pharmacodynamic evidence by using the most modern biomarkers in
Tau research. In addition, AHEAD 3-45 study (Phase III study) with LEQEMBI is steadily ongoing aiming to start treatment in
Preclinical AD.
Eisai will continue to commit to function as a pioneer at the forefront of the world in the field of AD, and further collaborate with medical
professionals and, above all, with patients/families to realize the day that AD will be changed to a curable disease as soon as possible.
