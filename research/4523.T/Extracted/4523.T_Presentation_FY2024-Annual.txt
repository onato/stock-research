                      Enhancing Value of LEQEMBI
          Potential new formulation and administration method
1. IV-Maintenance dosing (Once every 4 weeks after 18 months of initiation treatment) Approved in the U.S. in January 2025
   •    Ongoing treatment with once-every-four-week IV maintenance dosing with LEQEMBI can slow disease progression and prolong the
        benefits of therapy.
   •    Long-term benefits of IV maintenance treatment have reaffirmed the clinical value of LEQEMBI, and is contributing to increase of
        IV initiation treatment.
   •    Contributes to reducing the time and effort required for health care facility visits, reducing the frequency of administration by HCPs*1 and
        costs involved with infusion.
   •    Under regulatory review in Israel, UK, Hong Kong and South Korea and under discussion with authorities in Japan and China

2. SC-AI*2 Maintenance treatment*3 U.S. PDUFA*4: August 31, 2025
   •    Enables self-injection or administration by a caregiver at home and minimizes the frequency of health care facility visits.
   •    Time required for administration is shortened to 15 seconds on average.
        This may significantly reduce resources related to infusion by HCPs.
   •    In terms of safety, the occurrence of infusion reactions, which are the most frequent adverse events, is
        reduced. This is expected to reduce the burden during administration.
   •    The introduction of the easily administrated SC-AI may lower the hurdle for long-term treatment,
        which may contribute to increase IV initiation treatment.

           E2086: Orexin 2 Receptor (OX2R)*1 Selective Agonist
                     Steady progress in clinical development for narcolepsy
• Narcolepsy: A sleep disorder characterized by reduced orexin signaling, reduced activity in the arousal center, and
  excessive daytime sleepiness
• OX2R agonist: Activate the arousal center and inactivate the sleep center by activating the orexin system


Features of E2086
• Using a compound library of 250,000 compounds, we identified one true agonist through high-throughput
  screening (HTS).
• Leveraging our world-class chemistry capability, we created E2086, with a unique structure and a 10,000-fold
  increase in potency relative to the originally identified compound.
• Its pharmacokinetic profile may be suitable for the treatment of narcolepsy and appropriate effects are
