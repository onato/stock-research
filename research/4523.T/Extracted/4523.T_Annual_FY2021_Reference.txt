Risks and uncertainties that could cause significant fluctuations in the results of the Group or have a material effect on
investment decisions are as follows. However, these do not cover all of the risks and uncertainties faced by the Group, and it is

this point in time.

that they will occur or be achieved.
Risks factors include risks related to management based on the Corporate Philosophy, risks related to maximizing the value of

human resources, risks related to information security, risks related to COVID-19, risks related to climate change, risks related
