        ◼ To clarify the fundamental earnings, we adopted “Core operating profit” as a new performance indicator linked to
          mid-to long-term enhancement of corporate value, and will continue to disclose
        ◼ “Core operating profit” is defined as fundamental earnings calculated by excluding temporary income and expense items
          not directly attributable to future earnings, from operating profit
                                        Excluded items:
                                        (1) Gains or losses related to product out-licensing or divestiture, (2) Gains or losses on disposal of tangible fixed assets,
                                        (3) Termination benefit costs associated with business restructuring, (4) Goodwill impairment losses,
                                        (5) Significant litigation-related damages or settlement expenses

            Adoption of New Capital Efficiency Indicator “Adjusted ROIC”
      ◼ ROE is susceptible to the impact of temporary income and expenses, as well as equity components not directly linked to
        operating activities, such as foreign exchange translation adjustments. Since these account for approximately 30% of
        the Company’s equity, ROE has limitations as a metric for peer comparison.
      ◼ In line with our policy of utilizing debt financing to further accelerate growth investment,
        we adopted “Adjusted ROIC” starting in FY2026 — based on core operating profit, the Company’s equity excluding
        foreign currency translation adjustments, and net interest-bearing debt — as a new indicator linked to
        mid- to long-term corporate value enhancement.
        Alongside with ROE, this metric will be used to monitor capital efficiency over the mid- to long term.
