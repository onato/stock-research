                          Orexin 2 Receptor Agonist E2086
E2086 is a small molecule compound with a unique chemical structure discovered by leveraging
the strengths of our in-house orexin pathway platform and the experience and knowledge
accumulated through exploratory research on the orexin receptor antagonist DAYVIGO
◼ Expected to improve excessive daytime sleepiness and cataplexy, which are typical symptoms of
  narcolepsy (E2086 is considered to activate the wakefulness center and inactivate the sleep center
  by activating the orexin nervous system)
◼ Plan to initiate Phase II study (Study 202) in patients with narcolepsy type 1 (NT1) and type 2
  (NT2) using once-daily administration
◼ Aiming to be best-in-class in the field of sleep disorders (narcolepsy)
Favorable POM (Proof of Mechanism) study results
• A single-dose POM study (Study 101) has been completed in patients with NT1.
  It was shown that once-daily administration of E2086 significantly reduced excessive daytime
  sleepiness compared to placebo and the comparator, modafinil.
• At the dose that demonstrated efficacy after a single administration, there was no observation of liver
  dysfunction and visual abnormality, it was considered that E2086 was well tolerated and had
  favorable safety profile.                                                        Presented at World Sleep 2025
