                        Mungari
                        Mungari produced a total of 58,509oz of gold at an average C1 cash cost of $936/oz and an AISC of $1,169/oz.

                        extended high-grade mineralisation outside of existing resources. Full results from resource definition drilling

                        drilling is expected in the second half of the year based on the initial drilling results.

                        Financial Performance

                        $711.2 million). This is largely due to the inclusion of results from Ernest Henry totalling $175.3 million (31
