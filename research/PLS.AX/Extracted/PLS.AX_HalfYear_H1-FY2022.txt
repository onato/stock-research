                    Pilgangoora Operations
                    As market conditions continued to strengthen during the half year, demand for and pricing of
                    spodumene concentrate improved significantly, resulting in the Company achieving record
                    production, shipping and pricing outcomes.

                    Total material mined for the combined Pilgangoora operation was 8,241,069 wet metric tonnes
                    (wmt) across the Central, Monster and South pits (previous December half year: 1,789,198 wmt).
                    Total ore mined was 1,418,556 wmt, an increase of 69% on the prior half year (838,589 wmt). As
                    previously announced in June 2021, the Company mined at a higher strip ratio during the half year
                    to access sufficient ore to support plant throughput as operations emerged from a period of
                    moderated production. Higher waste movement will continue (along with the associated strip
                    ratio) over the next 18 months to provide better access to optimal ore to facilitate the expanded
                    production profile.

                    Total waste and ore movements for the half year were below plan resulting in access to ore blends
                    required to provide optimal ore feed to the plants being compromised. In response, the Company
                    commenced an owner-mining fleet during the December Quarter to complement the activities of
                    the mine contractor. Both contractor and owner mining fleets are required to support ongoing ore
                    requirements of both plants.

                    Ngungaju Plant Restart

                    Following Board approval for the staged restart of the Ngungaju Plant in June 2021, the restart of
                    the Ngungaju Plant commenced during the period. The restart is being completed in two phases
                    with capital works initially commencing on the coarse circuit, followed by the fines circuit.
                    Following the commissioning and ramp-up of both circuits, the facility is expected to produce
                    approximately 180,000 to 200,000 dmt of spodumene concentrate annually.

                    The feed stock for the recommencement of the coarse circuit initially utilised existing weathered
                    ore already stockpiled at Pilgangoora that was not amenable to the flotation process. These
                    stockpiles have provided an opportunity to provide the plant’s coarse circuit with low-cost feed ore
                    during the ramp-up stage. Commissioning of the coarse circuit commenced and first spodumene
                    concentrate was achieved in October 2021.
                    During the period when only the coarse circuit is operating, lower overall lithia recoveries are
                    expected, until such time as the fines circuit is brought online to help maximise lithia recovery.
                    Commencement of commissioning of the fines circuit is targeted for the latter part of the March
                    Quarter 2022, with completion of ramp up expected from the September Quarter 2022.

                    maintenance downtime required to allow key plant items to be replaced and/or upgraded. This
                    was further impacted by the industry wide labour shortages in the mining sector.

                    Mid-Stream Project

                    The Company executed a Memorandum of Understanding (MOU) with Calix Limited in May 2021
                    and has been undertaking a Scoping Study for a Demonstration Plant during the half year which
                    is nearing completion following the finalisation of technical and engineering works.

                    The purpose of the Scoping Study is to support the development of a Demonstration Plant at the
                    Pilgangoora operation to produce lithium salts from fines-flotation spodumene concentrate and
                    establish a pathway towards potential future commercial production of value-added lithium
                    products.

                    Economic and commercial evaluations to complete the Scoping Study are expected early in 2022
                    which, if successful, will progress works towards the formation of a joint venture and an agreed
                    work program to develop the Demonstration Plant at the Pilgangoora operation. In addition, the
                    joint venture will ultimately seek to commercialise the mid-stream product’s process technology
                    in respect of lithium phosphate applications on a worldwide basis.

                    POSCO
                    In October 2021 the Company executed a Shareholder Agreement for the formation of an
                    incorporated JV with POSCO to develop and operate a 43ktpa jointly owned LHM conversion
                    facility in Gwanyang province in South Korea.

                    The Company will initially hold an 18% interest in the JV (with the right to increase its interest to
                    30%). The initial interest is expected to be largely funded from a A$79.6M Convertible Bond
                    Agreement provided by POSCO. The Company will supply 315ktpa of chemical grade spodumene
                    concentrate on commercial terms to the Conversion Facility.

                    The development timeline is likely to see major construction commencing in mid-2022. The initial
                    formation of the JV remains subject to the satisfaction of certain conditions precedent. These
                    include the parties finalising an initial budget for estimated construction and ramp-up costs
                    following completion of engineering and design works, as well as certain routine foreign
                    investment regulatory approvals in South Korea. These conditions are expected to be finalised
                    during the March 2022 Quarter.

                    Ore Reserves and Mineral Resources
                    In the half year the Company announced substantial increases in both the Mineral Resource and
                    JORC Ore Reserve at the Pilgangoora Project following the discovery of new pegmatite domains,
                    together with the integration of the Ngungaju Mineral Resource and Ore Reserve for the first time
                    (refer ASX releases dated 6 September 2021 and 6 October 23021).

                    The updated JORC Mineral Resource represents a 39% increase in the total Measured, Indicated
                    and Inferred Resource to 308.9 million tonnes grading 1.14% Li2O, 105ppm Ta2O5 and 0.59% Fe2O3,
                    containing 3.5 million tonnes of lithium oxide and 71.7 million pounds of Ta2O5.

                    Based on this updated Mineral Resource estimate, the Company announced a restated and
                    combined JORC Ore Reserve in October 2021 (inclusive of Ngungaju operations) of 161.9 Mt grading
                    1.2% Li2O, 100 ppm Ta2O5 and 1.0% Fe2O3. This represented a 54% increase in total Proved and
                    Probable Ore Reserve Tonnes and a 47% increase in the contained lithium oxide at Pilbara’s 100%-
                    owned Pilgangoora Lithium-Tantalum Project.

                    The restated and combined Mineral Resource and Ore Reserve estimates reinforce the
                    Pilgangoora Project’s position as one of the world’s premier hard rock lithium operations1.

                    2.7.4 Tax benefit from share trust awards

                    issued via the Trust.
