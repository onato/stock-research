14   REVIEW OF GROUP PERFORMANCE

     Performance Review

     Group Revenue of $995.6m for 1Q16 was $32.1m or 3.3% higher compared to
     $963.5m for 1Q15. The growth came from the Bus Business, the Rail Business,
     the Taxi Business and the Driving Centre Business offset by decreases in the
     Automotive Engineering Services Business, the Inspection and Testing Services
     Business and the Bus Station Business. While Group Revenue actually increased
     by $43.0m, this was eroded by an unfavourable foreign currency translation of
     $10.9m from the weaker £, A$ and RMB.

     Group Operating Costs of $886.2m for 1Q16 were $25.8m or 3.0% higher
     compared to $860.4m for 1Q15. While operating costs actually increased by
     $35.3m, these were mitigated by a favourable foreign currency translation of
     $9.5m. The increase in operating costs was mainly due to higher staff costs, higher
     payments for contract services, higher repairs and maintenance costs, higher
     depreciation, higher insurance premiums and accident claims, higher premises
     costs, higher taxi drivers’ benefits and higher other operating costs offset by lower
     fuel and electricity costs and lower materials and consumables cost.

     Group Operating Profit of $109.4m for 1Q16 was $6.3m or 6.1% higher compared
     to $103.1m for 1Q15.

     Net Income from Investments of $3.2m for 1Q16 increased by $0.2m or 6.7% from
     $3.0m for 1Q15. Finance Costs of $4.0m for 1Q16 decreased by $0.7m or 14.9%
     from $4.7m in 1Q15 due mainly to lower interest expense in Australia with the
     repayment of borrowings.

     Share of Profit in Associate of $1.7m for 1Q16 was $0.2m or 13.3% higher than
     that for 1Q15.

     Group Profit before Taxation of $110.3m for 1Q16 was $7.4m or 7.2% higher
     compared to $102.9m for 1Q15.

     Taxation for the Group of $21.9m for 1Q16 was 19.9% of Group Profit before
     Taxation compared to $20.5m or 19.9% for 1Q15.

     Group Profit attributable to Shareholders of the Company of $73.4m for 1Q16
     increased by $5.8m or 8.6% compared to $67.6m for 1Q15.

     Group Profit attributable to Non-Controlling Interests of $15.0m for 1Q16 increased
     by $0.2m or 1.4% compared to $14.8m for 1Q15.

     Revenue from Overseas for 1Q16 was 37.8% of Group Revenue compared to
     38.3% for 1Q15. Operating Profit from Overseas for 1Q16 was 41.0% of Group
     Operating Profit compared to 45.3% in 1Q15.

     A segmental breakdown by Business is provided under item 18.

     Revenue from the Group’s Bus Business of $492.3m for 1Q16 was $14.3m or
     3.0% higher than the $478.0m for 1Q15. The actual increase in revenue of $23.5m
     was partially eroded by an unfavourable currency translation of $9.2m from the
     weaker A$ and £.

Revenue from the Group’s Taxi Business of $333.7m for 1Q16 was $11.9m or
3.7% higher compared to $321.8m for 1Q15. The actual increase in revenue of
$13.4m was partially eroded by an unfavourable currency translation of $1.5m
from the weaker £, RMB and A$.

Revenue from the Group’s Bus Station Business of $7.8m for 1Q16 decreased
by $0.1m or 1.3% from the $7.9m for 1Q15 due to the unfavourable currency
translation of the weaker RMB.

Revenue from the Group’s Rail Business of $65.0m for 1Q16 was $14.0m or
27.5% higher than the $51.0m for 1Q15 due mainly to the increase in average
daily ridership.

Revenue from the Group’s Automotive Engineering Services Business of
$85.1m for 1Q16 was $8.1m or 8.7% lower than the $93.2m for 1Q15 due mainly
to lower prices for diesel sold to our taxi drivers.

Revenue from the Group’s Inspection and Testing Services Business of
$26.5m for 1Q16 was $2.5m or 8.6% lower compared to $29.0m for 1Q15 due to
lower business volumes.

Revenue from the Group’s Driving Centre Business of $10.0m for 1Q16 was
$0.4m or 4.2% higher than the $9.6m for 1Q15 from more business from corporate
customers.

Revenue from the Group’s Car Rental and Leasing Business of $9.4m for 1Q16
was the same as that for 1Q15.


Statement of Financial Position

The financial position of the Group as at 31 March 2016 remained strong. Total
Equity increased by $39.0m from $3,012.6m as at 31 December 2015 to
$3,051.6m as at 31 March 2016 due mainly to profits earned for the quarter.

Total Assets decreased by $74.4m to $5,141.9m as at 31 March 2016 due to a
decrease in non-current assets by $115.5m offset by an increase in current assets
by $41.1m. The decrease in non-current assets was due mainly to the decrease in
vehicles, premises and equipment and other receivables and prepayments. The
increase in current assets was due mainly to an increase in short-term deposits
and bank balances offset by a decrease in other receivables and prepayments.

Total Liabilities decreased by $113.4m to $2,090.3m as at 31 March 2016 due
mainly to the reduction in borrowings and trade and other payables.


Cash Flow

The Group recorded a net cash inflow of $100.1m for 1Q16. As at 31 March 2016,
the Group had short-term deposits and bank balances of $887.9m. After
accounting for the borrowings of $487.0m, the Group had a net cash position of
$400.9m. The Group’s gross gearing ratio was 16.0% as at 31 March 2016
compared to 18.5% as at 31 December 2015.

16   GROUP OUTLOOK

     Revenue from the Bus Business in Singapore is expected to be lower with the fare
     reduction and the gradual transition out of the Bulim and Loyang bus services.
     Revenue from the Australia Bus Business is expected to be maintained whilst
     revenue from the UK Bus Business is expected to increase with new services.

     Revenue from the Rail Business is expected to be higher with the commencement
     of revenue service of the Downtown Line 2 offset by the reduction in fares.

     Revenue from the Bus Station Business in Guangzhou is expected to be lower
     with competition from the expanding high speed rail network.

     Revenue from the Taxi Business is expected to be maintained.

     Revenue from the Automotive Engineering Services Business is expected to be
     lower as the prices of diesel sold to our taxi drivers are expected to remain low.

     Revenue from the Driving Centre Business is expected to be maintained.

     Revenue from the Inspection and Testing Services Business is expected to be
     lower with the expected fall in business volumes.

     Revenue from the Car Rental and Leasing Business is expected to be maintained.

     Costs will continue to be under pressure.
