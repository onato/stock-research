Page 15
Business Outlook

• The 2019 novel coronavirus outbreak in Wuhan, China in December 2019 (“COVID-19”) quickly spread
      internationally despite efforts to contain it. How the outbreak will turn out is currently unclear and a
      prolonged outbreak is anticipated. COVID-19 and measures to fight it will result in the economic
      slowdown of affected countries. Our taxi, public transport and transport related businesses are
      witnessing lower ridership and volumes as we face significant operational challenges.
• Notwithstanding the current uncertainties, the Group maintains its long term focus on the mobility
      strategy and continues to transform and build capabilities.
