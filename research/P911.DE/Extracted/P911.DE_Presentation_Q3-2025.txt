Porsche AG – Performance
KE Y DE VELOPMENTS IN Q3 2025

MES SAGE

Pushing ahead with extensive strategic rescaling and recalibration measures:
Porsche is taking the final steps in the realignment of its product strategy and recalibration measures to secure long-
term success. Recent product decisions reflect changing customer needs and a slower adaption of electric mobility,
enhancing flexibility for the years ahead.

Extraordinary expenses:
As a result, Porsche expects incremental expenses of up to €1.8 billion in FY 25 – all fully booked in Q3 25. In total,
extraordinary expenses related to the strategic realignment will amount to approx. €3.1 billion for the year.

US Tariffs:
Porsche has implemented mitigation measures, utilizing pricing strategies and strong brand equity. North America
remained the largest market at YTD 64,446 units delivered with an increase of 5 % YoY.

Product Highlights:
Strong demand for the new 911 Turbo S and positive media und customer feedback underscore the enduring appeal
of Porsche’s flagship model.
