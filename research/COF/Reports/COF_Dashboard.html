Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: No driver change (one pass; judged a denominator artefact rather than an over-rich valuation). The P/E trip divides the weighted IV by FY2025 SBC-adjusted EPS ($2.63), which is depressed by the Q2 2025 GAAP loss of -$4,277m (Day-1 CECL build on acquired non-PCD Discover loans plus merger costs) and by only ~7.5 months of Discover earnings against a post-deal share count. Priority 1 (normalisation) is already the model's base: normalised FY2026E FCFE, with the Day-1 CECL, integration costs and the Q2-26 reserve release stripped out and a through-cycle provision charged. Priority 2 (WACC premium) was rejected: the regulatory risk is already charged in the bear flows and the 25% bear weight, and passing on FY2025 EPS would need IV < ~$107 (~1.2x required TCE at ~22% ROTE, implying CoE ~19%). Priority 3 (growth cap) does not bind: the through-cycle revenue CAGR of 8.7% organic / 13.4% statutory is above the 3-5% path. Priority 4 (EV/EBITDA exit cap) does not apply to a bank. The P/E trip stands as flagged, and the explanation is in valuation_philosophy.sanity_check_banner.
