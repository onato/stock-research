                                   F low T raders 3Q 2026 Trading Update
Amsterdam, the Netherlands                   - Flow Traders         Ltd . (Euronext:     FLOW)      announces its
unaudited 3Q 2026 results .

Highlights

▪      The third quarter of the year can generally be            characterized      by subdued market activity and
       lower volatility, which impacts the trading environment and consequently our financial results.
       Despite this, we continue to execute on our Horizon 2030 strategy, as demonstrated by the
       structural growth in our traditional business.
▪      In YTD26, Flow Traders' ET        F value traded increased by 1       5% to €1, 699 bn compared to YTD25.
       During 3Q26, Flow Traders'          ET F value traded decreased by 2% to €5           53 bn compared to 2Q26,
       while the market       ET F value traded decreased by 6%          .
▪      Flow Traders recorded net trading income (NTI) of €388.2m and total income of €391.6m                             in
       YTD26, increases of 7% and 9%            compared to YTD25,       respectively. In 3Q26, NTI decreased to
       €84.5m and total income to €86.4m, 43% and 41% lower than in 2Q26, respectively.
▪      The traditional business        achieved an NTI of €33 6 .4 m, a 2 6 % increase compared to YTD25               ,
       underlining its continued strong growth.             In 3Q26, traditional NTI decreased by 46% to €70.8m,
       from €131.7m in 2Q26        .
▪      The digital assets business achieved an NTI of €5             1.7m YTD26 , a decrease of 4 6 % compared to
       YTD25 , reflecting co ntinued lower activity in digital asset markets. In 3Q26, digital assets NTI
       decreased by 15% to €13.8m, from €16.1m in 2Q26                .
▪      Fixed operating expenses were €180.2m for YTD26, in line with our 2026 guidance of €235                            -245m.
       Fixed operating expenses increased by 19% compared to YTD25, driven by higher technology
       expenses, the addition of subject matter experts, and one                 -off expenses, a s we continue to invest
       in the capabilities required to deliver on our Horizon 2030 strategy. In 3Q26, fixed operating
       expenses increased by 9% to €64.8m, from €59.3m in 2Q26.
▪      Total operating expenses were €            238.2 m for YTD26, an increase of 14%         compared to      YTD25,
       driven by higher fixed operating expenses, with variable employee expenses flat                      . In 3Q26, total
       operating expenses decreased by 18% to €68.9m, from €83.8m in 2Q26, mainly reflecting lower
       variable employee expenses           .
▪      EBITDA was €153.4m for YTD26, a 2% increase compared to YTD25, with an EBITDA margin of
       39% compared to 42% in YTD25. In 3Q26, EBITDA decreased by 72% to €17.5m, from €63.6m in
       2Q26, amid lower market activity, with the EBITDA margin declining to 20% from                          43%.
▪      Net profit was €97.6m for YTD26, a 1% decrease compared to YTD25, with basic and diluted EPS
       of €2.22 and €2.13, respectively. In 3Q26, net profit decreased by 84% to €6.4m, from €40.8m in
       2Q26, with basic and diluted EPS of €0.15 and €0.14, respectively.
▪      Trading capital stood at €1,137m at the end of 3Q26, compared to                    € 1,150m at the end of 2Q26
       and €848m at the end of 3Q25, and generated                 a 30% return on trading capital,        compared with
       53% in 2Q26 and 37% in 3Q25.
▪      YTD26 return on trading capital           is 47% compared to 59% YTD25.
▪      The number of FTEs was 630 at the end of 3Q26,                up 1% from 622 at the end of 3Q25 and down 2%
       from 641 at the end of 2Q26.

Market e nvironment

Traditional

In 3Q26, the trading environment was marked by                 lower activity . Market ET F value traded declined
6% QoQ to €20,550bn, with traditional            ET F value traded down 7% to €20,241bn, driven by EMEA
(down 14%) and the U.S. (down 10%). The quarter was also               characterized   by lower volatility, with the
average VIX falling to 16.0 in 3Q26 from 18.3 in 2Q26 and 20.3 in 1Q26,             which impacted       the trading
environment.

Flow Traders' ET F value traded fell 2% QoQ, against a 6% decline in the market. Traditional NTI was
€70.8m in 3Q26,     down 46% compared with        € 131.7m in 2Q26 . On a year -to -date basis, traditional NTI
increased 26% to €336.   4 m, from €266.3m in YTD25.

These results reflect the continued structural growth of the          ET F market, with YTD traditional value
traded up 42%,      which support s our Horizon 2030 priority of expanding and scaling our           ET F business.

Digital assets

Since 10 October 2025, the bitcoin and altcoin market has decreased materially. Bitcoin was down
33% compared to its peak in October 2025, with the broader digital assets market                    capitalization
declining over the same period.        Digital asset m   arket trading volumes and volatility have remained
subdued, with EMEA digital assets          ET F market value traded down 35% YTD            .

Our digital assets results      declined in line with lower market activity YTD. In 3Q26, EMEA digital assets
ET F value traded grew 11% for the market and 21% for Flow Traders. That said,                      centralized exchange
trading volumes across the broader digital assets market declined, reaching                        multi -year lows during
the quarter. As our NTI is driven by activity across the wider digital assets market, digital assets NTI
was €13.8m in 3Q26, compared with €16.1m in 2Q26 and €36.1m in 3Q25.                           On a year -to -date basis, our
EMEA digital assets ETF value         traded decreased 33%, with digital assets NTI declining 46% to €51.7m
from €95.7m in YTD25, contributing 13% of total NTI compared with 26% in YTD25.                           The tokenized
RWA market continued to grow over the period, increasing to USD 39bn in 3Q26 from USD 36bn in
2Q26.

We continue to believe in the long      -term trajectory of digital assets and         tokenization , and remain
committed to our Horizon 2030 strategy and targets, including growing our digital assets franchise
and tokenized asset trading. The continued growth in the               tokenized RWA market further supports
our strategy and long     -term opportunity.

About Flow Traders

Flow Traders is a leading global          ET F and digital asset liquidity provider, on a mission to become the
liquidity provider of choice in a 24/7 global financial ecosystem. Founded in 2004, Flow Traders has
built on its heritage in European equity           ET F s to provide liquidity across more than 25,000 products in
ET F s, equities, fixed income, commodities, FX and digital assets, on over 150 venues globally. With
more than EUR 7 trillion in annual value traded and over 1,600 active counterparties, Flow Traders
plays a central role in ensuring markets remain resilient an                 d transparent. The Company is investing
in frontier technologies to drive innovation across traditional and digital asset markets. Operating
from eight offices across Europe, the Americas and Asia, Flow Traders brings together over 600
professionals repres       enting more than 60 nationalities.
