Outlook – FY27
  • As with FY26 we will be providing guidance on adjusted EBITDA, gross capex and capital
    contributions.
  • Adjusted EBITDA is forecast to increase on FY26.
         • Consistent with the DPP4 decision, and;
         • An additional net $54m of revenue wash-up and IRIS1 adjustments.
  • The forecast increase in capital spend is linked to increased levels of replacement capex.
    We are expecting customer driven activity to be broadly in line with FY26 but noting the
    lower range for contributions as this can vary substantially related to the timing driven by
    customers for major project connections.
  • For FY27 the guidance range is as follows.
         • Adjusted EBITDA: $540m - $560m
         • Gross capex: $605m - $635m
         • Capital contributions: $160m – $190m
