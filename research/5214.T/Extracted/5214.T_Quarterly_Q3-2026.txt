1. Operating results and financial position

(1) Overview of operating results
     During the nine months ended September 30, 2026 (January 1, 2026 to September 30, 2026),
    although sales in the displays business and the electronics business increased, sales in Europe and
    the United States decreased due to structural reforms in the composites business. As a result, net
    sales were on par with the same period of the previous fiscal year (January 1, 2025 to September 30,
    2025). Operating profit decreased year on year due to an increase in expenses in the displays
    business. Ordinary profit decreased year on year, despite the recording of foreign exchange gains,
    etc. Profit attributable to owners of parent decreased year on year due to the recording of business
    restructuring expenses, etc.

    Electronics and Information Technology:
      Net sales in the displays business increased year on year due to continued strong demand. In the
    electronics business, net sales increased year on year due to strong sales in information and
    communication applications, mainly for data centers, as well as steady sales in automotive and home
    appliance applications.

    Performance Materials:
      Net sales in the composites business decreased year on year due to a decrease in sales at bases in
    Europe and the United States as a result of structural reforms. Net sales in the medical care, heat-
    resistance and buildings businesses were on par with the same period of the previous fiscal year.

    (Profit/loss)
      Operating income decreased year-on-year due to soaring prices of raw materials, etc. caused by the
    situation in the Middle East and the depreciation of the yen, as well as an increase in expenses
    related to the conversion to all-electric melting facilities and periodic repairs in the displays
    business, and expenses related to the start-up of glass manufacturing facilities at Nippon Electric
    Glass (Malaysia) Sdn. Bhd. in the medical care business. Ordinary profit decreased year on year due
    to a decrease in operating profit, despite the recording of foreign exchange gains from the
    revaluation of receivables and payables related to borrowings of overseas subsidiaries. Profit
    attributable to owners of parent decreased year on year due to the recording of business restructuring
    expenses as an extraordinary loss in connection with the termination of production activities at and
    transfer of a U.S. subsidiary of the Company associated with structural reforms in the composites
    business, despite the recording of gain on sales of non-current assets and gain on sales of investment
    securities as extraordinary income.

(2) Overview of financial position
    (Assets)
     Total assets at the end of the third quarter of the fiscal year were 656.4 billion yen, a decrease of
    44.9 billion yen from the end of the previous fiscal year. In current assets, cash and deposits
    decreased due to the acquisition of non-current assets, the acquisition of treasury shares, and the
    payment of dividends, etc. In non-current assets, property, plant and equipment decreased due to
    depreciation and the recording of impairment losses associated with structural reforms in the
    composites business, despite capital investments mainly in the displays business.

     (Liabilities)
       Total liabilities at the end of the third quarter of the fiscal year decreased by 34.1 billion yen from
     the end of the previous fiscal year to 171.0 billion yen. Current liabilities decreased mainly due to
     redemption of bonds and repayment of borrowings.

     (Net assets)
      Total net assets at the end of the third quarter of the fiscal year were 485.3 billion yen, a decrease
     of 10.8 billion yen from the end of the previous fiscal year. While profit attributable to owners of
     parent was recorded, treasury shares were acquired and dividends were paid. In addition, the foreign
     currency translation adjustment increased due to the depreciation of the yen against major
     currencies, etc.

    (Notes to accounting treatment specific to the preparation of quarterly consolidated financial
    statements)
     (Calculation of tax expenses)
     Tax expenses are calculated by reasonably estimating the effective tax rate after applying tax
     effect accounting to profit before income taxes for the fiscal year, including the current quarterly
     consolidated accounting period, and multiplying quarterly profit before income taxes by the
     estimated effective tax rate. However, if the calculation of tax expenses using such estimated
     effective tax rate would significantly lack rationality, tax expenses are calculated using the
     statutory effective tax rate.
