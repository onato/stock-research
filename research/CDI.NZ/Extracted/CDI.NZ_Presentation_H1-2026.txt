                                          Meeting Results Announcement


26 May 2026

Results of CDL Investments New Zealand Limited Annual meeting of shareholders

At CDL Investments New Zealand Limited’s (NZX:CDI) shareholder meeting, held in Auckland and online
today, shareholders were asked to vote on three ordinary resolutions. As required by NZX Listing Rule
6.1, all voting was conducted by a poll.

The ordinary resolutions passed were:

    •   the re-election of Eik Sheng Kwek as a director of CDI;
    •   the election of Julian Smith as a director of CDI; and
    •   the Board of Directors be authorised to fix the auditor’s fees and expenses.

Details of the total number of votes cast in person/online, or by a proxy holder are:
