FY25 Operating highlights

•   A solid sales performance, despite some industry
    headwinds.

•   Price increases implemented in key markets,
    aligned with our premiumisation strategy.

•   Growth of our global distribution footprint.

•   Leveraging of deep market knowledge and strong
    distributor relationships to navigate tariff impacts.

•   Reinforce Oyster Bay’s brand affinity and value
    proposition as a trusted quality super premium
    wine brand.

•   Over 90 million global consumers engaged with
    increased marketing investment behind the brand
    both online and in-store

•   The 2025 harvest, yielded exceptional quality fruit
    across all three wine regions.

•   Our sustainability strategy continues to deliver
    tangible results across ESG metrics
