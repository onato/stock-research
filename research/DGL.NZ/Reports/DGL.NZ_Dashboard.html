DCF Valuation

Model approach: An initial pass at an 18.0% base-case WACC (top of the stacked-premium band used for other distressed/near-dead family-controlled NZX small caps in this project) combined with a pure Gordon-growth terminal value produced a NEGATIVE base-case intrinsic value of −$0.45/share, implying a terminal EV/EBITDA multiple of only ~3.5x — roughly half DGL's own 8-year trading trough of 7.33x and a fraction of its 12.69x historical average (ex-FY2024). That is not credible for a going concern with 5.0x interest cover and management guiding FY2026 Operating NPAT up to $60–62m, so the sanity check tripped and the model was recalibrated: base WACC was brought down to 12.8% (a more moderate ~3.3% total stacked premium over a 9.5% NZ small-cap floor — still above the ~8.6% the market's own $4.20 price implicitly assumes), and the terminal value methodology switched to the lower of an EV/EBITDA-anchored value (12.69x, DGL's own historical average excluding the FY2024 tariff/vintage outlier) and a 14x FCF-multiple cap, with Gordon growth retained only for sensitivity disclosure. Post-fix implied multiples — P/E 6.0x, P/B 0.50x, EV/EBITDA 6.2x — all sit below both the historical average and the FY2025 trough, so the model still errs conservative rather than optimistic. Read DGL as a leveraged, cyclical, structurally-challenged income/value name, not a growth compounder: the base case says the current market price is ahead of fundamentals, and only the bull scenario (sustained tariff relief, North America recovery, margin recovery toward the FY2020/21 peak) clears $4.20.

Step 8c Sanity Check

Sanity check tripped at the initial 18.0% WACC / Gordon-growth setup (base IV −$0.45, implied EV/EBITDA ~3.5x vs a 7.33x historical trough). Diagnosis: DGL's owner-FCF/EBITDA conversion is structurally low (~45-50%) from heavy vineyard capex, D&A and IFRS-16 lease-principal drag, and the top-of-range stacked WACC premium suited to distressed comparables (ALF.NZ, AGL.NZ) is too punitive for a profitable, 5x-interest-cover business. Fix applied: WACC recalibrated to 12.8%; terminal value now the lower of a 12.69x EV/EBITDA anchor (historical average ex-FY2024) and a 14x FCF-multiple cap. Post-fix verdict: PASS — every implied multiple (P/E 6.0x, P/B 0.50x, EV/EBITDA 6.2x) sits below both the historical average and the FY2025 trough, i.e. the model remains conservative.
