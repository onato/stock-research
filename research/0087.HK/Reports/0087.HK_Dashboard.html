Stock-Based Comp & Shares Outstanding

Model approach: Sum-of-the-parts: owner-FCF DCF of the consolidated operating businesses, plus listed stakes in Swire Properties and Cathay Pacific at market, less holdco net debt and NCI. A 35% holding-company discount is then applied to the undiscounted SOTP NAV (-HK$57,481m, -HK$8.52/B share, the same in every scenario), anchored on the 10-year P/B ceiling of 0.40x — the richest multiple the market has paid for the B shares against book, versus an undiscounted SOTP of about 0.62x book. The 10-year average P/B (0.30x) is deliberately not used: part of that historical gap is Swire Properties' own discount to its NAV, already captured by marking SP at market, so averaging would double-count it. This drops the weighted intrinsic value from HK$24.38 to HK$15.86 per B share; the operating DCF (8% WACC), stake marks and scenario weights are unchanged.
