             Highlights of the Financial Results


 Net sales          329.1 billion yen (up 22.8 billion yen YoY)
 Operating profit    12.8 billion yen (down 4.9 billion yen YoY)


The Group’s sales increased thanks to increased shipments of
glass products, centering on automotive glass, and chemicals,
as well as the continued depreciation of the yen.
On the other hand, the Group’s profits fell, affected by price
increases of fuels and raw materials, sales price declines in the
LCD glass substrate business and the architectural glass business
in Eastern Europe, and reduced profitability of foreign subsidiaries
due to the weak yen.
