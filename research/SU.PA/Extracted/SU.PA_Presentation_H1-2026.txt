2026 Target upgraded
 The Group upgrades its 2026 financial target as follows:




     2026 Adjusted EBITA growth of between +14% and 19% organic

The target would be achieved through a combination of organic revenue growth and margin improvement, currently
expected to be:

• Revenue growth of +10% to +13% organic

• Adjusted EBITA margin up +70bps to +100bps organic
