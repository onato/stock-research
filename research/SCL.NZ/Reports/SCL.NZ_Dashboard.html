Operating Cash Flow vs Free Cash Flow

H1 operating and free cash flow are structurally negative every year — working capital builds ahead of the apple harvest (inventory, receivables) and unwinds in H2 as fruit is sold. Not a distress signal; log scale is disabled on this chart because both series cross zero.
