                The Freight Cycle: Revenue, EBITDA & Margins

                Annual Revenue & EBITDA

                FY2022-FY2023 are the COVID freight-rate super-spike (not repeatable); FY2024 is the down-cycle trough; FY2026 revenue is a record but PBT/NPAT fell 8.5%.

                Half-Year Revenue (H1 seasonality & trend)

                Mainfreight reports HALF-YEARLY, not quarterly. Each H1 bar is six months to 30 September; compare H1-to-H1 only, never H1 against a full FY.

                Margins Through the Cycle

                No disclosed gross-margin/COGS line — operating, net and EBITDA margins are the available profitability lenses.

                Net Profit After Tax (Annual)

                FY2023 $426.5m was the COVID super-spike peak; FY2026 $251.0m is down 8.5% YoY but still the third-highest year on record.

                Cash Flow Quality: Reported vs Owner FCF

                Reported FCF vs Owner FCF (after IFRS16 Lease Principal)

                FY2026: reported FCF $392.1m vs owner FCF $182.6m after deducting $209.5m of IFRS16 lease-principal repayment — a 53% haircut. This gap is the single biggest driver of the DCF valuation.

                Capex & Operating Cash Flow

                Mainfreight is asset-medium (owns much of its warehouse property) so capex is structurally heavy — FY2027 capex is guided at $234m, up from $189m net capex in FY2026.

                Balance Sheet & Shareholder Returns

                Net Debt / (Net Cash) — Bank & Asset Finance Only

                Excludes ~$1.29bn of IFRS16 lease liabilities to avoid double-counting the same obligation already captured as a cash outflow in owner FCF. FY2026 net cash is $26.6m.

                Shareholders' Equity & ROE

                Equity compounds at ~14.2%/yr through-cycle — faster than revenue (~9.7%) — because zero dividends-out-of-capital and zero buybacks mean all retained earnings accumulate on a static share count.

                Dividend Per Share (NZ$, Annual)

                FY2026 total dividends of 172.0 cents were held FLAT versus FY2025 despite the profit decline — a signal of management confidence in the earnings trajectory. EPS/DPS are in whole DOLLARS, not cents.

                FY2026 Revenue by Division

                Domestic Transport 46% / Air & Ocean 37% / Warehousing 17% of FY2026 revenue. Air & Ocean is the most cyclical, most freight-rate-exposed division.

                Regional Mix

                FY2026 Revenue by Region

                66% of Group revenue is generated offshore. Australia's profitability now exceeds New Zealand's for a second consecutive year; Europe, Americas and Asia remain sub-scale (Americas PBT was negative in FY2026).
