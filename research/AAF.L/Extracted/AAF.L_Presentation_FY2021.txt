Covid-19: continuing to support economies and communities
  ✓ The Covid 19 pandemic has contributed to a rapid acceleration of already existing trends across the countries where we operate with
    people, businesses and governments seeking access to more and better connectivity and improved financial inclusion.

  ✓ Telecoms and mobile money have proven to be even more essential and critical during this period.

  ✓ A huge thank you to our employees, business partners, governments and regulators for their efforts and support to ensure our network
    remains fully operational and that customers can access our services, and we continue to support the economies of these countries and
    the communities we serve. This has included free data, waiving fees on certain mobile money transactions and free access to educational
    websites for students.

  ✓ Early and swift actions coupled with a continent which benefits from low population density, less frequent travel, and experience in
    dealing with contagious diseases resulted in lower infection rates in sub-Saharan Africa. While the spread of the virus generally eased
    across our footprint through the course of the year, more recently we have seen new and more intensive waves. Thus far, this has had no
    adverse impact on our business, but we are constantly monitoring the situation.

  ✓ We continue to focus on ensuring the safety of our employees, outsourced partners and customers; ensuring that our network and
    distribution channels remain fully operational and available; ensuring that customers have access to financial services, and ensuring
    that at Group level we are in the right financial position to meet our financial obligations at all times.
