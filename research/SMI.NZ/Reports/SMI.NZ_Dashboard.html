Project Economics &amp; the Permit Binary

Santana earns no revenue. Everything below is a claim on the Bendigo-Ophir project's PFS-modelled cash flows, contingent on one regulatory decision due 16 December 2026.

Cash on Hand &mdash; the Funding Runway

Balance-sheet cash only. Post the Feb-2026 A$134.1m raise, presented net cash is ~A$185m vs A$277m pre-production capex — a funding gap the base case closes with a further A$140m raise.

Shares Outstanding &mdash; the Dilution Story

Restated onto one consistent basis across a 1:70 consolidation (Oct-2020) and a 3:1 split (Oct-2024) — the jumps in the raw history are corporate actions, not errors. Fully-diluted count is now ~992.5m per the Aug-2026 presentation (986m FPO + 6.5m options), above the H1 FY2026 balance-sheet figure of 835.6m.

Exploration &amp; Development Investment

Negative values are exploration and development spend, not distress — this is a pre-production developer investing in the PFS, not an operating company. Scales toward the A$277m pre-production capex once construction begins.

Balance Sheet &amp; Corporate Burn

What it has spent and what it holds, not how it is performing — there is no revenue to measure performance against.

Shareholders' Equity vs Total Assets

The asset build is almost entirely capitalised exploration spend (Exploration & Evaluation Asset A$71.1m at H1 FY2026) funded by equity issuance, not retained earnings — there has never been a profitable period.

Net Income &mdash; Corporate Burn

Every period is a loss — this is a pre-revenue developer, not an underperforming operator. FY2021's larger loss reflects a one-off; the underlying run-rate is corporate overhead plus non-capitalised exploration costs.

Operating Cash Flow vs Free Cash Flow

Negative FCF is the point, not a warning sign: it is exploration and development capital being deployed ahead of first gold (targeted 2028), funded by the cash and equity raises shown elsewhere on this page.

Share-Based Compensation

Small in absolute terms (A$0.1-0.7m/yr) relative to the equity raises funding the project — the real dilution driver is capital raising, charted separately in Shares Outstanding, not options/incentives.
