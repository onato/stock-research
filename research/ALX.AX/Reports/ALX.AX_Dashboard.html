Equity-Accounted Economics

ALX's dominant value driver -- the 30.8% APRR stake -- is equity-accounted, so it never appears in statutory revenue or operating cash flow

Distributions from Associates vs Statutory Operating Cash Flow

Distributions from associates are booked in investing activities under IFRS equity accounting, not operating -- statutory OCF understates ALX's real cash generation by roughly 5-6x.
