Dear fellow Shareholders,

We are pleased to present our third-quarter results for the three months ending July 31, 2025. The macroeconomic environment has
yet to stabilize with events continuing to unfold and the unexpected becoming the norm as businesses struggle to cope in an increasingly
unpredictable market. Despite these economic headwinds, Enghouse remains focused on providing predictable, stable and positive cash
flows provided by operating activities and healthy margins. We have maintained steady revenues and, through proactive and focused
initiatives this quarter, have reduced our costs overall while strategically restructuring resources to secure our foundation for
sustainable, long-term growth.

Revenue for the third quarter of 2025 was $125.6 million compared to $130.5 million in the same period in the prior year and $124.8
million in the prior quarter. Revenue has remained steady over the last four quarters, consistent with our acquisition driven strategy,
and reflects the strength of our recurring revenue model, which accounted for 70% of our total revenue.

During the quarter, we took proactive steps to align costs with revenues through restructuring and operational adjustments. Special
charges of approximately $3.0 million were recorded as part of these actions as we scaled resources across both our core business and
acquired operations to match revenues. We expect our cost management measures will continue to yield benefits in the future as they
take full effect.

Net income was $17.2 million or $0.31 per diluted share in the quarter, compared to $20.6 million or $0.37 per diluted share, reported
in the same period last year. Adjusted EBITDA was $32.3 million or $0.58 per diluted share in the quarter, compared to $37.7 million in
the same period last year. Both measures have increased from the prior quarter and are trending towards prior year levels. We continue
to remain profitable and cash positive in a difficult market, which has also adversely impacted many of our competitors.

Net cash provided by operating activities, excluding changes in working capital and income taxes paid, was $30.9 million compared to
$37.4 million in the prior year. During the third quarter we returned $16.5 million to shareholders through dividends and paid $1.6
million for share repurchases. Enghouse closed the quarter with $271.6 million in cash, cash equivalents and short-term investments,
compared to $274.7 million at October 31, 2024, with no external debt financing. We remain well positioned to leverage our cash
resources to take advantage of buying opportunities in this market that meet our acquisition criteria.

We enter the final quarter of fiscal 2025 with a healthy balance sheet and a leaner cost structure. As broader macroeconomic uncertainty
and geopolitical risks persist, we remain agile in our ability to adapt to new technological trends and manage costs carefully. We continue
to focus on completing accretive acquisitions, maintaining profitability and generating positive cash flows provided by operating
activities.

Today, the Board of Directors approved an eligible quarterly dividend of $0.30 per common share, payable on November 28, 2025, to
shareholders of record at the close of business on November 14, 2025.

Corporate Overview
Enghouse is a Canadian publicly traded company (TSX: ENGH) that provides a wide range of mission critical vertically focused enterprise
software solutions. Our core technologies are used for contact centers, video communications, virtual healthcare, education,
telecommunications networks, IPTV, public safety and transit. The Company’s two-pronged strategy to grow earnings focuses on both
organic growth and acquisitions, which, to date, have been funded only through net cash provided by operating activities as the
Company has no outstanding external debt financing. The Company is organized around two business segments, the Interactive
Management Group (“IMG”) and the Asset Management Group (“AMG”) due to their unique customer segments and technology
offerings.

IMG specializes in contact center, video software and services solutions designed to enhance customer service by increasing efficiency
and managing customer communications across multiple types of interactions including voice, email, social channels, web chats, text
and video. Products include contact center, video collaboration, video health monitoring, video capture technology, video room
systems, interactive voice response, artificial intelligence tools, outbound dialers, attendant console, agent performance optimization,
customer survey, business intelligence and analytics that may be deployed in private cloud, multi-tenant cloud or on-premise
environments. IMG’s customers are varied and include financial services companies, media businesses, education, telecoms, business
process service providers, technology and health care providers.

AMG provides a portfolio of software and services solutions to network telecommunication providers, cable operators, media, transit,
transportation, defence, utilities, government and public safety companies. Its products include network infrastructure, Operations
Support Systems, Business Support Systems and revenue generation solutions such as video streaming and cloud IPTV solutions. AMG
also provides transit e-ticketing, automated fare collections, fleet routing, dispatch, scheduling, communications and emergency
control center solutions for the transportation, government, first responders and security sectors.

Enghouse continues to focus on building a vertically focused enterprise software company with a diversified product suite and global
market presence. Enghouse’s financial goals are to grow our business profitably, generating positive returns and net cash provided by
operating activities that we deploy on building high quality products, funding acquisitions and returning to shareholders through
dividends.

The Company generates the majority of its revenue from recurring revenue streams that increase the predictability of its operating
results. While Enghouse continues to develop and enhance its existing product portfolio to grow organically, including expanding our
Software as a Service (“SaaS”) offerings (both private cloud and multi-tenant cloud), we augment and expedite this strategy through
acquisitions. This dual-faceted approach enables us to broaden the spectrum of products and services available to our customer base
more quickly than through organic means alone.

Third quarter overview
In the third quarter of fiscal 2025, Enghouse continued to deliver steady performance despite persistent global economic uncertainty
and cautious enterprise spending. Revenue for the quarter was $125.6 million compared to $130.5 million in the same period last year
and $124.8 million in the prior quarter. Recurring revenue accounted for $87.8 million, or 70% of total revenue, reflecting the resilience
of our SaaS and maintenance services revenue, as well as the strength of long-term customer relationships.

During the quarter, the Company undertook a strategic alignment of our business to better match costs with revenues. As part of this
action, special charges increased to $3.0 million largely due to restructuring expenses. We expect our cost management measures will
continue to yield benefits in the future as they take full effect, positioning us to benefit from a leaner cost structure going forward. Even
with this expense, we reported net income of $17.2 million and adjusted EBITDA of $32.3 million, lower than the same quarter in the
prior year, due to other factors such as the ongoing shift in product mix to SaaS services revenue and increased operating costs, primarily
related to recent acquisitions that have not yet reached our target operating margins.

On April 14, 2025, the Company acquired Trafi, a Mobility-as-a-Service (“MaaS”) solutions provider based in Lithuania. Trafi offers a
scalable and comprehensive MaaS platform designed to integrate complex transportation networks into a single, user-centric application
that enables cities and public transportation authorities to connect various mobility options. During the quarter, we completed the
integration of Trafi into our AMG transportation portfolio.

During these times of uncertainty and our entry into new markets, we are carefully monitoring our cash activities to ensure diligence in
deal quality and customer creditworthiness. Our collections continue to be at heightened levels as shown by the reduction in accounts
receivable. Enghouse closed the quarter with $271.6 million in cash, cash equivalents, and short-term investments, with no external
debt, providing the financial flexibility to invest in innovation and pursue strategic acquisitions, while maintaining disciplined capital
allocation.

Looking ahead, the broader economic environment remains unpredictable, with geopolitical tensions, currency volatility, and cautious
customer investment decisions expected to continue. With a diversified global presence, a stable stream of recurring revenue, and a
robust balance sheet, we are well positioned to adapt to changing conditions and navigate uncertainty.

decline in SaaS and maintenance services revenue is a result of a decrease of $10.1 million due to expected attrition from the Lifesize

professional services revenue.

is primarily attributable to an increase of $9.7 million in SaaS and maintenance services revenue, including $4.1 million from current year
acquisitions, $2.0 million in professional services revenue largely attributable to the same acquisitions, a decline of $2.3 million in software
licenses revenue in Networks Middle East and European regions and a nominal decrease in hardware sales revenue.

Foreign Exchange
The majority of our revenue is from sales denominated in foreign currencies. We do not hedge foreign currency exposure as most of our
major international operations fund operating expenses with local surpluses in cash flow provided by operating activities that provide a
natural hedge. While foreign exchange may have a significant impact on both revenue and expenses, the net impact on results from
operating activities is lessened. This will also affect the relative cost of foreign currency denominated acquisitions stated in Canadian
dollars.

During the three months ended July 31, 2025, the Canadian dollar weakened against most major currencies compared to the same
period in the prior year. As result, in the third quarter, there was a positive impact to revenue of $3.9 million reported in Canadian
dollars, while operating expenses and direct costs were also higher by $2.9 million compared to the same period in the prior year. The
movements in foreign exchange rates were particularly prevalent with the weakening of the Canadian dollar against the pound sterling
and the euro.

For the three months ended July 31, 2025, we recognized foreign exchange losses of $0.6 million related to foreign currency
denominated monetary assets and liabilities compared to losses of $1.7 million in the same period in the prior year. The loss was a
result of the movement in non-functional currencies on monetary assets and liabilities relative to the functional currencies of the
entities recording the gain or loss.

For the nine months ended July 31, 2025, we recognized $2.2 million of foreign exchange losses related to foreign currency
denominated monetary assets and liabilities, compared to $3.6 million of foreign exchange losses in the same period in the prior year.

Translation gains or losses incurred upon consolidation of our foreign operations’ statements of financial position into Canadian dollars
are included in our Accumulated other comprehensive income account on the Unaudited Condensed Consolidated Interim Statements
of Financial Position.

Share Unit Plan
On March 9, 2023, a new Share Unit Plan (the “Unit Plan”) was approved by shareholders. Under the Unit Plan, certain officers and
employees may receive RSUs and PSUs. The maximum number of shares reserved for issuance under the Unit Plan is a fixed 700,000.
RSUs and PSUs will vest in a period specified by the Compensation Committee (“Committee”), which shall be not later than December
15th of the third year following the year in which the eligible participant performed the services related to the grant. PSUs will also be
subject to performance conditions that are approved by the Board and Committee.

Restricted Share Units
Upon vesting, RSUs will be paid in either cash or an issuance of common shares, at the discretion of the Committee. The Company
plans to settle all RSUs with common shares. The share-based payment expense will be recognized evenly over the vesting period. As
at July 31, 2025, 2,105 RSUs were outstanding.

Performance Share Units
PSUs granted will be a bonus for services in the year the award is granted. Depending on the specific purpose of the award, the
Committee will determine the associated performance metrics, weightings and performance period. Performance metrics may include
financial (including, without limitation, common share price), personal, operational or transaction-based performance criteria. These
metrics may be measured on an absolute basis, or at the sole election of the Board and Committee, may result in the percentage of
vested PSUs in a grant exceeding 100%, but to a maximum of 200%. Upon vesting, PSUs will be paid in either cash or an issuance of
common shares, at the discretion of the Committee. The Company plans to settle all PSUs with issuance of common shares. The share-
based payment expense relating to market-based performance conditions will be recognized evenly over the vesting period, while the
share-based payment expense relating to non-market performance conditions will be recognized over the vesting period, subject to
measurement adjustments. As at July 31, 2025, 10,489 PSUs were outstanding.

Deferred Share Unit Plan
On March 9, 2023, a new DSU Plan was approved by shareholders. The maximum number of common shares reserved for issuance
under the DSU Plan is a fixed 80,000. Under the DSU Plan, non-executive directors of the Company may elect to defer a portion of
their compensation until they leave the Board and have the value of such portion fluctuate with the value of common shares. The
purpose of the DSU Plan is to attract and retain qualified persons to serve on the Board. DSUs will be paid in either cash or an issuance
of common shares, at the discretion of the Committee. The Company plans to settle all DSUs with issuance of common shares. The
share-based payment expense of DSUs will be recognized upon grant, when the DSUs vest. As at July 31, 2025, 15,323 DSUs were
granted and vested.

1. Description of the Business and Reporting Entity

Enghouse Systems Limited (“Enghouse Systems”) and its subsidiaries (together “Enghouse”, “our” or the “Company”) provides a wide
range of mission critical vertically focused enterprise software solutions. Our core technologies are used for contact centers, video
communications, virtual healthcare, education, telecommunications networks, IPTV, public safety and transit. The Company’s two-
pronged strategy to grow earnings focuses on both organic growth and acquisitions, which, to date, have been funded only through
net cash provided by operating activities as the Company has no outstanding external debt financing. The Company is organized
around two business segments, the Interactive Management Group (“IMG”) and the Asset Management Group (“AMG”), due to their
unique customer segments and technology offerings.

IMG specializes in contact center, video software and services solutions designed to enhance customer service by increasing efficiency
and managing customer communications across multiple types of interactions including voice, email, social channels, web chats, text
and video. Products include contact center, video collaboration, video health monitoring, video capture technology, video room
systems, interactive voice response, artificial intelligence tools, outbound dialers, attendant console, agent performance optimization,
customer survey, business intelligence and analytics that may be deployed in private cloud, multi-tenant cloud or on-premise
environments. IMG’s customers are varied and include financial services companies, media businesses, education, telecoms, business
process service providers, technology and healthcare providers.

AMG provides a portfolio of software and services solutions to network telecommunication providers, cable operators, media, transit,
transportation, defence, utilities, government and public safety companies. Its products include network infrastructure, Operations
Support Systems, Business Support Systems and revenue generation solutions such as video streaming and cloud IPTV solutions. AMG
also provides transit e-ticketing, automated fare collections, fleet routing, dispatch, scheduling, communications and emergency
control center solutions for the transportation, government, first responders and security sectors.

Enghouse Systems Limited is a Canadian publicly traded company (TSX: ENGH) that is incorporated and domiciled in Canada. The
address of its registered office is 80 Tiverton Court, Suite 800, Markham, Ontario, L3R 0G4. The Company has offices around the world,
including the United States, the United Kingdom, Sweden, Norway, Denmark, the Netherlands, France, Belgium, Brazil, Germany,
Ireland, Australia, New Zealand, Israel, Lebanon, Romania, Italy, Spain, Japan, Colombia, Croatia, China, Portugal, Poland, Lithuania,
Slovenia and Austria.

12. Litigation and Contingencies
The Company provides its customers with a qualified indemnity against the infringement of third-party intellectual property rights.
From time to time, various owners of patents and copyrighted works send the Company or its customers letters alleging that the
Company’s products do or might infringe upon the owner’s intellectual property rights, and/or suggesting that the Company or its
customers should negotiate a license agreement with the owner. The Company’s policy is to never knowingly infringe upon any third
party’s intellectual property rights. Accordingly, where appropriate, the Company forwards any such allegation or licensing request to
its outside legal counsel for review. The Company generally attempts to resolve any such matter by informing the owner of the
Company’s position concerning non-infringement or invalidity. Even though the Company attempts to resolve these matters without
litigation, it is always possible that the owner of a patent or copyrighted work will sue the Company.

In response to correspondence from and, in a few instances, litigation instigated by, third-party patent holders, a few of the Company’s
customers have attempted to tender to the Company the defence of its products under contractual indemnity provisions. With respect
to this litigation, and any other litigation the Company becomes involved with, under a contractual indemnity or any other legal theory,
the Company has and will continue to consider all its options for resolution and vigorously assert all appropriate defences. There are
no material claims outstanding against the Company as at July 31, 2025.
