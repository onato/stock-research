­Vonovia strengthened its development business with the
 acquisition of the project developer Bien-Ries AG in early
 April 2020. Bien-Ries AG is a German project developer
 operating primarily in the Rhine-Main region.

The investment program was once again the main driver
behind the company’s organic growth. In the first three
months of 2020, investments in modernization and new
construction projects came to € 291.5 million, up 20.4% on
the prior-year value of € 242.1 million. Hembla made a full
contribution to the quarterly result for the first time.

Group FFO increased by 10.5% from € 303.6 million in the first
quarter of 2019 to € 335.5 million in the first quarter of 2020.
