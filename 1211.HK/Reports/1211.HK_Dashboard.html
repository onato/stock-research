Q1 2026 Significant Miss — Base Case Requires Re-validation

Q1 2026 RESULTS WERE A SIGNIFICANT MISS: Revenue -11.8% YoY (RMB 150.2bn vs RMB 170.4bn in Q1 2025), Net Profit -55.4% YoY (RMB 4.08bn). Finance expenses swung approximately RMB 4bn YoY due to FX losses as RMB strengthened against export currencies. Goldman Sachs identifies Q1 2026 as the likely profit trough, but full recovery trajectory is not confirmed. Domestic China NEV market share has compressed from ~34% (2024) to ~21% (April 2026). Treat DCF Base Case as requiring monitored re-validation through Q2 2026 results (expected August 2026).
