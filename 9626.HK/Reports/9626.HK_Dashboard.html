Company Overview

Bilibili is an iconic brand and a leading video community for young generations in China, offering a wide array of video-based content anchored by its pioneering "bullet chatting" feature and a deeply engaged Gen Z+ user base of 376M+ monthly active users.

The company completed its first full year of GAAP profitability in FY2025 and continues to expand margins through advertising monetization powered by AI and an anticipated new gaming upcycle. Bilibili is dual-listed on HKEX (9626.HK) and Nasdaq (BILI) and operates almost entirely within mainland China.

Business Model

Multi-revenue stream model anchored in a vibrant Gen Z video community. Advertising (~33% of FY2025 revenue) is now the largest and fastest-growing segment. VAS including premium memberships and live broadcasting (~39%) provides the largest absolute base. Mobile games (~21%) are in a near-term trough ahead of a new pipeline-driven upcycle in H2 2026 and 2027.

  • IP Derivatives & Others7% stable

Competitive Position

Competitors: Douyin (ByteDance), Kuaishou, Tencent Video, iQiyi, WeChat Channels, NetEase Games, miHoYo

Market Position: Dominant video community for Gen Z+ in China with a unique ACG/PUGV long-form niche. Smaller DAU than Douyin or Kuaishou but far higher engagement quality (119 min/day, 80% 12-month retention).

  • Community culture & bullet chat: Culturally embedded with Gen Z; virtually impossible to replicate authentically
  • Content creator ecosystem: PUGV creators with symbiotic relationships; daily submissions +19% YoY in Q1 2026 from AI tools
  • Gen Z brand affinity: Synonymous with Chinese internet subculture; authentic vs. algorithmically-homogenized rivals
  • AI advertising infrastructure: ECPM +10%+ YoY; AIGC ad materials >30% of covers; AI-sector advertiser budgets +170% YoY

Growth Drivers

  • Advertising revenue acceleration: +30% YoY in Q1 2026, outpacing China digital ad market (+12-16%); AI-sector budgets +170% YoY
  • 15 consecutive quarters of gross margin expansion (37.1% in Q1 2026); operating leverage increasingly visible
  • New gaming upcycle: Romance of the Three Kingdoms: Wangdaotianxia (Q4 2026 expected); Three Kingdoms: Battle for the Realm SLG launched July 2, 2026
  • AI flywheel: accelerating content supply (+19% daily submissions), creator productivity, recommendation quality, and ad efficiency simultaneously
  • User engagement deepening: 119 min daily time spent in Q1 2026 (+11 min YoY); total user time +19% YoY
  • New US$300M buyback program (June 2026); mid-term targets 40-45% gross margin, 15-30% adj operating margin

Key Risks

  • HIGH: Gaming revenue trough — Mobile games fell ~12% YoY in Q1 2026; new title execution is unproven
  • HIGH: Competition — Douyin, Kuaishou, Tencent have multiples of Bilibili's DAU and financial firepower
  • HIGH: China content regulation — Gaming licenses, algorithm rules, VIE structure all subject to change
  • MOD: Advertising growth sustainability — 30% YoY partly reflects AI-sector tailwinds that could normalize
  • MOD: MAU growth stagnating at 376M flat QoQ in Q1 2026
  • MOD: HFCAA/ADR delisting risk under Trump; HKEX dual listing significantly mitigates investor risk
  • MOD: RMB 4.86B short-term debt on Q1 2026 balance sheet (vs. RMB 24.19B cash — well covered)
  • LOW: RMB/USD currency fluctuation; AI content labeling regulations (manageable)

Bull Case

  • First GAAP-profitable full year (FY2025) extended in Q1 2026; 15 consecutive quarters of gross margin expansion is durable structural improvement
  • Advertising secular growth engine: 30% YoY in Q1 2026 with AI-sector tailwinds and AI-enhanced ECPM providing platform-specific advantage for multiple years
  • Gaming upcycle: three new titles plus Battle for the Realm (July 2, 2026); Morgan Stanley models 7.8B RMB game revenue in 2027 vs. 6.4B in FY2025
  • AI flywheel self-reinforcing: content supply +19%, recommendation precision, ad targeting — compounding advantage from uniquely rich behavioral data
  • Shareholder return scaling: new US$300M buyback (50% larger than prior program); funded from RMB 24.19B war chest
  • Undemanding valuation: 1.25x EV/Revenue, 8-analyst Strong Buy consensus with median target ~$31

Bear Case

  • Gaming trough with no certain recovery: Wangdaotianxia launch timing and commercial success unproven; disappointing launch would materially impair H2 2026 rebound thesis
  • Advertising growth may be peaking: 30% YoY partly reflects AI-sector spending that could normalize; smaller advertiser base is a liability if China ad demand softens
  • MAU growth plateaued at 376M; without new user cohorts, monetization must bear the entire revenue burden
  • Regulatory and geopolitical overhang: HFCAA enforcement revival, China content regulation unpredictability, VIE structure limits foreign shareholder recourse
  • Competition intensifying from all sides: Douyin's long-form push, WeChat Channels' creator tools, Tencent Video with multiples of Bilibili's budgets
  • Balance sheet leverage elevated relative to earnings maturity: only turned GAAP-profitable in FY2025
