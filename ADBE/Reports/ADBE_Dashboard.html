DCF Valuation

ⓘ Valuation Context — DCF Sanity Check: PASSED
Adobe at $197 is the inverse of a typical DCF risk situation. The common DCF error is over-optimism (extrapolating peak growth, using too-low a WACC). Here, the market has applied a ~56% discount to the base-case intrinsic value ($446). The base IV implies only 26.7x trailing P/E and 18x P/FCF — below Adobe's historical median P/E of ~35x and the low end of its 25–45x P/FCF range. The DCF is not inflating the target; it uses below-average terminal multiples.

The current stock price sits below even the bear-case IV ($273.55), implying the market is pricing Adobe more pessimistically than this model's worst scenario — which assumes FCF growth decelerating to 2% over 5 years at a 12% WACC. The wide bull/bear spread ($273–$662) correctly reflects the binary AI disruption question: strong fundamentals (96% subscription revenue, $9.8B FCF, $22.5B remaining performance obligations, ~11x forward P/E — cheapest mega-cap software) versus market fear that generative AI commoditizes professional creative tools.
