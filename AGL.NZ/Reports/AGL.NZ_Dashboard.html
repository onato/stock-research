                ⚠ Model Approach — Read Before the DCF Numbers Below

                This is not a quality-compounder DCF. Accordant is a distressed, post-rescue-raise cyclical with no moat (fragmented, low-switching-cost staffing market) and structurally thin margins — the best pre-downturn owner-FCF margin was under 4%, and the FY2019-23 ex-COVID average was just ~2.15% of revenue. The model is built mid-cycle: revenue recovers gradually from the FY2026 base ($165.1m) toward $195-234m over a 10-year explicit horizon (extended past the usual 5 years because margins are still maturing), and EBITDA margin ramps toward ~4.5% in the base case — anchored to the FY18/20/22/23 average, explicitly excluding both the FY2021 COVID wage-subsidy spike (8.5%) and the weak FY2019 print (2.9%).

                A high 17% base-case WACC (15.5% bull / 19% bear) is used, built from an 11-12% NZ small-cap base stacked with four documented structural premiums: micro-cap illiquidity (market cap ~$11.2m), extreme cyclicality (NPAT has swung ~$16m peak-to-trough on an equity base of only $20-40m), balance-sheet/leverage risk (pro-forma net debt ~$24.0m against thin interest cover of ~1.4x), and free-float/governance concentration (Hull family now 65.28% post-raise).

                The result: weighted intrinsic value is negative (−$0.10/share) because mature owner-FCF conversion (~2% of revenue) simply does not cover the debt load even after a full mid-cycle recovery is capitalised in perpetuity — base and bear scenarios both show negative equity value. This is corroborated independently: pro-forma tangible book value is also negative (~−$21.8m once $31.6m goodwill and $13.1m other intangibles are stripped from equity). Even the bull case is worth only $0.084/share — still below the current $0.166 price. AGL at today's price is best understood as a leveraged option on a NZ labour-market cyclical recovery and further balance-sheet de-risking, not as an undervalued cash-generative business; the sanity-check multiples (not the FCF DCF) provide the more useful floor on downside.
