⚠

Going-Concern Material Uncertainty — Read Before Proceeding

The FY2026 audited financial statements (Grant Thornton New Zealand) contain an explicit going-concern material uncertainty paragraph. AFC survives only on non-binding, discretionary support from its controlling shareholder, NZ Silveray Group Limited (NZSG) — which is simultaneously the company's largest creditor (NZ$1,170,427 in related-party loans) and its largest customer (99.7% of trade receivables). This support can be withdrawn at NZSG's sole discretion at any time.

The FY2026 audit carries a disclaimer / material uncertainty opinion. Working-capital deficit is NZ$1.3M; H1-2026 shareholders' equity was briefly negative (-NZ$120,066) before a year-end debt-forgiveness injection restored it to +NZ$206,390.

The stock is mechanically priced at the NZX minimum tick of NZ$0.001 — a market-structure price floor that has not moved in over four years — which is approximately 7x the probability-weighted intrinsic value of NZ$0.000136/share. This is not a conventional value or income investment. See the Model Approach warning above the DCF section for details.

                Model Approach: Asset/Scenario Shell Valuation, Not a Growth DCF

                A standard perpetuity-growth DCF tripped the sanity check for AFC.NZ and was replaced with an asset/scenario-based shell valuation. AFC has never generated repeatable positive owner free cash flow post-2020, revenue has collapsed 78% year-on-year, and the FY2026 audit carries a going-concern material uncertainty paragraph — there is no genuine cash-flow stream to project or discount.

                Instead, equity value is modelled as a probability-weighted mix of three scenarios: Bear — insolvency / wind-up, ordinary equity holders wiped out (NZ$0.0000/share); Base — shell persists on NZSG life support, valued at tangible book equity plus a modest listed-shell scarcity premium (NZ$0.000193/share); Bull — the Fast Track Group reverse takeover (RTO) completes and the clean listing re-rates toward the top of NZX shell-value precedent (NZ$0.000682/share).

                At the current NZ$0.001 minimum tick, implied Price/Book is approximately 17.7x FY2026 tangible equity. Every scenario — including the optimistic bull case — values the stock below the NZ$0.001 minimum tradeable tick. An income or value investment case does not exist at any currently tradeable price. To the extent AFC.NZ has any speculative appeal, it is purely as an option on the Fast Track Group reverse takeover completing on favourable terms — a binary, unpriced, MOU-stage event with no disclosed structure, exchange ratio, or timeline.
