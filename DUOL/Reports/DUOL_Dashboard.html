Company Overview

Duolingo is the leading mobile learning platform globally, offering language learning courses alongside Math, Music, and Chess. The company operates a freemium model with a free ad-supported tier and paid subscriptions (Super and Max).

With 52.7M daily active users, 133.1M MAUs, and 12.2M paid subscribers as of Q4 2025, Duolingo crossed $1 billion in annual bookings and $1 billion in annual revenue for the first time in FY2025. The company is now making a deliberate strategic shift to prioritize user growth over near-term monetization, targeting 100 million DAUs in the medium term.

Business Model

Freemium model with ~81% of revenue from subscriptions, supplemented by advertising, Duolingo English Test, and in-app purchases. FY2025: $1.037B revenue (+39%), $1.158B bookings (+33%), Adj. EBITDA margin 29.5%.

For FY2026, the company is deliberately moderating margins to ~25% to reinvest in user growth, accepting near-term financial deceleration for long-term durability.

Competitive Position

Competitors: Babbel, Rosetta Stone, Busuu, Memrise, ChatGPT and AI language tools

Market: Dominant leader. FY2025 revenue $1.037B vs. Babbel ~$270M. #1 grossing Education app on both Google Play and Apple App Store.

Moat factors:

  • Network effects: Friend Streaks, leaderboards. Over 10M users with 1+ year streaks.
  • Brand & viral marketing: AdAge Marketer of the Year. Massive social-media-driven word of mouth.
  • Data & AI advantage: Billions of sessions power personalization. 'Green Machine' A/B testing at scale.
  • Free tier funnel: 133.1M MAUs create conversion pipeline at scale competitors cannot replicate.
  • Weaknesses: Growth decelerating, platform dependency on Apple/Google, effectiveness debate vs. immersive learning.

Growth Drivers

  • Strategic priority shift: targeting 100M DAUs medium-term from 52.7M today; investing in free experience
  • Duolingo Max tier (AI features: Video Call, Roleplay, Explain My Answer) - growing adoption, higher ARPU
  • 'AI-first' declaration (April 2025) - company treating AI as platform-level transformation, like mobile in 2013
  • Chess course is fastest-growing subject; Math and Music expanding the multi-subject platform
  • International expansion - English learners in Asia (China, India, Japan, Korea) significantly underpenetrated
  • Duolingo English Test accepted at 5,500+ universities in 100+ countries - standalone revenue stream
  • $400M share buyback authorized (first capital return in company history), no expiration date

Key Risks

  • HIGH: DAU and bookings growth deceleration - DAU slowing from 54% peak to ~20% guided FY2026; bookings from 33% to 10-12%
  • HIGH: AI competition - ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude improving as free language tutors; Duolingo's 'AI-first' pivot is both response and acknowledgment
  • HIGH: EBITDA margin guided to compress ~450bps to ~25%; missed analyst consensus by ~$83M - significant credibility gap
  • HIGH: Strategic shift execution risk - unproven whether user growth investment translates to monetization
  • MOD: CFO transition - Gillian Munson started Feb 23, 2026, days before critical earnings; lacks institutional knowledge
  • MOD: Gross margin pressure from AI costs (Video Call, Max tier generative AI) - 80bps decline in FY2025
  • MOD: Platform dependency on Apple and Google (15-30% take rates on IAP)
  • MOD: Currency exposure - 50%+ bookings from outside US; $1M quarterly impact per 1% FX move

Bull Case

  • Milestone year: $1B+ revenue, $1B+ bookings, 50M+ DAUs all crossed in FY2025 - execution was strong
  • FY2026 mirrors the 2022-2023 investment cycle that drove DAUs from 20M to 50M+ - management has done this before
  • 100M DAU target leaves room to nearly double the user base; at current ARPU implies dramatically higher revenue
  • Fortress balance sheet: $1.036B cash, zero debt, >$350M FCF guided for FY2026 - no existential risk
  • ~23 analysts maintain Buy with avg. price target ~$267 (127% upside from $117.45)
  • 10M+ users with 1+ year streaks; 39.6% DAU/MAU ratio reflects exceptional daily engagement
  • AI-first strategy could widen moat as Duolingo's data + user scale compound over 2-3 years

Bear Case

  • Bookings guidance +10-12% vs. analyst consensus +34% - a $100M+ miss on the single most important forward metric
  • EBITDA guidance missed consensus by ~$83M ($302M vs. ~$385M) - magnitude of miss unusual; estimate resets ahead
  • Stock already -78% from May 2025 peak ($544); all investors above ~$150 are underwater including recent 'dip' buyers
  • 'Invest for growth' pivots can become permanent margin resets if user growth fails to reaccelerate materially
  • CFO transition risk at most critical strategic juncture; Munson delivered FY2026 guidance just days into her role
  • GenAI competitors (ChatGPT, Gemini, Claude) improve monthly as free language tutors, eroding unique value proposition
  • Even at $117.45: ~11x FY2026 revenue, ~39x FY2026 Adj EBITDA - not obviously cheap for decelerating growth
