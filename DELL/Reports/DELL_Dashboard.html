Valuation Commentary

Dell's DCF intrinsic value of $129 (base), $205 (bull), and $56 (bear) reflects the complex dynamics of a hardware company benefiting from the AI infrastructure cycle but constrained by thin margins and significant legacy debt (~$20.9B net debt). The base case offers only ~10% upside from the current $117, highlighting a thin margin of safety.

At ~13.5x FY2026 guided GAAP EPS and ~12x non-GAAP EPS, Dell is not obviously cheap for a commodity-exposed hardware business. The investment thesis depends critically on FCF recovery from the depressed FY2025 level ($1.9B) toward normalized levels ($5-7B) as AI server working capital dynamics stabilize.
